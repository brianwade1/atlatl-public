# Defense of the Lydian Republic

## Scenario Specification

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All countries, political events, military organizations, and geographic locations described in this document are invented for research and educational use.

## 1. Scenario Scope and Start Conditions

Play begins on **18 May 2030 at 0600 local time (H+48)**, forty-eight hours after the Vostian invasion of Lydia began. The scenario covers the next **10 days** of the campaign in **20 complete turns of 12 hours each** and focuses on brigade-level ground operations in eastern Lydia.

The Republic of Lydia is a small democratic state located along the frontier of the Federation of Vostia, a larger regional power. Lydia occupies strategically important territory between Vostia and several neighboring states beyond its western border. Its highways, rail lines, and energy infrastructure provide the principal overland connections across the region. Lydia has maintained close economic and security relationships with these states for more than a decade and is a partner—but not a formal member—of the fictional **Regional Defense Coalition (RDC)**. Lydia recently ratified a Defense Cooperation and Access Agreement permitting rotational coalition deployments, prepositioning of equipment, and political consultation in response to armed aggression.

Vostia considers Lydia part of its traditional sphere of influence. Vostian leaders claim that Lydia's growing relationship with the RDC threatens their national security and discriminates against ethnic Vostian communities living in Lydia's eastern provinces. Over several years, Vostia has used diplomatic pressure, economic coercion, information operations, cyberattacks, and covert assistance to separatist organizations in an attempt to weaken the Lydian government and reverse its coalition alignment.

The immediate crisis followed violent demonstrations in the eastern Lydian cities of Kirov and Aradesh. An explosion at a separatist political office killed several prominent activists, and Vostia accused Lydian security forces of responsibility. Lydia denied the allegation and claimed that the explosion was a Vostian provocation. Within days, armed separatists seized government buildings, and Vostia recognized self-declared republics centered on Kirov and Aradesh. Vostian forces already assembled near the border for a large-scale exercise then transitioned to combat deployment.

Vostia crossed into Lydia on 16 May 2030, describing its operation as a limited intervention to protect threatened communities and restore regional stability. Vostian armored and mechanized formations overwhelmed dispersed Lydian border forces and advanced toward Kirov and Aradesh. Lydia invoked its Defense Cooperation and Access Agreement and requested immediate RDC military assistance.

After emergency consultation, the RDC Council authorized coalition forces already in Lydia for **Lydian Shield 30** to defend internationally recognized Lydian territory. The multinational **RDC Coalition Land Corps** assumed responsibility for the ground defense with two divisions and a corps-reserve armored brigade. Some formations were already deployed for the exercise, while others completed accelerated movement or equipment draw during the first forty-eight hours. Vostia therefore retains a temporary advantage in mass, readiness, and operational momentum when play begins.

## 2. The Belligerents

### Blue: RDC Coalition Land Corps

Blue represents the multinational RDC Coalition Land Corps deployed at the request of the internationally recognized Lydian government. The corps controls the 1st Multinational Armored Division, 2nd Multinational Infantry Division, and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade. The RDC Council has authorized military operations within Lydian territory but has not authorized a general invasion of Vostia. National contingents remain under coalition operational control while retaining limitations imposed by their contributing governments.

Lydian military and security forces remain active in the wider conflict, but they are not represented by maneuver counters in the base scenario. They protect rear areas, provide local intelligence, control displaced civilians, and secure infrastructure behind coalition positions. This keeps the playable Blue force focused on the Coalition Land Corps while retaining Lydia's role in the larger narrative.

### Red: Vostian 3rd Field Army

Red represents the Vostian 3rd Field Army, which controls the 7th and 12th Maneuver Corps, four divisions, and two corps artillery groups. Vostia publicly characterizes the campaign as a limited peace-enforcement operation, but its military actions are intended to seize key Lydian territory, discredit the Defense Cooperation and Access Agreement, and demonstrate that the RDC cannot protect its partners.

Vostia seeks a rapid decision before international diplomatic and economic pressure rises or additional coalition combat power arrives. The 12th Maneuver Corps is the field-army main effort in the central and southern sectors; the 7th Maneuver Corps supports in the north.

## 3. Political Rationale and Strategic Objectives

| Side | Political rationale | Strategic interest | Military requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regional Defense Coalition | Fulfill the Defense Cooperation and Access Agreement and assist Lydia in defending its sovereignty. | Preserve coalition credibility, prevent Vostian domination of Lydia, and maintain regional access and stability. | Retain the principal cities and transportation network, deny the Arel crossings, contain penetrations, and preserve combat power for counterattack. |
| Vostia | Protect ethnic Vostian communities and respond to an alleged threat from expanding coalition military presence. | Return Lydia to Vostia's sphere of influence, weaken RDC regional relationships, and control strategic approaches to Vostia. | Seize the eastern cities and crossing network, displace the coalition westward, and consolidate before reinforcements change the balance. |

### 3.1 Coalition Objectives

The RDC Coalition Land Corps must:

1. Retain Aradesh and Kirov as the coalition's required end-state urban terrain and preserve the Aradesh–Belas connection.
2. Deny Vostian seizure or use of the Arel crossings at Kirov, Belas, and Velin and of the Northwest and Maren bridges.
3. Prevent either flank from being turned through the Drovna Heights or Maren Lowlands.
4. Fix or contain operational penetrations and commit the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade when required for the decisive block or counterattack.
5. Preserve sufficient combat power, essential infrastructure, and political cohesion through the 10-day operation.

### 3.2 Vostian Objectives

The Vostian 3rd Field Army must:

1. Seize Aradesh, Kirov, Belas, and Velin.
2. Seize and retain the Northwest Bridge and secure the Arel transportation network.
3. Advance through PL Punch and PL Hook to establish a defensible guard along PL Jab.
4. Protect the field-army main effort, preserve routes and corps seams, and defeat coalition counterattacks.
5. Retain the captured objectives long enough to establish a territorial fait accompli.

The four urban locations—Kirov, Belas, Velin, and Aradesh—are the engine-scored objectives. In the coalition operational end state, Aradesh and Kirov are required retention terrain; Belas and Velin are scored crossing locations whose operational importance is denial of usable Arel crossings. Other bridges, routes, and control measures are operational objectives defined by the mission orders and graphics rather than separate scored locations.

## 4. Area of Operations

The playable map is a **20-column by 14-row** hex grid at approximately **10 kilometers per hex**, representing an operational area of roughly 200 kilometers east–west by 140 kilometers north–south. The Vostian border lies along the eastern edge; the Lydian capital and coalition theater-support areas are off-map to the west.

### The Aradesh Plain

The center of the area is open agricultural terrain surrounding Aradesh. It supports rapid armored and mechanized movement and offers several possible avenues of approach. Aradesh is the largest city in the operational area and the principal eastern political objective.

### The Kirov Transportation Hub

Kirov sits on the northern east–west route near an Arel crossing and the road toward the Northwest Bridge. Control of the city supports movement between the northern sector and central Lydia. No vertical road directly links Kirov, Belas, and Velin.

### The Drovna Heights

Rough highlands dominate the northern portion of the area. Movement is slower, observation is restricted, and the terrain favors infantry. The Northwest Bridge and its approaches provide an additional crossing route through this sector.

### The Maren Lowlands

Marshes, drainage channels, and seasonally flooded ground restrict movement south of Aradesh. A limited number of firm routes pass through the lowlands, including the route through Velin and the additional Maren Bridge crossing.

### The Arel River Belt

The Arel River forms the last major natural obstacle before the approaches to the Lydian capital. The scored urban crossings at Kirov, Belas, and Velin are supplemented by the Northwest and Maren bridges. Atlatl has no distinct bridge terrain type; the additional bridges are represented by road paths across intentionally traversable river-gap edges.

## 5. Forces Represented

The playable order of battle contains **21 counters: nine Blue and twelve Red**. Headquarters appear in the command hierarchy and mission orders but are not playable counters.

### RDC Coalition Land Corps — 9 counters

- **1st Multinational Armored Division:** one armored brigade, two mechanized brigades, and one division artillery group.
- **2nd Multinational Infantry Division:** one mechanized brigade, two light infantry brigades, and one division artillery group.
- **Corps reserve:** the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade.

### Vostian 3rd Field Army — 12 counters

- **7th Maneuver Corps:** the 11th Armored Division, 14th Mechanized Division, and 7th Corps Artillery Group.
- **12th Maneuver Corps:** the 18th Armored Division, 22nd Mechanized Division, and 12th Corps Artillery Group.
- **Higher-headquarters reserves:** the 12th Armored Brigade for 7th Corps during Operational Phases I–III and as the field-army-directed reserve in Operational Phase IV; the 24th Mechanized Brigade for 12th Corps during Operational Phases I–III before its Operational Phase IV southern guard mission.

Every counter begins at 100 strength. The scenario uses the native Atlatl unit types `armor`, `mechinf`, `infantry`, and `artillery`. Detailed formation roles and command relationships are defined in the [order of battle](game/order_of_battle.md) and side-specific operations orders.

## 6. Initial Operational Situation

At H+48, Vostian forces have penetrated the Lydian border zone but have not captured the four urban objectives. The RDC Coalition Land Corps has established a coherent defense but remains outnumbered and responsible for widely separated terrain. All four urban objectives begin under Blue control.

The scenario supplies doctrinally sensible default positions, but players may reposition their forces during setup:

- Blue places nine units on eligible non-water hexes from the western map edge through column `x=14`, inclusive.
- Red places twelve units in columns `x=18` and `x=19` on the eastern map edge.
- Blue completes setup first, followed by Red. Regular play begins with Blue.

The coalition commander must determine which terrain is indispensable, how much space can be exchanged for time, and when to commit the corps armored reserve. The Vostian commander must sequence two maneuver corps, manage reserves and supporting fires, and seize the objective system without losing momentum or exposing retained gains.

## 7. Game Scale and Duration

| Element | Current representation |
| --- | --- |
| Ground scale | Approximately 10 kilometers per hex |
| Map | 20 columns × 14 rows |
| Time scale | 12 hours per complete Blue/Red turn |
| Scenario duration | 40 player phases; 20 complete turns; 10 days |
| Playable-unit scale | Maneuver brigades and artillery groups |
| Forces | 9 Blue; 12 Red; 21 total |
| Unit information | Open; all units are visible |
| Initial city ownership | Blue |

Atlatl calls each side's activation a **player phase**. Forty player phases therefore produce 20 complete two-sided turns. The Blue and Red setup activations neither count toward the 40 player phases nor award city-control points. Regular play begins with Blue after both setup activations, and the scenario runs from 18 May 2030 at 0600 local through 28 May 2030 at 0600 local.

This engine term is distinct from the capitalized **Operational Phases I–IV** used in the operations orders. Those are conditions-based planning stages and do not correspond one-for-one with Atlatl player phases.

## 8. Objectives and Scoring

| Scored location | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Kirov | `hex-5-3` |
| Belas | `hex-5-7` |
| Velin | `hex-5-11` |
| Aradesh | `hex-12-6` |

All four cities begin under Blue control. At the end of each regular player phase, an occupied urban hex changes to the occupier's faction; an empty city retains its existing owner. The scenario's `cityScore` of 60 is divided equally among the four urban hexes. Each Blue-controlled city therefore adds 15 points and each Red-controlled city subtracts 15 points at the end of the player phase. Changing a city from Blue to Red changes the per-phase award by 30 points.

The engine maintains a single score from Blue's perspective: higher scores favor Blue and lower scores favor Red. Each Blue strength point lost subtracts one point, while each Red strength point lost adds one point. A unit that falls below 50 percent strength becomes combat ineffective and is removed; all of its remaining strength is then counted as lost. The scenario does not end early if either side loses all of its units. It terminates after 40 regular player phases.

Atlatl reports the final raw score but does not apply scenario-specific victory bands. Until balance testing supports defensible thresholds, the final score should be used for comparative playtesting rather than labeled automatically as a major, minor, or marginal victory.

## 9. Atlatl Representation and Abstractions

The completed scenario directly models the hex map, terrain fills, units, strength, setup zones, movement and combat, open unit information, city ownership, duration, and scoring. Other operational detail is conveyed through the scenario narrative, player decisions, and side-specific orders.

- Headquarters, command relationships, main-effort designations, reserve-release authority, Operational Phases I–IV, boundaries, and reporting requirements are not enforced by the engine.
- Airpower, air defense, engineers, reconnaissance, electronic warfare, cyber effects, logistics, and medical support are incorporated into parent formations or represented through adjudication.
- Lydian forces secure rear areas and provide intelligence but are not playable maneuver units.
- Roads and river edges are visual overlays. Clear, rough, marsh, urban, and water terrain fills provide the implemented movement costs and combat terrain multipliers.
- Because fog of war is disabled, terrain does not restrict unit visibility. Narrative observation effects, such as restricted observation in the Drovna Heights, require player judgment or adjudication.
- Atlatl has no separate bridge type; bridges use intentionally traversable river-gap edges.
- Weather and light effects in the operations orders guide player decisions but are not automated game mechanics.
- Political restrictions on attacks into Vostia are enforced through the map boundary and mission orders.

## 10. Design Intent and Development Status

### 10.1 Central Operational Problem

The scenario asks whether a smaller multinational coalition force can prevent a larger Vostian force from achieving a rapid territorial fait accompli. The coalition must defend multiple politically important objectives, preserve combat power and political cohesion, and determine when and where to conduct a counterattack. Vostia must exploit its temporary advantage without dispersing its force, losing momentum, or suffering casualties that undermine the political purpose of the invasion.

### 10.2 Current Status

- **Review baseline:** validated on 1 September 2026 against Atlatl commit `94159b455a2dfbf7ab797d176671bb57a87e3b4a`.
- The `.scn` file is a complete playable baseline with the map, 21 units, setup zones, initial city ownership, and scoring.
- The map JSON is the canonical geographic source; the completed scenario embeds that geography and adds scenario-specific state.
- The placement OOB preserves the reusable 21-counter force without starting locations.
- Blue and Red operations orders, synchronization matrices, support orders, and operational graphics are available as player briefing products.
- The baseline is suitable for playtesting, but no scenario-specific victory bands have been defined. Holding all four cities for all 40 scored player phases is worth 2,400 points, compared with 900 initial Blue strength points and 1,200 initial Red strength points. Balance validation should determine whether the `cityScore` of 60 and later result bands produce the intended emphasis; later variants should be documented separately from runtime completeness.
- Some detailed control measures in the staff products remain subject to scenario design or player adjudication and do not prevent normal Atlatl play.

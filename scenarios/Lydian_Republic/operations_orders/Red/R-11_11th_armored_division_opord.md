- **Effective:** On receipt
- **Duration:** 20 turns / 10 days; 12 hours per turn

- **Role:** 7th Corps main effort
- **Supported parent tasks:** R-1C1, R-1C2, R-1C4, R-1C5, R-1C6

```text

|-- 11th Armored Brigade — Armor
```

The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. The division receives priority of corps fires before the 14th Mechanized Division in every phase. The 12th Armored Brigade remains organic to the division but may be committed during Phases I–III only by the 7th Corps commander. At the start of Phase IV, or earlier on field army order, it becomes the field-army-directed reserve and may be committed only on field army order.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Operations

The division attacks west through the northern Aradesh Plain toward Kirov and PL Jab. Open terrain favors armored maneuver but exposes routes and flanks. Kirov is the decisive transportation objective. The division must protect its northern boundary with the 14th Mechanized Division, its southern boundary with the 12th Corps, and the routes supporting artillery and sustainment.

### b. Enemy Forces

Lydian and RDC Coalition forces can delay east of PL Punch, defend Kirov and routes west of PL Hook, and counterattack an exposed flank, seam, or retaining force. Their most dangerous action is to fix the division near Kirov while attacking the corps seam or cutting the division’s eastern routes.

### c. Friendly Forces

- **7th Maneuver Corps:** Conducts movement to contact to PL Punch, seizes Kirov and OBJ Bridge, conducts movement to contact to PL Jab, and then guards while retaining both objectives. The 11th Armored Division is the corps main effort throughout.
- **14th Mechanized Division:** Advances in the Drovna Heights, seizes OBJ Bridge, retains the Northwest Bridge, and guards the northern PL Jab sector.
- **12th Maneuver Corps:** Field army main effort south of the corps seam.
- **7th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to 7th Corps; priority of fires to this division.

### d. Civil Considerations

Preserve Kirov’s road network and essential infrastructure when consistent with the mission. Avoid unnecessary damage to bridges, routes, and facilities needed for sustainment or follow-on operations.

### e. Assumptions

- The 12th Armored Brigade can perform corps- and field-army-directed reserve duties without changing its organic relationship.
- Exact brigade boundaries, routes, assembly areas, and guard sectors not shown on the approved overlay remain `TBD—SCENARIO DESIGN`.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated orders govern transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **11th Armored Division attacks to clear the northern Aradesh Plain through PL Punch; attacks to seize Kirov and clear through PL Hook; conducts a movement to contact to PL Jab while retaining Kirov; and then guards along its assigned PL Jab sector while continuing to retain Kirov in order to destroy northern coalition forces, secure the transportation hub, and prevent interference with the 12th Corps main effort.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Defeat coalition forces in the northern Aradesh Plain and secure Kirov so the field army main effort can advance without northern interference.

#### Key Tasks

- Clear the division sector to PL Punch and PL Hook.
- Seize and retain Kirov.
- Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab without abandoning Kirov, the corps seam, or supporting routes.
- Guard the assigned PL Jab sector during Phase IV.
- Preserve the 12th Armored Brigade for corps and later field army reserve employment.
- Maintain contact with adjacent divisions and continuous access for corps fires and sustainment.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Coalition forces in the division sector are destroyed, displaced west of PL Jab, or unable to interfere with the corps and field army main efforts.
- **Friendly:** The division remains coherent and mutually supporting; the reserve remains available unless committed by proper authority.
- **Terrain:** Kirov is retained, the assigned PL Jab sector is guarded, and division seams and routes remain secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division employs the 11th Armored Brigade as its maneuver main effort. The 13th Mechanized Brigade clears and secures the division’s supporting sector, then retains Kirov. The 12th Armored Brigade remains withheld for higher-headquarters reserve duties. Corps fires support the 11th Brigade’s decisive task while retaining coverage of the 13th Brigade and critical seams.

#### (2) Phase I — Clear to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 11th Armored Brigade attacks to clear the division’s primary axis through PL Punch. The 13th Mechanized Brigade attacks to clear its assigned sector and secures the southern seam and supporting routes. The 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when the division sector through PL Punch is controlled, enemy dispositions are sufficiently developed, routes and seams are secure, and the attack on Kirov is supportable.

#### (3) Phase II — Seize Kirov and Clear to PL Hook

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 11th Armored Brigade attacks to seize Kirov as division main effort. The 13th Mechanized Brigade attacks to clear bypassed or supporting positions through PL Hook and secures the Kirov approaches, division seam, and routes. The 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when Kirov is seized, the division area through PL Hook is controlled, routes and handovers are established, and the division can continue toward PL Jab while retaining Kirov.

#### (4) Phase III — Movement to Contact to PL Jab

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** The 11th Armored Brigade conducts a movement to contact to PL Jab, develops enemy positions, and destroys forces threatening the division or corps mission. The 13th Mechanized Brigade occupies to retain Kirov and secures the route connecting Kirov to the advancing force. The 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when the assigned PL Jab sector is controlled, Kirov remains secure, and the division is postured for the guard and reserve transition.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** The 11th Armored Brigade conducts a guard along the assigned PL Jab sector to provide warning and delay counterattacking coalition forces. The 13th Mechanized Brigade continues to occupy to retain Kirov. The 12th Armored Brigade occupies its directed assembly area as field-army-directed reserve.

### c. Scheme of Fires

The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. Division fire-support requests follow these priorities:

- **Phase I:** 11th Brigade clearing attack; then 13th Brigade clearing action and division seams.
- **Phase II:** Isolation and seizure of Kirov; then clearing through PL Hook and protection of routes.
- **Phase III:** 11th Brigade movement to contact; shift to the most dangerous threat to Kirov, the corps seam, or the reserve.
- **Phase IV:** Guard force and any field-army-directed reserve commitment; then Kirov retention.

### d. Tasks to Subordinate Brigades

#### (1) 11th Armored Brigade — Division Main Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to clear the primary division axis through PL Punch. **Purpose:** Defeat the northern defense and establish conditions for the attack on Kirov. Supports R-11D1 and R-11D5.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize Kirov and clear assigned terrain through PL Hook. **Purpose:** Control the northern transportation hub and break the coalition defense. Supports R-11D2.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and destroy identified forces threatening the division or corps. **Purpose:** Prevent formation of another northern defense and protect the field army main effort. Supports R-11D3 and R-11D5.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Conduct a guard along the assigned PL Jab sector. **Purpose:** Identify and delay counterattacking forces before they can disrupt consolidation or threaten Kirov. Supports R-11D4.

#### (2) 12th Armored Brigade — Higher-Headquarters Reserve

- **Phases I–III — Task:** Remain prepared as 7th Corps reserve and execute only an authenticated corps commitment order. **Purpose:** Restore the corps main effort, defeat a dangerous counterattack, or preserve a decisive objective. Supports R-11D6.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Occupy the directed assembly area as field-army-directed reserve and execute only an authenticated field army commitment order. **Purpose:** Provide the field army a mobile response to counterattack or operational opportunity. Supports R-11D6.
- Maintain readiness, route reconnaissance, liaison, sustainment, and plans for commitment toward either corps sector; do not become decisively engaged before release.

#### (3) 13th Mechanized Brigade — Supporting and Retain Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to clear the supporting division sector through PL Punch and secure the southern seam and routes. **Purpose:** Protect the main effort and prevent coalition reinforcement of Aradesh. Supports R-11D1 and R-11D5.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to clear assigned terrain through PL Hook and secure Kirov’s approaches and division routes. **Purpose:** Isolate the objective and protect the 11th Brigade attack. Supports R-11D2 and R-11D5.
- **Phases III–IV — Task:** Occupy to retain Kirov and secure the route to the forward division. **Purpose:** Preserve the decisive objective while the 11th Brigade advances and guards. Supports R-11D2 and R-11D5.

### e. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

- Establish contact with the smallest suitable force while preserving combat power to develop the situation.
- On contact: report, establish security, develop the enemy situation, and attack, defend, bypass, delay, or withdraw within authority and the higher purpose.
- Do not bypass an enemy force capable of threatening Kirov, the reserve, a seam, or a sustainment route without containing it or completing a confirmed handover.

#### (2) Reserve

- The division supports, but does not exercise commitment authority over, the 12th Armored Brigade while it serves as corps or field army reserve.
- Any commitment order must state task and purpose, route, supported force, objective or area, limit of advance, fires, sustainment, termination criteria, and follow-on posture.
- If committed during Phases I–III, the division immediately recommends a replacement reserve or reconstitution plan.

#### (3) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Recommend Phase II when PL Punch is controlled and the Kirov attack is supportable.
- **DP 2:** Request corps-reserve commitment if the main effort cannot be restored with organic committed forces or a decisive objective is threatened.
- **DP 3:** Recommend Phase III when Kirov is seized, PL Hook is controlled, and the 13th Brigade can assume the retain mission.
- **DP 4:** Establish the Phase IV guard and field-army reserve posture when PL Jab is controlled and corps directs transition.

#### (4) CCIR and Reports

**PIR:** Coalition defenses and reserves near Kirov and PL Jab; threats to the corps seam; routes of counterattack; artillery positions; and forces moving south toward the 12th Corps.

**FFIR:** Loss or threatened loss of Kirov; uncovered seam or route; inability of the 11th Brigade to continue; loss of contact during movement to contact; artillery unable to support; or reserve readiness below mission requirement.

Report contact, objective, route, seam, phase-line, retain, guard, reserve, strength, and artillery-supportability status once per turn and on significant change.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Priority supports the 11th Armored Brigade’s current decisive task, the 13th Mechanized Brigade’s retain and route responsibilities, and the 12th Armored Brigade’s reserve readiness. Maintain fuel, recovery, and ammunition capacity for rapid westward movement and a reserve commitment.

### b. Transportation, Maintenance, and Medical Support

Keep primary and alternate routes through Kirov open. Recovery priority follows the division main effort, reserve, and retain force. Casualty evacuation moves east along secured routes. Report immediately when losses or route conditions prevent accomplishment of an essential task.

### c. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated or adjudicated narratively and do not create additional counters unless implemented by scenario control.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The division exercises command of all three organic brigades.
- The 7th Corps commander retains commitment authority over the 12th Armored Brigade during Phases I–III; the field army commander holds that authority in Phase IV.
- The 11th Armored Brigade is the division maneuver main effort.

**Succession of command:** Division commander; deputy division commander; commander, 11th Armored Brigade; commander, 13th Mechanized Brigade.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the division tactical network; alternate means use brigade and corps networks and liaison. Phase transitions, changes to brigade tasks, reserve commitment, and changes to objectives or boundaries require authenticated orders.

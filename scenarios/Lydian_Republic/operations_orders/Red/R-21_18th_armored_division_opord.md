## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Operations

The division attacks west across the Aradesh Plain to seize Aradesh and Belas, then guards the southern Aradesh Plain and assigned PL Jab sector. Open ground supports armored maneuver but exposes flanks and routes. Aradesh and Belas are decisive urban and transportation objectives. The Maren Lowlands provide an approach for coalition counterattacks moving north into the Aradesh Plain.

### b. Enemy Forces

Lydian and RDC Coalition forces can defend Aradesh and Belas, delay along the connecting routes, reinforce from Velin and the Maren Lowlands, and counterattack north into the Aradesh Plain. Their most dangerous action is to hold the division at Belas while a southern counterattack cuts routes, isolates Aradesh, or interferes with the 22nd Division attack on Velin.

### c. Friendly Forces

- **12th Maneuver Corps:** Field army main effort. Seizes Aradesh, Belas, and Velin; clears to PL Jab; then guards and retains the objectives.
- **22nd Mechanized Division:** Develops the Maren Lowlands, fixes forces around Velin and the Maren Bridge, seizes Velin in Phase III, and guards the southern PL Jab sector in Phase IV.
- **12th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to 12th Corps with priority to this division in Phases I–II and IV.
- **7th Maneuver Corps:** Operates north of the corps seam and seizes Kirov and OBJ Bridge.

### d. Civil Considerations

Preserve the road network, bridges, and essential infrastructure in Aradesh and Belas when consistent with the mission. Establish objective security sufficient to prevent armed interference while avoiding unnecessary diversion of maneuver forces from assigned tasks.

### e. Assumptions

- The 22nd Division fixes forces near Velin and the Maren Bridge sufficiently to protect the Phase II attack on Belas.
- The approved operational graphics establish the division’s Aradesh–Belas sector, primary axes, objectives, phase lines, and southern Aradesh Plain–PL Jab guard sector. The 14th Armored Brigade owns the Aradesh attack and forward clearing task before assuming the guard; the 15th Armored Brigade clears south of Aradesh, seizes Belas, and retains it; the 23rd Mechanized Brigade isolates Aradesh and then retains it and its supporting route.
- A brigade’s clearing limit is the depicted division boundary laterally and the current phase line in depth unless changed by authenticated order. Retain areas include the named objective, its immediate approaches, and the route connecting it to the division. The division commander selects exact legal assembly hexes and movement routes during setup and records them in the backbrief.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated orders govern transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **18th Armored Division attacks to seize Aradesh and clear the Aradesh Plain through PL Punch, including a 15th Armored Brigade clearing operation south of the city; attacks to seize Belas and clear through PL Hook while retaining Aradesh; guards the southern Aradesh Plain against counterattacks from the Maren Lowlands while retaining Aradesh and Belas; and then continues the guard along its assigned PL Jab sector in order to secure the 12th Corps central objectives and protect the attack on Velin and subsequent field army consolidation.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Break the coalition defense at Aradesh and Belas, secure both cities, and prevent counterattacks from the Maren Lowlands from disrupting the corps attack on Velin or reversing field army gains.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize Aradesh and clear the division sector through PL Punch.
- Clear the Aradesh Plain south of Aradesh with the 15th Armored Brigade.
- Seize Belas and clear through PL Hook.
- Retain Aradesh with the 23rd Mechanized Brigade and Belas with the 15th Armored Brigade.
- Guard the southern Aradesh Plain with the 14th Armored Brigade.
- Identify and delay counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands.
- Maintain the corps seam, routes, objective handovers, and corps-fire supportability.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Coalition forces in the division sector are destroyed, displaced west of PL Jab, or unable to interfere with the corps mission.
- **Friendly:** The division maintains a coherent guard and retain posture linked to corps fires and the 22nd Division.
- **Terrain:** Aradesh and Belas are retained; the southern Aradesh Plain and assigned PL Jab sector are guarded; routes and seams remain secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division shifts its maneuver main effort by phase. The 14th Armored Brigade leads the attack on Aradesh, the 15th Armored Brigade clears south of the city and then leads the attack on Belas, and the 23rd Mechanized Brigade isolates Aradesh before retaining it. In Phases III–IV, the 14th Brigade guards against the southern counterattack while the other brigades retain the two cities.

#### (2) Phase I — Seize Aradesh and Clear to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 14th Armored Brigade, as division main effort, attacks to seize Aradesh. The 23rd Mechanized Brigade attacks to isolate the city, clears its assigned approaches, and prevents reinforcement or withdrawal that would interfere with the 14th Brigade. The 15th Armored Brigade conducts a clearing operation in the Aradesh Plain south of the city.

The phase ends when Aradesh is seized, the area south of the city and the division sector through PL Punch are cleared or controlled, routes and seams are secure, and the attack on Belas is supportable.

#### (3) Phase II — Seize Belas and Retain Aradesh

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 15th Armored Brigade becomes division main effort and attacks to seize Belas. The 14th Armored Brigade attacks to clear the division’s forward and southern approach through PL Hook and protects the main effort’s flank. The 23rd Mechanized Brigade occupies to retain Aradesh and secures the route from Aradesh to the forward division.

The phase ends when Belas is seized, Aradesh remains secure, the division sector through PL Hook is controlled, and the division can establish the Phase III guard and retain posture.

#### (4) Phase III — Guard the Southern Aradesh Plain

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** The 14th Armored Brigade conducts a guard on the southern end of the Aradesh Plain to identify and delay counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands. The 15th Armored Brigade occupies to retain Belas. The 23rd Mechanized Brigade continues to occupy to retain Aradesh. This division supports the 22nd Division attack on Velin by preventing southern counterattack from reaching its flank, routes, or retained objectives.

The phase ends when Velin is seized, the assigned PL Jab sector is controlled, Aradesh and Belas remain secure, and the division is postured to continue the guard.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** The 14th Armored Brigade continues the guard along the assigned PL Jab and southern Aradesh Plain sector. The 15th Armored Brigade continues to occupy to retain Belas, and the 23rd Mechanized Brigade continues to occupy to retain Aradesh.

### c. Scheme of Fires

The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. Division fire-support requests follow these priorities:

- **Phase I:** 14th Brigade attack on Aradesh and 15th Brigade clearing south of the city; then 23rd Brigade isolation and route security.
- **Phase II:** 15th Brigade attack on Belas; then 14th Brigade clearing and 23rd Brigade retention of Aradesh.
- **Phase III:** Corps priority shifts to the 22nd Division; division requests first protect the 14th Brigade guard and defeat counterattack from the Maren Lowlands, then support both retain forces.
- **Phase IV:** 14th Brigade guard and the most dangerous counterattack; then retention of Belas and Aradesh.

### d. Tasks to Subordinate Brigades

#### (1) 14th Armored Brigade — Aradesh Attack and Guard Force

- **Phase I — Division Main Effort. Task:** Attack to seize Aradesh and clear assigned terrain through PL Punch. **Purpose:** Break the coalition’s eastern anchor and open the central corridor. Supports R-21D1 and R-21D6.
- **Phase II — Supporting Effort. Task:** Attack to clear the forward and southern approach through PL Hook and protect the 15th Brigade’s flank. **Purpose:** Enable seizure of Belas and block southern interference. Supports R-21D3 and R-21D6.
- **Phases III–IV — Task:** Guard the southern Aradesh Plain and assigned PL Jab sector; identify and delay counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands. **Purpose:** Protect the attack on Velin, retained objectives, and corps consolidation. Supports R-21D5 and R-21D6.

#### (2) 15th Armored Brigade — Southern Clearing, Belas Attack, and Retain Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to clear the Aradesh Plain south of the city through PL Punch. **Purpose:** Prevent coalition forces from interfering with seizure of Aradesh from the south. Supports R-21D1, R-21D2, and R-21D6.
- **Phase II — Division Main Effort. Task:** Attack to seize Belas and clear assigned terrain through PL Hook. **Purpose:** Secure the central transportation corridor and set conditions for the guard. Supports R-21D3.
- **Phases III–IV — Task:** Occupy to retain Belas and secure its immediate approaches. **Purpose:** Preserve the Phase II gain while the division guards against southern counterattack. Supports R-21D4 and R-21D6.

#### (3) 23rd Mechanized Brigade — Aradesh Isolation and Retain Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to isolate Aradesh, clear assigned approaches, and prevent reinforcement or withdrawal. **Purpose:** Enable the 14th Brigade to seize the city and protect the division route network. Supports R-21D1 and R-21D6.
- **Phases II–IV — Task:** Occupy to retain Aradesh and secure the route connecting the city to the forward division. **Purpose:** Preserve the corps’ eastern objective while the division seizes Belas and guards the southern plain. Supports R-21D4 and R-21D6.

### e. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Objective and Retain Handovers

- A brigade retains responsibility for an objective, route, or contained enemy force until the receiving brigade confirms the handover.
- Retain forces maintain local security, route access, observation, and the ability to defeat or report threats within capability.
- No brigade leaves Aradesh or Belas without an authenticated relief or change of mission.

#### (2) Guard

- **Protected force:** 22nd Mechanized Division during its attack on Velin; thereafter the consolidating 12th Corps.
- **Guard purpose:** Provide early warning and delay forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands into the Aradesh Plain.
- The 14th Brigade reports enemy approach, composition, direction, and estimated time to the protected area; fights to gain time; and disengages or hands over only under division authority.

#### (3) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Recommend Phase II when Aradesh is seized, clearing south of the city is complete, and the attack on Belas is supportable.
- **DP 2:** Reinforce the clearing operation if coalition forces south of Aradesh can interfere with the city attack or division routes.
- **DP 3:** Recommend Phase III when Belas is seized, Aradesh is retained, and the guard can cover the Velin attack.
- **DP 4:** Shift fires and maneuver against a counterattack from the Maren Lowlands when it threatens the protected force or retained objectives.
- **DP 5:** Continue into Phase IV when Velin is seized and corps confirms the guard-and-retain posture.

#### (4) CCIR and Reports

**PIR:** Defenses at Aradesh and Belas; forces south of Aradesh; counterattack formations moving north from the Maren Lowlands; routes into the Aradesh Plain; artillery positions; and threats to retained objectives.

**FFIR:** Failure to clear south of Aradesh; loss or threatened loss of Aradesh or Belas; failure of the guard to provide required warning or delay; an uncovered seam or route; or artillery unable to support.

Report contact, objective, clearing, route, seam, phase-line, retain, guard, strength, and artillery-supportability status once per turn and on significant change.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Sustainment priority follows the phase main effort: 14th Brigade in Phase I, 15th Brigade in Phase II, and the 14th Brigade guard in Phases III–IV. Maintain sufficient support to both objective-retain forces throughout.

### b. Transportation, Maintenance, and Medical Support

Keep separate routes to Aradesh, Belas, and the guard sector where possible. Recovery priority follows the phase main effort, guard force, and objective-retain forces. Casualty evacuation moves east through secured routes. Report immediately when loss or route conditions prevent an essential task.

### c. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated into parent formations and do not create additional counters or game effects in baseline play.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The division exercises command of the 14th Armored, 15th Armored, and 23rd Mechanized Brigades.
- Division main effort: 14th Brigade in Phase I; 15th Brigade in Phase II; 14th Brigade guard in Phases III–IV.
- The 23rd Brigade retains Aradesh from Phase II onward; the 15th Brigade retains Belas from Phase III onward.

**Succession of command:** Division commander; deputy division commander; commander of the brigade designated division main effort; remaining brigade commanders by seniority.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the division tactical network; alternate means use brigade and corps networks and liaison. Phase transitions, main-effort shifts, changes to objective-retain responsibility, and changes to guard sectors require authenticated orders.

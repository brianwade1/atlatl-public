# 22nd Mechanized Division

> **FOR WARGAMING AND EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY — ENTIRELY FICTIONAL**

- **Issuing headquarters:** 22nd Mechanized Division
- **Date-time group:** 18 May 2030, 0600 local
- **Effective:** On receipt
- **Duration:** 20 turns / 10 days; 12 hours per turn
- **Parent order:** 12th Maneuver Corps OPORD R-20, OPERATION CENTRAL SPEAR
- **Role:** 12th Corps supporting effort during Phases I–II and IV; corps main effort during Phase III
- **Supported parent tasks:** R-2C1, R-2C2, R-2C3, R-2C4, R-2C5, R-2C6


The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. The division receives next priority of fires in Phases I–II, priority of fires during the Phase III attack on Velin, and next priority during Phase IV. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains organic to the division but may be committed during Phases I–III only by the 12th Corps commander. At the start of Phase IV, the corps releases it to the division for the southern guard.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Operations

The division operates in the Maren Lowlands, around the Maren Bridge, Velin, and the southern portion of PL Jab. Marsh and restricted roads canalize maneuver and favor infantry defense, delay, and ambush. Velin and the Maren Bridge control the principal southern routes. The division must prevent coalition forces in this sector from moving north against the 18th Division or the Aradesh Plain.

### b. Enemy Forces

Lydian and RDC Coalition forces can delay in the lowlands, defend Velin and the Maren Bridge, and reinforce or counterattack north toward Belas and Aradesh. Their most dangerous action is to hold the 32nd Brigade near Velin while moving another force north against the 18th Division attack or retained objectives.

### c. Friendly Forces

- **12th Maneuver Corps:** Field army main effort. Seizes Aradesh, Belas, and Velin; clears to PL Jab; then guards and retains the objectives.
- **18th Armored Division:** Corps main effort in Phases I–II and IV; seizes Aradesh and Belas and guards the southern Aradesh Plain. It supports this division during the Phase III attack by guarding against forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands.
- **12th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to 12th Corps; priority to this division during Phase III.

### d. Civil Considerations

Preserve the Maren Bridge, Velin road network, and essential infrastructure when consistent with the mission. Marsh routes and urban approaches must remain available for sustainment, casualty evacuation, and the Phase IV guard.

### e. Assumptions

- The 24th Mechanized Brigade can perform corps-reserve duties without changing its organic relationship to the division.
- If the corps commits the 24th Brigade before Phase IV, the corps will direct a replacement guard plan.
- The approved operational graphics establish the division’s Maren Lowlands–Velin sector, primary axis, objective, phase lines, and southern PL Jab guard sector. The 32nd Infantry Brigade advances on a firm route selected through the Maren Lowlands; its Phase II fixing area is limited to Velin, the Maren Bridge approaches, and enemy routes north within the depicted division sector. In Phase III it seizes Velin and its immediate approaches, then retains them. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve until released to guard the southern PL Jab sector in Phase IV.
- The division commander selects exact legal assembly hexes and movement routes during setup and records them in the backbrief. Any order committing the 24th Mechanized Brigade before Phase IV supplies its route, objective or area, limit of advance, termination criteria, and follow-on posture.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated orders govern transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **22nd Mechanized Division conducts a movement to contact through the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch; fixes coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge to prevent interference with the 18th Division attack on Belas; attacks to seize Velin as the 12th Corps main effort; and then guards the southern portion of PL Jab while retaining Velin in order to secure the corps southern flank and prevent coalition forces from reversing field army gains.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Develop and contain coalition forces in the Maren Lowlands, isolate them from the central fight, seize Velin, and establish a southern guard that protects the corps and field army consolidation.

#### Key Tasks

- Conduct movement to contact through the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch.
- Fix forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge during the attack on Belas.
- Attack to seize and retain Velin.
- Preserve the 24th Mechanized Brigade as corps reserve during Phases I–III.
- Employ the released 24th Brigade to guard the southern PL Jab sector in Phase IV.
- Secure routes, the corps boundary, the Maren Bridge approaches, and all handovers.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Coalition forces in the Maren sector are destroyed, fixed, displaced west of PL Jab, or unable to threaten the corps.
- **Friendly:** The 32nd Brigade retains Velin; the 24th Brigade guards the southern PL Jab sector; both remain supported by division and corps systems.
- **Terrain:** Velin is retained, the southern PL Jab sector is guarded, and the Maren routes and corps boundary remain secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The 32nd Infantry Brigade performs the division’s active maneuver tasks during Phases I–III while the 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve. The division develops the enemy situation, fixes the Velin and Maren Bridge force during the attack on Belas, then becomes corps main effort and seizes Velin. In Phase IV, the 24th Brigade returns to division employment for the guard while the 32nd Brigade retains Velin.

#### (2) Phase I — Movement to Contact to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 32nd Infantry Brigade conducts a movement to contact through the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch, develops enemy dispositions, and fixes or destroys forces that can interfere with the 18th Division attack on Aradesh. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when the division controls its PL Punch sector, enemy dispositions around Velin and the Maren Bridge are sufficiently developed, routes and the corps boundary are secure, and the Phase II fixing action is supportable.

#### (3) Phase II — Fix Velin and Maren Bridge Forces

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 32nd Infantry Brigade fixes identified coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge, preventing their movement against the 18th Division attack on Belas. The brigade maintains the fix until corps confirms that those forces can no longer interfere or directs transition. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when Belas is seized, coalition forces at Velin and the bridge are unable to interfere, and the attack on Velin is supportable.

#### (4) Phase III — Seize Velin

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** The division becomes the 12th Corps main effort. The 32nd Infantry Brigade attacks to seize Velin, secures the city and immediate approaches, and establishes conditions for the retain mission. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve. The 18th Division guard protects the attack from forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands.

The phase ends when Velin is seized, the assigned PL Jab sector is controlled, routes are secure, and the 24th Brigade can assume the Phase IV guard.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** The corps releases the 24th Mechanized Brigade to the division. The 24th Brigade conducts a guard along the southern portion of PL Jab to identify and delay counterattacking forces. The 32nd Infantry Brigade occupies to retain Velin and secures its immediate approaches and routes.

### c. Scheme of Fires

The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. Division fire-support requests follow these priorities:

- **Phase I:** 32nd Brigade actions on contact and protection of routes; corps priority remains with the 18th Division.
- **Phase II:** 32nd Brigade fixing action at Velin and the Maren Bridge; corps priority remains with the 18th Division attack on Belas.
- **Phase III:** Isolation and seizure of Velin; counterfire and interdiction against reinforcement; protection of the 32nd Brigade routes. The division has corps priority of fires.
- **Phase IV:** 24th Brigade guard and the most dangerous southern counterattack; then 32nd Brigade retention of Velin. Corps priority returns to the 18th Division.

### d. Tasks to Subordinate Brigades

#### (1) 24th Mechanized Brigade — Corps Reserve and Phase IV Guard Force

- **Phases I–III — Task:** Remain prepared as 12th Corps reserve and execute only an authenticated corps commitment order. **Purpose:** Restore the corps main effort, defeat a dangerous counterattack, or preserve Aradesh, Belas, or Velin. Supports R-22D6.
- **Phase IV — Task:** On corps release, conduct a guard along the southern portion of PL Jab. **Purpose:** Identify and delay counterattacking forces before they threaten Velin or the consolidating corps. Supports R-22D4 and R-22D5.
- Maintain readiness, route reconnaissance, liaison, sustainment, and plans for commitment toward either division during Phases I–III; do not become decisively engaged before corps release.

#### (2) 32nd Infantry Brigade — Division Maneuver and Retain Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Conduct movement to contact through the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch; fix or destroy identified forces that can interfere with the attack on Aradesh. **Purpose:** Develop the southern enemy situation and protect the corps main effort. Supports R-22D1 and R-22D5.
- **Phase II — Task:** Fix coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge until corps releases the fixing force. **Purpose:** Prevent interference with the 18th Division attack on Belas. Supports R-22D2 and R-22D5.
- **Phase III — Task:** Attack to seize Velin and secure its immediate approaches. **Purpose:** Complete the corps southern objective system. Supports R-22D3 and R-22D5.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Occupy to retain Velin and secure the city’s approaches and supporting routes. **Purpose:** Preserve the Phase III gain while the 24th Brigade guards PL Jab. Supports R-22D3 and R-22D5.

### e. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Movement to Contact and Fixing Action

- Establish contact with the smallest suitable force while preserving combat power to develop the situation.
- On contact: report, secure, develop, and attack, defend, bypass, delay, or withdraw within authority and the higher purpose.
- The Phase II fix must deny the enemy freedom of movement without forcing the 32nd Brigade into an unnecessary attack before corps shifts the main effort and fires.
- Maintain observation, effective fires or maneuver pressure, and routes that permit transition from fix to attack.

#### (2) Reserve and Release

- The division supports, but does not exercise commitment authority over, the 24th Brigade during Phases I–III.
- A corps commitment order states task and purpose, route, supported force, objective or area, limit of advance, fires, sustainment, termination, and follow-on posture.
- At the start of Phase IV, corps release returns employment authority to the division for the assigned guard.
- If committed before Phase IV, immediately develop and report a replacement guard plan.

#### (3) Guard and Retain

- The 24th Brigade guard protects Velin and the consolidating 12th Corps by providing warning and delay in the southern PL Jab sector.
- The 32nd Brigade retains Velin until relieved by authenticated order and maintains local security and route access.
- Guard and retain handovers require confirmation by both units and the division command post.

#### (4) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Recommend Phase II when PL Punch is controlled and forces around Velin and the bridge are developed sufficiently to fix.
- **DP 2:** Request reserve commitment if a southern force threatens the corps main effort or a decisive objective.
- **DP 3:** Recommend Phase III when Belas is seized, the fix is effective, and the attack on Velin is supportable.
- **DP 4:** Recommend Phase IV when Velin is seized, PL Jab is controlled, and the 24th Brigade is available for release.
- **DP 5:** Establish a replacement guard if the 24th Brigade is committed before Phase IV.

#### (5) CCIR and Reports

**PIR:** Defenses at Velin and the Maren Bridge; forces capable of moving north toward Belas or Aradesh; reinforcement and withdrawal routes; artillery positions; and threats to the corps boundary.

**FFIR:** Fix broken; loss or threatened loss of Velin; 24th Brigade unavailable for reserve or guard duty; uncovered route or boundary; attack unable to continue; or artillery unable to support.

Report contact, fix, objective, route, boundary, phase-line, retain, guard, reserve, strength, and artillery-supportability status once per turn and on significant change.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Sustainment preserves 24th Brigade reserve readiness while supporting the 32nd Brigade’s movement, fix, attack, and retain tasks. Phase III priority goes to the attack on Velin; Phase IV priority balances the 24th Brigade guard and 32nd Brigade retain force.

### b. Transportation, Maintenance, and Medical Support

Control movement on restricted lowland routes and keep at least one route open to Velin and one to the guard sector. Recovery priority follows the active main effort, reserve, and guard. Casualty evacuation moves east along secured routes. Report immediately when terrain, route, or loss conditions prevent an essential task.

### c. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated into parent formations and do not create additional counters or game effects in baseline play.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The division exercises command of the 24th Mechanized and 32nd Infantry Brigades.
- The 12th Corps commander retains commitment authority over the 24th Brigade during Phases I–III.
- The 32nd Brigade is the division maneuver main effort during Phases I–III; the 24th Brigade conducts the division’s forward guard in Phase IV.

**Succession of command:** Division commander; deputy division commander; commander, 32nd Infantry Brigade; commander, 24th Mechanized Brigade.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the division tactical network; alternate means use brigade and corps networks and liaison. Phase transitions, corps-reserve commitment or release, termination of the fixing action, and changes to objectives or guard sectors require authenticated orders.

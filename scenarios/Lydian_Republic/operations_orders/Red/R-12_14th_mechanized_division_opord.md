## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Operations

The division operates through the Drovna Heights, the Northwest Bridge, and the northern approaches to PL Jab. Rough terrain and limited roads restrict maneuver, complicate mutual support, and favor infantry defense and delay. The Northwest Bridge is OBJ Bridge and controls a key northern crossing. The southern boundary with the 11th Armored Division and routes supporting the 7th Corps must remain secure.

### b. Enemy Forces

Lydian and RDC Coalition forces can delay in the heights, defend the Northwest Bridge, destroy or block approaches, and counterattack isolated units along restricted routes. Their most dangerous action is to fix the division in the heights while striking the 11th Division flank, the bridge approaches, or the corps seam.

### c. Friendly Forces

- **7th Maneuver Corps:** Conducts movement to contact to PL Punch, seizes Kirov and OBJ Bridge, conducts movement to contact to PL Jab, and then guards while retaining both objectives.
- **11th Armored Division:** Corps main effort south of the division; seizes and retains Kirov.
- **7th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to 7th Corps; next priority to this division after the 11th Armored Division.

### d. Civil Considerations

Preserve the Northwest Bridge and its road approaches when operationally practicable. Control displaced civilians and traffic away from restricted routes needed for maneuver, artillery displacement, casualty evacuation, and sustainment.

### e. Assumptions

- The Northwest Bridge is usable unless scenario control confirms otherwise.
- Exact brigade boundaries, routes, passage points, and guard sectors not shown on the approved overlay remain `TBD—SCENARIO DESIGN`.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated orders govern transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **14th Mechanized Division conducts a movement to contact through the Drovna Heights to PL Punch; attacks to seize OBJ Bridge and clears the Drovna sector through PL Hook; conducts a movement to contact to PL Jab while retaining the Northwest Bridge; and then guards along its assigned PL Jab sector while continuing to retain the bridge in order to secure the 7th Corps northern flank and prevent coalition forces from interfering with the corps main effort.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Find and defeat coalition forces in the Drovna Heights, secure the Northwest Bridge, and deny the enemy a northern route against the 7th Corps and field army main efforts.

#### Key Tasks

- Conduct movement to contact through restricted terrain to PL Punch.
- Seize and retain OBJ Bridge.
- Clear the division sector through PL Hook.
- Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab while maintaining bridge security.
- Guard the assigned northern PL Jab sector.
- Maintain contact with the 11th Armored Division and keep routes open for corps fires and sustainment.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Coalition forces in the Drovna sector are destroyed, displaced west of PL Jab, or unable to threaten the bridge or corps main effort.
- **Friendly:** Both brigades remain mutually supporting and connected to corps fires and sustainment.
- **Terrain:** The Northwest Bridge is retained, the northern PL Jab sector is guarded, and division routes and seams are secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division employs the 21st Mechanized Brigade as its maneuver main effort. The 31st Infantry Brigade clears and secures restrictive terrain, protects the division seam, and later retains the Northwest Bridge. The division advances by bounds through the heights so neither brigade loses contact with the other or moves beyond available corps fires.

#### (2) Phase I — Movement to Contact to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 21st Mechanized Brigade conducts a movement to contact along the division’s primary axis to PL Punch. The 31st Infantry Brigade conducts a movement to contact and clears assigned restrictive terrain along the supporting axis while securing the southern seam and routes.

The phase ends when the division controls its PL Punch sector, enemy dispositions are developed, routes and the seam are secure, and the attack on OBJ Bridge is supportable.

#### (3) Phase II — Seize OBJ Bridge and Clear to PL Hook

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 21st Mechanized Brigade attacks to seize OBJ Bridge and secure the crossing and western bridgehead. The 31st Infantry Brigade attacks to clear remaining enemy forces in the Drovna Heights through PL Hook and secures the bridge approaches and division seam.

The phase ends when OBJ Bridge is seized and usable, the Drovna sector through PL Hook is controlled, and the 31st Brigade is ready to assume the retain mission.

#### (4) Phase III — Movement to Contact to PL Jab

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** The 21st Mechanized Brigade conducts a movement to contact to PL Jab and destroys forces threatening the corps northern flank. The 31st Infantry Brigade occupies to retain the Northwest Bridge and secures routes connecting the crossing to the forward force.

The phase ends when the assigned PL Jab sector is controlled, the bridge remains secure, and the division is postured for the Phase IV guard.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** The 21st Mechanized Brigade conducts a guard along the assigned northern PL Jab sector to provide warning and delay counterattacking coalition forces. The 31st Infantry Brigade continues to occupy to retain the Northwest Bridge and its approaches.

### c. Scheme of Fires

The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. Fire-support requests are integrated with the higher priority 11th Division effort and follow these division priorities:

- **Phase I:** Actions on contact by the 21st Brigade; then clearing restrictive terrain and protecting the seam.
- **Phase II:** Isolation and seizure of OBJ Bridge; then clearing through PL Hook and protecting bridge approaches.
- **Phase III:** 21st Brigade movement to contact; shift to threats against the bridge, division seam, or corps main effort.
- **Phase IV:** 21st Brigade guard; then Northwest Bridge retention.

### d. Tasks to Subordinate Brigades

#### (1) 21st Mechanized Brigade — Division Main Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Conduct movement to contact along the primary axis to PL Punch and fix or destroy identified forces within capability. **Purpose:** Develop the enemy situation and prevent interference with the 11th Division. Supports R-12D1 and R-12D6.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize OBJ Bridge and secure the crossing and western bridgehead. **Purpose:** Control the northern crossing and protect the corps flank. Supports R-12D2.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and destroy forces threatening the northern corps sector. **Purpose:** Prevent formation of another defense or counterattack against the field army. Supports R-12D4 and R-12D6.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Conduct a guard along the assigned northern PL Jab sector. **Purpose:** Identify and delay counterattacking forces before they threaten the bridge or Kirov. Supports R-12D5.

#### (2) 31st Infantry Brigade — Supporting and Retain Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Conduct movement to contact and clear assigned restrictive terrain to PL Punch; secure the southern seam and division routes. **Purpose:** Protect the division main effort and prevent coalition use of the Drovna Heights. Supports R-12D1, R-12D3, and R-12D6.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to clear the Drovna sector through PL Hook and secure the eastern bridge approaches. **Purpose:** Isolate OBJ Bridge and preserve access to the crossing. Supports R-12D2, R-12D3, and R-12D6.
- **Phases III–IV — Task:** Occupy to retain the Northwest Bridge and secure its approaches. **Purpose:** Deny coalition use of the crossing while the 21st Brigade advances and guards. Supports R-12D2 and R-12D6.

### e. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

- Establish contact with the smallest suitable force and preserve enough combat power to develop the situation.
- On contact: report, secure, develop, and attack, defend, bypass, delay, or withdraw within authority and the higher purpose.
- Do not bypass forces capable of interdicting the bridge, seam, artillery route, or sustainment route without containment or a confirmed handover.

#### (2) Bridge and Route Control

- The unit controlling the Northwest Bridge also controls the immediate approaches until a confirmed handover.
- Report bridge condition, obstruction, route capacity, and enemy observation immediately.
- Maintain at least one usable route to each brigade and one route for corps artillery displacement.

#### (3) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Recommend Phase II when PL Punch is controlled and the attack on OBJ Bridge is supportable.
- **DP 2:** Shift fires or maneuver to protect the 11th Division seam if coalition forces threaten the corps main effort.
- **DP 3:** Recommend Phase III when the bridge is seized, PL Hook is controlled, and the 31st Brigade can assume the retain mission.
- **DP 4:** Establish the Phase IV guard when PL Jab is controlled and corps directs transition.

#### (4) CCIR and Reports

**PIR:** Coalition defenses at the Northwest Bridge; enemy forces in the Drovna Heights; counterattack routes; bridge condition; artillery positions; and threats to the 11th Division seam.

**FFIR:** Loss or threatened loss of the bridge; a blocked route; loss of brigade mutual support; an uncovered seam; artillery unable to support; or inability to establish the Phase IV guard.

Report contact, bridge, route, seam, phase-line, retain, guard, strength, and artillery-supportability status once per turn and on significant change.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Sustainment priority follows the 21st Brigade’s current decisive task, the 31st Brigade’s bridge and route responsibilities, and forces protecting the seam. Stock fuel and ammunition forward before each movement beyond a phase line.

### b. Transportation, Maintenance, and Medical Support

Restricted routes require movement control and rapid recovery of disabled vehicles. Casualty evacuation moves east through secured bridge approaches and division routes. Report immediately when route, bridge, loss, or recovery conditions prevent an essential task.

### c. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated or adjudicated narratively and do not create additional counters unless implemented by scenario control.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The division exercises command of the 21st Mechanized and 31st Infantry Brigades.
- The 21st Mechanized Brigade is the division maneuver main effort.
- Changes to bridge-retain responsibility require division approval and a confirmed handover.

**Succession of command:** Division commander; deputy division commander; commander, 21st Mechanized Brigade; commander, 31st Infantry Brigade.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the division tactical network; alternate means use brigade and corps networks and liaison. Phase transitions, changes to brigade tasks, and changes to objectives or boundaries require authenticated orders.

# 12th Maneuver Corps

- **Effective:** On receipt
- **Duration:** 20 turns / 10 days; 12 hours per turn

The corps exercises operational control over both divisions. The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. It gives priority of fires to the 18th Armored Division during Phases I–II, shifts priority to the 22nd Mechanized Division during Phase III, and returns priority to the 18th Armored Division during Phase IV.

The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains organic to the 22nd Mechanized Division and serves as the corps reserve during Phases I–III. At the start of Phase IV, the corps releases it to the 22nd Mechanized Division to conduct the southern guard while the 32nd Infantry Brigade retains Velin.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The corps area of interest extends from the Vostian frontier west across the Aradesh Plain to Aradesh, Belas, Velin, and PL Jab; south through the Maren Lowlands; north to the seam with the 7th Maneuver Corps; and west far enough to identify Lydian and RDC Coalition counterattack forces approaching the corps objectives or routes.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Aradesh Plain:** Open terrain favors armored maneuver and long-range fires but exposes routes and flanks. The area south of Aradesh provides a maneuver corridor that must be cleared during Phase I.
- **Aradesh:** Aradesh is the initial corps objective and the central base for continued attack. It must be retained after seizure.
- **Belas:** Belas (`hex-5-7`) controls the central Arel corridor and is the corps Phase II objective.
- **Maren Lowlands:** Marsh and drainage features channel movement toward Velin and north into the Aradesh Plain. The lowlands are both an avenue for the corps supporting effort and the principal counterattack threat to the southern Aradesh Plain.
- **Maren Bridge and Velin:** Coalition forces near the bridge and city can interfere with the attack on Belas. Velin (`hex-5-11`) is the corps Phase III objective and anchors the southern Phase IV guard.
- **PL Punch, PL Hook, and PL Jab:** These phase lines control westward progression, objective handovers, artillery displacement, and transition to guard.
- The approved operational graphics establish the division sectors, primary axes, objectives, phase lines, and guard sectors. The 18th Armored Division owns the Aradesh–Belas sector and the central guard against forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands. The 22nd Mechanized Division owns the Maren Lowlands–Velin sector and the southern PL Jab guard sector.
- Each division selects exact movement routes and local support areas within its depicted sector during setup and records them in its backbrief. The 24th Mechanized Brigade has no standing release point; each commitment order assigns its assembly area, route, release point, objective or area, limit of advance, termination criteria, and return or follow-on posture.

#### (2) Weather and Light

The R-00 weather forecast applies. Dry conditions through Turn 6 favor the attack on Aradesh and clearing south of the city. Rain during Turns 7–12 channels heavy formations onto roads, slows artillery displacement, and increases congestion around Belas, the Maren Bridge, and Velin approaches. Fog may conceal movement but delay warning of a counterattack from the Maren Lowlands. Weather effects not native to Atlatl provide planning context only and cause no additional baseline game modifier.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

Lydian and RDC Coalition forces defend Aradesh, the Aradesh Plain, Belas, the Maren Lowlands, the Maren Bridge, and Velin. Coalition armored and mechanized forces can counterattack on the plain or reinforce the central corridor. Infantry in the lowlands and Velin can delay movement and threaten routes. Coalition artillery can mass against an objective attack, bridge approach, or exposed guard force.

#### (2) Most Likely Course of Action

Coalition forces defend Aradesh during Phase I, withdraw or reposition toward Belas and Velin when the city becomes untenable, and use forces in the Maren Lowlands to delay the southern supporting effort. They counterattack a Vostian formation that outruns its artillery, sustainment, or flank security.

#### (3) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Coalition forces permit limited Vostian gains, then mass armored and mechanized forces from the Maren Lowlands north into the Aradesh Plain while the 22nd Mechanized Division attacks Velin. This could isolate the Velin attack, threaten Aradesh and Belas, and sever the corps’ central-southern routes.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

The Vostian 3rd Field Army attacks west to seize Aradesh, Kirov, OBJ Bridge, Belas, and Velin; clears Lydian and RDC Coalition forces to PL Jab; and then guards along PL Jab while retaining the seized objectives. The 12th Maneuver Corps is the field army main effort throughout.

#### (2) Adjacent and Supporting Units

- **7th Maneuver Corps:** Supporting effort north of the seam. It develops and fixes northern forces, seizes Kirov and the Northwest Bridge, and prevents interference with this corps.
- **12th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to the corps with phase-specific priorities in Paragraph 3.d.
- Other reconnaissance, aviation, engineering, air defense, EW/cyber, sustainment, and medical capabilities are incorporated into parent formations and provide planning context only in baseline play.

### e. Civil Considerations

Aradesh, Belas, and Velin contain civilians, administrative institutions, and transportation infrastructure. The corps preserves usable roads, bridges, communications nodes, and public facilities where operationally practicable. Urban security and handover requirements must not consume the maneuver forces needed for the next phase.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at scenario start. Cross-attachment with the 7th Corps requires field army direction.

### g. Assumptions

- Aradesh, Belas, Velin, the Maren Bridge, and approaches to PL Jab begin under Lydian or RDC Coalition control.
- The 7th Corps maintains enough northern pressure to prevent major reinforcement into this sector.
- The 24th Mechanized Brigade can serve as corps reserve during Phases I–III without changing its organic relationship.
- Bridge status changes only through implemented Atlatl mechanics. Any optional bridge rule must be agreed before setup.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **12th Maneuver Corps attacks to seize OBJ Aradesh and clear its area to PL Punch; attacks to seize OBJ Belas, clears to PL Hook, and retains Aradesh; attacks to seize OBJ Velin, clears to PL Jab, retains Aradesh and Belas, and guards the southern Aradesh Plain; and then guards along PL Jab while retaining Aradesh, Belas, and Velin in order to destroy the Lydian and RDC Coalition defense in the central and southern sectors and secure the Vostian 3rd Field Army’s decisive territorial objectives.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Seize and retain the central and southern objective system while preventing coalition forces from the Maren Lowlands from disrupting the field army main effort.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize and retain Aradesh, Belas, and Velin in sequence.
- Clear the corps area successively to PL Punch, PL Hook, and PL Jab.
- Conduct movement to contact in the Maren Lowlands when enemy dispositions remain unclear.
- Clear the Aradesh Plain south of Aradesh during Phase I.
- Fix coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge during Phase II.
- Guard the southern Aradesh Plain during Phases III–IV.
- Retain the 24th Mechanized Brigade as corps reserve during Phases I–III, then release it for the Phase IV southern guard.
- Maintain the corps seam, routes, objective handovers, and artillery coverage.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Lydian and RDC Coalition forces are destroyed, fixed, or displaced west of PL Jab and cannot immediately retake the corps objectives or disrupt consolidation.
- **Friendly:** Both divisions remain coherent; the corps artillery can cover the guard; and the 24th Mechanized Brigade is integrated into the Phase IV guard.
- **Terrain:** Aradesh, Belas, and Velin are retained; the southern Aradesh Plain is protected; the corps portion of PL Jab is guarded; and supporting routes remain usable.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The corps conducts successive attacks on Aradesh, Belas, and Velin. The 18th Armored Division is the corps main effort during Phases I–II and IV. The 22nd Mechanized Division is the corps main effort during Phase III. Previously seized objectives are retained by designated brigades as the corps continues west.

#### (2) Phase I — Seize Aradesh and Clear to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 18th Armored Division, as corps main effort, attacks to seize Aradesh and clears the Aradesh Plain through PL Punch. The **15th Armored Brigade conducts the clearing operation in the Aradesh Plain south of the city**, protecting the division’s southern flank and preventing coalition forces from interfering with seizure of Aradesh. The division assigns its other maneuver brigades to the attack on and around Aradesh.

The 22nd Mechanized Division conducts a movement to contact in the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch to establish contact and develop the enemy situation. On contact, it fixes or destroys forces that can interfere with the 18th Division. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when Aradesh is seized, the Aradesh Plain south of the city and both division sectors through PL Punch are cleared or controlled, the corps seam and routes are secure, and the attack toward Belas is supportable.

#### (3) Phase II — Seize Belas and Retain Aradesh

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 18th Armored Division remains the corps main effort. It attacks to seize Belas and clears its area to PL Hook. The 23rd Mechanized Brigade retains Aradesh.

The 22nd Mechanized Division fixes identified coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge until the 18th Division’s attack on Belas no longer requires protection. The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when Belas is seized, Aradesh is retained, coalition forces at Velin and the Maren Bridge cannot interfere with the main effort, and both divisions can continue toward PL Jab.

#### (4) Phase III — Seize Velin and Guard the Southern Aradesh Plain

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** The 22nd Mechanized Division becomes the corps main effort and attacks to seize Velin. The 32nd Infantry Brigade conducts the objective attack while the 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve.

The 18th Armored Division guards the southern Aradesh Plain and retains Aradesh and Belas. The **14th Armored Brigade conducts the guard to identify and delay counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands into the Aradesh Plain**. The 15th Armored Brigade retains Belas, and the 23rd Mechanized Brigade retains Aradesh.

The phase ends when Velin is seized, the designated corps sector of PL Jab is controlled, Aradesh and Belas remain secure, and both divisions are postured for the Phase IV guard and retain missions.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** The 18th Armored Division returns to corps main effort. It conducts a guard along its assigned PL Jab and southern Aradesh Plain sector while retaining Aradesh and Belas. The 14th Armored Brigade conducts the guard, the 15th Armored Brigade retains Belas, and the 23rd Mechanized Brigade retains Aradesh.

The 22nd Mechanized Division conducts a guard along the southern portion of PL Jab. The corps releases the 24th Mechanized Brigade from reserve to conduct the guard while the 32nd Infantry Brigade retains Velin.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The 18th Armored Division drives the corps from Aradesh to Belas, secures each objective with a designated brigade, and then protects the corps against counterattack from the Maren Lowlands. The 22nd Mechanized Division develops the southern enemy situation, fixes forces around Velin during Phase II, seizes Velin during Phase III, and guards the southern PL Jab sector during Phase IV.

The corps will not leave Aradesh, Belas, Velin, the Maren approaches, the corps seam, or a bypassed enemy force without an assigned retaining, guarding, containing, or handover force.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps.

- **Phase I:** Priority of fires to the 18th Armored Division attack on Aradesh and the 15th Armored Brigade clearing south of the city; next priority to the 22nd Mechanized Division movement to contact.
- **Phase II:** Priority to the 18th Armored Division attack on Belas; next priority to the 22nd Mechanized Division fixing action near Velin and the Maren Bridge.
- **Phase III:** Priority shifts to the 22nd Mechanized Division attack on Velin; sufficient fires protect the 18th Armored Division guard against counterattack from the Maren Lowlands.
- **Phase IV:** Priority returns to the 18th Armored Division guard and the most dangerous counterattack; next priority to the 22nd Mechanized Division guard.

Fires isolate objectives, suppress defenders, prevent reinforcement, disrupt counterattack formations, protect routes and retain forces, and cover artillery displacement.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Formations

#### (1) 18th Armored Division

- **Phase I — Corps Main Effort. Task:** Attack to seize Aradesh and clear the division area through PL Punch; employ the 15th Armored Brigade to clear the Aradesh Plain south of the city. **Purpose:** Remove the coalition’s eastern anchor and protect the attack from southern interference. Supports R-2C1 and R-2C2.
- **Phase II — Corps Main Effort. Task:** Attack to seize Belas, clear the division area through PL Hook, and retain Aradesh with the 23rd Mechanized Brigade. **Purpose:** Secure the central corridor while preserving the Phase I gain. Supports R-2C1 and R-2C3.
- **Phase III — Supporting Effort. Task:** Guard the southern Aradesh Plain with the 14th Armored Brigade; retain Belas with the 15th Armored Brigade and Aradesh with the 23rd Mechanized Brigade. **Purpose:** Identify and delay counterattack from the Maren Lowlands and protect the attack on Velin. Supports R-2C1, R-2C3, and R-2C5.
- **Phase IV — Corps Main Effort. Task:** Continue the guard and retain Aradesh and Belas. **Purpose:** Protect corps consolidation and preserve the central objectives. Supports R-2C1, R-2C3, and R-2C6.

#### (2) 22nd Mechanized Division

- **Phase I — Supporting Effort. Task:** Conduct movement to contact in the Maren Lowlands to PL Punch; fix or destroy identified forces; provide the 24th Mechanized Brigade as corps reserve. **Purpose:** Develop the southern enemy situation and protect the attack on Aradesh. Supports R-2C1 and R-2C2.
- **Phase II — Supporting Effort. Task:** Fix identified coalition forces in Velin and near the Maren Bridge; continue to provide the 24th Mechanized Brigade as corps reserve. **Purpose:** Prevent interference with the attack on Belas. Supports R-2C3 and R-2C4.
- **Phase III — Corps Main Effort. Task:** Attack to seize Velin with the 32nd Infantry Brigade while the 24th Mechanized Brigade remains corps reserve. **Purpose:** Complete the corps southern objective system. Supports R-2C5.
- **Phase IV — Supporting Effort. Task:** Guard the southern portion of PL Jab with the released 24th Mechanized Brigade and retain Velin with the 32nd Infantry Brigade. **Purpose:** Protect consolidation and retain the southern objective. Supports R-2C5 and R-2C6.

#### (3) 12th Corps Artillery Group — General Support

- Provide general support to the corps and execute the priorities in Paragraph 3.d.
- Maintain range to the current corps main effort while preserving minimum support to retain and guard forces.
- Displace by echelon so the corps does not lose all indirect-fire support during phase transitions.
- Report inability to support the current or next phase immediately.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Control Measures and Phase Transitions

- Phase lines, east to west: PL Punch, PL Hook, and PL Jab.
- Objectives: OBJ Aradesh, OBJ Belas, and OBJ Velin.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated corps orders govern transition.
- A division retains responsibility for an objective, route, seam, guard sector, contained force, or handover until the receiving formation confirms transfer.

#### (2) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

- Conduct movement to contact when enemy dispositions are insufficiently clear for an attack.
- Establish contact with the smallest suitable force while preserving combat power to develop the situation.
- On contact, report, establish local security, develop the enemy situation, and attack, defend, bypass, delay, or withdraw within authority and the higher purpose.
- A movement-to-contact mission does not authorize abandonment of a retain, seam, route, phase-line, reserve, or supporting responsibility.

#### (3) Reserve

- The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains withheld from the initial action during Phases I–III.
- The corps may commit it during Phases I–III to restore the main effort, defeat a dangerous counterattack, or prevent loss of Aradesh or Belas.
- If committed before Phase IV, the corps must establish a replacement plan for the 22nd Division’s Phase IV guard.
- At the start of Phase IV, release the brigade to the 22nd Mechanized Division for the southern guard.
- A commitment or release order specifies task and purpose, route, supported force, objective or area, fires, sustainment, termination, handover, and follow-on posture.

#### (4) Guard

- The Phase III 18th Division guard protects the 22nd Division attack on Velin and the corps routes and retained objectives.
- The 14th Armored Brigade identifies and delays counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands into the Aradesh Plain.
- Guard orders identify the protected force, sector, observation and reporting requirements, engagement criteria, delay expectations, disengagement criteria, and rearward handover.

#### (5) CCIR

**PIR:** Coalition reserves and counterattack forces; forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands; enemy defenses at Aradesh, Belas, Velin, and the Maren Bridge; bridge and route usability; artillery positions; and threats to the corps seam.

**FFIR:** Loss or threatened loss of Aradesh, Belas, or Velin; failure to clear south of Aradesh; an uncovered corps seam; failure of the Phase III guard; artillery unable to support; loss of a main-effort route; or 24th Mechanized Brigade unavailable for its current reserve or guard responsibility.

#### (6) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Transition to Phase II when Aradesh is seized, the area south of the city and division sectors through PL Punch are controlled, and the attack on Belas is supportable.
- **DP 2:** Commit the corps reserve during Phases I–III when required to restore the main effort, defeat counterattack, or preserve a decisive objective.
- **DP 3:** Transition to Phase III when Belas is seized, Aradesh is retained, and coalition forces near Velin and the Maren Bridge cannot interfere with the attack.
- **DP 4:** Transition to Phase IV when Velin is seized, PL Jab sectors are controlled, and guard and retain responsibilities are established.
- **DP 5:** Release the 24th Mechanized Brigade for the Phase IV guard or designate a replacement if it was committed earlier.

#### (7) Required Reports

- Contact, objective, route, seam, phase-line, guard, retain, reserve, artillery-supportability, and strength reports.
- Immediate report of coalition forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands.
- Phase-transition recommendations with enemy, terrain, route, fires, sustainment, seam, objective, and guard status.
- Any conflict between this order, Annex C, the approved overlay, R-00, or a later authenticated order.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Sustainment follows the corps main effort as it shifts between divisions. Priorities are the current decisive task, artillery support, forces retaining Aradesh, Belas, and Velin, the Phase III guard, and the 24th Mechanized Brigade when serving as reserve or preparing for the Phase IV guard.

### b. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and ammunition priorities follow the phase main effort. The corps maintains separate central and southern routes where possible, controls congestion around Aradesh, Belas, the Maren Bridge, and Velin, and preserves access for artillery displacement and reserve movement.

### c. Maintenance, Recovery, and Medical Support

Recovery priority is the phase main effort, artillery, retained objectives, guard force, and reserve. Casualty evacuation moves east along secured routes. A division reports when losses or route conditions prevent performance of its essential task.

### d. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated into parent formations and do not create additional counters or game effects in baseline play.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The 12th Maneuver Corps exercises operational control of the 18th Armored Division, 22nd Mechanized Division, and 12th Corps Artillery Group.
- The 18th Armored Division is corps main effort during Phases I–II and IV; the 22nd Mechanized Division is corps main effort during Phase III.
- The 12th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support.
- The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains organic to the 22nd Mechanized Division while serving as corps reserve.
- Cross-attachment or movement across the field army seam requires field army authority.

**Succession of command:** Corps commander; deputy corps commander; commander, 18th Armored Division; commander, 22nd Mechanized Division.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the corps tactical network; alternate means use division networks and liaison; contingency means use scheduled reports and prearranged actions. Phase transitions, main-effort shifts between divisions, corps-reserve commitment or release, and changes to objectives or division boundaries require authenticated corps direction.

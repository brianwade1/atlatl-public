# 7th Maneuver Corps

At the start of Phase IV, or earlier on field army order, the corps holds it as the field-army-directed reserve. It remains organic to the 11th Armored Division and may be committed only on field army order after that transition.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The corps area of interest extends from the Vostian frontier west through the northern Aradesh Plain, Drovna Heights, Kirov, the Northwest Bridge, and PL Jab. It includes the seam with the 12th Maneuver Corps, coalition routes from Belas and central Lydia, and assembly areas from which Lydian and RDC Coalition forces could counterattack the corps or move south against the field army main effort.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Northern Aradesh Plain:** Open terrain supports armored and mechanized movement but exposes formations to observation, fires, and counterattack.
- **Drovna Heights:** Rough terrain restricts armored movement, channels the 14th Mechanized Division onto limited routes, and favors dispersed defenders.
- **Kirov:** Kirov (`hex-5-3`) controls the northern transportation network and is a decisive corps objective.
- **Northwest Bridge:** The bridge north of Kirov is OBJ Bridge. It controls a northern crossing and route needed to secure the corps flank and retain freedom of movement.
- **PL Punch, PL Hook, and PL Jab:** These phase lines control the corps’ westward progression and transition from movement to contact to objective attacks and guard.
- **Corps seam:** The southern boundary with the 12th Corps must remain covered to prevent coalition forces from moving between corps or striking the main effort’s northern flank.
- The approved operational graphics establish the division sectors, primary axes, objectives, phase lines, and Phase IV guard sectors. The 14th Mechanized Division owns the northern Drovna Heights–Northwest Bridge sector and the northern PL Jab guard sector. The 11th Armored Division owns the Kirov–northern Aradesh Plain sector, the southern division seam, and the remainder of the corps PL Jab guard sector.
- Each division selects exact movement routes and local support areas within its depicted sector during setup and records them in its backbrief. The 12th Armored Brigade has no standing release point; each commitment order assigns its assembly area, route, release point, objective or area, limit of advance, termination criteria, and return or follow-on posture.

#### (2) Weather and Light

The R-00 weather forecast applies. Dry conditions through Turn 6 favor the opening movement to contact and armored clearing operations. Rain during Turns 7–12 increases reliance on roads and junctions, slows artillery displacement, and restricts maneuver in the Drovna Heights. Fog may conceal movement but can delay identification of counterattacking forces. Weather effects not native to Atlatl provide planning context only and cause no additional baseline game modifier.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

Vostian intelligence assesses that Lydian and RDC Coalition forces defend the northern Aradesh Plain, Drovna routes, Kirov, and the Northwest Bridge. Infantry and mechanized formations can delay in the heights and defend the city and bridge. Coalition artillery can mass on restricted routes, and armored reserves may reinforce Kirov or counterattack an exposed corps seam or shoulder.

#### (2) Most Likely Course of Action

Coalition forces delay east of PL Punch, defend Kirov and the Northwest Bridge in depth, and preserve routes west of PL Hook. They trade less important terrain for time and counterattack a Vostian formation that becomes separated from adjacent units, artillery, or sustainment.

#### (3) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Coalition forces fix the 14th Mechanized Division in the Drovna Heights or at OBJ Bridge while massing armored and mechanized forces against the 11th Armored Division near Kirov or along the corps seam. This could isolate the corps main effort and permit movement south against the 12th Corps.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

The Vostian 3rd Field Army attacks west to seize Aradesh, Kirov, OBJ Bridge, Belas, and Velin; clears Lydian and RDC Coalition forces to PL Jab; and then guards along PL Jab while retaining the seized objectives. The 12th Maneuver Corps is the field army main effort throughout. The 7th Corps supports by developing and fixing northern forces, seizing Kirov and OBJ Bridge, retaining those objectives, and securing the northern flank.

#### (2) Adjacent and Supporting Units

- **12th Maneuver Corps:** Field army main effort south of the corps seam. It seizes Aradesh, Belas, and Velin. The corps prevents northern coalition forces from interfering with those attacks.
- **7th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to the corps with phase-specific priorities established in Paragraph 3.d.
- Other reconnaissance, aviation, engineering, air defense, EW/cyber, sustainment, and medical capabilities are incorporated into parent formations and provide planning context only in baseline play.

### e. Civil Considerations

Kirov and the Northwest Bridge are critical transportation infrastructure. The corps preserves usable roads, bridge structures, administrative facilities, and communications nodes where operationally practicable. Civil-security activity remains secondary to retaining objectives, guarding PL Jab, and supporting the field army main effort.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at scenario start. Cross-attachment with the 12th Corps requires field army direction.

### g. Assumptions

- Kirov, OBJ Bridge, and the approaches to PL Jab begin under Lydian or RDC Coalition control.
- The 12th Corps maintains sufficient pressure to prevent major coalition reinforcement of this sector.
- The 12th Armored Brigade can serve as corps and later field-army-directed reserve without creating another counter or changing its organic relationship.
- Bridge status changes only through implemented Atlatl mechanics. Any optional bridge rule must be agreed before setup.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local, the **7th Maneuver Corps conducts a movement to contact through the northern Aradesh Plain and Drovna Heights to PL Punch; attacks to seize OBJ Kirov and OBJ Bridge and clear its area to PL Hook; conducts a movement to contact to PL Jab; and then guards along PL Jab while retaining Kirov and the Northwest Bridge in order to prevent Lydian and RDC Coalition forces from interfering with the 12th Corps main effort and to secure the field army’s northern flank.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Develop, fix, and defeat coalition forces in the northern sector so they cannot reinforce or counterattack the 12th Corps main effort, while securing the northern transportation and crossing network through scenario completion.

#### Key Tasks

- Conduct movement to contact to PL Punch in both division sectors.
- Clear the northern Aradesh Plain and Drovna Heights successively to the assigned phase lines.
- Seize and retain Kirov and the Northwest Bridge.
- Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and destroy identified forces that threaten the corps or field army main effort.
- Maintain the corps seam and secure primary and alternate routes.
- Guard the northern portion of PL Jab during Phase IV.
- Hold the 12th Armored Brigade as corps reserve during Phases I–III and as field-army-directed reserve during Phase IV.
- Maintain artillery coverage during every advance and transition.

Stable corps task identifiers:

| Task ID | Corps task | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R-1C1** | Conduct movement to contact and clear assigned terrain to PL Punch. | Establish contact, develop the enemy situation, and prevent reinforcement of Aradesh. |
| **R-1C2** | Attack to seize and retain OBJ Kirov. | Control the northern transportation hub and deny coalition lateral movement. |
| **R-1C3** | Attack to seize and retain OBJ Bridge while clearing the Drovna sector. | Secure the northern crossing and protect the corps flank. |
| **R-1C4** | Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and destroy identified threatening forces. | Prevent the coalition from forming another northern defense or massing against the main effort. |

| **R-1C6** | Guard along PL Jab and preserve the field-army-directed reserve. | Protect consolidation and provide a mobile response to counterattack. |

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Northern Lydian and RDC Coalition forces are destroyed, fixed, or displaced west of PL Jab and cannot interfere with the 12th Corps.
- **Friendly:** Both divisions remain mutually supporting; the 7th Corps Artillery Group can cover the guard; and the 12th Armored Brigade is available as field-army-directed reserve unless committed.
- **Terrain:** Kirov and the Northwest Bridge are retained; the corps portion of PL Jab is guarded; and the corps seam and supporting routes remain secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The corps conducts two movement-to-contact periods separated by deliberate objective attacks. The 11th Armored Division is the corps main effort throughout. The 14th Mechanized Division supports in the Drovna Heights and at OBJ Bridge. Both divisions maintain the seam and secure routes needed for artillery and sustainment.

#### (2) Phase I — Movement to Contact to PL Punch

**Estimated Turns 1–6.** The 11th Armored Division, as corps main effort, attacks to clear the northern Aradesh Plain through PL Punch. The 11th Armored and 13th Mechanized Brigades conduct the division’s clearing action while the 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

The 14th Mechanized Division conducts a movement to contact through the Drovna Heights to PL Punch to establish contact and develop the enemy situation. On contact, it fixes or clears identified forces that can interfere with the corps main effort or reinforce Aradesh.

The phase ends when both division sectors reach or control PL Punch, routes and the corps seam are secure, enemy dispositions support an attack on Kirov and OBJ Bridge, and the artillery group can support Phase II.

#### (3) Phase II — Seize Kirov and OBJ Bridge

**Estimated Turns 3–12.** The 11th Armored Division attacks to seize OBJ Kirov and clears its area to PL Hook. The 14th Mechanized Division attacks to seize OBJ Bridge and clears the Drovna sector to PL Hook. The 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

The phase ends when Kirov and OBJ Bridge are seized, routes and bridge approaches are usable, the division seam is secure, and both divisions can continue toward PL Jab without risking the retained objectives.

#### (4) Phase III — Movement to Contact to PL Jab

**Estimated Turns 7–18.** Both divisions conduct movement to contact to PL Jab to establish or regain contact and develop the enemy situation. The 11th Armored Division remains the corps main effort and retains Kirov with the 13th Mechanized Brigade. The 14th Mechanized Division retains the Northwest Bridge with the 31st Infantry Brigade. The 12th Armored Brigade remains corps reserve.

On contact, divisions fix and destroy coalition forces that threaten retained objectives, the corps seam, or the 12th Corps main effort.

The phase ends when the corps controls its assigned portion of PL Jab, Kirov and the Northwest Bridge remain secure, and the corps is postured for the Phase IV guard and reserve transition.

#### (5) Phase IV — Guard and Retain

**Estimated Turns 15–20.** Both divisions conduct a guard along assigned sectors of PL Jab to provide early warning, prevent enemy ground observation and direct fire against the consolidating field army, and fight to gain time. The 13th Mechanized Brigade retains Kirov; the 31st Infantry Brigade retains the Northwest Bridge.

The 12th Armored Brigade transitions to field-army-directed reserve and occupies its directed assembly area. It remains organic to the 11th Armored Division but may be committed only on field army order.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The 11th Armored Division advances on the northern Aradesh Plain–Kirov axis and is responsible for the corps’ decisive tasks. The 14th Mechanized Division advances through the Drovna Heights toward OBJ Bridge, prevents interference with the main effort, and secures the northern flank. During Phases III–IV, each division assigns a brigade to retain its seized objective while the remaining maneuver force advances or guards.

The corps will not leave Kirov, OBJ Bridge, the seam, or a bypassed enemy force without an assigned retaining, containing, or handover force. Divisions coordinate contact points and routes before crossing each phase line.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps.

- **Phase I:** Priority of fires to the 11th Armored Division clearing the northern Aradesh Plain; next priority to the 14th Mechanized Division in the Drovna Heights.
- **Phase II:** Priority to the 11th Armored Division attack on Kirov; next priority to the 14th Mechanized Division attack on OBJ Bridge.
- **Phase III:** Priority to the 11th Armored Division movement to contact; shift temporarily to defeat the most dangerous threat to Kirov, OBJ Bridge, or the corps seam.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the 11th Armored Division guard and any field-army-directed reserve commitment; next priority to the 14th Mechanized Division guard.

Fires support movement to contact, isolate objectives, suppress defenders, disrupt reinforcement and counterattack, protect routes and bridges, and cover artillery displacement.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Formations

#### (1) 11th Armored Division — Corps Main Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to clear the northern Aradesh Plain through PL Punch while providing the 12th Armored Brigade as corps reserve. **Purpose:** Develop and defeat the northern defense and prevent reinforcement of Aradesh. Supports R-1C1 and R-1C5.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize Kirov and clear the division area through PL Hook. **Purpose:** Control the northern transportation hub. Supports R-1C2 and R-1C5.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and retain Kirov. **Purpose:** Destroy threats to the corps while preserving the decisive northern objective. Supports R-1C2, R-1C4, and R-1C5.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Conduct a guard along the assigned PL Jab sector and retain Kirov; hold the 12th Armored Brigade as field-army-directed reserve. **Purpose:** Protect consolidation and preserve a decisive mobile response. Supports R-1C2 and R-1C6.

#### (2) 14th Mechanized Division — Supporting Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Conduct movement to contact through the Drovna Heights to PL Punch; fix and clear identified forces. **Purpose:** Prevent interference with the corps main effort and develop the northern flank. Supports R-1C1, R-1C3, and R-1C5.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize OBJ Bridge and clear the division area through PL Hook. **Purpose:** Secure the northern crossing and protect the corps flank. Supports R-1C3 and R-1C5.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Jab and retain the Northwest Bridge. **Purpose:** Destroy threats in the Drovna sector while preserving the crossing. Supports R-1C3, R-1C4, and R-1C5.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Conduct a guard along the assigned PL Jab sector and retain the Northwest Bridge. **Purpose:** Protect consolidation and deny coalition use of the crossing. Supports R-1C3 and R-1C6.

#### (3) 7th Corps Artillery Group — General Support

- Provide general support to the corps and execute the priorities in Paragraph 3.d.
- Maintain range to the corps main effort while preserving minimum support to the supporting division.
- Displace by echelon so the corps does not lose all indirect-fire support during a phase transition.
- Report inability to support the current or next phase immediately.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Control Measures and Phase Transitions

- Phase lines, east to west: PL Punch, PL Hook, and PL Jab.
- Objectives: OBJ Kirov and OBJ Bridge at the Northwest Bridge.
- Phase windows are estimates; conditions and authenticated corps orders govern transition.
- A division retains responsibility for a route, seam, objective, contained force, or handover until the receiving unit confirms transfer.

#### (2) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

- Conduct movement to contact when enemy dispositions are insufficiently clear for an attack.
- Establish contact with the smallest suitable force while preserving combat power to develop the situation.
- On contact, report, establish local security, develop the enemy situation, and attack, defend, bypass, delay, or withdraw within authority and the higher purpose.
- A movement-to-contact mission does not authorize abandonment of a retain, seam, route, phase-line, or supporting responsibility.

#### (3) Reserve

- The 12th Armored Brigade remains withheld from the initial action during Phases I–III.
- The corps may commit it during Phases I–III to restore the main effort, defeat a dangerous counterattack, or prevent loss of Kirov or OBJ Bridge.
- At the start of Phase IV, or earlier on field army order, corps commitment authority ends and the brigade becomes field-army-directed reserve.
- A commitment order specifies task and purpose, route, supported force, objective or area, limit of advance, fires, sustainment, termination, and follow-on posture.

#### (4) CCIR

**PIR:** Coalition reserves or counterattack forces moving toward Kirov, OBJ Bridge, the corps seam, or PL Jab; enemy withdrawal routes; bridge usability; artillery locations; and forces capable of interfering with the 12th Corps.

**FFIR:** Loss or threatened loss of Kirov or OBJ Bridge; an uncovered corps seam; a division unable to reach its phase line; artillery unable to support; loss of a primary route; or the 12th Armored Brigade unavailable for reserve duties.

#### (5) Decision Points

- **DP 1:** Transition to Phase II when both division sectors control PL Punch and attacks on Kirov and OBJ Bridge are supportable.
- **DP 2:** Commit the corps reserve during Phases I–III when required to restore the main effort, defeat counterattack, or preserve a decisive objective.
- **DP 3:** Transition to Phase III when Kirov and OBJ Bridge are seized and both divisions can continue toward PL Jab.
- **DP 4:** Transition the 12th Armored Brigade to field-army-directed reserve at the start of Phase IV or on field army order.
- **DP 5:** Transition to Phase IV when PL Jab sectors are controlled and guard and retain responsibilities are established.

#### (6) Required Reports

- Contact, objective, bridge, route, seam, phase-line, guard, retain, reserve, artillery-supportability, and strength reports.
- Immediate report of coalition reserve commitment or counterattack.
- Phase-transition recommendations with enemy, terrain, route, fires, sustainment, seam, and objective status.
- Any conflict between this order, Annex C, the approved overlay, R-00, or a later authenticated order.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept and Priorities

Sustainment supports the 11th Armored Division main effort while preserving the 14th Mechanized Division’s ability to seize and retain OBJ Bridge. Priorities are the current decisive task, artillery support, forces retaining Kirov and the Northwest Bridge, and the 12th Armored Brigade when preparing for reserve duties.

### b. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and ammunition priority follow the current phase’s main effort. The corps maintains separate primary and alternate routes where possible, controls movement at restricted Drovna and Kirov junctions, and preserves access for artillery displacement and reserve movement.

### c. Maintenance, Recovery, and Medical Support

Recovery priority is the main-effort division, artillery, retained objectives, and reserve. Casualty evacuation moves east along secured routes. A division reports when losses or route conditions prevent performance of its essential task.

### d. Atlatl Abstraction

Sustainment, engineers, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, EW/cyber, and medical functions are incorporated into parent formations and do not create additional counters or game effects in baseline play.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

- The 7th Maneuver Corps exercises operational control of the 11th Armored Division, 14th Mechanized Division, and 7th Corps Artillery Group.
- The 11th Armored Division is the corps main effort throughout.
- The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support.
- The 12th Armored Brigade remains organic to the 11th Armored Division when serving as corps or field-army-directed reserve.
- Cross-attachment or movement across the field army seam requires field army authority.

**Succession of command:** Corps commander; deputy corps commander; commander, 11th Armored Division; commander, 14th Mechanized Division.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use the corps tactical network; alternate means use division networks and liaison; contingency means use scheduled reports and prearranged actions. Phase transitions, corps-reserve commitment, and changes to objectives or division boundaries require authenticated corps direction. Commitment of the field-army-directed reserve requires authenticated field army direction.

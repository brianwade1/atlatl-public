# Red Master Synchronization Crosswalk

> **Side-specific product:** For the Red player and Red staff. An optional controller may also use it, but controller participation is not required for baseline play. Do not provide it to the Blue player during closed-plan play.

## 1. Purpose and Authority

This crosswalk provides one side-wide view of the Vostian 3rd Field Army plan for **OPERATION CRIMSON THRESHOLD**. It links the field-army tasks in R-00 to the corps, division, and artillery orders; operational phases; main-effort shifts; objective attacks and handovers; movement-to-contact, fixing, clearing, guard, and retain missions; reserve authorities; fires; and player synchronization checks. It summarizes rather than replaces the signed orders.

Use the following precedence when sources differ:

1. A later authenticated fragmentary order or in-play decision by the proper Red headquarters.
2. The applicable signed operations or support order.
3. The operational graphics for the location of depicted control measures.
4. This master crosswalk.

Report a conflict rather than silently choosing the most favorable interpretation. The approved operational graphics govern the depicted locations of axes, boundaries, objectives, phase lines, and guard sectors. They intentionally do not publish hex-coordinate translations; the Red player interprets the graphics against the game map. Exact movement routes, reserve release points, passage points, local support areas, and mission-specific limits of advance are selected during setup or by the proper headquarters in the applicable commitment order and recorded in the Red backbrief or side log. Baseline play requires no controller. An optional controller may add injects or adjudications only under procedures agreed before play.

A phase transition does not itself complete an objective handover or release a reserve.

## 9. Red Player Synchronization Checks

In controller-free baseline play, the Red player or Red team uses these checks to record decisions and maintain the order hierarchy. An optional controller may use the same list without changing the baseline authorities.

- Confirm that phase transitions are based on conditions and authenticated decisions; the overlapping turn windows alone do not authorize transition.
- Keep the 12th Corps as the field-army main effort unless an authenticated field-army order changes it. Record internal 12th Corps shifts from 18th Division to 22nd Division in Phase III and back to 18th Division in Phase IV.
- In Phase I, verify that the 15th Armored Brigade clears the Aradesh Plain south of the city while the 14th Armored Brigade attacks Aradesh and the 23rd Mechanized Brigade isolates it.
- In Phase III, verify that the 14th Armored Brigade guards against counterattacking forces moving north from the Maren Lowlands while the 22nd Division attacks Velin.
- Record every objective seizure, control/usability check, retain handover, and accepting formation for Aradesh, Kirov, the Northwest Bridge, Belas, and Velin.
- Keep the 12th Armored Brigade as 7th Corps reserve through Phase III unless committed; transition it to field-army-directed reserve only on authenticated field-army direction.
- Keep the 24th Mechanized Brigade as 12th Corps reserve through Phase III unless committed. If committed early, require a replacement Phase IV guard plan. In Phase IV, record its release to 22nd Division before it begins the southern guard.
- Record the protected force, guard sector, observation and reporting requirements, engagement criteria, required delay, disengagement criteria, and rearward handover for every guard mission.
- Record all field-army, corps, and artillery main-effort or priority shifts and the condition that justified each change.
- Deconflict corps seams, movement routes, bridge approaches, artillery displacement, reserves, casualty evacuation, and sustainment traffic.
- Do not allow a formation to cross a corps boundary, change a named objective, advance beyond an assigned phase line, or commit the field-army-directed reserve without the required authority.
- Require each corps to report once per turn and on significant change: phase/task status; objectives, bridges, routes, seams, shoulders, guards, retains, and passage; reserve status; artillery priority and supportability; sustainment reach; accepted risk; and transition recommendation.
- At scenario completion, record objective and bridge control, guard-sector status, remaining Red combat power, reserve availability or commitment, major handovers, and unresolved order conflicts for balance analysis.

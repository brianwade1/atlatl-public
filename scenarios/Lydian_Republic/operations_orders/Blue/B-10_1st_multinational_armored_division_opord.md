## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends east of Aradesh toward the Vostian advance, north toward the seam with the 2nd Multinational Infantry Division and Kirov routes, south toward firm routes emerging from the Maren Lowlands, and west through Belas and the central Arel crossing. Actions in the Kirov and southern sectors may shift Vostian combat power toward or around the division.

The division must monitor routes that allow Vostian armored forces to bypass Aradesh, the status of the Belas crossing and westward routes, and corps-reserve movement corridors from west of the Arel.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Aradesh Plain:** Open terrain around Aradesh enables rapid armored and mechanized movement and creates multiple eastern approaches. Defenders must prevent Vostian freedom to maneuver while avoiding exposure to concentrated combat power.
- **Aradesh:** The city is the political and administrative center of eastern Lydia. Urban terrain may slow an attack, but isolation would threaten the defense more than loss of outlying ground. The division must retain a usable connection west.
- **Belas and central Arel approaches:** Belas and its crossing connect the division to central Lydia. Loss of the route or crossing would isolate the central defense and permit Vostian exploitation.
- **Northern-central seam:** Routes toward Kirov and the 2nd Division sector enable mutual support but also create a bypass around the northern side of Aradesh.
- **Southern approaches:** Firm routes north of or through the Maren Lowlands may threaten Aradesh from the south or draw the division away from its central task.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

Corps intelligence assesses that the Vostian 12th Maneuver Corps is the principal threat in the central and southern area. It includes an armored division, a mechanized division, and corps artillery. Elements of the Vostian 7th Maneuver Corps may influence the northern division boundary or reinforce a central penetration.

Leading mechanized forces approach Aradesh while follow-on armored forces retain the capability to penetrate, bypass, or exploit. Infantry and mechanized forces may secure the southern flank and routes through the Maren approaches. Exact formation locations and commitment of Vostian exploitation forces remain uncertain.

#### (2) Capabilities and Limitations

Vostian forces can fix the 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade at Aradesh, mass armored forces against a seam, bypass the city toward Belas, attack from more than one axis, and use artillery to suppress defensive or reserve movement. They may also threaten the division's northern or southern flank to delay release of the armored reserve.

Vostian forces are vulnerable when armored elements advance beyond mechanized flank security, when they become fixed in Aradesh, when a penetration is canalized on a limited route, or when they disperse to secure captured terrain.

#### (3) Most Likely Course of Action

Vostia uses mechanized forces to attack and isolate Aradesh while an armored formation penetrates on a favorable route north or south of the city. Follow-on armor exploits toward Belas, and mechanized forces secure the shoulders of the penetration and prevent coalition reinforcement.

#### (4) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Vostia bypasses Aradesh with concentrated armored combat power, penetrates the seam between the 2nd and 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigades or the seam with the 2nd Division, and reaches Belas before the 1st Coalition Armored Brigade can counterattack. Simultaneous pressure against Aradesh and the southern approaches prevents the division from restoring the route west.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

The RDC Coalition Land Corps conducts an area defense, organized in depth, in eastern Lydia to retain Aradesh and Kirov, deny Vostian use of the Arel River crossings, defeat or contain operational penetrations, and preserve conditions for coalition reinforcement and restoration of Lydian territorial integrity.

The corps purpose is to deny Vostia a rapid territorial fait accompli. The 1st Multinational Armored Division is the initial corps main effort. The corps expects the division to retain Aradesh and Belas, deny the central crossing, fix and defeat central penetrations, preserve combat power, and support employment of the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade if the threat exceeds division capability.

#### (2) Adjacent and Supporting Units

- **2nd Multinational Infantry Division:** Conducts an area defense to retain Kirov, block the Drovna and Maren routes, and deny assigned northern and southern crossings. Its 4th Coalition Mechanized Brigade connects the northern and central sectors. Coordination along the division seam is essential.
- **7th Coalition Armored Brigade:** Corps reserve west of the Arel. On corps order, it may block or counterattack in the division area. The division must be prepared to provide passage, flank security, route priority, and supporting fires.
- **Lydian forces:** Protect rear areas, infrastructure, displaced civilians, and government facilities. They retain these responsibilities and are not assigned division maneuver missions.

### e. Civil Considerations

Aradesh contains a large civilian population, competing political authorities, government buildings, transportation infrastructure, and separatist activity. The division will prevent unnecessary damage to critical infrastructure, maintain a western route for civil and military movement, and coordinate with Lydian authorities. Civilian congestion or unrest may affect movement and route availability.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade is not attached and remains under corps control unless a corps order directs otherwise.

### g. Assumptions

- Aradesh and Belas begin under coalition/Lydian control and the Belas crossing is usable.
- The division receives no additional formation during the operation unless directed by corps.
- The 2nd Division maintains responsibility north and south of the division sector unless boundaries are changed by corps order.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 18 May 2030 at 0600 local time, the **1st Multinational Armored Division conducts an area defense, organized in depth, in the Aradesh–Belas sector to retain Aradesh and Belas, deny Vostian access to the central Arel crossing, fix the central attack, and set conditions to defeat an armored penetration by counterattack.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander's Intent

#### Purpose

Preserve the corps central defensive framework and prevent Vostia from converting tactical success around Aradesh into operational access to central Lydia.

#### Key Tasks

- Retain Aradesh and a secure route from Aradesh toward Belas.
- Deny Vostian use of the Belas crossing and central Arel approaches.
- Identify the Vostian penetration or exploitation force before releasing the division reserve.
- Fix and expose penetrating armored forces for counterattack.
- Maintain coordination with the 2nd Division and prevent a seam penetration.
- Preserve passage routes and support for the corps reserve.
- Avoid unnecessary attrition and retain mobile combat power through 28 May 2030, 0600 local.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Central Vostian attacks are halted, contained, or compelled to culminate; no armored force can exploit west through Belas.
- **Friendly:** The division retains Aradesh, remains connected to the Arel defense, and retains or restores a mobile armored capability.
- **Terrain:** Aradesh and Belas remain under coalition/Lydian control; the central crossing remains denied to Vostia; lateral routes to the 2nd Division remain usable where terrain and route conditions permit.
- **Civil and political:** The Lydian administration retains a presence in Aradesh, essential infrastructure is protected consistent with mission requirements, and the defense remains within Lydian territory.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division conducts an **area defense, organized in depth**, centered on Aradesh and the approaches to Belas. The **2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade** is the initial main effort and retains Aradesh while fixing the central attack. The **3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade** is the supporting effort and retains Belas, denies the central crossing, and secures the seam and passage routes. The **1st Coalition Armored Brigade** remains the division reserve. The **1st Division Artillery Group** provides general support.

The forward defense will cause Vostian forces to deploy, reveal their main axis, and lose freedom of maneuver. The division will not allow the defense of outlying terrain to consume the armored reserve or isolate Aradesh. When a significant penetration is fixed and a viable route exists, the division will shift the main effort to the 1st Coalition Armored Brigade and mass fires to restore the defense. If the penetration exceeds division capability, the division will contain it, preserve passage routes, and request the corps reserve. **Annex C synchronizes the task and purpose of each subordinate formation by phase; the conditions in this paragraph and paragraph 3.f govern transitions.**

#### (2) Phase I — Establish the Defense

**Planning window: 18–19 May 2030.** The 2nd Mechanized Brigade occupies positions at Aradesh that block the principal eastern avenues and secures the westward route. The 3rd Mechanized Brigade occupies positions to retain Belas, deny the central crossing approaches, and secure the route and division seam. The 1st Armored Brigade occupies a reserve location with access to more than one response route. Division artillery positions to support Aradesh while remaining able to shift toward Belas or a counterattack.

This phase ends when the defense and lateral routes are established and the division can assess the most dangerous Vostian axis, or enemy action forces an earlier transition.

#### (3) Phase II — Deny and Fix

**Planning window: 19–24 May 2030.** The 2nd Mechanized Brigade retains Aradesh and fixes attacking forces. The 3rd Mechanized Brigade retains Belas, denies the central crossing, secures the division seam, and supports the Aradesh connection without uncovering the crossing. The division may exchange nonessential ground east of the city for time, but it will not surrender the western route or allow an unopposed advance to Belas.

This phase ends when an armored penetration threatens Aradesh or Belas, the division reserve release criteria are met, a corps-reserve operation begins, or Vostian forces culminate.

#### (4) Phase III — Counterattack and Restore

**Planning window: 21–26 May 2030.** On division order, the 1st Armored Brigade counterattacks a fixed penetration or blocks its most dangerous avenue as the division main effort. A direction to reinforce a sector will state the brigade's tactical task and purpose. The 2nd and 3rd Mechanized Brigades fix the enemy, secure passage and counterattack flanks, and retain essential terrain. Division artillery shifts priority to the counterattack unless immediate crossing denial requires priority fires.

The counterattack terminates when the penetration is defeated or contained and the defensive framework is restored, or when continued attack would expose Aradesh, Belas, or the counterattack force to unacceptable risk.

#### (5) Phase IV — Consolidate and Preserve

**Planning window: 25–27 May 2030.** The division reestablishes mutually supporting positions, secures Aradesh and Belas, repositions artillery, restores an armored reserve when possible, and prepares to defeat renewed local attacks or support corps follow-on operations.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The 2nd Mechanized Brigade conducts an area defense, organized in depth, centered on Aradesh and its western connection. It may occupy forward positions to delay and identify the attack, but it contracts before an outlying position becomes isolated. If it cannot retain both an outlying position and the city, it retains Aradesh and secures the usable route toward Belas. Displacement west of Aradesh requires division approval unless communications are unavailable and movement is necessary to prevent destruction.

The 3rd Mechanized Brigade retains Belas, denies the central crossing approaches, secures the Aradesh–Belas route and contact with the 2nd Division, and controls passage preparations for division or corps reserve movement. It supports the Aradesh defense only on division order and only after confirming that Belas remains retained and the crossing approaches remain denied. When Belas or the central crossing is directly threatened, retention and crossing denial take precedence over movement east.

The 1st Armored Brigade remains concentrated and uncommitted, maintains two feasible response routes, and updates passage and support requirements every 12 hours. It is released only by authenticated division order after the division commander determines that a significant Vostian penetration threatens operationally important terrain, the defending brigade cannot restore the situation, a time-sensitive opportunity exists to defeat the penetrating force, and routes, passage, flank security, and artillery support are available. Preferred employment is a supported counterattack against an exposed penetration flank. When that opportunity is absent, the brigade blocks the most dangerous avenue threatening Aradesh, Belas, or their connecting route.

The counterattack terminates when the penetration is defeated or contained, the central defense is restored, or continued action would exceed supporting fires, expose essential terrain, or prevent response to a renewed threat. The brigade then consolidates on defensible terrain and reports whether it can continue, hold, or reconstitute as division reserve.

If the corps commits the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade in the division area, the division fixes the enemy, establishes movement priority, secures passage and at least one counterattack flank, provides route and enemy information, coordinates sustainment and terrain handover, and shifts artillery priority when the corps reserve becomes the decisive operation. Transfer of operational control, if any, requires a corps order; the division may not divide or retask the corps reserve outside that order.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 1st Division Artillery Group remains in general support of the division.

Initial priority of fires:

1. 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade defending Aradesh.
2. 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade if Belas or the central crossing is directly threatened.
3. 1st Coalition Armored Brigade upon commitment.
4. 7th Coalition Armored Brigade if corps commits it as the decisive operation in the division area.

Fires will disrupt Vostian concentrations, blunt a penetration, support the defense of Aradesh and Belas, and enable counterattack. Priority shifts to the division or corps counterattack force when it becomes the main effort, unless immediate crossing denial takes precedence.

#### (1) 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade — Initial Main Effort

- **Task:** Retain Aradesh and fix the central attack on its principal eastern approaches. **Purpose:** Deny Vostia its central political objective and expose attacking forces to division counterattack. Supports B-C1, B-C5, and B-C6.
- **Task:** Secure a usable route west toward Belas. **Purpose:** Prevent isolation and enable support, passage, or controlled displacement.
- **Task:** Contract from nonessential approach terrain before isolation; if unable to retain both an outlying position and the city, retain Aradesh and the westward route. **Purpose:** Preserve the essential defense and avoid loss of the brigade. Supports B-C1 and B-C6.

- **Task:** Report penetration indicators and inability to hold essential terrain immediately. **Purpose:** Enable timely division and corps reserve decisions.
- **On Order:** When the division reserve is committed, continue fixing the enemy and provide passage and one protected counterattack flank for the 1st or 7th Coalition Armored Brigade. **Purpose:** Enable restoration of the central defense. Supports B-C5.

#### (2) 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade — Supporting Effort

- **Task:** Retain Belas and deny the central Arel crossing approaches. **Purpose:** Prevent Vostian access to the crossing and preserve the division route to central Lydia. Supports B-C1, B-C3, and B-C6.
- **Task:** Secure lateral contact with the 2nd Mechanized Brigade and the 2nd Division. **Purpose:** Prevent seam penetration and isolation of Aradesh.
- **On Order:** Support the Aradesh defense after confirming that Belas remains retained and the crossing approaches remain denied. The order will state the tactical task and purpose for any committed movement. **Purpose:** Balance the city and crossing requirements without creating a direct route into central Lydia.
- **Task:** Maintain route, traffic-control, passage, and flank-security preparations for a division or corps counterattack. **Purpose:** Enable B-C5 without interfering with crossing defense.
- **Task:** Give Belas and central-crossing denial precedence when either is directly threatened. **Purpose:** Accomplish B-C3 while preserving the division's western connection.

#### (3) 1st Coalition Armored Brigade — Division Reserve

- **Task:** Remain concentrated and uncommitted; maintain two feasible response routes, current passage plans, and readiness to reach either central-sector axis. **Purpose:** Preserve division freedom of action while the Vostian main effort remains uncertain. Supports B-C5 and B-C6.
- **On Order:** Block or counterattack a Vostian armored penetration threatening Aradesh, Belas, or the route between them. **Purpose:** Restore the defense and prevent operational breakthrough. Supports B-C1, B-C5, and B-C6.
- **Task:** Prefer a supported counterattack against an exposed flank; when conditions are unsuitable, block the most dangerous avenue within supporting range. **Purpose:** Defeat or contain the penetration without sacrificing the division reserve. Supports B-C5 and B-C6.
- **Task:** Terminate under division criteria, consolidate on defensible terrain, and report ability to continue, hold, or reconstitute. **Purpose:** Restore a mobile capability for subsequent operations. Supports B-C6.

#### (4) 1st Division Artillery Group — General Support

- **Task:** Provide general support to the division. **Purpose:** Disrupt Vostian concentrations, blunt penetrations, and enable decisive maneuver.
- **Task:** Maintain ability to shift priority among the Aradesh defense, Belas crossing denial, and a committed counterattack force. **Purpose:** Mass limited fires at the decisive point.
- **Task:** Displace when required to preserve support range and survivability. **Purpose:** Maintain fires through the 10-day operation.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Effective Time

This order is effective on receipt. Phase I begins 18 May 2030 at 0600 local.

#### (2) Control Measures

- Control measures for Aradesh and Belas are established in the operations
  overlay.
- Exact division boundaries, battle positions, passage lanes, artillery
  areas, and phase lines are established in the operations overlay.
  Subordinate commanders coordinate directly and retain flexibility within
  assigned tasks until changes are published by fragmentary order.
- The western route and central crossing are not to be blocked by stationary friendly units in a manner that prevents reserve passage unless required by immediate combat.
- Coalition forces will not cross into Vostia without new political authorization.

#### (3) Commander's Critical Information Requirements

**PIR**

1. Which Vostian armored formation is the central exploitation force, and where will it commit?
2. Is Vostia attacking through Aradesh, bypassing north, or bypassing south toward Belas?
3. Is a Vostian formation capable of reaching Belas or isolating Aradesh within 24 hours?
4. Has a penetration exposed a flank suitable for division counterattack?
5. Is Vostian artillery positioned to disrupt reserve movement or mass against Aradesh?

**FFIR**

1. 2nd or 3rd Mechanized Brigade at or below 70 percent combat power or unable to perform its essential task.
2. Loss or interdiction of the Aradesh–Belas route or the Belas crossing.
3. 1st Armored Brigade not ready, committed, at or below 70 percent combat power, or unable to reach the threatened sector.
4. Loss of contact with the 2nd Division along the seam.
5. Inability of division artillery to support Aradesh, Belas, or a counterattack.
6. Requirement for corps-reserve commitment.

#### (4) Division-Reserve Release Criteria

The division commander may release the 1st Coalition Armored Brigade when:

- a Vostian maneuver formation penetrates the primary defense and threatens Aradesh, Belas, or the route between them;
- the 2nd or 3rd Mechanized Brigade cannot restore the situation;
- a time-sensitive opportunity exists to defeat a significant Vostian force; and
- viable routes, passage, flank security, and artillery support are available.

#### (5) Division-Reserve Employment Procedures

**Before commitment**

- The 1st Coalition Armored Brigade remains concentrated, avoids decisive engagement, and maintains one feasible response toward Aradesh and one toward Belas or a bypass route.
- The brigade reports strength, readiness, primary and alternate routes, estimated travel time, passage requirements, and sustainment or fires shortfalls every 12 hours.
- Repositioning to preserve route access or survivability does not constitute release. The brigade will not begin the planned counterattack without authenticated division direction.

**Committing order**

The division fragmentary order specifies the task and purpose, objective or blocking area, route, passage point, release time, supported and supporting units, artillery priority, limit of advance, termination criteria, and consolidation or reconstitution requirement.

**Passage and flank responsibilities**

- The 2nd Mechanized Brigade fixes the enemy threatening Aradesh and secures the passage area and designated counterattack flank when the reserve moves through its sector.
- The 3rd Mechanized Brigade controls movement near Belas, preserves crossing defense, and secures the passage route or designated flank when the reserve moves through its sector.
- Both brigades maintain terrain responsibility until handover is confirmed and keep the designated route clear unless immediate combat requires otherwise.
- The 1st Division Artillery Group shifts priority to the reserve when it becomes the division main effort unless immediate Belas crossing denial takes precedence.

**Communications failure and termination**

- Communications failure does not delegate planned counterattack authority. Before release, the brigade may reposition to avoid destruction or block an immediate threat to its own position, then reports at the first opportunity.
- After release, the brigade continues within the last authenticated task, route, objective, and limit of advance.
- Terminate when the penetration is defeated or contained, the central framework is restored, or continued action would exceed supporting fires, expose Aradesh or Belas, sever the route west, or prevent response to renewed attack.

#### (6) Brigade Decision Rules

- The 2nd Mechanized Brigade contracts from nonessential terrain before isolation and retains Aradesh and the westward route when it cannot retain all forward positions.
- The 3rd Mechanized Brigade moves east only on division order that specifies its tactical task and purpose, and only after confirming continued retention of Belas and denial of the central crossing.
- Neither mechanized brigade abandons its essential terrain responsibility until a receiving formation confirms handover.
- If the penetration exceeds division capability, preserve passage, fix or contain the threat, and request the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade under B-00 criteria.
- If the corps reserve is committed, comply with the receiving-division responsibilities in B-00 paragraph 3.f.(5).

#### (7) Risk Controls and Constraints

- Do not commit the 1st Armored Brigade solely to recover nonessential forward terrain.
- Do not support Aradesh by abandoning retention of Belas or denial of the crossing without division approval and an authenticated change of task.
- Counterattack limits of advance will preserve artillery support, the route west, and ability to respond to a renewed threat.
- Avoid simultaneous movement of the division and corps reserves on the same route without corps coordination.
- Protect civilians and critical infrastructure consistent with the mission.

#### (8) Required Reports

- Contact and identified-enemy report after each new engagement.
- Strength and mission-capability report at 0600 and 1800 local each day and upon crossing an FFIR threshold.
- Aradesh, Belas, route, and crossing status whenever control or access changes.
- Division-reserve strength, readiness, primary and alternate route, estimated travel time, passage status, and support shortfall report every 12 hours until commitment.
- Counterattack movement start, passage complete, contact, strength after each engagement, limit of advance, termination, consolidation, and ability-to-continue or reconstitute reports.
- Brigade route, seam, handover, and passage readiness whenever status changes.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Division support flows from west of the Arel through Belas and along the main route to Aradesh. The division will preserve at least one support route and avoid locating all support functions on the same exposed axis. Support shifts with the main effort and must enable movement of the armored reserve.

### b. Priorities of Support

1. 2nd Mechanized Brigade during the initial defense of Aradesh.
2. 1st Armored Brigade upon commitment.
3. 3rd Mechanized Brigade when Belas or the crossing is threatened.
4. 1st Division Artillery Group supporting the decisive operation.
5. Recovery and reconstitution of a mobile reserve.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel priority supports the armored reserve and mechanized formations required to maintain the Aradesh–Belas connection. Ammunition priority supports the defending main effort and artillery enabling counterattack. Reserve movement has priority on the division-designated route after release; subordinate units will clear passage without abandoning essential defensive tasks.

Before 21 May 2030, 0600 local, the division confirms primary and alternate reserve routes and pre-positions critical fuel, ammunition, recovery, and medical support without exposing them to the initial attack. During 21–23 May 2030, route plans account for rain, reduced cross-country mobility, congestion, and possible conflict between division-reserve, corps-reserve, artillery, and sustainment movement.

### d. Maintenance and Recovery

Priority is the 1st Armored Brigade, followed by formations retaining Aradesh and Belas. Recovery actions will preserve formations from avoidable loss below the point of mission failure when displacement is compatible with the higher mission.

### e. Medical and Casualty Evacuation

Casualty evacuation moves west through Belas when the route is open, with alternate routing coordinated through the 2nd Division or corps when required. Formations use organic medical assets and coordinate additional support through division medical channels.

### f. Personnel, Detainees, and Displaced Civilians

Lydian authorities retain primary responsibility for civilian movement and local security. Brigades report displaced-person concentrations that obstruct routes or positions. Detainee handling follows RDC policy and Lydian law.

### g. Integrated Support Capabilities

Supply, medical, engineer, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, airpower, electronic warfare, and recovery support are integrated within parent formations and coordinated through the division staff. Commanders synchronize these capabilities within assigned authorities and request additional support through corps channels.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

#### (1) Command Relationships

- The 1st Multinational Armored Division has operational control of its three maneuver brigades.
- The 1st Division Artillery Group is in general support.
- The 1st Coalition Armored Brigade remains division reserve until released by the division commander.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control unless corps directs a temporary transfer after commitment. If transferred, the division may not divide or retask it outside the corps committing order and returns it under the stated termination conditions.

#### (2) Command Posts and Commander Location

The division main command post remains west of Aradesh along the route toward Belas at an overlay-designated location. The division tactical command element positions where it can observe or control the main effort and counterattack while avoiding the reserve movement route.

#### (3) Succession of Command

1. Commander, 1st Multinational Armored Division.
2. Division deputy commander.
3. Commander, 1st Coalition Armored Brigade.
4. Commander, 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade.
5. Commander, 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade.

#### (4) Liaison

The division maintains liaison with corps and the 2nd Division. The 1st Armored Brigade maintains current contact with the 2nd and 3rd Mechanized Brigades for passage planning. If the corps reserve is oriented toward the division, it will exchange liaison with the division tactical command element.

### b. Signal

- Primary communications are the division coalition network; alternate communications use brigade national networks through liaison teams; contingency communications use relay, prearranged reports, and messenger procedures.
- Subordinate commanders continue within the division commander's intent when communications are degraded. The 1st Armored Brigade will not initiate a planned division counterattack without authenticated release, but may move to avoid destruction or block an immediate threat to its own position.
- Crossing, route, reserve, and FFIR reports have precedence.
- Friendly recognition and passage procedures follow coalition standing operating procedures; adjacent formations confirm identity before fires in seam or passage areas.

# Blue Master Synchronization Crosswalk

> **Side-specific product:** For the Blue player, Blue staff, and scenario controller. Do not provide it to the Red player during closed-plan play.

## 1. Purpose and Authority

This crosswalk provides one side-wide view of the RDC Coalition Land Corps plan for **OPERATION LYDIAN BULWARK**. It links the corps tasks in B-00 to the division and fire-support orders, operational phases, retained terrain, passages, main-effort shifts, reserve decisions, fires, handoffs, and controller checks. It summarizes rather than replaces the signed orders.

Use the following precedence when sources differ:

1. A later authenticated controller ruling or fragmentary order.
2. The applicable signed operations or support order.
3. This master crosswalk.
4. The operational graphics.

Report a conflict rather than silently choosing the most favorable interpretation. The operations overlay governs the exact locations of boundaries, battle positions, assembly areas, passage points, routes, crossings, objectives, artillery areas, and limits of advance.

The **Counterattack North**, **Counterattack Central**, and **Counterattack South** arrows on the published graphic are alternative corps branches. They do not direct three simultaneous counterattacks. Phase III begins only after the corps selects the decisive branch and authenticates the supported formation, committed force, passage, fires, limit of advance, and termination criteria.

## 6. Passage, Handoff, and Retention Controls

| Event | Releasing / supporting formation | Receiving / moving formation | Minimum confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Division-reserve passage in the central sector | 2nd and 3rd Mechanized Brigades / 1st Division | 1st Coalition Armored Brigade | Authenticated division release; Aradesh and Belas remain covered; route and passage point secure; one counterattack flank secured; fires, sustainment, objective or block, LOA, and termination confirmed. |
| Corps-reserve passage into the central sector | 1st Division | 7th Coalition Armored Brigade | Authenticated corps committing order; penetration fixed; route, release point, passage lane, movement priority, flank security, fires, sustainment, and command relationship confirmed. |
| Corps-reserve passage into a northern or southern sector | 2nd Division | 7th Coalition Armored Brigade | Authenticated corps committing order; Kirov and essential crossing responsibilities retained or explicitly accepted; route and passage secure; artillery and 4th Brigade movement deconflicted; one counterattack flank secured. |
| 4th Mechanized Brigade lateral response | 2nd Division / brigade retaining or handing over Kirov | Threatened northern or southern sector | Authenticated division order; Kirov retention, handover, or accepted risk specified; least force and shortest viable route selected; tactical task, purpose, fires, termination, and return or follow-on task confirmed. |
| Essential terrain, route, seam, or crossing handover | Current responsible formation | Designated receiving formation | Positive contact and acknowledgment; control and usability status; enemy situation; route and passage condition; fires and sustainment responsibilities; effective transfer time. |
| Counterattack termination and reserve reconstitution | Supported division and committed reserve | Parent headquarters / follow-on defense | Penetration defeated or contained; required terrain effect established; LOA and support limits observed; follow-on position and handover confirmed; strength, mobility, route access, and renewed-threat assessment reported. |

No formation abandons Aradesh, Kirov, Belas, Velin, a designated crossing, a route block, or a seam responsibility until the receiving headquarters acknowledges the handover or the proper commander explicitly accepts the risk. Reorientation within a reserve area is not commitment.

## 9. Controller Synchronization Checks

- Confirm that all four phases are adjudicated by conditions and authenticated decisions; planning dates alone do not trigger transition.
- Treat the three dashed Phase III axes as alternatives. Record the selected branch, main effort, supported division, committed force, route, passage point, fires priority, LOA, and termination criteria.
- Keep the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade under corps control and uncommitted until an authenticated corps order is issued.
- Keep the 1st Coalition Armored Brigade in division reserve until authenticated release by the 1st Division commander.
- Do not treat the 4th Coalition Mechanized Brigade as the 2nd Division reserve. Record any lateral mission and the accompanying Kirov handover or accepted risk.
- Record every main-effort and artillery-priority shift, including the threat or opportunity that justified it.
- Record passage readiness and acceptance for division- or corps-reserve movement; deconflict artillery displacement, maneuver routes, and sustainment traffic.
- Require positive handover before ending responsibility for Aradesh, Kirov, Belas, Velin, an assigned crossing, a route block, or a division seam.
- Enforce LOA Steel and the prohibition on a general attack into Vostia.
- Track twice-daily strength, mission capability, route and crossing status, reserve readiness, artillery supportability, and phase-transition recommendations.
- At mission completion, record retained cities and crossings, remaining Blue combat power, reserve status, major handovers, and any unresolved task or order conflict for balance analysis.

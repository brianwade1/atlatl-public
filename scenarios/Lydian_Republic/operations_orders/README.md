# Lydian Republic Operations Orders

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All military organizations, operations, control measures, and geographic locations in these products are invented for wargaming and educational use.

This directory contains the side-specific player briefings for **Defense of the Lydian Republic**. This README is the authoritative index for the order hierarchy, support orders, operational graphics, and disclosure guidance. Each base order includes its synchronization matrix as Annex C.

## Disclosure for Opposed Play

Blue and Red orders and operational graphics are normally private to their respective players. The shared scenario specification, road to war, order of battle, map products, game configuration, and open unit information remain available to both sides. Groups may instead agree to play with fully open plans.

## Using the Orders

Read each side's products from the senior order down through the applicable subordinate and support orders. The operational graphics provide the overall scheme and phase overlays; the written orders provide task organization, mission, intent, tasks, constraints, decision points, and synchronization.

The orders guide player decisions but do not add automated Atlatl mechanics. Players observe command relationships, reserve-release authority, main-effort and fires-priority shifts, operational phases, weather effects, control measures, and reporting requirements. An optional controller may assist under procedures agreed before play.

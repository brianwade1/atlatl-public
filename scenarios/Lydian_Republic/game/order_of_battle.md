# Defense of the Lydian Republic

## Order of Battle

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All countries, military organizations, unit designations, and geographic locations in this document are invented for research and educational use.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document defines the brigade-level order of battle and command relationships for the **Defense of the Lydian Republic** scenario. It expands the original divisional concept into a corps-level coalition defense against a larger Vostian field army.

Play begins on **18 May 2030 at 0600 local time (H+48)**, forty-eight hours after the Vostian invasion. The playable force consists of:

- **Blue:** the Regional Defense Coalition (RDC) Coalition Land Corps, with two divisions and one corps-reserve brigade.
- **Red:** the Vostian 3rd Field Army, with two maneuver corps and four divisions.

The OOB contains **nine Blue counters** and **twelve Red counters**. Headquarters are represented in the command hierarchy but are not playable Atlatl counters. Lydian military and security forces remain active in the scenario narrative but are not represented by maneuver counters.

## 2. Atlatl Representation

Each playable counter represents a brigade-sized maneuver formation or an artillery group. Atlatl provides four unit types:

| Atlatl type | Scenario representation | Principal characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `armor` | Armored brigade | Mobile striking force for counterattack, penetration, and exploitation in open terrain. |
| `mechinf` | Medium or heavy mechanized brigade | General-purpose formation for mobile defense, urban operations, flank security, and reinforcement. |
| `infantry` | Light infantry brigade | Formation suited to rough terrain, urban defense, bridge security, and economy-of-force missions. |
| `artillery` | Division or corps artillery group | General-support fires with greater range than the maneuver formations. |

The following capabilities are incorporated into parent formations rather than represented as separate counters:

- Headquarters and command posts
- Reconnaissance and surveillance
- Engineers and mobility support
- Air defense
- Electronic warfare and cyber support
- Logistics and medical support
- Coalition airpower and attack aviation

Atlatl does not enforce the command relationships in this document. Unit names preserve organizational affiliation, while the hierarchy and command notes below provide the authoritative task organization.

Red possesses approximately 1.33 times as many counters as Blue. This represents Vostia's temporary advantage in mass and readiness while preserving enough coalition combat power for a viable defense and counterattack.

## 4. Blue Force: RDC Coalition Land Corps

The **RDC Coalition Land Corps** is a multinational headquarters exercising operational control over coalition formations deployed in Lydia. The corps must defend several widely separated objectives while preserving sufficient combat power to prevent a Vostian breakthrough across the Arel River.

The corps headquarters directly controls two divisions and the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade. The latter remains the corps commander's principal uncommitted reserve at the start of the scenario.

### 4.1 Blue Command Relationship

```text
RDC Coalition Land Corps
|-- 1st Multinational Armored Division
|   |-- 1st Coalition Armored Brigade
|   |-- 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   `-- 1st Division Artillery Group
|-- 2nd Multinational Infantry Division
|   |-- 4th Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 5th Coalition Light Infantry Brigade
|   |-- 6th Coalition Light Infantry Brigade
|   `-- 2nd Division Artillery Group
`-- 7th Coalition Armored Brigade — Corps Reserve
```

### 4.2 1st Multinational Armored Division

The 1st Multinational Armored Division is the corps' principal mobile formation. It is oriented on the Aradesh Plain and the central approaches to Belas. The division must prevent Vostian armored forces from converting a local penetration into an operational breakthrough.

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st Coalition Armored Brigade | `armor` | Conduct counterattacks and defeat Vostian armored penetrations. |
| 2nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Defend Aradesh and the principal eastern approaches. |
| 3rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Cover Belas, maintain lateral mobility, and reinforce adjacent sectors. |
| 1st Division Artillery Group | `artillery` | Provide general support to the division's central-sector operations. |

The division commander normally retains the 1st Coalition Armored Brigade as a local reserve until the Vostian main effort becomes apparent. The mechanized brigades may defend forward, delay toward the Arel River, or establish blocking positions around Aradesh and Belas.

### 4.3 2nd Multinational Infantry Division

The 2nd Multinational Infantry Division is responsible for the more restrictive northern and southern portions of the area of operations. Its formations defend the Drovna Heights, Kirov, the Northwest Bridge, the Maren approaches, and selected Arel River crossings.

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4th Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Defend Kirov and connect the northern and central sectors. |
| 5th Coalition Light Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | Defend the Drovna Heights, northern routes, and Northwest Bridge. |
| 6th Coalition Light Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | Defend the southern Arel crossings and block the Maren approaches. |
| 2nd Division Artillery Group | `artillery` | Support the northern and southern defensive sectors. |

The division must balance two separated terrain problems. The 4th Coalition Mechanized Brigade provides the division's principal means of shifting combat power between Kirov and the other sectors.

### 4.4 Corps Reserve

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7th Coalition Armored Brigade | `armor` | Serve as the corps-level reserve and conduct the decisive counterattack. |

The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade begins under direct corps control. It may reinforce either division, block a Vostian breakthrough, or attack an exposed Vostian flank. Premature commitment could leave the corps without the means to respond to a second penetration.

## 5. Red Force: Vostian 3rd Field Army

The **Vostian 3rd Field Army** commands the invasion force operating in eastern Lydia. The **12th Maneuver Corps is the field-army main effort**, and the **7th Maneuver Corps is the supporting effort**. Each corps contains one armored division, one mechanized division, and a corps artillery group.

The field army seeks to capture Aradesh, Kirov, Belas, and Velin; secure the Northwest Bridge and Arel crossing network; and establish a defensible guard along PL Jab before additional coalition formations can enter the theater.

### 5.1 Red Command Relationship

```text
Vostian 3rd Field Army
|-- 7th Maneuver Corps — Field-Army Supporting Effort
|   |-- 11th Armored Division — Corps Main Effort
|   |   |-- 11th Armored Brigade
|   |   |-- 12th Armored Brigade — 7th Corps Reserve, Phases I–III; Field-Army-Directed Reserve, Phase IV
|   |   `-- 13th Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 14th Mechanized Division — Corps Supporting Effort
|   |   |-- 21st Mechanized Brigade — Division Main Effort
|   |   `-- 31st Infantry Brigade — Supporting and Retain Force
|   `-- 7th Corps Artillery Group — General Support; Priority to 11th Division
`-- 12th Maneuver Corps — Field-Army Main Effort
    |-- 18th Armored Division
    |   |-- 14th Armored Brigade
    |   |-- 15th Armored Brigade
    |   `-- 23rd Mechanized Brigade
    |-- 22nd Mechanized Division
    |   |-- 24th Mechanized Brigade — 12th Corps Reserve, Phases I–III; Phase IV Guard Force
    |   `-- 32nd Infantry Brigade — Division Maneuver and Retain Force
    `-- 12th Corps Artillery Group — General Support
```

### 5.2 7th Maneuver Corps

The 7th Maneuver Corps is the field-army supporting effort in the northern portion of the operational area. The 11th Armored Division is the corps main effort and attacks to seize Kirov before advancing to PL Jab. The 14th Mechanized Division is the supporting effort through the Drovna Heights and attacks to seize the Northwest Bridge. The 12th Armored Brigade remains organic to the 11th Division while serving as the 7th Corps reserve during Phases I–III and transitions to field-army-directed reserve duty in Phase IV.

#### 11th Armored Division

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11th Armored Brigade | `armor` | Lead the division's movement to contact and subsequent attack to seize Kirov. |
| 12th Armored Brigade | `armor` | Serve as the 7th Corps reserve during Phases I–III and the field-army-directed reserve in Phase IV. |
| 13th Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Clear the supporting sector, secure the corps seam and routes, and later retain Kirov. |

#### 14th Mechanized Division

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21st Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Lead the division movement to contact, seize the Northwest Bridge, advance to PL Jab, and conduct the Phase IV guard. |
| 31st Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | Clear restrictive terrain, secure the division seam and routes, and retain the Northwest Bridge. |

#### 7th Corps Fires

### 5.3 12th Maneuver Corps

The 12th Maneuver Corps is the field-army main effort and is oriented on the central and southern portions of the operational area. It attacks sequentially to seize Aradesh, Belas, and Velin while protecting the southern Aradesh Plain and retaining earlier gains.

#### 18th Armored Division

| Formation | Atlatl type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14th Armored Brigade | `armor` | Seize Aradesh, clear through PL Hook, and then guard the southern Aradesh Plain and assigned PL Jab sector. |
| 15th Armored Brigade | `armor` | Clear south of Aradesh, seize Belas in Phase II, and retain the city during Phases III–IV. |
| 23rd Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | Isolate Aradesh during its seizure and retain the city and its connecting route during later phases. |

## 6. Command and Support Relationships

### Blue

- The RDC Coalition Land Corps exercises operational control over both divisions and the corps-reserve brigade.
- The 1st and 2nd Division Artillery Groups remain in general support of their parent divisions.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control until formally committed.
- Cross-attachment of brigades may occur in the narrative or mission orders, but the base OOB preserves the organizational relationships shown above.
- National restrictions and multinational coordination friction are represented through mission orders and player decisions rather than separate game mechanics.

### Red

- The Vostian 3rd Field Army exercises operational control over the 7th and 12th Maneuver Corps.
- The 12th Maneuver Corps is the field-army main effort; the 7th Maneuver Corps is the supporting effort unless changed by a subsequent field-army order.
- Each corps controls one armored division, one mechanized division, and one artillery group.
- Corps artillery remains in general support and may shift fires between the corps' subordinate divisions.
- The 12th Armored Brigade remains organic to the 11th Armored Division while serving as the 7th Corps reserve during Phases I–III. In Phase IV it becomes the field-army-directed reserve; commitment authority shifts accordingly without permanently detaching the brigade.
- The 21st Mechanized Brigade is the 14th Mechanized Division's maneuver main effort. The 31st Infantry Brigade remains the supporting and retain force for the Northwest Bridge.
- The 7th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support with priority of fires to the 11th Armored Division. Support to the 14th Division or committed corps reserve requires corps direction except for emergency fires under established procedures.
- The 24th Mechanized Brigade remains organic to the 22nd Mechanized Division while serving as the 12th Corps reserve during Phases I–III. The corps releases it to the division for the Phase IV southern guard.
- The 32nd Infantry Brigade conducts the 22nd Mechanized Division's active maneuver tasks during Phases I–III and retains Velin in Phase IV.
- The 14th Armored Brigade's Phase III guard mission is a division task within the 12th Maneuver Corps main effort; it is distinct from the 31st Infantry Brigade's continuing guard mission on the 7th Maneuver Corps southern flank.
- The field army has no separate reserve counter; it uses the 12th Armored Brigade as its directed reserve in Phase IV.

## 7. Initial Strength and Readiness

Every unit begins with:

```text
currentStrength: 100
fullStrength: 100
```

This provides a transparent baseline for initial playtesting. Later scenario variants may reduce selected formations to reflect losses during the first forty-eight hours of the invasion, incomplete coalition deployment, or differing readiness levels.

Because Atlatl removes a unit when it falls below 50 percent strength, initial-strength adjustments have a substantial effect on unit survivability and should be introduced only after the baseline OOB has been tested.

## 8. Relationship to the Atlatl Files

The companion `oob.json` contains the 21 playable units listed in this document. It deliberately excludes headquarters and non-counter enabling formations.

- Each unit uses a unique `longName` that includes its parent division or corps.
- `homeOrgId` and `taskOrgId` remain `null` because Atlatl does not enforce this command hierarchy.
- Unit locations in `oob.json` remain `null` because it is intended for the Atlatl unit-placement tool.
- The completed `defense_of_the_lydian_republic.scn` assigns default starting locations while preserving player setup freedom inside each side's setup zone.
- All units use the native Atlatl types `armor`, `mechinf`, `infantry`, and `artillery`.

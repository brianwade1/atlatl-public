# Ordan Basin Operations Orders

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All military organizations, operations, control measures, and geographic locations in these products are invented for wargaming and educational use.

This directory contains the side-specific player briefings for **The Race for the Ordan Basin**. This README is the authoritative index for the order hierarchy, master synchronization crosswalks, support orders, operational graphics, and disclosure guidance.

## Disclosure for Opposed Play

Blue and Red orders, crosswalks, and operational graphics are normally private to their respective players. The shared scenario specification, road to war, order of battle, map products, game configuration, and open unit information remain available to both sides. Groups may instead agree to play with fully open plans.

All written orders use the common baseline: **12 September 2031**, **18 complete turns / 9 days**, neutral initial ownership for all seven cities, and no playable unit initially occupying a city.

## Using the Orders

Read each side's products from the senior order through the applicable subordinate and support orders, then use the side's master crosswalk to reconcile responsibilities at headquarters seams. The operational graphics show the scheme and phase overlays; the written orders provide task organization, mission, intent, tasks, constraints, decision points, and detailed synchronization matrices.

The orders do not add automated Atlatl mechanics. Players observe command relationships, reserve-release authority, main-effort and fires-priority shifts, operational phases, weather effects, control measures, and reporting requirements. An optional controller may assist under procedures agreed before play. The crosswalks summarize those relationships but do not supersede the signed orders.

- **Issuing headquarters:** 12th Armored Division
- **Place of issue:** Division Main Command Post, Selin eastern approach
- **Date-time group:** 12 September 2030, 0600 local
- **Time zone used throughout:** Local Ordan time

- **Scenario duration:** 20 turns / 10 days; 12 hours per turn
- **Parent order:** 9th Maneuver Corps OPORD R-20, OPERATION NORTHERN REACH
- **Role:** Corps supporting effort in Phases I and III–IV; corps main effort in Phase II
- **References:** R-00; R-20; `ordan_basin_scenario.md`; `order_of_battle.md`; Ordan Basin Area of Operations map; approved Red operational graphics

The division exercises operational control over both brigades. The 9th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. This division receives priority of corps fires during Phase II and sufficient support for its Phase III–IV guard and Eren retain tasks. The division has no dedicated maneuver reserve. Organic reconnaissance, engineers, air defense, sustainment, medical, and command capabilities are incorporated into parent counters or represented through scenario-control adjudication.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from Selin and the Karsovian eastern support route west through the southeastern marshlands, Eren, PL Push, PL Punch, the Mora approaches, and the southern portion of LOA Block. It includes the boundary with 21st Mechanized Division, the Veyra–Eren route, and Blue routes capable of reinforcing Eren or attacking the corps southern flank.

The division monitors Blue forces defending Eren, forces concealed in or moving through the Mora Hills, the 6th Compact Mechanized Division, the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, Blue artillery able to interdict the Eren crossing, and any force capable of turning north against the Veyra attack or east against the Selin line of communication.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Selin:** Southern-eastern junction and initial support location. Its security opens the route to Eren and protects the division rear.
- **Eren:** Principal southern Arven crossing. Its seizure gives 9th Corps an alternate westward route and protects the Veyra operation from the south.
- **Southeastern marshlands:** Restrict heavy cross-country maneuver and channel the division onto the Selin–Eren road. They increase the importance of route security and recovery.
- **Mora Hills:** Restrictive terrain along the southern flank can conceal Blue infantry, observers, or counterattack forces. A force remaining there can threaten Eren or the division guard’s displacement route.
- **PL Push and PL Punch:** Control the westward advance and guard posture. Reaching a line does not complete the mission unless contact, route security, and guard conditions are met.
- **LOA Block:** Forward limit of the Phase IV guard. Movement beyond it requires Eastern Army authorization.
- **Road network:** The Selin–Eren route carries maneuver, artillery support, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and recovery. The division must preserve an alternate route or contingency plan whenever possible.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may conceal Blue forces in the Mora Hills or on the western Eren approaches.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on the Selin–Eren road and may slow armored movement and recovery near the marshlands.
- Limited visibility requires positive identification during the bridge attack, passage, and guard handover.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The division expects elements of the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division and 6th Compact Mechanized Division to defend Eren and the southern approaches. Blue can reinforce with the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade or shift artillery from Novar and Veyra.

Blue can delay along the marsh-constrained road, defend Eren from prepared positions, mass artillery on the crossing approaches, and counterattack an isolated bridgehead from the Veyra route or Mora Hills. Blue’s most dangerous action is to allow the 121st Brigade to cross, then counterattack its northern flank while artillery disrupts the eastern approach and prevents the 122nd from following.

Blue’s most likely action is to retain Eren with an Ordan force, reinforce as the attack develops, and withdraw toward PL Punch before encirclement while preserving a counterattack option against the division flank.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Novar and Eren, attacks to seize Veyra, then retains the objectives and guards central and southern LOA Block.
- **21st Mechanized Division:** Corps main effort in Phases I and III–IV. Seizes Novar; in Phase II isolates Veyra and employs the 213th Brigade to guard the corps boundary and delay forces reinforcing Eren; restores Novar and seizes Veyra in Phase III; and guards the central portion of LOA Block.
- **9th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to the corps, with priority to this division during the Phase II Eren attack.
- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes the northern crossings and protects the Veyra attack’s northern flank.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve after securing Ruda. It remains under Army control.

### e. Civil Considerations

Selin and Eren contain civilians, transportation infrastructure, utilities, and medical facilities. Civilian movement may congest the sole high-capacity route. Preserve Eren crossing and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment. Transfer Selin security only after council-aligned authorities can protect the route.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Corps fires support does not change the division task organization.

### g. Assumptions

- Eren crossing begins trafficable.
- During Phase II, the 213th Brigade guards from PL Push to the Arven River along the corps boundary and finds and delays forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren; Novar is not continuously defended.
- The 122nd Brigade can transfer Selin security and reposition to support the Eren attack.
- Corps provides sufficient artillery windows for Phase II.
- No reinforcement is guaranteed; the division accomplishes its essential tasks with two brigades.
- Corps and Eastern Army authorize phase transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **12th Armored Division attacks to secure Selin, fixes and then seizes Eren, advances from PL Push to PL Punch, and subsequently retains Eren while guarding along the southern portion of LOA Block in order to open 9th Corps’ southern Arven crossing, protect the attack on Veyra, and deny Blue access to the corps southern flank and line of communication.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Create a secure southern crossing and flank that allow 9th Corps to concentrate on Veyra without exposing its rear or depending solely on Novar.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize Selin and protect the eastern support route.
- Fix Blue at Eren while 21st Division seizes Novar.
- Reposition the 122nd Brigade from Selin to follow and support the Eren attack.
- Seize Eren with both approaches secured and trafficability confirmed.
- Prevent isolation of the 121st Brigade west of the river.
- Advance from PL Push to PL Punch and establish the southern guard.
- Retain Eren and maintain the Selin–Eren line of communication.
- Detect and delay Blue counterattacks from the Mora or western approaches.
- Preserve freedom to disengage and request corps assistance early.

#### End State

- Selin and the eastern division route are secure.
- Eren is controlled, trafficable, and retained.
- The 121st Brigade guards the southern LOA Block sector with viable displacement routes.
- The 122nd Brigade protects Eren and can support guard disengagement.
- The division southern flank and boundary with 21st Division remain secure.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Secure Selin and Fix Eren

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The 122nd Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort and attacks to seize Selin to secure the division support base and route. After local security is established or transferred, the 122nd repositions to follow and support the 121st Armored Brigade. The 121st fixes Blue at Eren to prevent reinforcement of Novar and to develop the crossing defense.

The phase ends on corps order when Selin is secure, the route is open, Eren’s defense is fixed and developed, and both brigades are postured for Phase II.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize Eren

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The division becomes the corps main effort. The 121st Brigade becomes the division main effort and attacks to seize Eren and the western bridge approach. The 122nd follows and supports, defeats or contains bypassed threats, secures the crossing approaches and Selin–Eren route, and prepares to assume the Eren retain mission. Along the northern boundary, the 213th Brigade guard finds and delays forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren. The division synchronizes contact reporting and fires with that guard while coordinating movement, recovery, and traffic control to prevent isolation of the 121st.

The phase ends on corps order when both approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, movement control is established, the Blue counterattack threat is defeated or contained, and the division is postured for PL Punch.

#### (3) Phase III — Advance to PL Punch and Guard the Veyra Attack

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The division supports the 21st Division attack on Veyra. The 121st Brigade conducts movement to contact from PL Push to PL Punch and establishes a guard oriented west and south to prevent Blue interference with the Veyra operation. The 122nd Brigade retains Eren, secures the Selin–Eren route, and prepares to support guard disengagement. The 121st does not attack Veyra unless reassigned by corps.

The phase ends on corps order when Veyra is seized and organized resistance defeated, the 121st is established at PL Punch, Eren remains secure, and the division can assume Phase IV guard and retain tasks.

#### (4) Phase IV — Guard LOA Block and Retain Eren

**Projected Turns 16–20.** The 121st Brigade remains the division main effort and guards along the southern portion of LOA Block. It maintains contact, identifies the Blue main body, delays as required, and displaces before becoming decisively engaged. The 122nd Brigade retains Eren and prepares to reinforce the guard, block a penetration, or cover the 121st’s disengagement on division order.

The division end state is achieved when the guard has provided required warning and delay, Eren remains controlled, and both brigades preserve freedom for continued defense.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The division uses the 122nd Brigade to secure Selin while the 121st fixes Eren. Once Selin security is transferred, the 122nd closes toward Eren so the division can mass for the crossing attack. The 121st leads; the 122nd follows and supports and secures the route and approaches.

After Eren is seized, the 121st advances toward PL Punch while the 122nd assumes the retain mission. This separation is deliberate: the guard develops and delays the Blue counterattack while the retain force preserves the crossing and supports withdrawal. The 122nd is not a formal reserve, but it provides the division’s only local response after assuming Eren.

The division has no dedicated reserve during Phases I–III. Report before both brigades become decisively engaged, the Eren route becomes vulnerable, or the 21st Division boundary is uncovered. A request for the 5th Guards Armored Brigade goes through 9th Corps and Eastern Army.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The division receives support from the 9th Corps Artillery Group according to corps priorities.

- **Phase I:** Support the 122nd Brigade at Selin as required; fix and develop Eren; conduct counterfire and interdict reinforcement toward Novar.
- **Phase II:** Priority to the 121st Brigade. Suppress and obscure Eren defenses, isolate the western approach, attack forces detected by the 213th guard moving to reinforce Eren, interdict Blue reserve routes, and conduct counterfire.
- **Phase III:** Support the 121st movement to contact and guard while preserving fires for Eren; corps priority remains the 21st Division attack on Veyra.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the 121st when in contact or disengaging, then the 122nd defending Eren.

The division nominates targets and coordinates observation but does not redirect corps artillery outside authenticated priorities. Cross-boundary fires require corps clearance.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 121st Armored Brigade

- **Phase I — Task:** Fix Blue forces defending Eren and develop the crossing defense. **Purpose:** Prevent reinforcement of Novar and set conditions for the Eren attack.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize Eren and its western approach. **Purpose:** Open the southern Arven crossing and alternate corps route.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact from PL Push to PL Punch and establish a southern guard. **Purpose:** Protect the Veyra attack from counterattack through the southern approaches.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Guard along the southern portion of LOA Block, maintain contact, delay, and displace within division criteria. **Purpose:** Provide warning and time while protecting Eren and the corps southern flank.

#### (2) 122nd Mechanized Brigade

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to seize Selin, establish or transfer local security, then follow and support the 121st Brigade. **Purpose:** Secure the division support base and mass the division for Eren.
- **Phase II — Task:** Follow and support the Eren attack; secure both approaches and the Selin–Eren route. **Purpose:** Prevent isolation of the main effort and preserve crossing trafficability.
- **Phase III — Task:** Retain Eren and secure the division line of communication. **Purpose:** Sustain the southern guard and preserve an alternate route for 9th Corps.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain Eren and prepare to reinforce, block, or cover the 121st Brigade’s disengagement. **Purpose:** Preserve the crossing and provide the division’s local response capability.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Main Effort

- Phase I: 122nd Brigade.
- Phases II–IV: 121st Brigade.

Temporary fires or sustainment priority may shift to the 122nd to prevent loss of Eren or the division route, but this does not change the phase main effort without an authenticated order.

#### (2) Actions on Contact

Report, secure, and develop contact before committing the second brigade. Do not bypass a force capable of interdicting Eren, the Selin route, the Mora flank, corps artillery, or the 21st Division boundary without containment or confirmed handover.

During Phase II, exchange contact, target, and passage information with the 213th Brigade guard along the northern boundary. The 213th delays forces approaching Eren but does not assume responsibility for the Eren objective or the 121st Brigade’s northern flank inside the 12th Division area.

#### (3) Crossing Control

Eren is not seized until both approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, and movement control is established. The 121st controls the objective until the division confirms handover to the 122nd. Report damage, obstruction, observation, or capacity reduction immediately.

#### (4) Guard and Disengagement

The 121st establishes observation, engagement criteria, primary and alternate displacement routes, and a battle handover with the 122nd at Eren. Disengage when directed, when required delay is achieved, or when continued contact would prevent orderly withdrawal under preapproved division criteria.

#### (5) Reserve and Risk

The division has no dedicated reserve. The 122nd provides limited local response only after assuming Eren and only if the crossing remains retained. Request corps assistance before both brigades lose freedom of maneuver. The division cannot commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.

#### (6) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Reposition 122nd:** Selin secure; local security transferred; route to Eren open.
- **DP 2 — Recommend Phase II:** Eren defense developed; brigades, fires, recovery, and routes ready.
- **DP 3 — Complete Eren seizure:** Both approaches secured; trafficability and movement control confirmed; counterattack contained.
- **DP 4 — Recommend Phase III:** Eren retain handover ready; 121st mission capable; PL Push route and fires coordinated.
- **DP 5 — Establish Phase IV guard:** Veyra seized; 121st at PL Punch; 122nd retaining Eren; routes and handover ready.
- **DP 6 — Disengage guard:** Required delay achieved or continued contact threatens withdrawal.

#### (7) CCIR

**PIR:** Eren defenses and obstacles; Blue artillery; forces in Mora Hills; 6th Division and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade; reinforcement routes from Veyra; crossing condition; threats to the 21st Division boundary.

**FFIR:** Either brigade at or below 70 strength; Selin–Eren route blocked; crossing unusable; brigades unable to maintain mutual support; artillery unavailable for Phase II; guard unable to disengage; division boundary uncovered; or corps assistance required.

#### (8) Reports and Constraints

Report contact, strength, Selin/Eren status, crossing and route condition, boundary, guard, fires, sustainment, and phase readiness once per turn and on significant change. Do not destroy Eren crossing, cross LOA Block, attack Veyra, or begin a new phase without authenticated authority. Protect civilians and critical infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows through Selin along the Eren route. Before the crossing attack, distribute fuel, ammunition, recovery, and medical support so loss of one node does not halt both brigades. After seizure, establish movement control across Eren without blocking the 121st advance or the 122nd retain position.

### b. Priorities of Support

- Phase I: 122nd Brigade at Selin; 121st Eren fix; crossing-attack preparation.
- Phase II: 121st Brigade; 122nd approach security; recovery and traffic control.
- Phase III: 121st guard movement; 122nd Eren and route security.
- Phase IV: 121st guard in contact/disengaging; 122nd crossing defense; reconstitution.

### c. Supply, Maintenance, and Transportation

Fuel, direct-fire ammunition, and recovery are pacing. Remove disabled equipment from the marsh-constrained road and bridge approaches. Separate maneuver, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and corps-artillery traffic. Begin wet-weather movement early enough to preserve support for the next turn when practicable.

### d. Medical and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through Eren to Selin. Establish an alternate collection and transfer plan before Phase II. Reconstitution priority follows the current main effort, then the brigade essential to Eren retention. A brigade unable to perform its essential task reports for relief or mission adjustment.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The division command post initially operates east of Selin and displaces toward Eren after route security permits. The tactical command post follows the current main effort. Command-post movement does not compete with the Eren assault, guard route, or corps artillery.

Succession of command is division commander, deputy division commander, 121st Brigade commander, then 122nd Brigade commander. Maintain liaison with 9th Corps, 21st Division, the 9th Corps Artillery Group, and units controlling adjacent guard sectors.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use division and corps networks. Alternate means use brigade networks and liaison; contingency means use relay, schedules, and messenger. Crossing status, brigade separation, artillery windows, phase transition, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed task, retain Eren, and preserve brigade mutual support. Do not begin a new phase, attack Veyra, or move beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless necessary to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The group area of interest extends from the Karsovian eastern support area through Kasar and Dalen to North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, PL Punch, Veyra, and the northern and central portions of LOA Block. It includes Blue artillery positions capable of supporting Dalen, the crossings, Novar, or Veyra; Blue reserve routes; and all movement corridors affecting the group’s range, survivability, ammunition supply, or displacement.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Kasar–Dalen routes:** Primary initial routes for ammunition and displacement. Congestion can prevent timely support to both divisions.
- **North Pass and Saren Heights:** Restrictive terrain limits position-area choice, masks observation, and exposes predictable roads to counterfire.
- **Central Link Bridge:** Separate from Dalen and Novar. The group must range the crossing attack without assuming that a Dalen position automatically covers all required targets.
- **Arven crossings:** Canalize maneuver and logistics. Artillery must displace without blocking bridge approaches or follow-and-support forces.
- **Western woods:** Reduce observation and complicate target acquisition west of the river.
- **Veyra approaches:** Phase III reinforcing fires require sufficient range, positive clearance, and coordination across the corps boundary.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce acquisition, adjustment, and battle-damage assessment.
- Wet conditions increase road dependence and artillery displacement time, especially near the Saren Heights and crossings.
- Displacement and ammunition movement begin early enough to preserve support across phase transitions.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

Blue fields three artillery groups and mobile armored and mechanized formations capable of counterfire, route interdiction, and attacks against exposed position areas. Blue is expected to use artillery to defend Dalen and the crossings, disrupt Karsovian bridge traffic, and isolate leading brigades west of the Arven.

The most likely enemy action is phased counterfire and interdiction against artillery positions and bridge approaches while maneuver forces defend the current objective. The most dangerous action is a concentrated counterfire and armored raid that forces the group to displace during a crossing attack or breaks the ammunition route when the supported maneuver formation is decisively engaged.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; advances to PL Punch; then guards and retains the northern crossing network.
- **15th Mechanized Division:** Corps main effort. Seizes Dalen and Central Link Bridge and establishes the central guard.
- **8th Guards Armored Division:** Secures Kasar, seizes North Pass Bridge, and establishes the northern guard.
- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Eastern Army main effort in Phase III. Attacks to seize Veyra.
- **9th Corps Artillery Group:** Receives reinforcing fires from this group during Phase III and coordinates the supported effects through the Army and corps fires cells.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve. May receive fires if committed in the 4th Corps area, subject to corps priority.

### e. Civil Considerations

Dalen, Kasar, the crossings, and Veyra contain civilians and protected infrastructure. Target development must account for hospitals, utilities, government sites, transportation facilities, and displaced civilians. Preserve bridges unless Eastern Army authorizes destruction. Effects in Veyra require positive clearance and the restrictive measures established by 9th Corps.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. The Phase III reinforcing mission does not attach or detach the group.

### g. Assumptions

- The group remains a single playable counter and cannot provide full simultaneous effects to widely separated objectives.
- Range, movement, target eligibility, and combat effects follow Atlatl and scenario adjudication.
- 4th Corps authenticates priority changes within its area; Eastern Army coordinates cross-corps priorities.
- The 9th Corps Artillery Group can receive and coordinate reinforcing fires during Phase III.
- Position-area selection remains subordinate to the approved overlay, route status, and current threat.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **4th Corps Artillery Group provides general support to 4th Maneuver Corps; masses fires to enable seizure of Dalen, Central Link Bridge, and North Pass Bridge; changes to a general support–reinforcing mission during Phase III to reinforce 9th Corps Artillery for the attack on Veyra while preserving required coverage of 4th Corps; and then supports the northern guards and crossing defense in order to enable the northern hook, isolate the Veyra fight, and preserve 4th Corps freedom of action.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Concentrate the group’s limited effects at the corps decisive action, then extend those effects to Veyra without creating an uncovered northern bridgehead or an unsupportable displacement.

#### Key Tasks

- Maintain centralized control and a clear tactical mission in every phase.
- Support the 15th Division first during the Dalen and Central Link attacks.
- Support the 8th Division at Kasar and North Pass according to corps priority.
- Suppress or obscure objective defenses and isolate crossing approaches.
- Conduct counterfire against Blue artillery affecting the current decisive action.
- Interdict Blue reserves and counterattack forces on identified routes.
- In Phase III, reinforce 9th Corps Artillery while preserving minimum required coverage for 4th Corps guards and crossings.
- Displace by echelon through position areas without blocking maneuver, bridge, sustainment, or guard routes.
- Preserve ammunition for Veyra reinforcing fires and Phase IV guard disengagement.
- Report any support gap, range limitation, route loss, or incompatible priority immediately.

#### End State

- Each 4th Corps decisive action received timely massed fires.
- 9th Corps received coordinated reinforcing fires for Veyra without transfer of command.
- Dalen, North Pass, Central Link, guard routes, and corps seams were not lost for lack of reported artillery support.
- The group remains mobile, survivable, supplied, and capable of supporting the corps through Turn 18.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Support Kasar and Dalen

**Projected Turns 1–5. Tactical mission: General support.** Priority of fires is the 15th Mechanized Division, specifically the 151st Brigade attack on Dalen; next priority is the 8th Guards Armored Division, first the 82nd Brigade at Kasar as required and then the 81st Brigade fixing North Pass.

The group suppresses and isolates Dalen, interdicts movement from Central Link, conducts counterfire, supports the Kasar seizure, and develops the North Pass defense. It occupies an initial position area east of Kasar with access to primary and alternate displacement routes.

The phase ends when Dalen and Kasar are secure, North Pass is fixed, the corps route is open, and the group can support both Phase II bridge attacks.

#### (2) Phase II — Support Central Link and North Pass

**Projected Turns 6–10. Tactical mission: General support.** Priority of fires remains the 15th Division and 151st Brigade at Central Link Bridge; next priority is the 8th Division and 81st Brigade at North Pass.

The group suppresses and obscures bridge defenders, isolates western approaches, interdicts Blue reserve routes, and conducts counterfire. It displaces by echelon into position areas around Dalen and the secured eastern bridge approaches while preserving continuous coverage and avoiding congestion.

The phase ends when both crossings are seized and trafficable, their approaches and routes are controlled, and the group is positioned to support the PL Punch guards and Phase III reinforcing mission.

#### (3) Phase III — Reinforce the Veyra Attack

**Projected Turns 11–15. Tactical mission: General support–reinforcing.** The group supports 4th Corps as a whole and reinforces the 9th Corps Artillery Group. Priority of reinforcing fires is the 21st Mechanized Division and 212th Brigade attack on Veyra until the city is seized. The group maintains minimum required fires for the 15th and 8th Division guards, retained crossings, and the corps seam.

The group targets Veyra defenders, Blue artillery, reserve routes, and counterattack forces affecting the main effort. The 9th Corps fires cell clears targets in its area; Eastern Army resolves cross-corps competition. The group displaces only when range, survivability, or next-phase support requires movement and reports any gap before displacement.

The phase ends when Veyra is seized, organized resistance is defeated, 4th Corps guards are established at PL Punch, and the group can resume exclusive parent-corps priority.

#### (4) Phase IV — Support Guards and Crossing Defense

**Projected Turns 16–18. Tactical mission: General support.** The group returns to general support without the reinforcing requirement. Priority is the guard formation in contact or disengaging, then the formation defending the most threatened retained crossing, then the 5th Guards Armored Brigade if committed in the corps area.

The group disrupts Blue counterattack forces, supports guard displacement and battle handover, conducts counterfire, and preserves crossings and routes. It occupies survivable position areas able to cover LOA Block and the crossing network while retaining an eastward displacement route.

The phase ends at scenario completion when guards have provided required warning and delay, crossings remain controlled, and the group remains capable of continued support.

### c. Scheme of Fires

Within each phase priority, the target hierarchy is:

1. Blue artillery capable of disrupting the current decisive action or guard.
2. Armored and mechanized reserves capable of counterattacking the main effort.
3. Defenders, obstacles, and positions controlling Dalen or a bridge approach.
4. Reinforcement and withdrawal forces affecting the current objective or Veyra.
5. Forces threatening a corps seam, artillery position, ammunition route, or guard displacement route.

The group commander recommends priority changes when target value, supportability, range, route status, or survivability changes. A division request does not change corps priority unless authenticated or executed under an approved emergency procedure.

### d. Movement and Survivability

- Use descriptive artillery position areas approved through the corps overlay: initial eastern support area, Dalen support area, secured bridgehead support area, and Phase IV crossing-defense area.
- Reconnoiter primary and alternate routes before movement.
- Displace by echelon or under alternate control arrangements so the group never voluntarily loses all coverage.
- Do not block Kasar, Dalen, North Pass, Central Link, guard, casualty-evacuation, or Army-reserve routes.
- Begin wet-weather displacement early enough to preserve next-turn support.
- Displace when required targets move outside range, the current area becomes untenable, the ammunition route is threatened, or support to the next phase cannot be established in place.
- Report displacement start, expected completion, coverage retained, and any support gap.

### e. Tasks to Organic Capabilities

- **Target acquisition:** Locate Blue artillery, crossing defenders, armored reserves, reinforcement routes, and guard threats in order to enable R-1F1 through R-1F5.
- **Fire direction and liaison:** Maintain continuous coordination with 4th Corps, both divisions, Eastern Army fires, and 9th Corps Artillery during Phase III in order to mass fires and prevent conflicting expectations.
- **Sustainment:** Maintain ammunition, fuel, maintenance, recovery, and route plans sufficient for displacement and Phase III reinforcing fires.
- **Local security and survivability:** Protect position areas, reduce signatures, prepare alternates, and preserve an eastward route.

These tasks do not create additional playable counters.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Priority of Fires

- Phase I: 15th Division / 151st Brigade; 8th Division / 82nd then 81st.
- Phase II: 15th Division / 151st Brigade; 8th Division / 81st Brigade.
- Phase III: Reinforcing priority to 21st Division / 212th Brigade; minimum coverage to 15th and 8th Division guards and crossings.
- Phase IV: Guard in contact or disengaging; threatened retain force; committed Army reserve.

#### (2) Fire-Support Triggers

- Shift to a crossing attack when the supported division reports assault posture and the corps confirms priority.
- Engage a Blue reserve when it enters a route or area from which it can disrupt the current decisive action.
- Shift counterfire priority when Blue artillery affects a bridge, main effort, guard disengagement, or ammunition route.
- Begin Phase III reinforcing fires only after Eastern Army and both corps confirm the mission, target clearance, and communications.
- Shift Phase IV priority to a disengaging guard before its route becomes directly threatened.

#### (3) Cross-Boundary Fires

During Phase III, the 9th Corps fires cell clears targets inside its area. The 4th Corps retains command of this group and establishes the amount of capability available to reinforce. Eastern Army resolves conflicts between Veyra support and minimum 4th Corps coverage. No fire is delivered near uncertain friendly positions or protected Veyra sites without positive clearance.

#### (4) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Displace toward Dalen:** Dalen route secure; Phase II targets beyond reliable support from the initial area.
- **DP 2 — Shift between Central Link and North Pass:** Corps changes priority based on assault timing, resistance, and counterattack threat.
- **DP 3 — Assume GSR mission:** Phase II complete; 4th Corps minimum coverage defined; 9th Corps liaison, clearance, range, and communications established.
- **DP 4 — Emergency retain priority:** A guard, crossing, or artillery route faces a threat that cannot wait for normal reallocation.
- **DP 5 — End reinforcing mission:** Veyra seized or Eastern Army terminates the requirement; 4th Corps resumes exclusive priority.
- **DP 6 — Displace for guard support:** Current position cannot range LOA Block and retain a viable route or survivability.

#### (5) CCIR

**PIR:** Blue artillery locations; armored and mechanized reserve locations; crossing defenses and obstacles; Veyra reinforcement routes; forces threatening guard routes or artillery position areas.

**FFIR:** Group at or below 70 strength; unable to range the current priority; ammunition insufficient for the decisive task and next phase; primary and alternate routes blocked; communications lost with supported headquarters; required coverage gap; or position area under imminent attack.

#### (6) Reports and Constraints

Report target acquisition, fires delivered, priority, ammunition, range, displacement, route, survivability, liaison, and supportability once per turn and on significant change. Do not occupy maneuver support-by-fire positions, divide the aggregate counter, fire across a boundary without clearance, block a crossing route, or assume that a phase change automatically modifies the tactical mission. Protect civilians and infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows through Kasar and Dalen along routes separate from maneuver whenever possible. Ammunition and fuel are pre-positioned by phase without creating a single vulnerable stockpile. The group maintains sufficient emergency stocks for counterfire, guard disengagement, and survival during route interruption.

### b. Priorities

- Phase I: Dalen attack ammunition; Kasar and North Pass support; initial displacement preparation.
- Phase II: Central Link attack; North Pass attack; counterfire; bridgehead displacement.
- Phase III: Veyra reinforcing fires; 4th Corps minimum coverage; Phase IV reserve stocks.
- Phase IV: Guard disengagement; threatened crossing; counterfire; reconstitution.

### c. Transportation, Maintenance, and Recovery

Artillery displacement and ammunition movement yield to an active maneuver assault or guard withdrawal when routes conflict. Recovery priority is command/fire direction, mobility, and systems required for the current task. Disabled equipment will not block a crossing or main route. Report before movement or maintenance creates a support gap.

### d. Medical and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through the nearest secure crossing and Kasar route. Reconstitution priority restores command, communications, movement, and fires capability needed for the current main effort, then Phase IV coverage.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The artillery command post initially operates in the Kasar eastern support area and displaces by echelon toward Dalen as the operation develops. The group remains under 4th Corps control. In Phase III, liaison with 9th Corps Artillery coordinates reinforcing effects but does not establish command authority over the group.

Succession of command is group commander, deputy commander, then senior organic fires commander. Liaison remains with 4th Corps, the 8th and 15th Divisions, Eastern Army fires, and 9th Corps Artillery during Phase III.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use 4th Corps fires and command networks. Alternate communications use Eastern Army and 9th Corps fires networks through liaison. Contingency means use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Priority changes, crossing attacks, GSR activation, cross-boundary clearance, displacement, support gaps, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, support the last authenticated priority, preserve the group, and avoid firing near uncertain friendly positions. Do not independently assume or terminate the Phase III reinforcing mission. Report at the first opportunity.

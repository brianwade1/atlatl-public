## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The corps area of interest extends from the Karsovian approaches to Ruda and Selin west through Novar, Eren, PL Push, PL Punch, Veyra, and the central and southern portions of LOA Block. It includes the southeastern marshlands, Mora Hills, the corps boundary with 4th Maneuver Corps, the Veyra–Novar and Veyra–Eren routes, and western approaches through which Blue may reinforce or counterattack.

The corps monitors Blue forces defending Novar, Eren, and Veyra; the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade; the 6th Compact Mechanized Division; Blue artillery able to interdict crossings or the Veyra attack; and any force capable of moving through the corps seam or Mora approaches. Activity north of the corps boundary matters when it can interfere with Novar, the Veyra attack, or the 4th Corps guard.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Ruda:** Central-eastern road junction and staging area. It connects the corps’ northern and southern routes and supports the Army reserve’s initial operation.
- **Selin:** Southern-eastern junction and support location. Its security opens the route to Eren and protects the corps rear.
- **Novar:** The central Arven crossing and most important transportation junction in the corps area. It is the base for the attack on Veyra and the principal route for corps sustainment and Army-reserve movement.
- **Eren:** Southern Arven crossing and gateway to the Mora approaches. Its control secures the corps southern flank and creates an alternate route west.
- **Veyra:** Political center and decisive objective. The surrounding road and wooded terrain permit reinforcement and local counterattack; seizure requires immediate consolidation and route security.
- **Southeastern marshlands:** Restrict heavy maneuver and channel forces onto the Ruda–Novar and Selin–Eren roads. They complicate lateral support between divisions.
- **Mora Hills:** Restrictive terrain protects and threatens the southern flank. A Blue force concealed there may interdict the Eren route or attack the 12th Division flank.
- **PL Push and PL Punch:** Control measures governing the corps’ westward security and attack posture. Reaching a phase line does not end a task unless the required contact, guard, route, and objective conditions are met.
- **LOA Block:** Forward limit for the Phase IV guards. Movement beyond it requires Eastern Army authority.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during every night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce observation and delay identification of defenders, reserves, or counterattacks.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on the Novar and Eren crossings and the limited roads around the marshlands and Mora Hills.
- Artillery, sustainment, and the Army reserve must not compete with the 21st Division for the Novar–Veyra route during Phase III.
- Guards require overlapping observation, contact plans, and early displacement decisions during limited visibility.
- Unrepresented weather effects require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The principal enemies in the corps area are the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division, 6th Compact Mechanized Division, and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, supported by up to three artillery groups. Elements of the 3rd Compact Armored Division may affect the northern boundary or Novar.

Blue can defend Novar and Eren in restrictive crossing terrain; reinforce Veyra using interior routes; mass artillery against a crossing or the Veyra attack; conduct an eastward attack with the 6th Division; and commit the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade against the corps flank or main effort.

Blue’s most likely course is to hold Novar and Eren long enough to consolidate Veyra, then counterattack the first Karsovian bridgehead that becomes isolated. Blue may use understrength Ordan forces to retain urban terrain while preserving Compact formations for mobile response.

Blue’s most dangerous course is to concede an outlying crossing, mass the 6th Division and corps reserve against the Novar–Veyra axis, and strike while the 21st Division is attacking the city and the 12th Division is separated to the south. Concentrated artillery could sever the Novar crossing and isolate the main effort.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **Eastern Army:** Attacks west to seize the Arven crossings and Veyra, then retains the objectives and guards along LOA Block. This corps becomes the Army main effort in Phase III.
- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Operates north of the boundary; seizes Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; guards the northern approaches; and reinforces this corps’ fires during Phase III.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Secures Ruda in Phase I, then repositions near Novar as the Army reserve. It remains under Army control.
- **9th Corps Artillery Group:** Provides general support to this corps and coordinates Phase III reinforcing fires.

### e. Civil Considerations

Ruda, Selin, Novar, Eren, and Veyra contain civilians, government facilities, hospitals, utilities, and transportation infrastructure. Veyra’s political importance magnifies the effects of civilian harm or destruction of public services. Population movement may obstruct the same routes required by the corps main effort, artillery, sustainment, and Army reserve. Transfer civil-security duties only when local authorities can assume them without threatening the tactical mission.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at operation start. Cross-attachment of brigades requires Eastern Army approval. Reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery do not transfer that group to 9th Corps operational control.

### g. Assumptions

- Novar and Eren crossings begin trafficable.
- The 5th Guards Armored Brigade secures Ruda and subsequently leaves the central route available for corps use.
- The 4th Corps protects the northern flank and crossing network during the Veyra attack.
- Phase timelines are estimates; Eastern Army authorizes all phase transitions.
- No Army-reserve support is guaranteed until a commitment order is received.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **9th Maneuver Corps secures Selin and initially seizes Novar; seizes Eren while guarding its northern boundary and accepting a temporary Novar security gap; restores Novar and attacks to seize Veyra; and then retains Veyra, Novar, and Eren while guarding along the central and southern portions of LOA Block in order to capture Orda’s political center, secure the Eastern Army’s central and southern mobility corridors, and prevent Blue from restoring control of the basin.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Convert control of the central and southern Arven crossings into seizure of Veyra while preserving enough combat power and route depth to defeat the expected Blue counterattack.

#### Key Tasks

- Secure Selin and the southern-eastern support route.
- Seize Novar, accept a temporary Phase II security gap, and restore and retain the city before committing the Veyra attack unless Eastern Army accepts the isolation risk.
- Fix Blue at Eren, then seize and retain the crossing.
- Isolate Veyra before the decisive attack.
- Shift the corps decisive operation from Novar to Eren and then to Veyra while deliberately managing the temporary loss of security at Novar.
- Attack to seize Veyra and defeat organized resistance in and around the city.
- Protect the corps boundary, Mora flank, and the Veyra–Novar and Veyra–Eren routes.
- Establish guards along LOA Block while retaining Veyra, Novar, and Eren.
- Integrate reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery during Phase III.
- Preserve freedom to disengage and request the Army reserve before a decisive route or objective is lost.

#### End State

- Veyra is seized and retained, with organized Blue resistance defeated.
- Novar and Eren are controlled, trafficable, and connected to protected eastern routes.
- The corps has established guards along its portions of LOA Block.
- The northern boundary and southern flank remain secure.
- Both divisions and the artillery group remain capable of defeating a Blue counterattack or continuing operations.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Secure Selin, Seize Novar, and Fix Eren

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The corps supports 4th Corps, the Eastern Army main effort. The 21st Mechanized Division is the corps main effort and attacks to seize Novar. The 213th Infantry Brigade attacks to seize the city, crossing, and junction; the 212th Mechanized Brigade follows and supports and prepares to continue toward Veyra; the 211th Mechanized Brigade fixes or blocks Blue forces west of Novar and protects the northern flank. The 12th Armored Division is the supporting effort. The 122nd Mechanized Brigade attacks to seize Selin, then follows and supports the 121st Armored Brigade, which fixes Blue at Eren.

The 5th Guards Armored Brigade secures Ruda under Eastern Army control. The corps coordinates routes and liaison but does not redirect the brigade.

This phase ends on Army order when Novar is seized, Selin and Ruda are secured, Blue at Eren is fixed, the corps routes are open, and both divisions are postured for Phase II.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize Eren and Isolate Veyra

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The 12th Armored Division becomes the corps main effort and attacks to seize Eren with the 121st Armored Brigade. The 122nd follows and supports and secures the Eren route and bridge approaches. The 21st Mechanized Division conducts security operations that isolate Veyra and protect the Eren attack: the 211th advances to PL Push and establishes a guard oriented west; the 212th blocks the Veyra approaches; and the 213th establishes a guard from PL Push east to the Arven River along the corps boundary to find and delay forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren. This allocation deliberately leaves Novar without a retain force. The corps monitors the city and crossing, pre-positions essential supplies west of the river, protects movement through the area when possible, and prepares the 213th to restore control in Phase III.

This phase ends on Army order when Eren is seized and trafficable, Veyra is isolated sufficiently for attack, the 211th and 213th guards have achieved the required security and delay, the Mora flank is protected, and the 21st Division is postured to restore Novar and attack Veyra.

#### (3) Phase III — Restore Novar and Attack to Seize Veyra

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The corps becomes the Eastern Army main effort. The 21st Mechanized Division is the corps main effort. As the enabling action, the 213th Brigade attacks to seize Novar if Blue occupies it, or reoccupies it if vacant, confirms crossing trafficability and route control, and transitions to retain the city. Once the Novar route is restored—or on specific Eastern Army authority accepting the isolation risk—the 212th Mechanized Brigade attacks to seize Veyra as the division decisive main effort. The 211th advances from PL Push to PL Punch, conducts movement to contact to gain and develop contact, and establishes a guard protecting the attack. The 12th Armored Division supports the decisive operation: the 121st advances from PL Push to PL Punch and establishes a guard protecting the southern flank; the 122nd retains Eren and secures the southern route. The 9th Corps Artillery Group supports restoration of Novar, then masses fires for Veyra and integrates reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery.

This phase ends on Army order when Veyra is seized, organized resistance is defeated, Novar is restored and Novar and Eren are secure, the 12th Division is established at PL Punch, and the corps can assume Phase IV retain and guard tasks.

#### (4) Phase IV — Retain Veyra and the Crossings; Guard LOA Block

**Projected Turns 16–20.** The 21st Division remains the corps main effort. The 212th Brigade retains Veyra; the 213th retains Novar; and the 211th guards the central portion of LOA Block to protect Veyra and the corps center. The 12th Division retains Eren with the 122nd Brigade and guards the southern portion of LOA Block with the 121st Brigade. Guards maintain contact, identify the Blue main body, delay as required, and displace before losing freedom of maneuver. The 5th Guards Armored Brigade remains the sole Army mobile reserve.

The corps end state is achieved when Veyra, Novar, and Eren remain controlled through scenario completion, guards have provided warning and delay, and the corps retains protected routes and a viable response to counterattack.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The corps initially establishes depth by securing Novar and Selin while fixing Eren. It then shifts the decisive operation south to seize Eren. The 21st Division uses the forces west of Novar to isolate Veyra and establishes intersecting guards with the 211th at PL Push and the 213th along the corps boundary from PL Push to the Arven River. This accepts a deliberate Phase II gap at Novar in order to find and delay reinforcement of Eren.

Once Eastern Army shifts the main effort, the 213th restores Novar and the central route as an enabling action. The 21st Division then attacks Veyra while the 12th Division secures the southern flank and Eren route. The 211th and 121st Brigades advance to PL Punch and establish guards on the northern and southern shoulders of the attack. They do not attack Veyra unless reassigned; their purpose is to prevent Blue relief or counterattack from disrupting the 212th Brigade.

After seizure, the corps separates retain and guard responsibilities. The 212th and 213th Brigades retain Veyra and Novar; the 122nd retains Eren. The 211th and 121st provide the forward security zone along LOA Block. This prevents a unit retaining a city from also being treated as the corps reserve.

The corps accepts risk by having no organic maneuver reserve and by leaving Novar without a retain force in Phase II. Division commanders preserve local flexibility where possible, and the corps requests the 5th Guards Armored Brigade before a Blue force exploiting the Novar gap threatens Veyra, Eren, or a corps line of communication. The Army reserve is not used merely to replace the deliberately released Novar retain force.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 9th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support and occupies position areas that preserve range, survivability, and displacement routes.

- **Phase I:** Priority to 21st Division and 213th Brigade at Novar; next to 12th Division, with fires supporting the Selin attack and Eren fix.
- **Phase II:** Priority to 12th Division and 121st Brigade at Eren, including fires against forces detected by the 213th guard moving to reinforce Eren; next to 21st Division isolation of Veyra and the 211th guard.
- **Phase III:** Initially support the 213th Brigade restoring Novar and the central route; then priority to the 21st Division and 212th Brigade at Veyra. Integrate reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery. Maintain sufficient fires to protect the 211th and 121st guards, Novar, Eren, and the Novar–Veyra route.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the guard formation in contact or disengaging; next to the force retaining the threatened city or crossing; then to the 5th Guards Armored Brigade if committed in the corps area.

Target priorities are Blue artillery; Veyra defenders; armored and mechanized reserves; forces moving toward the attack’s flanks; units threatening Novar or Eren; and forces capable of cutting guard displacement or corps sustainment routes. Cross-boundary fires during Phase III require the corps fires cell and Eastern Army coordination.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 21st Mechanized Division

**Task:** Seize Novar; establish Phase II guards that isolate Veyra and protect the Eren attack while accepting a temporary Novar security gap; restore and retain Novar; attack to seize Veyra; then retain both cities while guarding the central portion of LOA Block.

**Purpose:** Secure the corps’ central crossing and capture the decisive political objective while denying Blue the ability to restore the basin.

- **Phase I:** 213th Brigade seizes Novar; 212th follows and supports and prepares toward Veyra; 211th fixes or blocks Blue west of Novar and protects the northern flank.
- **Phase II:** 211th advances to PL Push and guards west; 212th blocks the Veyra approaches; 213th guards from PL Push to the Arven River along the corps boundary and delays forces reinforcing Eren; Novar lacks a retain force.
- **Phase III:** 213th attacks to seize Novar if occupied, or reoccupies it if vacant, and transitions to retain; after route restoration, 212th attacks to seize Veyra while 211th advances from PL Push to PL Punch and guards the attack.
- **Phase IV:** 212th retains Veyra; 213th retains Novar; 211th guards central LOA Block.

#### (2) 12th Armored Division

**Task:** Secure Selin; fix and seize Eren; advance to PL Punch; then retain Eren and guard the southern portion of LOA Block.

**Purpose:** Open the southern crossing and protect the Veyra attack and corps line of communication from the Mora approaches.

- **Phase I:** 122nd Brigade seizes Selin, then follows and supports 121st; 121st fixes Blue at Eren.
- **Phase II:** 121st Brigade attacks to seize Eren; 122nd follows and supports and secures the route and approaches.
- **Phase III:** 121st advances from PL Push to PL Punch and establishes the southern guard; 122nd retains Eren.
- **Phase IV:** 121st guards southern LOA Block; 122nd retains Eren and prepares to reinforce the guard.

#### (3) 9th Corps Artillery Group

**Task:** Provide general support to 9th Corps; mass fires for the initial seizure and Phase III restoration of Novar, seizure of Eren and Veyra, and protection of the Phase II boundary guard; coordinate reinforcing fires during Phase III; and support Phase IV guards and retained objectives.

**Purpose:** Enable the corps to seize the central and southern crossings and Veyra while preserving freedom to defeat or delay a Blue counterattack.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Command Relationships and Main Effort

- Phase I: 21st Division main effort; 12th Division supporting effort.
- Phase II: 12th Division main effort; 21st Division supporting effort.
- Phase III: 21st Division main effort; 12th Division supporting effort.
- Phase IV: 21st Division main effort; 12th Division supporting effort.

#### (2) Crossing and Objective Control

A division does not report Novar or Eren seized until immediate approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, and a responsible force controls movement. In Phase II, the corps deliberately releases the 213th from Novar without assigning a replacement retain force. This is an accepted gap, not a handover. In Phase III, the 213th reports Novar restored only after it defeats or clears any occupying force, confirms the crossing and route, and establishes the retain posture. Veyra is not considered seized until organized resistance is defeated and the 212th Brigade can secure essential sites and routes.

#### (3) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

The 211th, 213th, and 121st Brigades gain contact with the smallest suitable force, report, develop the situation, and retain enough combat power to accomplish their guard tasks. On contact, fix, attack, bypass, block, or displace consistent with the higher purpose. The 213th gives priority to finding and delaying forces moving from the Ordan Basin toward Eren. Do not bypass a force able to threaten Veyra, Eren, a crossing, artillery, the corps boundary, or a sustainment route without containment or handover.

#### (4) Guard and Disengagement

Each division establishes guard boundaries, observation, engagement criteria, primary and alternate displacement routes, battle-handover locations, and responsibilities to rearward retain forces. Recommend disengagement when the required warning or delay is achieved or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal. Corps authorizes displacement within Army criteria.

#### (5) Reserve Requests

The corps has no authority to commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade. A request will identify the threat or opportunity, proposed task and purpose, route, support, timing, risk elsewhere, and termination condition. The brigade will not be requested merely to retain Veyra, Novar, or Eren.

#### (6) Fire-Support Coordination

During Phase III, the 9th Corps fires cell clears fires within the corps area and coordinates reinforcing fires through Eastern Army. Establish restrictive measures around Veyra, friendly guards, and uncertain boundaries. A division request does not change corps priority unless authenticated by the corps commander or executed under an approved emergency procedure.

#### (7) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Recommend Phase II:** Novar seized; Selin and Ruda secure; Eren fixed; routes and divisions ready.
- **DP 2 — Shift corps main effort to 12th Division:** Eren attack is supportable; the 211th, 212th, and 213th can isolate Veyra and protect the Eren attack; the Novar security risk is accepted.
- **DP 3 — Request Army reserve:** A penetration threatens Veyra, Novar, Eren, the corps seam, or a main route; organic action is insufficient.
- **DP 4 — Recommend Phase III:** Eren seized and trafficable; Veyra isolated; Phase II guards have achieved required delay; 213th is postured to restore Novar; fires and sustainment are ready.
- **DP 5 — Shift corps main effort to 21st Division:** Army authorizes Phase III; 213th restores Novar and the central route; reinforcing fires are coordinated; 212th is released for the Veyra attack when route criteria are met or Army accepts the isolation risk.
- **DP 6 — Recommend Phase IV:** Veyra seized; resistance defeated; Novar/Eren secure; PL Punch guards established.
- **DP 7 — Disengage guard:** Delay achieved or continued contact threatens guard preservation or the displacement route.

#### (8) CCIR

**PIR:** Blue defenses at Novar, Eren, and Veyra; 6th Division and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade locations; Blue artillery; crossing status; Veyra reinforcement routes; counterattack routes through western woods and Mora Hills; threats to the corps boundary.

**FFIR:** Main-effort brigade at or below 70 strength; Novar/Eren crossing or main route unusable; Veyra attack unable to continue; division boundary or flank uncovered; artillery unable to support; guard unable to disengage; need for Army reserve; or sustainment unable to support the next phase.

#### (9) Required Reports

Report contact, objective, crossing, route, boundary, guard, strength, artillery, sustainment, and phase-transition status once per turn and on significant change. During Phase II, report Novar status and 213th guard contact each turn. During Phase III, report Novar restoration, crossing trafficability, Veyra resistance, 212th Brigade capability, guard contact, Blue reserve indicators, reinforcing-fire status, and route capacity each turn.

#### (10) Rules and Constraints

- Do not destroy a crossing without Eastern Army authorization.
- Do not cross LOA Block without Eastern Army authorization.
- Do not treat the 212th Brigade as a reserve while it retains Veyra.
- Do not commit both divisions fully without reporting the loss of corps response capacity.
- Do not promise simultaneous full artillery support to separated actions.
- Protect civilians and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows from Karsovia through Ruda and Selin and then across Novar and Eren. During Phase II, when Novar lacks a retain force, the corps pre-positions essential support west of Novar, emphasizes the Eren corridor, and limits nonessential traffic through the city. Phase III restoration of Novar reopens the principal Veyra and Army-reserve route. Support nodes displace only after route and local security are confirmed.

### b. Priorities of Support

- **Phase I:** 21st Division/213th Brigade; 12th Division/122nd and 121st Brigades; artillery; route security.
- **Phase II:** 12th Division/121st Brigade; 213th guard support; 21st Division isolation tasks; pre-positioning west of Novar; artillery displacement; Eren repair and traffic control.
- **Phase III:** Restore Novar and its route; 21st Division/212th Brigade; 9th Corps Artillery and reinforcing-fire coordination; 211th and 121st guards; retained Eren; Army reserve if committed.
- **Phase IV:** Guard in contact or disengaging; threatened retain force; Army reserve; artillery; reconstitution.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and artillery ammunition are pacing requirements. Before releasing the 213th from Novar in Phase II, stock essential supplies west of the city without blocking the attack route and establish alternate evacuation and sustainment through Eren. In Phase III, movement control gives priority to restoring Novar before opening the route to 21st Division maneuver, artillery, sustainment, recovery, casualty evacuation, and any Army-reserve movement. Report when wet weather or enemy action reduces either crossing’s capacity.

### d. Maintenance, Recovery, and Medical

Recovery priority follows the current main effort, then a threatened guard or crossing. Disabled equipment will not block Novar, Eren, an urban junction, or a displacement route. Casualty evacuation moves east through Novar to Ruda or through Eren to Selin; alternate routes are confirmed before each phase transition.

### e. Civil Support and Reconstitution

Council-aligned authorities assume local security and essential services when conditions permit. Reconstitution priority is the 21st Division after seizure of Veyra, followed by forces essential to Novar and Eren. A unit unable to perform its essential task receives a revised mission or relief plan.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The corps main command post initially operates east of Ruda and displaces toward Novar when crossing and route conditions permit. The tactical command post follows the current main effort: 21st Division in Phase I, 12th Division in Phase II, and 21st Division in Phases III–IV. Command-post movement will not compete with the Veyra attack, artillery, or Army-reserve routes.

Succession of command is corps commander, deputy corps commander, 21st Mechanized Division commander, then 12th Armored Division commander. Liaison remains with Eastern Army, 4th Corps, both divisions, the 9th Corps Artillery Group, the 4th Corps Artillery Group during Phase III, and the 5th Guards Armored Brigade when Army identifies it for possible commitment.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use corps command and fires networks. Alternate communications use division and Eastern Army networks through liaison. Contingency communications use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Veyra status, crossing status, phase transition, reinforcing fires, Army-reserve request, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed phase and task, retain assigned cities and crossings, and preserve guard freedom to disengage. Do not begin a new phase, redirect reinforcing fires, commit the Army reserve, or advance beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless required to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

- **Scenario duration:** 18 turns / 9 days; 12 hours per turn
- **Parent order:** 9th Maneuver Corps OPORD R-20, OPERATION CAPITAL CORRIDOR
- **Role:** Corps main effort during Phases I and III–IV; supporting effort during Phase II
- **References:** R-00; R-20; [scenario specification](../../ordan_basin_scenario.md); [order of battle](../../game/order_of_battle.md); [Ordan Basin AO map](../../map/ordan_basin_ao.map.json); [Red operational graphics](Red_operational_graphics.pdf); [Red master synchronization crosswalk](red_master_synchronization_crosswalk.md)


The division exercises operational control over all three brigades. The 9th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps. The division receives priority during Phases I and III–IV and reinforcing fires from the 4th Corps Artillery Group during Phase III as allocated through corps. The division has no dedicated maneuver reserve during the decisive Veyra attack because all three brigades have essential guard, attack, or retain tasks.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from Ruda west through Novar, PL Push, PL Punch, Veyra, and the central portion of LOA Block. It includes the northern boundary with 4th Maneuver Corps, the southern boundary with 12th Armored Division, the western wooded approaches to Veyra, the Veyra–Novar road, and routes through which Blue can reinforce or counterattack from Lestara.

The division monitors Blue forces defending Novar and Veyra, the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, the 6th Compact Mechanized Division, Blue artillery able to interdict the Novar crossing or Veyra attack, and forces capable of moving through either division boundary against the main effort or line of communication.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Novar:** Principal central Arven crossing and road junction. It is the division’s initial decisive objective and logistical pivot. The Phase II decision to release its retain force creates a deliberate security and sustainment risk that must be reduced before the Veyra attack.
- **Veyra:** Political center and decisive Army objective. Dense urban terrain and converging routes favor defense, reinforcement, and local counterattack. Seizure requires control of essential sites and the eastern approaches.
- **Arven River:** Canalizes the division through Novar. Loss or obstruction of the crossing can isolate forces operating toward Veyra.
- **Western woods:** Conceal movement and counterattack forces between Novar and Veyra but limit observation and control during movement to contact.
- **PL Push and PL Punch:** Sequence the division security operation and protect the Veyra attack. The 211th Brigade advances through both lines before establishing the forward guard.
- **LOA Block:** Forward limit of the Phase IV guard. Movement beyond it requires Eastern Army authorization.
- **Boundaries:** The northern boundary connects to 4th Corps’ Central Link and central guard; the southern boundary connects to the 12th Division’s Eren and southern guard. Both must remain secure during the Veyra attack.
- **Road network:** The Ruda–Novar–Veyra route carries the main effort, artillery, sustainment, recovery, and possibly the Army reserve. Movement control is decisive.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may conceal Blue movement in the western woods and delay recognition of reserves around Veyra.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on Novar and the Veyra road and may slow mechanized movement and recovery.
- Limited visibility increases the need for positive identification and restrictive fire measures around the city and guards.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The division expects the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division to defend Novar and Veyra, reinforced by the 6th Compact Mechanized Division, the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, or elements of the 3rd Compact Armored Division. Blue can mass up to three artillery groups against the crossing or city approach.

Blue can defend Novar and Veyra as mutually supporting positions; use interior routes to reinforce the city; counterattack the 212th Brigade’s flank; interdict the Novar crossing; and exploit a gap between the 211th guard and the 12th Division.

Blue’s most likely action is to delay at Novar, withdraw toward Veyra, and defend the city while preserving the corps reserve for a counterattack against the exposed main effort. Blue’s most dangerous action is to fix the 212th at Veyra, strike the Novar–Veyra route with the 6th Division and 7th Brigade, and use artillery to isolate the division west of the Arven.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Novar and Eren, attacks to seize Veyra, then retains the cities and guards LOA Block.
- **12th Armored Division:** Seizes Eren and establishes the southern guard, protecting the Veyra attack from the Mora approaches.
- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes northern crossings and establishes guards north of the Veyra operation.
- **9th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to 9th Corps, with priority to this division in Phases I and III–IV.
- **4th Corps Artillery Group:** Reinforces 9th Corps Artillery during Phase III, with priority of reinforcing fires to this division until Veyra is seized.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve after securing Ruda. It remains under Army control.

### e. Civil Considerations

Novar and Veyra contain dense civilian populations, government facilities, hospitals, utilities, cultural sites, and transportation infrastructure. Civilian movement may congest the main route and complicate target identification. Preserve enough public infrastructure for council-aligned authorities to function after seizure. Tactical units retain security responsibility until an authenticated handover.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Reinforcing fires do not transfer the 4th Corps Artillery Group to division control.

### g. Assumptions

- Novar crossing begins trafficable.
- The 12th Division fixes and later seizes Eren, protecting the southern flank.
- The 4th Corps establishes northern security and provides reinforcing fires during Phase III.
- Corps allocates sufficient fires and movement priority for the Veyra attack.
- Phase timelines are estimates; corps and Eastern Army authorize transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **21st Mechanized Division attacks to seize Novar; conducts security operations at PL Push and along the corps boundary while accepting a temporary Novar security gap; restores and retains Novar; attacks to seize and retain Veyra; and guards along the central portion of LOA Block in order to secure 9th Corps’ central Arven crossing, protect the attack on Eren, capture Orda’s decisive political center, and prevent Blue from restoring control of the basin.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Convert control of Novar into rapid isolation and seizure of Veyra while preserving the single central route and protecting both flanks of the decisive attack.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize Novar and confirm crossing trafficability.
- Protect the northern boundary during the Novar attack.
- Follow and support the initial main effort without prematurely committing the Veyra force.
- Establish the 213th Brigade guard from PL Push to the Arven River to find and delay forces moving toward Eren.
- Accept and continuously monitor the Phase II security gap at Novar.
- Restore Novar and its crossing before committing the Veyra attack unless Eastern Army accepts the isolation risk.
- Advance the 211th Brigade through PL Push to PL Punch and establish a guard.
- Block Veyra approaches to isolate the objective before the attack.
- Attack to seize Veyra and defeat organized resistance.
- Separate Phase IV guard responsibilities from Veyra and Novar retain tasks.
- Maintain the Veyra–Novar line of communication and coordinate reinforcing fires.
- Report before all three brigades lose freedom of maneuver or the Novar route becomes vulnerable.

#### End State

- Novar and Veyra are controlled and retained.
- The Novar crossing is trafficable and the Veyra–Novar route is protected.
- The 211th Brigade guards central LOA Block with viable displacement routes.
- The 212th and 213th Brigades retain Veyra and Novar as separate responsibilities.
- Division boundaries remain secure and the division can defeat or delay counterattack.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Seize Novar

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The 213th Infantry Brigade is the division main effort and attacks to seize Novar, the central crossing, and the road junction. The 212th Mechanized Brigade follows and supports, defeats or contains bypassed threats, and prepares to continue toward Veyra. The 211th Mechanized Brigade fixes or blocks Blue forces west of Novar and protects the northern flank and corps boundary.

The phase ends on corps order when Novar is seized, both approaches are secured, trafficability and movement control are confirmed, the route from Ruda is open, and the division is postured for Phase II.

#### (2) Phase II — Isolate Veyra and Establish Security at PL Push

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The 212th Brigade becomes the division main effort and blocks the Veyra approaches to prevent reinforcement or organized withdrawal and posture for the attack. The 211th Brigade advances to PL Push and establishes a guard oriented west, protecting the western shoulder and developing Blue forces. The 213th Brigade leaves Novar and establishes a guard from PL Push east to the Arven River along the corps boundary. It finds and delays forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren and coordinates contact and fires with the 12th Division and adjacent 4th Corps elements. Novar has no retain force during this phase. The division monitors the city and crossing, controls movement when possible, and prepares the 213th to restore control in Phase III.

The phase ends on corps order when Eren is seized and trafficable, the Veyra approaches are blocked sufficiently to support attack, the 211th and 213th guards have achieved the required security and delay, and the division is postured to restore Novar and attack Veyra.

#### (3) Phase III — Restore Novar and Attack to Seize Veyra

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The division is the corps and Eastern Army main effort. The 212th Brigade remains the division decisive main effort. Before its attack is committed, the 213th Brigade attacks to seize Novar if Blue occupies it, or reoccupies it if vacant, confirms both approaches, crossing trafficability, and movement control, and transitions to retain the city and secure the Veyra–Novar route. Once the route is restored—or on specific Eastern Army authority accepting the isolation risk—the 212th attacks to seize Veyra and defeat organized resistance. The 211th Brigade advances from PL Push to PL Punch using movement to contact, gains and develops contact, and establishes a guard protecting the attack from counterattack or relief. Corps artillery first supports restoration of Novar and then masses fires for the 212th Brigade while integrating reinforcing fires from 4th Corps.

The phase ends on corps and Army order when Veyra is seized, organized resistance is defeated, Novar is restored and the connecting route is secure, the 211th is established at PL Punch, and the division can assume Phase IV responsibilities.

#### (4) Phase IV — Retain Veyra and Novar; Guard LOA Block

**Projected Turns 16–18.** The 212th Brigade remains the division main effort and retains Veyra, including essential government and transportation sites. The 213th Brigade retains Novar and the central crossing. The 211th Brigade guards along the central portion of LOA Block, maintains contact, identifies the Blue main body, delays as required, and displaces before becoming decisively engaged. Priority may shift temporarily to the 211th when in contact or disengaging without changing the division’s essential focus on retaining Veyra.

The division end state is achieved when Veyra and Novar remain controlled, the guard has provided required warning and delay, and the division maintains a protected route and coherent force.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The division uses the 213th Brigade to seize Novar because infantry is suited to securing the urban crossing and because this preserves the mechanized brigades for the westward operation. The 212th follows and supports rather than becoming simultaneously engaged at Novar. The 211th protects the northern flank and develops the Blue situation.

During Phase II, the 212th blocks the Veyra approaches while the 211th establishes the western guard at PL Push and the 213th guards the corps boundary from PL Push to the Arven River. The 213th guard supports the Eren attack by finding and delaying forces moving south from the Ordan Basin. This concentration deliberately leaves Novar undefended. The division mitigates the risk through observation, fires, pre-positioned supplies west of the river, and preparation to restore the crossing.

During Phase III, the 213th restores Novar as an enabling action while the 211th advances to the forward guard. The 212th attacks Veyra after the Novar route is restored unless Eastern Army specifically authorizes simultaneous action and accepts the isolation risk. During Phase IV, all three brigades retain separate essential tasks. The 212th is not described as a reserve while it retains Veyra.

The division has no dedicated reserve. The 213th may provide limited route or local support only if Novar remains retained. Any need for a mobile counterattack beyond local action is reported through 9th Corps for Eastern Army decision.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The division receives support from the 9th Corps Artillery Group and, during Phase III, reinforcing fires from the 4th Corps Artillery Group through corps coordination.

- **Phase I:** Priority to the 213th Brigade at Novar. Suppress crossing defenses, isolate the objective, interdict reinforcement from Veyra, and conduct counterfire.
- **Phase II:** Corps priority remains the 12th Division at Eren. Within allocated support, engage forces detected by the 213th guard moving to reinforce Eren, then support the 212th Brigade’s isolation of Veyra and the 211th guard. Monitor but do not imply continuous control of Novar.
- **Phase III:** Initially support the 213th Brigade restoring Novar and the central route. After route confirmation, priority shifts to the 212th Brigade at Veyra. Isolate Veyra, suppress defenders, obscure exposed movement, counterfire, and disrupt Blue reserve forces while maintaining sufficient fires for the 211th guard and Novar route.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the 211th Brigade when in contact or disengaging, then the 212th at Veyra and 213th at Novar according to the most dangerous threat.

The division nominates targets and coordinates observation but does not redirect corps or reinforcing artillery without authenticated authority. Restrictive measures protect friendly guards, urban sites, boundaries, and uncertain positions.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 211th Mechanized Brigade — Security and Guard Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Fix or block Blue forces west of Novar and protect the northern flank. **Purpose:** Prevent interference with the seizure of Novar and secure the corps boundary.
- **Phase II — Task:** Advance to PL Push and establish a guard oriented west. **Purpose:** Develop Blue forces, protect the division flank, and support isolation of Veyra.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact from PL Push to PL Punch, gain and develop contact, and establish a guard protecting the Veyra attack. **Purpose:** Prevent Blue relief or counterattack from disrupting the 212th Brigade.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Guard along the central portion of LOA Block, maintain contact, delay, and displace within division criteria. **Purpose:** Provide warning and time while protecting Veyra and the corps center.

#### (2) 212th Mechanized Brigade — Veyra Main Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Follow and support the 213th Brigade and prepare to continue toward Veyra. **Purpose:** Preserve momentum at Novar while retaining combat power for the decisive objective.
- **Phase II — Task:** Block the Veyra approaches and prevent reinforcement or organized withdrawal. **Purpose:** Isolate the objective and set conditions for attack.
- **Phase III — Task:** Attack to seize Veyra and defeat organized resistance in and around the city. **Purpose:** Capture Orda’s decisive political and transportation center.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain Veyra and secure essential government and transportation sites. **Purpose:** Prevent Blue from restoring the elected government’s control.

#### (3) 213th Infantry Brigade — Boundary Guard and Novar Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to seize Novar, its crossing, and road junction. **Purpose:** Open the central Arven route and base the division’s Veyra operation.
- **Phase II — Task:** Establish a guard from PL Push east to the Arven River along the corps boundary; find and delay forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren. **Purpose:** Protect the 12th Division’s crossing attack and the corps boundary while the division isolates Veyra.
- **Phase III — Task:** Attack to seize Novar if occupied, or reoccupy it if vacant; confirm both approaches, crossing trafficability, and movement control; then retain the city and secure the Veyra–Novar route. **Purpose:** Restore the central line of communication and prevent isolation of the Veyra main effort.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain Novar and secure the central crossing and route. **Purpose:** Preserve the division line of communication and Army crossing network.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Main Effort

- Phase I: 213th Brigade.
- Phases II–IV: 212th Brigade.

Fires or sustainment priority shifts temporarily to the 213th for restoration of Novar and may shift to the 211th when it is in contact or disengaging. These supporting priorities do not change the 212th Brigade’s status as the Phase II–IV division main effort unless authenticated.

#### (2) Follow and Support

The 212th maintains enough distance to avoid simultaneous engagement with the 213th at Novar but remains close enough to defeat bypassed threats, secure routes, or continue west. It does not become fixed in Novar unless required to prevent loss of the crossing.

#### (3) Novar and Veyra Control

Novar is not reported restored in Phase III until both approaches are secured, crossing trafficability is confirmed, movement control is established, and the 213th can assume the retain task. Releasing the 213th for its Phase II guard deliberately ends the continuous Novar retain mission without transferring it to another brigade. Veyra is not seized until organized resistance is defeated and essential government, transportation, and route sites are controlled. The 212th retains Veyra; the 213th retains Novar after restoration.

#### (4) Movement to Contact and Guard

The 211th and 213th gain contact with the smallest suitable force, report and develop the situation, and preserve enough combat power to accomplish their guard tasks. The 213th gives priority to forces moving from the Ordan Basin toward Eren. Neither guard bypasses a force capable of threatening Veyra, Eren, a division boundary, artillery, or sustainment without containment or handover.

#### (5) Guard Disengagement

The 211th establishes observation, engagement criteria, primary and alternate displacement routes, and a battle handover with the Veyra and Novar retain forces. The 213th establishes the same measures for its Phase II boundary sector and disengages toward Novar on the Phase III order after achieving the required delay or handing off remaining contact. A guard disengages when directed, when required delay is achieved, or when continued contact would prevent orderly withdrawal under preapproved criteria.

#### (6) Reserve and Risk

The division has no dedicated reserve. During Phase II all three brigades have essential block or guard tasks and Novar has no retain force. During Phases III–IV, the 213th provides limited local response only while Novar remains retained; the 212th is never treated as reserve while attacking or retaining Veyra. Request corps assistance before all three brigades become decisively engaged, the Novar route is threatened, or a guard cannot disengage. The division cannot commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.

#### (7) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Complete Novar seizure:** Both approaches secured; crossing trafficable; movement control and route security established.
- **DP 2 — Recommend Phase II:** Initial Novar seizure complete; 212th postured to block; 211th and 213th guard routes ready; corps accepts the Novar security gap.
- **DP 3 — Recommend Phase III:** Veyra sufficiently isolated; 211th and 213th guards achieved required effects; Eren secure; 213th postured to restore Novar; fires and sustainment ready.
- **DP 4 — Restore Novar:** Corps authorizes Phase III; 213th defeats or clears any occupying force, confirms the crossing and route, and assumes the retain task.
- **DP 5 — Commit Veyra attack:** Novar route restored or Eastern Army accepts the isolation risk; 212th mission capable; guards and reinforcing fires coordinated.
- **DP 6 — Complete Veyra seizure:** Organized resistance defeated; essential sites and routes controlled; 212th capable of retaining.
- **DP 7 — Establish Phase IV posture:** 211th guard route ready; 212th and 213th retain responsibilities confirmed.
- **DP 8 — Disengage guard:** Required delay achieved or continued contact threatens withdrawal.

#### (8) CCIR

**PIR:** Defenses at Novar and Veyra; Blue artillery; 6th Division and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade; reinforcement routes from Lestara; forces in western woods; crossing condition; threats to either division boundary.

**FFIR:** Any brigade at or below 70 strength; Blue occupies Novar; Novar crossing or Veyra route blocked; 212th unable to continue attack; 211th or 213th unable to guard or disengage; 213th unable to restore or retain Novar; artillery or reinforcing fires unavailable; boundary uncovered; or corps assistance required.

#### (9) Reports and Constraints

Report contact, strength, Novar/Veyra status, crossing and route condition, block and guard status, boundaries, fires, sustainment, and phase readiness once per turn and on significant change. Do not destroy Novar crossing, cross LOA Block, or begin a new phase without authenticated authority. Protect civilians and critical infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support initially flows through Ruda and across Novar along the Veyra route. Before the 213th leaves Novar in Phase II, the division pre-positions essential supplies west of the river, establishes alternate support and casualty evacuation through Eren, and limits nonessential movement through the unsecured city. In Phase III, movement priority first restores Novar and then supports the 212th attack while preserving access for artillery, casualty evacuation, recovery, and any Army-reserve movement.

### b. Priorities of Support

- Phase I: 213th Brigade; 212th follow-and-support posture; 211th boundary security.
- Phase II: 212th block; 211th PL Push guard; 213th corps-boundary guard; pre-positioning west of Novar.
- Phase III: 213th restoration of Novar and route; then 212th Veyra attack; artillery coordination; 211th guard.
- Phase IV: 211th guard in contact/disengaging; threatened retain force; reconstitution.

### c. Supply, Maintenance, and Transportation

Fuel, ammunition, and Novar route capacity are pacing. Stock essential supplies west of Novar before releasing its retain force without creating a vulnerable concentration. Maintain Eren as the alternate route during the gap. After the 213th restores Novar, separate main-effort maneuver, artillery, sustainment, recovery, casualty evacuation, and Army-reserve movement. Remove disabled equipment from the crossing and Veyra road immediately.

### d. Medical, Civil Support, and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through Novar to Ruda. Establish alternate collection points and emergency routing before Phase III. Coordinate local services and civilian movement with council-aligned authorities. Reconstitution priority follows the 212th Brigade, then the formation essential to Novar retention or guard disengagement.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The division command post initially operates east of Novar and displaces across the river after the crossing and route are secure. During the Phase II security gap, the command post remains outside the unsecured city and preserves communications with both guards. The tactical command post follows the current main effort: 213th in Phase I and 212th thereafter, while maintaining control of the 213th’s Phase III enabling attack. Command-post movement will not compete with restoration of Novar, the Veyra attack, or artillery and reserve routes.

Succession of command is division commander, deputy division commander, 212th Brigade commander, 213th Brigade commander, then 211th Brigade commander. Maintain liaison with 9th Corps, 12th Division, 4th Corps boundary elements, the 9th Corps Artillery Group, and reinforcing-fire representatives during Phase III.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use division and corps networks. Alternate means use brigade networks and liaison; contingency means use relay, schedules, and messenger. Novar/Veyra status, block and guard posture, reinforcing fires, boundaries, phase transition, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed task, retain objectives assigned in the current phase, and preserve guard freedom to disengage. Do not independently release the 213th from its Phase II guard, begin the Veyra attack, redirect reinforcing fires, or move beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless necessary to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

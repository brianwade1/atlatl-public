## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The corps area of interest extends from Kasar and the Karsovian eastern support routes west through Dalen, North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, PL Push, PL Punch, and LOA Block. It includes the Saren Heights, western woods, the corps boundary with 9th Maneuver Corps, the Novar–Veyra road network, and Blue routes capable of supporting counterattack into the northern or central basin.

The corps monitors Blue forces defending the northern crossings, forces capable of reinforcing from Veyra or Novar, the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, and any 6th Compact Mechanized Division movement against the corps flank or rear. Activity south of the corps boundary matters when it can interfere with the Central Link Bridge attack, the Phase III Veyra operation, or the corps’ east–west sustainment routes.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Kasar:** Eastern road junction and initial support location. Its security opens the northern axis and protects the corps line of communication.
- **Dalen:** Major northern road junction and the base for the hook toward Central Link Bridge. Securing Dalen does not by itself control the bridge.
- **North Pass Bridge:** Restrictive northern crossing dominated by the Saren Heights. The approach favors prepared defense and canalizes armored movement.
- **Central Link Bridge:** Stand-alone central crossing south of Dalen. Its separation from both Dalen and Novar requires independent approach security and bridge control.
- **Saren Heights:** Rough terrain can conceal Blue forces and observation. It threatens the northern flank of the 151st Brigade attack unless blocked or guarded.
- **Western woods:** Conceals movement west of the Arven but limits observation and complicates control during movement to contact.
- **Southeastern marshlands:** Primarily outside the corps sector but capable of masking a threat against the 15th Division’s southern flank and the corps boundary.
- **Road network:** The Kasar–Dalen route, North Pass road, and Central Link approaches are the corps’ principal maneuver and sustainment corridors. Disabled units or poorly timed artillery displacement can stop movement through the crossing network.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during every night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, and darkness may reduce observation and slow recognition of a Blue counterattack.
- Wet ground increases dependence on roads and bridges, especially in the Saren Heights and near the river.
- Artillery and sustainment displacement must not compete with maneuver forces at Dalen or either bridge.
- Guard forces require overlapping observation and earlier displacement decisions during limited visibility.
- Unrepresented effects require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The principal enemy in the corps area is the 3rd Compact Armored Division, supported by its artillery group and potentially reinforced by the 6th Compact Mechanized Division or 7th Coalition Armored Brigade. Blue may also employ Ordan infantry at Dalen or the crossings.

Blue can defend the canalized North Pass approach; concentrate artillery at Dalen or Central Link; counterattack an exposed crossing force; and use the western road network to shift a reserve between the northern and central sectors. Its most dangerous action is to fix one division at a bridge, counterattack the other division’s flank from the Veyra–Novar network, and use artillery to sever the corps route east of the Arven.

Blue’s most likely action is a layered defense of Dalen and the bridges, followed by a local counterattack against whichever crossing force first becomes isolated. Blue may trade outlying ground for time while preserving a force west of the river.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **Eastern Army:** Attacks west to seize the Arven crossings and Veyra, then retains the objectives and guards along LOA Block.
- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Operates south of the corps boundary. It seizes Novar and Eren, isolates Veyra, becomes the Army main effort in Phase III, and retains the central-southern basin in Phase IV.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Secures Ruda in Phase I, then repositions near Novar as the Army reserve. It remains under Army control.
- **4th Corps Artillery Group:** Provides general support to this corps and reinforces 9th Corps Artillery in Phase III.

### e. Civil Considerations

Kasar and Dalen contain civilians, transportation facilities, medical sites, utilities, and local administrative centers. The bridge approaches and road junctions may be congested by displaced persons. Preserve bridges and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment. Transfer local security to council-aligned authorities only after tactical and route conditions permit.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at operation start. Cross-attachment of brigades requires Eastern Army approval. The Phase III reinforcing-fire mission does not transfer operational control of the 4th Corps Artillery Group.

### g. Assumptions

- North Pass Bridge and Central Link Bridge begin trafficable.
- Kasar and the eastern corps route remain supportable during Phase I.
- The 9th Corps secures Novar and protects the southern side of the corps boundary.
- Phase timelines are estimates; Eastern Army authorizes all phase transitions.
- No Army-reserve support is guaranteed until a commitment order is received.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **4th Maneuver Corps attacks through the northern and central-northern Ordan Basin to seize Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; advances to PL Punch; and then retains the northern crossing network while guarding along the northern portion of LOA Block in order to create the Eastern Army’s northern hook, protect 9th Corps’ attack on Veyra, and preserve operational mobility across the Arven River.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Turn Blue’s northern crossing defense, create depth across the Arven, and isolate the Veyra fight from interference from the north.

#### Key Tasks

- Secure Kasar and the corps’ eastern line of communication.
- Seize Dalen and preserve it as the base of the northern hook.
- Fix, then seize, North Pass Bridge.
- Attack from Dalen to seize Central Link Bridge independently of Dalen.
- Protect the division seam and the corps boundary with 9th Corps.
- Advance to PL Punch and establish guards that protect the Veyra operation.
- Retain Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge.
- Reinforce 9th Corps fires in Phase III without uncovering the corps guard or crossing network.
- Preserve freedom to disengage from LOA Block and avoid dependence on the Army reserve.

#### End State

- Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge are controlled, trafficable, and connected to protected eastern routes.
- The corps has established security at PL Punch and a guard along its portion of LOA Block.
- Blue forces cannot interfere with the Veyra operation from the northern basin.
- The corps boundary and division seam remain secure.
- Both divisions and the artillery group remain capable of continued defense or follow-on attack.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Secure Kasar and Seize Dalen

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The corps is the Eastern Army main effort. The 15th Mechanized Division is the corps main effort and attacks to seize Dalen. The 151st Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort; the 152nd follows and supports, then blocks approaches from Central Link Bridge; the 153rd secures Dalen and prepares to retain it. The 8th Guards Armored Division is the supporting effort. Its 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade attacks to seize Kasar, then repositions to follow and support the subsequent North Pass Bridge attack. The 81st Guards Armored Brigade fixes Blue at North Pass Bridge.

This phase ends on Army order when Dalen is seized, Kasar is secured, Blue at North Pass Bridge is fixed, the corps route is open, and both divisions are postured for Phase II.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize North Pass and Central Link

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The corps remains the Eastern Army main effort. The 15th Division remains the corps main effort and attacks from Dalen to seize Central Link Bridge with the 151st Brigade. The 152nd supports the attack and secures the bridge approaches. The 153rd blocks oriented north to prevent Blue movement from the Saren Heights and guards the 151st flank; it is prepared to reorient west against forces emerging from the marshlands. The 8th Division attacks to seize North Pass Bridge with the 81st Brigade while the 82nd follows and supports and secures the approaches.

This phase ends on Army order when both bridges are seized and trafficable, approaches and routes are controlled, the division seam is secure, and 9th Corps is postured to attack Veyra.

#### (3) Phase III — Advance to PL Punch and Isolate Veyra

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The corps becomes the Army supporting effort. Both divisions conduct movement to contact toward PL Punch, secure the crossing network, and establish guards oriented west. The 15th Division is the corps main effort because its central guard protects the corps boundary and the northern side of 9th Corps’ Veyra attack. Within the 8th Division, the 82nd Brigade becomes the division main effort, conducts movement to contact to PL Punch, and establishes the northern guard while the 81st Brigade retains North Pass Bridge and the division route. The corps artillery group remains in general support to the corps and reinforces 9th Corps Artillery with priority to the 21st Division.

This phase ends on Army order when Veyra is seized, organized resistance is defeated, both divisions are established at PL Punch, and the corps can assume Phase IV retain and guard tasks.

#### (4) Phase IV — Retain the Crossings and Guard LOA Block

**Projected Turns 16–18.** The 15th Division remains the corps main effort. It retains Dalen and Central Link Bridge and guards the central portion of the corps frontage. The 8th Division retains North Pass Bridge and guards the northern portion of LOA Block. Guards identify the Blue main body, delay it as required, and displace along preplanned routes before losing freedom of maneuver. The artillery group resumes exclusive priority to parent-corps tasks.

The corps end state is achieved when assigned objectives remain controlled through scenario completion, guards have provided warning and delay, and both divisions remain capable of receiving or defeating a Blue counterattack.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The corps attacks on two mutually supporting axes. The 15th Division uses Dalen to establish the central-northern bridgehead and turns south-west toward Central Link Bridge. The 8th Division secures Kasar and applies pressure against North Pass Bridge. The divisions avoid simultaneous frontal commitment against separate bridges before Dalen and Kasar are secure.

After the crossings are seized, both divisions move toward PL Punch while retaining bridge security and eastward sustainment routes. The 15th Division’s central guard protects the seam with 9th Corps and the northern shoulder of the Veyra operation. The 8th Division’s guard denies Blue freedom to move through the Saren Heights or turn the corps’ northern flank.

The corps accepts risk by operating without an organic maneuver reserve. Division commanders preserve the smallest feasible uncommitted capability, maintain lateral routes, and report before all subordinate brigades become decisively engaged. A request for the 5th Guards Armored Brigade must reach Eastern Army before a threatened objective or route is lost.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 4th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support. It occupies artillery position areas and displaces by echelon; it does not occupy a maneuver support-by-fire position.

- **Phase I:** Priority to 15th Division and the 151st Brigade at Dalen; next to 8th Division, first the 82nd at Kasar and then the 81st fixing North Pass.
- **Phase II:** Priority to 15th Division and the 151st Brigade at Central Link; next to 8th Division and the 81st Brigade at North Pass.
- **Phase III:** Maintain support to corps guards and crossings while reinforcing 9th Corps Artillery. Priority of reinforcing fires is 21st Division until Veyra is seized. Cross-boundary fires require Army coordination and 9th Corps clearance in its area.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the guard formation in contact or disengaging, then the threatened retained crossing, then an Army-reserve action in the corps area.

Target priorities are Blue artillery, crossing defenders, armored or mechanized counterattack forces, reserves moving from Veyra or Novar, forces threatening guard routes, and units capable of severing the corps line of communication.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 8th Guards Armored Division

**Task:** Secure Kasar; fix and seize North Pass Bridge; advance to PL Punch; then retain North Pass Bridge and guard the northern portion of LOA Block.

**Purpose:** Open the northern axis and prevent Blue forces from turning the corps flank or interfering with the Army’s Veyra operation.

- **Phase I:** 82nd Brigade seizes Kasar, then repositions to follow and support 81st; 81st fixes North Pass Bridge.
- **Phase II:** 81st Brigade attacks to seize North Pass Bridge; 82nd follows and supports, secures the approaches, and prepares for the forward guard.
- **Phase III:** 82nd Brigade conducts movement to contact to PL Punch and establishes the northern guard; 81st Brigade retains North Pass Bridge and prepares to reinforce or cover the guard.
- **Phase IV:** 82nd Brigade guards along LOA Block; 81st Brigade retains North Pass Bridge and prepares to reinforce the guard.

#### (2) 15th Mechanized Division

**Task:** Seize and retain Dalen and Central Link Bridge; advance to PL Punch; then guard the central portion of the corps frontage.

**Purpose:** Create the northern hook, secure the corps’ central crossing, and protect the northern flank of 9th Corps’ attack on Veyra.

- **Phase I:** 151st Brigade seizes Dalen; 152nd follows and supports and blocks Central Link approaches; 153rd secures Dalen and prepares to retain.
- **Phase II:** 151st Brigade seizes Central Link Bridge; 152nd supports and secures approaches; 153rd blocks north and guards the 151st flank, prepared to reorient west.
- **Phase III:** Advance to PL Punch, establish a central guard, retain Dalen and Central Link, and protect the corps boundary.
- **Phase IV:** 151st Brigade guards LOA Block; 152nd retains Central Link Bridge; 153rd retains Dalen and secures the connecting route.

#### (3) 4th Corps Artillery Group

**Task:** Provide general support to 4th Corps; mass fires for Dalen, Central Link, and North Pass; reinforce 9th Corps Artillery during Phase III; then support the Phase IV guard and crossing defense.

**Purpose:** Enable the corps’ decisive crossing operations, isolate the Veyra fight, and preserve the northern bridgehead against counterattack.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Command Relationships and Main Effort

- Phase I: 15th Division main effort; 8th Division supporting effort.
- Phase II: 15th Division main effort; 8th Division supporting effort.
- Phase III: 15th Division main effort for the central guard; 8th Division supporting effort on the northern flank.
- Phase IV: 15th Division main effort; 8th Division supporting effort.

#### (2) Crossing Control

A division does not report a bridge seized until both immediate approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, and a responsible force controls movement. The seizing division controls the bridge until a confirmed handover. Preserve an alternate route whenever possible.

#### (3) Movement to Contact and Actions on Contact

During Phase III, gain contact with the smallest suitable force, report, develop the situation, and retain sufficient combat power to establish the guard. Do not bypass a force capable of threatening a bridge, artillery position, corps boundary, or sustainment route without containment or handover.

#### (4) Guard and Disengagement

Each division establishes guard boundaries, observation, engagement criteria, primary and alternate displacement routes, battle-handover locations, and rearward retain responsibilities. Recommend disengagement when required delay is achieved or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal. Corps authorizes displacement within Army criteria.

#### (5) Reserve Requests

The corps has no authority to commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade. A request will identify the threat or opportunity, proposed task and purpose, route, support, timing, risk elsewhere, and expected termination condition. Do not plan routine missions that depend on reserve commitment.

#### (6) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Recommend Phase II:** Dalen seized; Kasar secured; North Pass fixed; routes and divisions ready.
- **DP 2 — Request Army reserve:** A penetration threatens a crossing, route, corps seam, or main effort; organic action is insufficient.
- **DP 3 — Recommend Phase III:** North Pass and Central Link seized and trafficable; routes controlled; corps able to guard while reinforcing 9th Corps fires.
- **DP 4 — Establish Phase III guards:** Divisions reach PL Punch with sufficient strength, routes, and artillery coverage.
- **DP 5 — Recommend Phase IV:** Veyra seized; organized resistance defeated; both divisions established at PL Punch; retained objectives secure.
- **DP 6 — Disengage guard:** Delay achieved or continued contact threatens the guard’s preservation or withdrawal route.

#### (7) CCIR

**PIR:** Blue defense at Dalen and both bridges; 3rd and 6th Division main efforts; 7th Coalition Armored Brigade location; Blue artillery; crossing status; counterattack routes from Veyra, Novar, Saren Heights, and the western woods.

**FFIR:** Main-effort brigade at or below 70 strength; bridge or route unusable; division seam or corps boundary uncovered; artillery unable to support; no formation available to guard; guard unable to disengage; need for Army reserve; or sustainment unable to support the next phase.

#### (8) Required Reports

Report contact, objective, bridge, route, seam, boundary, guard, strength, artillery, sustainment, and phase-transition status once per turn and on significant change. Report immediately when a subordinate task conflicts with R-00, this order, the approved overlay, or a later authenticated order.

#### (9) Rules and Constraints

- Do not destroy a bridge without Eastern Army authorization.
- Do not cross LOA Block without Eastern Army authorization.
- Do not commit both divisions fully without reporting the loss of corps response capacity.
- Do not use the 4th Corps Artillery Group as a maneuver unit or promise simultaneous full support to separated actions.
- Protect civilians and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows from the Karsovian forward area through Kasar and then along separate northern and central routes to Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge. The corps sustains two axes so loss or congestion of one crossing does not halt the entire operation. Support nodes displace only after routes are secured and will not occupy bridge approaches or guard displacement routes.

### b. Priorities of Support

- **Phase I:** 15th Division/151st Brigade; 8th Division/82nd Brigade; artillery; route security.
- **Phase II:** 15th Division/151st Brigade; 8th Division/81st Brigade; artillery displacement; crossing repair and traffic control.
- **Phase III:** 15th Division central guard; artillery reinforcing mission; 8th Division/82nd northern guard; 81st at North Pass; retained crossings.
- **Phase IV:** Guard in contact or disengaging; threatened retain force; artillery; reconstitution.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and artillery ammunition are pacing requirements. Separate maneuver, artillery, sustainment, recovery, and casualty-evacuation movement at Kasar, Dalen, and the bridge approaches. Stock supplies before Phase III because wet weather or Blue interdiction may slow movement across the river.

### d. Maintenance, Recovery, and Medical

Recovery priority follows the current main effort, then the threatened bridge or guard. Disabled equipment will not block an urban junction, crossing, or displacement route. Casualty evacuation moves east through Dalen or North Pass to Kasar; each division designates an alternate route before crossing the Arven.

### e. Reconstitution

Reconstitution priority is the main-effort division, followed by the formation essential to bridge retention. A formation unable to accomplish its essential task receives a revised mission or relief plan; it does not remain nominally responsible for decisive terrain.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The corps main command post initially operates east of Kasar and displaces toward Dalen when the route and crossings permit. The tactical command post follows the 15th Division during Phases I–II and positions to control both guards during Phases III–IV. Command-post movement will not compete with artillery or main-effort movement.

Succession of command is corps commander, deputy corps commander, 15th Mechanized Division commander, then 8th Guards Armored Division commander. Liaison remains with Eastern Army, 9th Corps, both divisions, the corps artillery group, and the 5th Guards Armored Brigade when Army identifies it for possible commitment.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use corps command and fires networks. Alternate communications use division and Eastern Army networks through liaison. Contingency communications use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Bridge status, phase transition, corps boundary, reinforcing fires, reserve request, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed phase and task, retain assigned crossings, and preserve guard freedom to disengage. Do not begin a new phase, request fires across the 9th Corps boundary without clearance, or advance beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless required to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

## Acknowledgment

Division and artillery-group commanders acknowledge and backbrief phase tasks, decisive operations, crossing responsibilities, seams and boundaries, Phase III reinforcing fires, reserve-request procedures, guard disengagement, routes, sustainment priorities, and degraded-communications procedures.

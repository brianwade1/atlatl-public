## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The group area of interest extends from the Ruda and Selin support areas west through Novar and Eren to PL Push, PL Punch, Veyra, the Mora approaches, and the central and southern portions of LOA Block. It includes Blue artillery positions capable of affecting either crossing or the Veyra attack; routes used by the 6th Compact Mechanized Division and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade; and all corridors affecting range, survivability, ammunition supply, or displacement.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Ruda–Novar route:** Principal central route for the corps main effort, artillery, ammunition, recovery, and possible Army-reserve movement. Congestion directly limits fire support.
- **Selin–Eren route:** Southern route and alternate corps line of communication. Restrictive approaches can delay displacement and ammunition movement.
- **Novar and Eren crossings:** Canalize maneuver and sustainment. Artillery movement must not obstruct bridge approaches, assault forces, casualty evacuation, or traffic control.
- **Western woods:** Mask Blue movement between the crossings and Veyra, reduce observation, and create likely counterattack and infiltration routes.
- **Veyra:** Dense urban terrain with protected sites, uncertain friendly locations, and converging routes. Fire planning requires positive clearance and carefully controlled effects.
- **Mora approaches:** Expose the corps southern flank and Eren route to Blue movement from the hills.
- **PL Push, PL Punch, and LOA Block:** Sequence security operations and establish the final guard limit. Position areas must support guards without placing the artillery beyond protected routes.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce target acquisition, adjustment, and battle-damage assessment around crossings, woods, and Veyra.
- Wet conditions increase road dependence and artillery displacement time, particularly on the Novar–Veyra and Selin–Eren routes.
- Displacement and ammunition movement begin early enough to preserve support across phase transitions.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

Blue can employ up to three artillery groups with the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division, 6th Compact Mechanized Division, 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, and elements of the 3rd Compact Armored Division. Blue artillery can interdict Novar or Eren, disrupt the Veyra attack, isolate a guard, or force this group to displace.

The most likely enemy action is to defend the crossings and Veyra in depth while using counterfire and interdiction against bridge approaches, the Novar–Veyra route, and known position areas. The most dangerous action is a coordinated armored counterattack from the western woods or Mora approaches combined with counterfire that separates the 212th Brigade from Novar, threatens Eren, and forces the group to choose between the decisive attack and crossing defense.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Novar and Eren, attacks to seize Veyra, then retains the cities and crossings while guarding along LOA Block.
- **21st Mechanized Division:** Corps main effort during Phases I and III–IV. Seizes Novar and Veyra and establishes the central guard.
- **12th Armored Division:** Corps main effort during Phase II. Secures Selin, seizes Eren, and establishes the southern guard.
- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes the northern and central crossings and establishes security north of the Veyra operation.
- **4th Corps Artillery Group:** Changes to general support–reinforcing in Phase III and reinforces this group with priority to the 21st Division and 212th Brigade.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve after securing Ruda. It may receive fires if committed in the corps area, subject to Army and corps priority.

### e. Civil Considerations

Ruda, Selin, Novar, Eren, and Veyra contain civilians, hospitals, utilities, government facilities, cultural sites, and transportation infrastructure. Preserve the crossings unless Eastern Army authorizes destruction. Effects in Veyra require positive clearance and compliance with corps restrictive measures. Civilian movement may obstruct the same routes required for artillery displacement and supply.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Phase III reinforcing fires do not attach the 4th Corps Artillery Group to this headquarters.

### g. Assumptions

- The group remains a single playable counter and cannot provide full simultaneous effects to separated objectives.
- Range, movement, target eligibility, and combat effects follow Atlatl and scenario adjudication.
- 9th Corps authenticates priorities within its area; Eastern Army resolves cross-corps competition.
- The 4th Corps Artillery Group can reinforce during Phase III after range, liaison, communications, and clearance requirements are met.
- Position-area selection remains subordinate to the approved overlay, route status, and current threat.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **9th Corps Artillery Group provides general support to 9th Maneuver Corps; masses fires to enable the initial seizure and Phase III restoration of Novar, seizure of Eren, isolation and seizure of Veyra, and the subsequent guard and defense of retained objectives; and integrates reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery during Phase III in order to secure the corps crossing network, enable capture of Orda’s political center, and preserve 9th Corps freedom of action against Blue counterattack.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Concentrate the corps’ limited artillery effects at each decisive action, culminating at Veyra, while retaining enough responsiveness to protect the crossings, guards, and single routes sustaining the attack.

#### Key Tasks

- Maintain centralized control and general support to 9th Corps throughout.
- Support the 21st Division and 213th Brigade first during the Novar attack.
- Shift priority to the 12th Division and 121st Brigade for the Eren attack.
- Support the 213th Brigade guard against forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren.
- Restore fires to the 213th Brigade for the Phase III attack to seize Novar if required.
- Mass corps and reinforcing fires for the 21st Division and 212th Brigade at Veyra.
- Suppress or obscure objective defenses and isolate Novar, Eren, and Veyra.
- Conduct counterfire against Blue artillery affecting the current decisive action, a crossing, guard, or corps route.
- Interdict Blue armored reserves, reinforcements, and counterattack forces on identified routes.
- Support Phase IV guard contact, delay, disengagement, and battle handover.
- Displace by echelon without blocking maneuver, bridge, sustainment, recovery, or Army-reserve routes.
- Preserve ammunition for the Veyra attack and guard disengagement.
- Report any range limitation, support gap, route loss, incompatible priority, or reinforcing-fire failure immediately.

#### End State

- Novar, Eren, and Veyra each received timely massed fires during the decisive action.
- Phase III reinforcing fires were integrated without transferring command of the 4th Corps group.
- The central and southern guards could gain time and disengage under supporting fires.
- The crossings and Novar–Veyra route were not lost for lack of reported artillery support.
- The group remains mobile, survivable, supplied, and capable of supporting the corps through Turn 20.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Support Novar, Selin, and the Eren Fix

**Projected Turns 1–5. Tactical mission: General support.** Priority of fires is the 21st Mechanized Division, specifically the 213th Infantry Brigade attack on Novar. Next priority is the 12th Armored Division, supporting the 122nd Brigade at Selin and the 121st Brigade fixing Eren according to corps allocation.

The group suppresses and isolates Novar, interdicts movement from Veyra, conducts counterfire, supports the Selin seizure, and prevents Blue at Eren from interfering with corps preparation. It occupies an initial position area in the Ruda–Selin eastern support area with access to both axes and alternate displacement routes.

The phase ends when Novar is seized, Selin and Ruda are secure, Eren is fixed, corps routes are open, and the group can mass fires for the Eren attack.

#### (2) Phase II — Support the Eren Attack and Veyra Isolation

**Projected Turns 6–10. Tactical mission: General support.** Priority of fires shifts to the 12th Armored Division and 121st Armored Brigade at Eren. Fires against forces detected by the 213th Brigade guard moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren support that priority. Next priority is the 21st Mechanized Division, first the 212th Brigade isolating Veyra and then the 211th Brigade establishing security at PL Push.

The group suppresses and obscures Eren defenses, isolates both approaches, conducts counterfire, and interdicts reinforcement from the Mora approaches and from the Ordan Basin when detected by the 213th guard. It also disrupts Blue movement into or out of Veyra and monitors threats to the deliberately unsecured Novar crossing as supportability permits. The group displaces by echelon into position areas associated with the Novar and Selin–Eren routes while preserving coverage of the main effort.

The phase ends when Eren is seized and trafficable, Veyra is sufficiently isolated, the 211th and 213th guards have achieved required effects, the 213th is postured to restore Novar, and Phase III reinforcing-fire coordination is complete.

#### (3) Phase III — Restore Novar and Mass Fires for Veyra

**Projected Turns 11–15. Tactical mission: General support.** The group initially uses 9th Corps artillery effects to support the 213th Infantry Brigade’s attack to seize Novar if occupied, or its reoccupation and transition to retain if vacant. Reinforcing fires from the 4th Corps Artillery Group remain prepared and prioritized for the 212th Brigade’s Veyra attack unless Eastern Army directs otherwise. Once the Novar crossing and route are confirmed—or Eastern Army accepts simultaneous-action risk—priority of 9th Corps fires shifts to the 21st Mechanized Division and 212th Mechanized Brigade attack on Veyra. The group integrates reinforcing fires from the 4th Corps Artillery Group while maintaining sufficient support for the 211th and 121st Brigade guards, Novar, Eren, and the Novar–Veyra route.

The group first suppresses and isolates any force occupying Novar and protects the 213th Brigade’s restoration of the crossing. It then isolates Veyra, suppresses defenders, obscures exposed movement, conducts counterfire, and disrupts the 6th Division, 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, or other reserves capable of attacking the 212th Brigade or its route. This headquarters coordinates the timing and effects of reinforcing fires inside the 9th Corps area but does not command the 4th Corps group. Eastern Army resolves competition between reinforcing fires and 4th Corps minimum coverage.

The phase ends when Veyra is seized, organized resistance is defeated, Novar and Eren remain secure, both guards are established at PL Punch, and the corps can assume Phase IV responsibilities.

#### (4) Phase IV — Support Guards and Retained Objectives

**Projected Turns 16–20. Tactical mission: General support.** Priority is the guard formation in contact or disengaging, then the formation defending the most threatened retained city or crossing, then the 5th Guards Armored Brigade if committed in the corps area.

The group disrupts Blue counterattack forces, supports guard delay and displacement, conducts counterfire, and protects Veyra, Novar, Eren, and the corps routes. It occupies survivable position areas able to range the central and southern portions of LOA Block while preserving an eastward displacement and ammunition route.

The phase ends at scenario completion when guards have provided required warning and delay, retained objectives remain controlled, and the group remains capable of continued support.

### c. Scheme of Fires

Within each phase priority, the target hierarchy is:

1. Blue artillery capable of disrupting the current decisive action, crossing, or guard.
2. Forces occupying Novar during its Phase III restoration, followed by Veyra defenders.
3. The 6th Compact Mechanized Division, 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, or other mobile reserve capable of counterattacking the main effort.
4. Defenders, obstacles, and positions controlling Novar or Eren approaches.
5. Forces reinforcing, withdrawing from, or counterattacking Veyra.
6. Forces threatening a corps boundary, position area, ammunition route, bridge, or guard displacement route.

The group commander recommends changes when target value, supportability, range, route status, or survivability changes. A division request does not change corps priority unless authenticated or executed under an approved emergency procedure.

### d. Movement and Survivability

- Use descriptive artillery position areas approved through the corps overlay: initial Ruda–Selin support area, Novar/Eren approach areas, secured bridgehead support areas, Veyra support area, and Phase IV retained-objective defense area.
- Reconnoiter primary and alternate routes before movement.
- Displace by echelon or under alternate control arrangements so the group never voluntarily loses all coverage.
- Do not block Ruda, Selin, Novar, Eren, Veyra, guard, casualty-evacuation, or Army-reserve routes.
- Begin wet-weather displacement early enough to preserve next-turn support.
- Displace when required targets move outside range, the current area becomes untenable, the ammunition route is threatened, or support to the next phase cannot be established in place.
- Report displacement start, expected completion, coverage retained, and any support gap.

### e. Tasks to Organic Capabilities

- **Target acquisition:** Locate Blue artillery, objective defenders, armored reserves, reinforcement routes, crossing threats, and guard threats in order to enable R-2F1 through R-2F5.
- **Fire direction and liaison:** Maintain continuous coordination with 9th Corps, both divisions, Eastern Army fires, and 4th Corps Artillery during Phase III in order to mass effects and prevent conflicting expectations.
- **Sustainment:** Maintain ammunition, fuel, maintenance, recovery, and route plans sufficient for each displacement, the Veyra massing requirement, and Phase IV guard support.
- **Local security and survivability:** Protect position areas, reduce signatures, prepare alternates, and preserve an eastward route.

These tasks do not create additional playable counters.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Priority of Fires

- Phase I: 21st Division / 213th Brigade at Novar; then 12th Division / 122nd at Selin and 121st at Eren.
- Phase II: 12th Division / 121st Brigade at Eren, including forces detected by the 213th guard moving to reinforce Eren; then 21st Division / 212th and 211th Brigades for Veyra isolation and security.
- Phase III: 21st Division / 213th Brigade restoring Novar, then 212th Brigade at Veyra; sufficient coverage for 211th and 121st guards, Novar, Eren, and the main route.
- Phase IV: Guard in contact or disengaging; threatened retain force; committed Army reserve.

#### (2) Fire-Support Triggers

- Shift to an objective attack when the supported division reports assault posture and the corps confirms priority.
- Engage a Blue reserve when it enters a route or area from which it can disrupt the current decisive action or sever a crossing route.
- Shift counterfire priority when Blue artillery affects a bridge, main effort, guard disengagement, or ammunition route.
- Shift from Novar restoration to the Veyra attack when the 213th confirms the crossing and route, or when Eastern Army authorizes simultaneous action and accepts the isolation risk.
- Accept and integrate Phase III reinforcing fires only after Eastern Army and both corps confirm the mission, target clearance, communications, and available capability.
- Shift Phase IV priority to a disengaging guard before its route becomes directly threatened.

#### (3) Reinforcing and Cross-Boundary Fires

During Phase III, the 9th Corps fires cell clears targets inside the corps area and coordinates desired timing and effects. The 4th Corps retains command of its artillery group and determines the capability available after satisfying required 4th Corps coverage. Eastern Army resolves cross-corps conflicts. This group may coordinate but may not independently reposition, redirect, or terminate the reinforcing group’s mission. No fire is delivered near uncertain friendly positions, a boundary, or protected Veyra sites without positive clearance.

#### (4) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Shift mass from Novar to Eren:** Phase I objectives achieved; Eren assault posture and routes confirmed.
- **DP 2 — Displace toward the crossings:** Novar or Selin route secure; current position cannot support Phase II or preserve survivability.
- **DP 3 — Establish Novar restoration posture:** Eren seized; Veyra sufficiently isolated; 213th prepared to restore Novar; range, route, ammunition, and clearance requirements met.
- **DP 4 — Integrate reinforcing fires:** Eastern Army activates the relationship; 4th Corps available capability and minimum coverage confirmed.
- **DP 5 — Emergency crossing or guard priority:** A crossing, guard, or artillery route faces a threat that cannot wait for routine reallocation.
- **DP 6 — End reinforcing coordination:** Veyra seized or Eastern Army terminates the requirement.
- **DP 7 — Displace for LOA Block:** Current position cannot range the guards and retain a viable route or survivability.

#### (5) CCIR

**PIR:** Blue artillery locations; Veyra defenses; 6th Division and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade locations; crossing defenses and obstacles; Veyra reinforcement routes; forces threatening guard routes, boundaries, or position areas.

**FFIR:** Group at or below 70 strength; unable to range the current priority; ammunition insufficient for the decisive task and next phase; primary and alternate routes blocked; communications lost with supported headquarters or reinforcing liaison; required coverage gap; crossing trafficability lost; or position area under imminent attack.

#### (6) Reports and Constraints

Report target acquisition, fires delivered, priority, ammunition, range, displacement, route, survivability, reinforcing status, and supportability once per turn and on significant change. Do not occupy maneuver support-by-fire positions, divide the aggregate counter, fire across a boundary without clearance, block a crossing route, or assume that a phase change automatically modifies corps priority. Protect civilians and infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support initially flows through Ruda and Selin, then through Novar and Eren as trafficability and movement control permit. Before Novar becomes unsecured in Phase II, essential ammunition and fuel are positioned west of the crossing while alternate support shifts through Eren. The group maintains emergency stocks for Novar restoration, counterfire, guard disengagement, and survival during route interruption without creating one vulnerable stockpile.

### b. Priorities

- Phase I: Novar attack ammunition; Selin and Eren support; initial displacement preparation.
- Phase II: Eren attack and 213th guard; Veyra isolation; counterfire; crossing-area displacement.
- Phase III: Novar restoration; Veyra attack and counterfire; guard and crossing coverage; Phase IV reserve stocks.
- Phase IV: Guard disengagement; threatened retained objective; counterfire; reconstitution.

### c. Transportation, Maintenance, and Recovery

Artillery displacement and ammunition movement yield to an active maneuver assault or guard withdrawal when routes conflict. Phase III movement is separated from the 212th Brigade, Army reserve, casualty evacuation, and recovery traffic on the Novar–Veyra route. Recovery priority is command/fire direction, mobility, and systems required for the current task. Disabled equipment will not block a crossing or main route. Report before movement or maintenance creates a support gap.

### d. Medical and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through the nearest secure crossing toward Ruda or Selin. Reconstitution priority restores command, communications, movement, and fires capability needed for the current main effort, then Phase IV coverage.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The artillery command post initially operates in the Ruda eastern support area and displaces by echelon toward Novar when crossing and route conditions permit. The group remains under 9th Corps control throughout. In Phase III, liaison with the 4th Corps Artillery Group coordinates reinforcing effects but does not establish command authority over that group.

Succession of command is group commander, deputy commander, then senior organic fires commander. Liaison remains with 9th Corps, the 21st and 12th Divisions, Eastern Army fires, and 4th Corps Artillery during Phase III.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use 9th Corps fires and command networks. Alternate communications use Eastern Army and 4th Corps fires networks through liaison. Contingency means use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Priority changes, crossing attacks, reinforcing-fire activation, cross-boundary clearance, displacement, support gaps, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, support the last authenticated priority, preserve the group, and avoid firing near uncertain friendly positions. Do not independently assume, modify, or terminate Phase III reinforcing arrangements. Report at the first opportunity.

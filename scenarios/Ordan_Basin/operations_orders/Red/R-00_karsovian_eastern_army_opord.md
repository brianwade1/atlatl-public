## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The Eastern Army area of interest extends from Karsovia’s western military districts through the eastern approaches to Kasar, Ruda, and Selin; across the Ordan Basin and Arven River crossing network; and west through Veyra to the Lestaran approaches. It includes the Saren Heights, Mora Hills, southeastern marshlands, all routes capable of supporting Blue reinforcement or counterattack, and the political and logistical systems that sustain the elected Ordan government.

Events outside the immediate area that may affect the operation include the arrival of additional Western Compact formations from Lestara, changes in support for the Ruda emergency council, disruption of Karsovian lines of communication, and intervention by non-playable air, cyber, or diplomatic capabilities through scenario control. No additional playable Eastern Army formation is expected during the 10-day operation unless introduced by scenario control.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Ordan Basin:** Open agricultural terrain supports rapid armored and mechanized movement, long-range observation, and massed fires. Forces moving without flank security are exposed, particularly west of the Arven.
- **Arven River:** The river is the principal operational obstacle. Heavy formations depend on the represented crossings at North Pass Bridge, Dalen, Central Link Bridge, Novar, and Eren. A seized crossing is useful only when trafficable, controlled on both approaches, and connected to a protected route.
- **North Pass Bridge and Saren Heights:** Restrictive northern terrain channels movement and can conceal Blue infantry, artillery observers, and counterattacking forces. Control of the bridge opens the northern approach west and protects the Army’s northern flank.
- **Dalen and Central Link Bridge:** Dalen is a major road junction, but the Central Link Bridge is a separate crossing south of the city. Control of Dalen alone does not control the Central Link Bridge. Both are required to create depth and lateral mobility for the northern hook.
- **Novar:** The principal central crossing and transportation junction. Control of Novar connects the northern and southern corps, protects the approach to Veyra, and supports the Army’s main-effort shift.
- **Eren and Mora Hills:** Eren controls the southern crossing and route network. The Mora Hills restrict lateral movement, offer concealed approaches, and can isolate a force that advances without route security.
- **Southeastern marshlands:** Marsh terrain restricts heavy maneuver and channels movement onto the Ruda–Novar and Selin–Eren roads. It also creates a potential western threat to the 15th Division’s flank during the attack on the Central Link Bridge.
- **Veyra:** Veyra is the national political center, a major road hub, and the decisive objective of the operation. Its seizure must be followed immediately by security, civil-control, route, and sustainment measures.
- **Road network:** The Kasar–Dalen, Ruda–Novar, and Selin–Eren routes carry maneuver, artillery, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and reserve movement. Congestion or interdiction on any one route can delay a phase transition and divide the Army.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Scenario control has not imposed a separate weather advantage. Commanders plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and the possibility of morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

Operational effects are as follows:

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce observation, delay recognition of the Blue main effort, and complicate passage and battle handover.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on roads, bridges, and traffic control, particularly near the southeastern marshlands and the river crossings.
- Artillery displacement and ammunition movement must be synchronized with crossing operations and the shift of the Army main effort from 4th Corps to 9th Corps.
- Guards must account for reduced observation by maintaining contact, overlapping observation, and preplanned displacement criteria.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and do not create an automatic advantage for either side.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

The Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps controls three divisions and a corps reserve:

- **1st Ordan Mechanized Division:** one mechanized brigade, two infantry brigades, and one artillery group. It is expected to protect Veyra, Novar, and Eren. Its Ordan formations begin below full strength.
- **3rd Compact Armored Division:** one armored brigade, two mechanized brigades, and one artillery group. It is expected to contest Dalen, North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, and the central crossing network.
- **6th Compact Mechanized Division:** one armored brigade, two mechanized brigades, and one artillery group. It is capable of passing east of the Arven and attacking toward Kasar, Ruda, and Selin.
- **7th Coalition Armored Brigade:** corps reserve, capable of reinforcing a threatened crossing or conducting a counterattack against an exposed Karsovian flank.

Blue fields 13 counters with approximately 1,210 initial strength points. It has one more artillery group than Eastern Army, but four Ordan formations begin at reduced strength. Blue’s principal advantage is proximity to Veyra and several crossings; its principal vulnerability is the requirement to defend separated objectives while preserving a mobile reserve.

#### (2) Capabilities and Limitations

Blue can rapidly reinforce the Arven crossing network; defend cities and bridge approaches in restrictive terrain; mass three artillery groups against a crossing or exposed concentration; pass the 6th Division east of the river; and commit the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade against a penetration or vulnerable flank.

Blue is constrained by separated crossing responsibilities, reduced strength in the 1st Ordan Division, congestion on west-bank routes, and the political requirement to protect Veyra and essential infrastructure. A premature eastward attack can expose a crossing; excessive concentration at Veyra can concede the river network.

#### (3) Most Likely Course of Action

Blue establishes a defense centered on Dalen, Novar, Eren, and the remaining crossings while retaining the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade near the central road network. The 3rd Division contests the northern and central crossings, the 1st Division protects Veyra and the southern crossings, and the 6th Division prepares to counterattack east once a secure passage is available. Blue artillery concentrates against the Karsovian formation threatening the most important crossing or Veyra.

#### (4) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Blue accepts risk at an outlying crossing, masses the 3rd and 6th Divisions with the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade against one corps, and conducts an early counterattack while Eastern Army formations are divided by the river. Simultaneous artillery attacks against crossing sites, reserve routes, and Karsovian artillery could isolate the forward corps and prevent the Army reserve from restoring the situation.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

Karsovian Joint Command supports the Ruda emergency council and directs Eastern Army to secure the central and western Ordan Basin, displace the elected government’s effective control, and establish a continuous protected corridor from Karsovia to the Arven crossings and Veyra.

The higher commander’s purpose is to create an irreversible political and military position before additional Western Compact forces can intervene. The desired end state is the Ruda council exercising effective authority from Veyra, the principal transportation network under Karsovian control, and Eastern Army postured to defeat a Western Compact counteroffensive.

#### (2) Ruda Council and Local Forces

Council-aligned police, militia, and civil authorities protect selected facilities, provide local information, assist traffic control, and assume civil-security duties after tactical conditions permit. They are not represented by playable counters and do not relieve Army formations of assigned retain, guard, or route-security tasks.

#### (3) Adjacent and Supporting Forces

Karsovian air, aviation, reconnaissance, engineers, air defense, electronic warfare, logistics, medical, and civil-affairs capabilities are incorporated into parent formations or represented by scenario-control adjudication. They are not separate Atlatl counters. Additional ground formations remain outside the immediate area and will not arrive during the operation unless introduced by scenario control.

### e. Civil Considerations

Veyra, Dalen, Novar, Eren, Kasar, Ruda, and Selin contain dense civilian populations, government facilities, medical sites, utilities, and transportation infrastructure. Displaced civilians may congest the same roads required for maneuver, artillery displacement, sustainment, and casualty evacuation. Competing political authorities will attempt to influence or exploit the population.

Commanders will protect bridges, rail facilities, hospitals, utilities, government records, and cultural sites consistent with mission accomplishment. The seizure of Veyra must preserve enough infrastructure for the Ruda council to function. Tactical formations will transfer civil-security responsibilities only after local authorities can assume them without exposing the force or its routes.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at operation start. Cross-attachment of brigades between corps requires Eastern Army approval. Temporary fire-support relationships may be modified by authenticated order without changing the maneuver task organization.

### g. Assumptions

- All represented Arven crossings begin trafficable. A change in bridge status requires a confirmed game effect or scenario-control ruling.
- The Ruda, Kasar, and Selin eastern routes remain available for initial sustainment and movement.
- No additional playable Karsovian formation arrives during the 20-turn operation.
- The 5th Guards Armored Brigade can reposition near Novar after the Phase I Ruda operation without losing its ability to support either corps, provided the central route remains open.
- Seizure of Veyra does not by itself end the operation; the Army must retain the city, Novar, and the crossing network while guarding along LOA Block.
- Phase timelines are planning estimates. Conditions, not turn numbers, govern transition.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **Karsovian Eastern Army attacks west through the Ordan Basin to seize the North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, Novar, Eren, and Veyra; retains Veyra and the crossing network; and establishes guards along LOA Block in order to control the basin’s political and transportation centers, preserve operational mobility across the Arven River, and prevent a Western Compact–Ordan counterattack from restoring the elected government’s control.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Create a defensible political and military position in the Ordan Basin before Blue can concentrate its superior artillery and mobile reserve against the Army’s separated axes.

#### Key Tasks

- Secure Kasar, Ruda, and Selin and preserve continuous east–west lines of communication.
- Seize Dalen and Novar to control the principal road junctions.
- Seize and maintain trafficability of North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, and Eren crossing.
- Use the northern hook to gain crossing depth and prevent Blue from concentrating against a single frontal approach.
- Shift the Army main effort from 4th Corps to 9th Corps without interrupting fires, sustainment, or route control.
- Attack to seize Veyra and defeat organized resistance in and around the city.
- Establish guards along LOA Block to detect and delay counterattacks while retaining Veyra, Novar, Dalen, and the crossings.
- Preserve the 5th Guards Armored Brigade as the Army’s mobile reserve after its initial Ruda task.
- Protect essential infrastructure and rapidly transfer civil functions to council-aligned authorities.

#### End State

- Veyra is seized and retained, with organized Blue resistance in the city defeated.
- Novar, Dalen, North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, and Eren remain controlled and trafficable.
- 4th and 9th Corps maintain continuous protected routes to the eastern map edge.
- Guards along LOA Block provide warning and delay without becoming decisively engaged beyond their ability to disengage.
- The 5th Guards Armored Brigade remains capable of counterattacking the most dangerous Blue penetration.
- Eastern Army remains coherent, supportable, and postured for a follow-on defense or renewed offensive.

### b. Concept of Operations

The operation consists of four condition-based phases. Projected turn windows support planning but do not authorize transition by time alone.

#### (1) Phase I — Secure the Eastern Basin and Initial Arven Objectives

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The Eastern Army attacks on three axes to secure the eastern support base, seize Dalen and Novar, and fix Blue at North Pass Bridge and Eren. The 4th Maneuver Corps is the Army main effort. Its 15th Mechanized Division attacks to seize Dalen while the 8th Guards Armored Division seizes Kasar and fixes the North Pass Bridge defense. The 9th Maneuver Corps secures Selin, seizes Novar, and fixes Blue at Eren. The 5th Guards Armored Brigade attacks to secure Ruda, then repositions near Novar and resumes its role as Eastern Army reserve.

This phase ends when Dalen and Novar are seized; Kasar, Ruda, and Selin are secured; Blue forces at North Pass Bridge and Eren are fixed; and both corps are postured to attack the remaining crossings.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize the Remaining Arven Crossings

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The 4th Maneuver Corps remains the Army main effort. The 15th Mechanized Division conducts the northern hook from Dalen to seize Central Link Bridge while securing Dalen and blocking interference from the Saren Heights. The 8th Guards Armored Division attacks to seize North Pass Bridge. The 9th Maneuver Corps attacks to seize Eren while the 21st Mechanized Division isolates Veyra and establishes intersecting guards: the 211th at PL Push and the 213th from PL Push east to the Arven River along the corps boundary. The 213th finds and delays forces moving from the Ordan Basin to reinforce Eren. This deliberately leaves Novar without a retain force during Phase II; 9th Corps protects essential movement through the area by observation, route control, and fires while preparing to restore control in Phase III. Corps artillery groups occupy position areas and provide fires against crossing defenses, enemy artillery, and counterattack routes.

This phase ends when North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, and Eren are seized and trafficable; the crossing approaches and connecting routes are controlled; and 9th Corps is postured to attack Veyra.

#### (3) Phase III — Restore Novar and Seize Veyra

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The Army main effort shifts to 9th Maneuver Corps. As an enabling action, the 213th Infantry Brigade attacks to seize Novar if Blue occupies it, or reoccupies it if vacant, confirms the crossing and main route, and transitions to retain the city. Once the Novar route is restored—or on specific Army authority accepting the isolation risk—the 21st Mechanized Division attacks to seize Veyra with the 212th Mechanized Brigade as its main effort. The 211th Mechanized Brigade advances from PL Push to PL Punch, gains contact, develops the situation, and establishes a guard protecting the attack. The 12th Armored Division advances to PL Punch and establishes southern flank security while retaining Eren. Within the 8th Guards Armored Division, the 82nd Brigade conducts movement to contact to PL Punch and establishes the northern guard while the 81st retains North Pass Bridge. The 15th Division establishes the central guard. The 4th Corps Artillery Group reinforces 9th Corps Artillery, with priority to the 21st Division.

This phase ends when Veyra is seized; organized resistance in and around the city is defeated; Novar is restored and Novar and Eren are secure; and the 8th, 15th, and 12th Divisions have established security at PL Punch.

#### (4) Phase IV — Consolidate, Retain, and Guard

**Projected Turns 16–20.** The 9th Maneuver Corps remains the Army main effort for consolidation of Veyra and Novar. Both corps establish guards along LOA Block, retain the seized cities and Arven crossings, restore routes, and prepare to defeat a Blue counterattack. Guard forces maintain contact, identify the enemy main effort, delay as required, and displace before they become decisively engaged beyond the commander’s intent. The 5th Guards Armored Brigade remains the only Eastern Army mobile reserve and is not committed to routine terrain-holding tasks.

The operation ends when Veyra and the crossing network remain under Eastern Army control through scenario completion, Blue counterattack forces have been detected and delayed west of LOA Block, and the Army retains a mobile response capability.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The 4th Maneuver Corps operates on the northern and central-northern axes. In Phases I and II, it creates the northern hook by securing Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge. In Phase III, it advances to PL Punch and guards the northern approaches to isolate the Veyra fight. In Phase IV, it retains the northern crossing network and guards the northern portion of LOA Block.

The 9th Maneuver Corps operates on the central-southern axes. In Phase I, it secures Selin and Novar while fixing Blue at Eren. In Phase II, it seizes Eren, isolates Veyra, and accepts the temporary Novar security gap created by the 213th Brigade boundary guard. In Phase III, it becomes the Army main effort, restores Novar, and attacks to seize Veyra. In Phase IV, it retains Veyra, Novar, and Eren and guards the central and southern portions of LOA Block.

The 5th Guards Armored Brigade secures Ruda in Phase I for the purpose of protecting the Army’s central lateral route and preventing interference with corps staging. Once Ruda is secured and local security is transferred, the brigade repositions near Novar, reports readiness each turn, and remains uncommitted unless released by the Eastern Army commander.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The Army masses fires at the point of the current main effort while retaining counterfire and crossing-interdiction capability. Artillery groups occupy position areas rather than support-by-fire positions and displace by echelon to maintain continuous coverage.

- **Phase I:** Priority of Army-coordinated fires is 4th Corps, then 9th Corps, then the 5th Guards Armored Brigade. Within 4th Corps, priority is the 15th Division attack on Dalen, then the 8th Division at Kasar and North Pass. Within 9th Corps, priority is the 21st Division at Novar, then the 12th Division at Eren and Selin.
- **Phase II:** Priority remains 4th Corps. Within 4th Corps, priority is 15th Division at Central Link Bridge, then 8th Division at North Pass Bridge. Within 9th Corps, priority is 12th Division at Eren, then 21st Division security operations.
- **Phase III:** Priority shifts to 9th Corps. The 9th Corps Artillery Group first supports the 213th Brigade’s restoration of Novar, then masses fires for the 21st Division and 212th Brigade attack on Veyra. The 4th Corps Artillery Group provides general support to 4th Corps and reinforces 9th Corps Artillery, with priority of reinforcing fires to the 212th Brigade at Veyra until the city is seized. Cross-boundary fires require positive clearance through the Army fires cell.
- **Phase IV:** Each artillery group resumes primary general support to its parent corps. Priority is to the guard formation in contact or disengaging, followed by the formation retaining the threatened city or crossing and then the Army reserve if committed.

Target priorities are Blue artillery; forces defending or counterattacking a crossing; armored and mechanized concentrations; reserves on the Veyra–crossing road network; forces threatening guard displacement routes; and command, sustainment, and movement-control nodes represented or adjudicated in the scenario. Exact effects follow Atlatl and scenario-control rules.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 4th Maneuver Corps

**Task:** Attack through the northern Ordan Basin to seize Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; advance to PL Punch; then guard along the northern portion of LOA Block while retaining the northern crossing network.

**Purpose:** Create the northern hook, secure operational mobility across the Arven, and prevent Blue forces north of Veyra from interfering with 9th Corps’ decisive operation.

- Phase I: Serve as Army main effort; seize Dalen and Kasar and fix Blue at North Pass Bridge.
- Phase II: Remain Army main effort; seize North Pass Bridge and Central Link Bridge and protect their connecting routes.
- Phase III: Advance to PL Punch; establish northern and central guards; retain the crossings; support 9th Corps with reinforcing fires.
- Phase IV: Retain Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; guard the northern portion of LOA Block; prepare to receive or counter a Blue attack.

#### (2) 9th Maneuver Corps

**Task:** Secure the central-southern basin; seize Novar and Eren; establish the Phase II boundary guard while accepting a temporary Novar security gap; restore Novar; isolate and attack to seize Veyra; then retain Veyra, Novar, and Eren while guarding along the central and southern portions of LOA Block.

**Purpose:** Capture Orda’s political center, secure the Army’s central and southern mobility corridors, and prevent Blue from restoring control of the basin.

- Phase I: Support 4th Corps by securing Selin and Novar and fixing Blue at Eren.
- Phase II: Seize Eren, isolate Veyra, establish the corps-boundary guard, and accept the temporary Novar security gap.
- Phase III: Become Army main effort; restore and retain Novar, then attack to seize Veyra while securing Eren and the corps flanks.
- Phase IV: Retain Veyra, Novar, and Eren; guard along LOA Block; protect the Army’s southern flank.

#### (3) 5th Guards Armored Brigade — Army Reserve

**Task:** In Phase I, attack to secure Ruda, transfer local security, and reposition near Novar; thereafter remain prepared to counterattack a Blue penetration, reinforce either corps, or exploit a decisive opportunity.

**Purpose:** Secure the Army’s central lateral route and preserve a mobile armored force capable of restoring the operation at the decisive point.

The brigade will not be committed piecemeal or assigned a routine retain mission. Its priority is 4th Corps during Phases I–II, 9th Corps during Phase III, and the most dangerous threat to Veyra or the crossing network during Phase IV.

#### (4) 4th Corps Artillery Group

**Task:** Provide general support to 4th Corps; in Phase III reinforce 9th Corps Artillery with priority to 21st Division; resume primary support to 4th Corps for the Phase IV guard and crossing defense.

**Purpose:** Enable seizure of the northern and central crossings, isolate the Veyra fight, and preserve the northern guard and crossing network.

#### (5) 9th Corps Artillery Group

**Task:** Provide general support to 9th Corps, mass fires for the seizure of Eren, restoration of Novar, and seizure of Veyra, coordinate reinforcing fires from 4th Corps Artillery, and support the Phase IV guards and retained objectives.

**Purpose:** Enable 9th Corps to seize the decisive political objective and defeat or delay Blue forces attempting to restore the central basin.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Phase Transition Authority

Only the Eastern Army commander authorizes transition between phases. A corps may execute local preparations for the next phase but will not begin a new major objective fight before the Army confirms the transition.

#### (2) Main Effort

- Phases I–II: 4th Maneuver Corps.
- Phases III–IV: 9th Maneuver Corps.

The shift occurs only after Phase II conditions are met and the Army confirms that 9th Corps can sustain the Veyra attack while 4th Corps protects the northern flank and crossing network.

#### (3) Reserve Commitment Criteria

The Eastern Army commander may commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade when one or more of the following conditions exist:

- A Blue penetration threatens Veyra, Novar, an Arven crossing, or an Eastern Army line of communication.
- A corps cannot accomplish its essential phase task with organic forces and fires.
- Blue commits the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade and its location and likely purpose are sufficiently confirmed.
- A fleeting opportunity exists to destroy a separated Blue formation or secure a decisive objective, and doing so will not expose Veyra or the crossing network to a more dangerous threat.

Every commitment order will state the brigade’s task, purpose, supported headquarters, route, fire-support priority, limit of advance, sustainment plan, and termination or reconstitution criteria.

#### (4) Guard Instructions

Guard forces maintain contact with the enemy main body, report its composition and axis, and delay it sufficiently to protect retained terrain and permit reserve commitment. Each corps establishes primary and alternate displacement routes, disengagement criteria, battle-handover locations, and responsibilities at unit boundaries. Guards will not remain forward merely to hold nonessential ground.

#### (5) Fire-Support Coordination

During Phase III, 9th Corps controls clearance within its area of operations. The Army fires cell coordinates cross-boundary targets, changes to corps boundaries, reinforcing fires, and restrictive measures around Veyra. Positive identification and clearance are required before firing near friendly guards, urban objectives, or uncertain unit boundaries.

#### (6) Rules and Constraints

- Preserve bridges and transportation infrastructure unless destruction is specifically authorized by Eastern Army.
- Do not assume control of a crossing until both approaches are secured and trafficability is confirmed.
- Do not commit the Army reserve piecemeal or use it to hold routine terrain.
- Do not allow all formations of a division to become decisively engaged without a corps-approved risk decision.
- Do not pursue west of LOA Block without Eastern Army approval.
- Protect civilians and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.
- Capabilities not represented by counters require scenario-control adjudication.

#### (7) Commander’s Critical Information Requirements

**Priority intelligence requirements**

- Location, strength, and movement of the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade.
- Location and main effort of the 3rd and 6th Compact Divisions.
- Blue artillery concentrations and displacement affecting a crossing or Veyra.
- Crossing status, obstacles, or prepared demolition at North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, Novar, and Eren.
- Indicators of a Blue counterattack against a corps seam, exposed flank, Veyra, or the central route.
- Additional Western Compact forces entering from Lestara.

**Friendly-force information requirements**

- Any main-effort formation at or below 70 strength or unable to complete its essential task.
- Loss, blockage, or interdiction of a required crossing or east–west support route.
- A corps or division without an effective reserve or ability to disengage.
- The 5th Guards Armored Brigade unable to reach either corps within the required time.
- A guard force at risk of becoming decisively engaged or losing its displacement route.
- Artillery unable to support the current main effort or expected phase transition.
- Civilian congestion or infrastructure damage that prevents required movement or sustainment.

#### (8) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — End Ruda operation:** Ruda secured; local security transferable; route to Novar open. Reposition the 5th Guards Armored Brigade and restore Army reserve status.
- **DP 2 — Begin Phase II:** Dalen and Novar seized; Kasar, Ruda, and Selin secured; North Pass Bridge and Eren defenders fixed; both corps postured for crossing attacks.
- **DP 3 — Commit Army reserve:** A decisive threat or opportunity meets the reserve criteria; route, fires, sustainment, and risk elsewhere are understood.
- **DP 4 — Begin Phase III / shift main effort:** North Pass Bridge, Central Link Bridge, and Eren seized and trafficable; 9th Corps postured for Veyra; 4th Corps able to protect the northern flank.
- **DP 5 — Begin Phase IV:** Veyra seized; organized resistance defeated; Novar and Eren secure; 8th, 15th, and 12th Divisions established at PL Punch.
- **DP 6 — Disengage a guard:** Required warning or delay achieved, or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal and preservation of the force.

#### (9) Required Reports

- Contact report after first engagement and upon identification of a new Blue formation.
- Strength and mission-capability report at the end of each turn and immediately upon crossing an FFIR threshold.
- Objective, city, crossing, route, guard, and artillery status whenever control, trafficability, supportability, or freedom to disengage changes.
- Phase-transition recommendation when a corps commander assesses conditions are met.
- Army reserve readiness, location, primary and alternate routes, travel time, liaison, and support status each turn.
- Guard contact, delay achieved, displacement-route status, disengagement readiness, and battle-handover reports during Phase IV.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Sustainment flows from Karsovia through the Kasar, Ruda, and Selin entry corridors and then west across the seized Arven crossings. The Army maintains more than one usable east–west line of communication and avoids placing all artillery ammunition, recovery, medical evacuation, or reserve movement on the central route.

Support priorities follow the Army main effort while preserving minimum support to crossing security, route control, and the Army reserve. Logistics nodes displace by echelon and remain east of the most recently secured phase line until routes and local security permit forward movement.

### b. Priorities of Support

- **Phase I:** 4th Corps and 15th Division; 9th Corps and the Novar effort; 5th Guards Armored Brigade at Ruda; remaining formations.
- **Phase II:** 4th Corps, with priority to 15th Division and then 8th Division; 9th Corps at Eren; Army reserve readiness.
- **Phase III:** 9th Corps and 21st Division; 4th Corps guard and reinforcing fires; 12th Division flank security; Army reserve.
- **Phase IV:** Guard formation in contact or disengaging; formation retaining the threatened city or crossing; Army reserve; reconstitution and route restoration.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and artillery ammunition are pacing requirements. Main-effort formations receive first priority, but each corps preserves emergency stocks for the next phase, guard disengagement, and a reserve commitment. Movement-control plans separate maneuver, artillery, sustainment, recovery, and casualty-evacuation traffic at crossings and urban junctions.

During Phase III, priority of movement on central routes is the 213th Brigade’s restoration of Novar, followed by the 21st Division attack, 9th Corps Artillery, supporting sustainment, and the 5th Guards Armored Brigade if committed. During Phase IV, priority is guard displacement, artillery echeloning, casualty evacuation, reserve movement, and restoration of damaged routes.

### d. Maintenance and Recovery

Recovery priority follows the current main effort, then the next phase main effort, then the Army reserve. Disabled equipment will not block a bridge, city junction, reserve route, or guard displacement route. Equipment that cannot be recovered without unacceptable risk is handled under scenario-control procedures.

### e. Medical and Casualty Evacuation

Casualty evacuation moves east through the nearest controlled crossing and the Kasar, Ruda, or Selin corridor. Alternate routes are designated before each phase. During Phase IV, evacuation is synchronized with guard displacement so medical movement does not obstruct withdrawing combat formations or artillery.

### f. Personnel, Detainees, and Civilians

Council-aligned authorities assume detainee and civil-security responsibilities when conditions permit. Corps report population movement that threatens route capacity or exposes civilians to combat. Treatment of detainees and civilians follows the fictional scenario’s policy framework and scenario-control instructions.

### g. Reconstitution

Reconstitution priority is the 5th Guards Armored Brigade if committed, followed by the main-effort corps and formations essential to crossing retention. Units unable to perform their essential task report immediately and receive a revised task, relief, or consolidation order rather than remaining nominally responsible for decisive terrain.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The Eastern Army Main Command Post initially operates in the Karsovian forward support area and displaces west by echelon when route security and communications permit. The tactical command post follows the current main effort. Command-post movement will not compete with main-effort maneuver, artillery displacement, or reserve movement at a crossing.

Succession of command is Eastern Army commander, deputy commander, 4th Maneuver Corps commander, then 9th Maneuver Corps commander. The 4th Corps is the supported corps in Phases I–II; the 9th Corps is the supported corps in Phases III–IV. Liaison teams remain at both corps, both artillery groups, and the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use Eastern Army command and fires networks. Alternate communications use corps networks and liaison. Contingency communications use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Main-effort shifts, phase transitions, crossing status, Veyra status, reserve commitment, cross-boundary fires, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, subordinate headquarters continue the last confirmed phase and task. A corps may take immediate action to preserve a crossing, protect Veyra, or prevent destruction of a formation, but will not initiate a new phase, commit the Army reserve, or advance beyond LOA Block without authenticated Army authority unless delay would result in destruction. Report at the first opportunity.

The division exercises operational control over all three brigades. ## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from the Kasar–Dalen eastern approach west through Dalen, Central Link Bridge, PL Push, PL Punch, and the central-northern portion of LOA Block. It includes the Saren Heights, western wooded approaches, the southeastern marshlands insofar as they threaten the division flank, the boundary with 8th Guards Armored Division, and the corps boundary with 9th Maneuver Corps.

The division monitors Blue forces defending Dalen and Central Link Bridge, forces moving from the Saren Heights, counterattack forces moving from Novar or Veyra, Blue artillery covering the crossings, and any penetration that threatens the corps boundary or Dalen line of communication.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Dalen:** Major northern junction and initial decisive objective. It controls routes toward North Pass and Central Link but is not itself the Central Link crossing.
- **Central Link Bridge:** A stand-alone crossing south of Dalen, separated from both Dalen and Novar by water terrain. It requires a separate attack, approach-security plan, and retain force.
- **Saren Heights:** Rough terrain north of the division axis can conceal forces able to strike the 151st Brigade’s flank or interfere with Dalen.
- **Western woods:** Conceal movement toward PL Punch but reduce observation and complicate movement to contact and guard establishment.
- **Southeastern marshlands:** Restrict heavy movement and may channel a Blue force west of the division axis toward the corps boundary. The 153rd Brigade must be prepared to reorient if this threat materializes.
- **Division and corps seams:** The northern seam connects to the 8th Division at North Pass; the southern seam connects to 9th Corps near Novar and the Veyra approach. Loss of either seam can isolate the bridgehead.
- **Road network:** The Dalen and Central Link routes support maneuver, artillery, sustainment, and casualty evacuation. Traffic control must preserve space for the 151st Brigade attack and later guard displacement.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may conceal Blue forces in the heights or woods and delay recognition of counterattack.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on roads and bridges and can slow infantry repositioning and recovery.
- Limited visibility requires positive identification at Dalen, the Central Link approaches, and the corps boundary.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The division expects elements of the 3rd Compact Armored Division and 1st Ordan Mechanized Division to contest Dalen, Central Link Bridge, and the approaches from Novar or Veyra. Blue may reinforce with the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade or elements of the 6th Compact Mechanized Division.

Blue can defend Dalen and the bridge as separate positions, mass artillery on the crossing approaches, use the Saren Heights or western woods for flank attacks, and counterattack the 151st Brigade while the 152nd and 153rd are securing separate terrain.

Blue’s most likely action is to delay at Dalen, defend Central Link in depth, and withdraw toward PL Punch while preserving a counterattack force. Its most dangerous action is to fix the 151st at the bridge, attack Dalen from the north or west, and sever the division route while artillery prevents the 152nd or 153rd from restoring the situation.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge, advances to PL Punch, and guards the northern and central portions of LOA Block.
- **8th Guards Armored Division:** Secures Kasar, fixes and seizes North Pass Bridge, and guards the corps northern flank.
- **9th Maneuver Corps:** Operates south of the corps boundary, seizes Novar and Eren, accepts a temporary Phase II Novar security gap while the 213th Brigade guards the corps boundary, restores Novar in Phase III, and attacks Veyra.
- **4th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to the corps, with priority to this division during Phases I–II. During Phase III it also reinforces the 9th Corps Artillery Group.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve after securing Ruda. It is not under division or corps control.

### e. Civil Considerations

Dalen contains civilians, government facilities, medical sites, utilities, and transportation infrastructure. The Central Link Bridge is critical to both civilian movement and military mobility. Preserve the bridge and essential facilities consistent with mission accomplishment. Displaced civilians may congest the Dalen junction and bridge approaches; coordinate traffic before the Phase II attack and Phase III movement.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Corps fires support does not change the task organization.

### g. Assumptions

- Dalen and Central Link Bridge begin accessible and the bridge begins trafficable.
- The 8th Division secures Kasar and fixes the North Pass defense during Phase I.
- In Phase II, the 213th Brigade guards the 9th Corps side of the boundary from PL Push to the Arven River; Novar is not continuously defended.
- Corps allocates sufficient fires for the Dalen and Central Link attacks.
- Phase timelines are estimates; corps and Eastern Army authorize transitions.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **15th Mechanized Division attacks to seize and retain Dalen and Central Link Bridge, advances to PL Punch, and subsequently guards along the central-northern portion of LOA Block while securing the corps boundary in order to create the Eastern Army’s northern hook, protect 9th Corps’ attack on Veyra, and preserve the 4th Corps central crossing and line of communication.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Turn Blue’s central-northern defense by using Dalen as the base for a separate attack on Central Link Bridge, then protect the resulting bridgehead and the Veyra operation.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize Dalen rapidly and secure the junction.
- Separate Dalen security from the Central Link attack.
- Follow and support the 151st Brigade without duplicating brigade tasks.
- Block interference from the Central Link approaches during Phase I.
- Block threats from the Saren Heights and protect the 151st Brigade flank during Phase II.
- Be prepared to reorient the block west against a threat emerging from the marshlands.
- Seize Central Link Bridge with both approaches controlled and trafficability confirmed.
- Advance to PL Punch while retaining Dalen, the crossing, and division routes.
- Establish a central guard that protects the 9th Corps seam and Veyra attack.
- Preserve freedom to disengage and report early when corps assistance is required.

#### End State

- Dalen and Central Link Bridge are controlled, trafficable, and connected to the eastern support route.
- The division has established security at PL Punch and a guard along central LOA Block.
- The northern and southern seams remain protected.
- Blue forces cannot interfere with the Veyra operation through the division sector.
- All three brigades remain capable of retaining assigned terrain or continuing operations.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Seize Dalen

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The 151st Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort and attacks to seize Dalen. The 152nd Mechanized Brigade follows and supports, defeats or contains threats bypassed by the 151st, and then blocks approaches from Central Link Bridge to prevent Blue interference with Dalen. The 153rd Infantry Brigade follows the main effort, secures Dalen after seizure, and prepares to retain the city and junction.

The division does not treat Dalen’s seizure as control of Central Link Bridge. The phase ends on corps order when Dalen is secured, the eastern route is open, Blue interference from the Central Link direction is blocked, the 153rd is prepared to retain Dalen, and the division is postured for Phase II.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize Central Link Bridge

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The 151st Brigade remains the division main effort and attacks from Dalen to seize Central Link Bridge. The 152nd follows and supports the attack, secures the bridge approaches, and prepares to retain the crossing. The 153rd blocks oriented north to prevent Blue forces moving out of the Saren Heights and guards the 151st Brigade’s flank. It is prepared to reorient the block west to prevent interference from forces emerging from the marshlands.

The phase ends on corps order when both bridge approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, movement control is established, Dalen remains secure, the seams are protected, and the division is postured to move to PL Punch.

#### (3) Phase III — Advance to PL Punch and Establish the Central Guard

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The 151st Brigade conducts movement to contact toward PL Punch as the division main effort and establishes the central guard protecting the northern side of 9th Corps’ Veyra attack. The 152nd Brigade secures Central Link Bridge, follows and supports as required, and prepares to retain the crossing. The 153rd Brigade retains Dalen and protects the division line of communication. The division advances only as fast as it can preserve the crossing, Dalen, and the corps boundary.

The phase ends on corps order when the division is established at PL Punch, Veyra is seized and organized resistance defeated, Dalen and Central Link remain secure, and Phase IV guard and retain responsibilities are ready.

#### (4) Phase IV — Guard LOA Block and Retain Dalen and Central Link

**Projected Turns 16–18.** The 151st Brigade remains the division main effort and guards along the central portion of LOA Block. It maintains contact, identifies the Blue main body, delays as required, and displaces before becoming decisively engaged. The 152nd Brigade retains Central Link Bridge and secures both approaches. The 153rd Brigade retains Dalen and the route connecting Dalen, Central Link, and the eastern support area.

The division end state is achieved when the guard provides required warning and delay, Dalen and Central Link remain controlled, the corps boundary remains secure, and the division preserves freedom for follow-on operations.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The division sequences its combat power rather than attacking Dalen and Central Link as one continuous objective. The 151st Brigade leads both attacks. In Phase I, the 152nd protects the main effort from the bridge direction while the 153rd assumes Dalen security. This releases the 151st to continue the hook.

In Phase II, the 153rd’s primary orientation is north toward the Saren Heights to protect the 151st flank. Its westward reorientation against a marshlands threat is a be-prepared-to task, not a simultaneous second block. The division commander orders the reorientation only after confirming that the northern threat is contained or accepted.

In Phase III, the 151st advances to the guard line while the 152nd and 153rd preserve the bridge and Dalen. In Phase IV, the division deliberately separates guard and retain responsibilities. None of the retain forces is described as the division reserve.

The division has no dedicated reserve during the crossing fights. The 152nd’s follow-and-support role provides limited flexibility but is not an uncommitted reserve. Report to corps before all three brigades become decisively engaged or before either seam loses protection.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The division receives priority from the 4th Corps Artillery Group during Phases I–II.

- **Phase I:** Priority to the 151st Brigade at Dalen, then the 152nd Brigade’s blocking task. Suppress defenders, isolate Dalen, interdict movement from Central Link, and conduct counterfire.
- **Phase II:** Priority to the 151st Brigade at Central Link, then the most dangerous force threatening the 153rd block or Dalen. Suppress and obscure bridge defenses, isolate the western approach, and interdict Blue reserves.
- **Phase III:** Support the 151st Brigade’s movement to contact and guard establishment while maintaining defensive fires for Dalen and Central Link. Corps artillery also reinforces 9th Corps; report support limitations immediately.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the 151st Brigade when in contact or disengaging, then the 152nd or 153rd Brigade at the threatened retained objective.

The division nominates targets and coordinates observation but does not redirect the corps artillery group outside authenticated corps priorities. Cross-boundary fires require clearance.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 151st Mechanized Brigade — Division Main Effort

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to seize Dalen. **Purpose:** Secure the junction and establish the base for the northern hook.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack from Dalen to seize Central Link Bridge and its western approach. **Purpose:** Open the division’s Arven crossing and create depth for 4th Corps.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Punch and establish a central guard. **Purpose:** Protect the northern side of 9th Corps’ Veyra attack and develop the Blue counterattack.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Guard along the central portion of LOA Block; maintain contact, delay, and displace within division criteria. **Purpose:** Provide warning and time while protecting Dalen, Central Link, and the corps boundary.

#### (2) 152nd Mechanized Brigade — Follow-and-Support and Crossing Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Follow and support the 151st Brigade, then block approaches from Central Link Bridge. **Purpose:** Preserve momentum at Dalen and prevent interference from the crossing direction.
- **Phase II — Task:** Follow and support the Central Link attack; secure both bridge approaches and prepare to retain the crossing. **Purpose:** Prevent isolation of the main effort and preserve bridge trafficability.
- **Phase III — Task:** Secure Central Link Bridge and the division route; follow and support the forward guard as directed. **Purpose:** Sustain the advance without uncovering the crossing.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain Central Link Bridge and secure its approaches. **Purpose:** Preserve the corps central crossing and support the guard’s displacement.

#### (3) 153rd Infantry Brigade — Dalen Security and Blocking Force

- **Phase I — Task:** Secure Dalen after seizure and prepare to retain the city and junction. **Purpose:** Release the 151st Brigade for the Central Link attack and protect the division line of communication.
- **Phase II — Task:** Block oriented north against forces moving from the Saren Heights and guard the 151st Brigade flank; be prepared to reorient west against forces emerging from the marshlands. **Purpose:** Prevent Blue interference with the Central Link attack.
- **Phase III — Task:** Retain Dalen and secure the route to Central Link. **Purpose:** Protect the division rear and maintain support to the forward guard.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain Dalen and secure the connecting route. **Purpose:** Preserve the division support base and crossing network.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Main Effort

The 151st Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort in every phase. Priority of fires, sustainment, movement, and recovery follows the 151st unless the division commander temporarily shifts priority to prevent loss of Dalen, Central Link, a seam, or a disengaging guard.

#### (2) Follow and Support

The 152nd maintains enough distance to avoid simultaneous engagement with the 151st but closes rapidly enough to defeat bypassed threats, secure routes, or assume the crossing approaches. Follow and support does not authorize independent pursuit or abandonment of Central Link security.

#### (3) Block Orientation

During Phase II, the 153rd’s primary block is oriented north. Reorientation west is executed only on division order or under an authenticated contingency trigger. Before reorienting, confirm responsibility for the Saren threat and the 151st northern flank.

The division coordinates observation, contact reporting, boundary fires, and handover with the 213th Brigade guard operating on the 9th Corps side of the boundary from PL Push to the Arven River. Report immediately if the 213th cannot maintain the boundary guard or if Blue movement through the unsecured Novar area threatens Central Link, Dalen, or the corps seam.

#### (4) Crossing and Objective Control

Dalen and Central Link are separate objectives. Central Link is not seized until both approaches are secure, trafficability is confirmed, and movement control is established. The 151st controls the objective until the division confirms handover to the 152nd.

#### (5) Guard and Disengagement

The 151st establishes observation, engagement criteria, primary and alternate displacement routes, and a rearward handover with the 152nd and 153rd. Disengage when directed, when required delay is achieved, or when continued contact would prevent orderly withdrawal under preapproved criteria.

#### (6) Reserve and Risk

The division has no dedicated reserve in Phases I–II. The 152nd’s follow-and-support posture supplies limited response capacity only while it remains uncommitted. Request corps assistance before all three brigades become decisively engaged, a seam is uncovered, or the division risks losing both Dalen and Central Link. The division cannot commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.

#### (7) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Handover Dalen security:** 151st controls the city; 153rd can secure the objective and route; 152nd positioned against Central Link approaches.
- **DP 2 — Recommend Phase II:** Dalen secure; Central Link defense developed; brigades, fires, and routes ready.
- **DP 3 — Reorient 153rd:** A western threat becomes more dangerous than the Saren threat and northern flank responsibility is confirmed.
- **DP 4 — Complete Central Link seizure:** Both approaches secure; bridge trafficable; movement control established; counterattack contained.
- **DP 5 — Recommend Phase III:** Dalen/Central Link secure; routes and seams protected; division capable of PL Punch movement.
- **DP 6 — Establish Phase IV posture:** Veyra seized; 151st guard ready; 152nd and 153rd able to retain objectives.
- **DP 7 — Disengage guard:** Required delay achieved or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal.

#### (8) CCIR

**PIR:** Defenses at Dalen and Central Link; Blue artillery; movement from Saren Heights, the unsecured Novar area, Veyra, western woods, or marshlands; 7th Coalition Armored Brigade; bridge status; threats to either seam.

**FFIR:** Any brigade at or below 70 strength; Dalen or Central Link route blocked; bridge unusable; 151st isolated; 153rd unable to protect the required orientation; division seam uncovered; corps fires unavailable; guard unable to disengage; or corps assistance required.

#### (9) Reports and Constraints

Report contact, strength, objective, bridge, block orientation, route, seam, guard, fires, sustainment, and phase readiness once per turn and on significant change. Do not destroy Central Link Bridge, cross LOA Block, or begin a new phase without authenticated authority. Protect civilians and critical infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows along the eastern route through Dalen and then across Central Link Bridge. The division establishes Dalen as the initial support pivot without congesting the junction. Supplies for the Phase II attack move forward only after approach security is confirmed. After crossing, distribute support so loss of the bridge approach does not immediately isolate the guard.

### b. Priorities of Support

- Phase I: 151st Brigade; 152nd follow-and-support force; 153rd Dalen security.
- Phase II: 151st Brigade; 152nd bridge approaches; 153rd block; crossing repair and recovery.
- Phase III: 151st forward guard; 152nd Central Link security; 153rd Dalen route.
- Phase IV: 151st guard in contact/disengaging; threatened retain brigade; reconstitution.

### c. Supply, Maintenance, and Transportation

Fuel, ammunition, recovery, and bridge-route capacity are pacing. Keep the Dalen junction and Central Link approaches clear. Separate maneuver, artillery, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and guard-displacement traffic. Begin wet-weather movement early enough to avoid losing support to the next turn when practicable.

### d. Medical and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through Dalen; establish an alternate collection and transfer plan before crossing. Reconstitution priority follows the 151st Brigade, then the brigade essential to crossing or Dalen retention. A brigade unable to perform its essential task reports for mission adjustment or relief.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The division command post initially operates east of Dalen and displaces into the Dalen area after the city and route are secure. The tactical command post follows the 151st Brigade while maintaining communications with the 153rd at Dalen and the 152nd at Central Link. Command-post movement will not compete with the main-effort or bridge approach traffic.

Succession of command is division commander, deputy division commander, 151st Brigade commander, 152nd Brigade commander, then 153rd Brigade commander. Maintain liaison with 4th Corps, 8th Division, 9th Corps boundary elements, and the 4th Corps Artillery Group.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use division and corps command networks. Alternate means use brigade networks and liaison; contingency means use relay, schedules, and messenger. Objective handover, block reorientation, bridge status, corps boundary, artillery priority, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed task, retain Dalen and Central Link, preserve seams, and maintain guard freedom to disengage. Do not reorient the 153rd block, begin a new phase, or move beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless required to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

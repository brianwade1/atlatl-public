# 8th Guards Armored Division

> **FOR WARGAMING AND EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY — ENTIRELY FICTIONAL**

- **Issuing headquarters:** 8th Guards Armored Division
- **Place of issue:** Division Main Command Post, Kasar eastern approach
- **Date-time group:** 12 September 2030, 0600 local
- **Time zone used throughout:** Local Ordan time
- **Effective:** On receipt
- **Scenario duration:** 20 turns / 10 days; 12 hours per turn
- **Parent order:** 4th Maneuver Corps OPORD R-10, OPERATION NORTHERN REACH
- **Role:** Corps supporting effort; northern crossing and flank force
- **References:** R-00; R-10; `ordan_basin_scenario.md`; `order_of_battle.md`; Ordan Basin Area of Operations map; approved Red operational graphics

### Task Organization

```text
8th Guards Armored Division
|-- 81st Guards Armored Brigade — North Pass Seize and Retain Force
`-- 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade — Kasar / Follow-and-Support / Forward Guard Force
```

The division exercises operational control over both brigades. The 4th Corps Artillery Group remains in general support to the corps; the division receives fires according to the priorities established in R-10. The division has no dedicated maneuver reserve. Organic reconnaissance, engineers, air defense, logistics, medical, and command capabilities are incorporated into the division and brigade counters or adjudicated by scenario control.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from Kasar west through the northern Arven approaches, North Pass Bridge, PL Punch, and the northern portion of LOA Block. It includes the Saren Heights, the boundary with 15th Mechanized Division, routes from Dalen and Veyra that can reinforce the bridge defense, and the eastern line of communication to Karsovia.

The division monitors Blue formations capable of defending or counterattacking North Pass Bridge; movement through the Saren Heights; Blue artillery able to interdict the Kasar–North Pass route; and the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade or 6th Compact Mechanized Division if committed against the northern corps sector.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Kasar:** Initial eastern objective and logistical junction. Its security is necessary to sustain the bridge attack and protect the division rear.
- **North Pass Bridge:** Canalized northern crossing. Prepared defenders can engage formations confined to the road and bridge approaches. Seizure requires control of both approaches and confirmed trafficability.
- **Saren Heights:** Restrictive terrain provides concealment and observation over the northern route. A Blue force remaining there can threaten the bridge, artillery displacement, or the division flank.
- **Western approaches:** Terrain west of the river permits Blue reinforcement from Dalen or Veyra but also provides the division room to establish a guard after crossing.
- **Road network:** The Kasar–North Pass road is the primary maneuver, sustainment, recovery, and evacuation route. The division must prevent simultaneous blockage by maneuver and support traffic.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce observation and conceal Blue movement in the Saren Heights.
- Wet conditions increase road and bridge dependence and may slow armored movement, recovery, and artillery support.
- Limited visibility requires positive identification during passage, bridge occupation, and guard handover.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and create no automatic advantage.

### c. Enemy Forces

The division expects elements of the 3rd Compact Armored Division and Ordan infantry to defend North Pass Bridge and the Saren approaches. Blue may reinforce with mechanized forces from Dalen or commit the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade after the Karsovian axis becomes clear.

Blue can exploit restrictive terrain, mass artillery on the bridge approaches, block the crossing with a small force, and counterattack the lead brigade before the second brigade can support. Blue’s most dangerous action is to allow the 81st Brigade to cross, then counterattack its flank while artillery closes or disrupts the eastern approach and isolates it west of the river.

Blue’s most likely action is to defend in depth on the western bridge approach, delay in the Saren Heights, and withdraw toward PL Punch when continued defense risks destruction.

### d. Friendly Forces

- **4th Maneuver Corps:** Seizes Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and Central Link Bridge; advances to PL Punch; then retains the crossings and guards LOA Block.
- **15th Mechanized Division:** Corps main effort. Seizes Dalen and Central Link Bridge, protects the division boundary, and establishes the central corps guard.
- **4th Corps Artillery Group:** General support to the corps. Priority is normally below the 15th Division during Phases I–II, with fires allocated to this division for Kasar, the North Pass fix, and the bridge attack.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve after securing Ruda. It is not under division or corps control.

### e. Civil Considerations

Kasar and the North Pass road contain civilian traffic and critical transportation infrastructure. Preserve the bridge unless destruction is authorized by Eastern Army. Coordinate displaced civilians so they do not obstruct the sole primary route. Council-aligned authorities may assume Kasar security only after the division confirms that the route remains protected.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Fires support does not change the task organization.

### g. Assumptions

- North Pass Bridge begins trafficable.
- The 15th Division secures Dalen and prevents Blue from freely reinforcing North Pass from the south.
- The corps assigns sufficient artillery windows to suppress the bridge defense during Phase II.
- No reinforcement is guaranteed; the division plans to accomplish its essential tasks with two brigades.
- Phase transitions require corps and Army authorization.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **8th Guards Armored Division attacks to secure Kasar, fixes and then seizes North Pass Bridge, advances to PL Punch, and subsequently retains North Pass Bridge while guarding along the northern portion of LOA Block in order to open the northern Arven crossing, protect 4th Corps’ northern flank, and prevent Blue interference with the Eastern Army attack on Veyra.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander’s Intent

#### Purpose

Secure the northern crossing without allowing the division’s two brigades to become separated or isolated in the restrictive North Pass terrain.

#### Key Tasks

- Seize Kasar and protect the eastern line of communication.
- Fix Blue at North Pass Bridge while the corps secures Dalen.
- Reposition the 82nd Brigade to support the bridge attack after Kasar is secured.
- Seize North Pass Bridge with both approaches controlled and trafficability confirmed.
- Maintain contact and mutual support between brigades during the crossing.
- Pass the forward effort to the 82nd Brigade after North Pass is secured.
- Advance the 82nd Brigade to PL Punch without bypassing forces that can threaten the bridge or route.
- Establish a northern guard along LOA Block with protected displacement routes.
- Retain North Pass Bridge and preserve a local response capability.
- Report early when corps or Army reinforcement may be required.

#### End State

- Kasar and the Kasar–North Pass route are secure.
- North Pass Bridge is controlled, trafficable, and retained.
- The division has established security at PL Punch and a guard along the northern LOA Block sector.
- The Saren threat is contained or monitored sufficiently to protect the division flank.
- Both brigades remain mutually supporting and capable of continued operations.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I — Secure Kasar and Fix North Pass

**Projected Turns 1–5.** The 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort and attacks to seize Kasar in order to secure the division support base and northern route. After local security is established or transferred, the 82nd repositions toward North Pass Bridge and prepares to follow and support the 81st Brigade. The 81st Guards Armored Brigade fixes Blue at North Pass Bridge to prevent reinforcement of Dalen and preserve contact with the crossing defense.

The phase ends on corps order when Kasar is secure, the eastern route is open, the North Pass defense is fixed and developed, and both brigades are postured for the attack.

#### (2) Phase II — Seize North Pass Bridge

**Projected Turns 6–10.** The 81st Guards Armored Brigade becomes the division main effort and attacks to seize North Pass Bridge. The 82nd Brigade follows and supports, suppresses or fixes threats not defeated by the 81st, secures the eastern and western approaches, and prepares to assume the forward guard. The division synchronizes artillery windows, passage, traffic control, recovery, and approach security so the 81st is not isolated west of the river.

The phase ends on corps order when both approaches are secured, bridge trafficability is confirmed, movement control is established, Blue forces capable of immediate counterattack are defeated or contained, and the division is postured to advance to PL Punch.

#### (3) Phase III — Advance to PL Punch and Establish Northern Security

**Projected Turns 11–15.** The 82nd Brigade becomes the division main effort, conducts movement to contact toward PL Punch, gains and develops contact, and establishes the forward guard protecting the corps northern flank. The 81st Brigade retains North Pass Bridge, secures both approaches and the division route, and prepares to reinforce, block, or cover the 82nd Brigade if required. The division maintains sufficient combat power at the crossing while the 82nd establishes the western security zone.

The phase ends on corps order when the division is established at PL Punch, North Pass Bridge and the eastern route remain secure, the corps northern flank is protected, and the division can transition to the Phase IV guard and retain tasks.

#### (4) Phase IV — Guard LOA Block and Retain North Pass

**Projected Turns 16–20.** The 82nd Brigade remains the division main effort and continues the guard along the northern portion of LOA Block. It maintains contact, identifies the Blue main body, delays as required, and displaces before becoming decisively engaged. The 81st Brigade continues to retain North Pass Bridge and prepares to reinforce the guard, block a penetration, or cover the 82nd Brigade’s disengagement on division order.

The division end state is achieved when the guard has provided required warning and delay, North Pass Bridge remains controlled, and both brigades retain freedom to continue the defense.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The division uses the 82nd Brigade to secure Kasar and free the 81st Brigade to maintain pressure at North Pass. After Kasar is secure, the 82nd closes on the 81st rather than remaining tied to the city. The bridge attack is sequenced so one brigade leads and the other secures approaches, follows, and supports; both are not committed independently on separated axes.

At the beginning of Phase III, the 81st transfers the forward effort to the 82nd and assumes the North Pass retain mission. The 82nd then leads west to PL Punch, establishes the forward guard, and remains the division main effort through Phase IV. The 81st preserves the crossing and supplies the division’s only practical local response. It is not a formal reserve; any competing task must be reported to corps.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The division receives support from the 4th Corps Artillery Group according to corps priorities.

- **Phase I:** Suppress Blue forces interfering with the seizure of Kasar; fix and develop the North Pass defense; counterfire against artillery covering the bridge.
- **Phase II:** Priority to the 81st Brigade. Suppress and obscure bridge defenders, isolate the western approach, interdict reinforcements from Dalen or Veyra, and conduct counterfire.
- **Phase III:** Priority within the division is the 82nd Brigade’s movement to contact and guard establishment, followed by the 81st Brigade at North Pass, while the corps artillery group also reinforces 9th Corps. Report any support gap immediately.
- **Phase IV:** Priority to the 82nd Brigade when in contact or disengaging, then the 81st Brigade defending the crossing.

The division does not direct corps fires outside allocated windows or across boundaries without clearance. Organic capabilities nominate targets and maintain observation; the division does not treat the corps artillery counter as attached.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Units

#### (1) 81st Guards Armored Brigade

- **Phase I — Task:** Fix Blue forces defending North Pass Bridge. **Purpose:** Prevent reinforcement of Dalen and preserve conditions for the subsequent crossing attack.
- **Phase II — Task:** Attack to seize North Pass Bridge and its western approach. **Purpose:** Open the northern Arven crossing for 4th Corps.
- **Phase III — Task:** Retain North Pass Bridge, secure both approaches and the division route, and prepare to reinforce or cover the 82nd Brigade. **Purpose:** Preserve the crossing and sustain the forward guard.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Retain North Pass Bridge and prepare to reinforce or cover the 82nd Brigade. **Purpose:** Preserve the crossing and provide the division’s local response capability.

#### (2) 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade

- **Phase I — Task:** Attack to seize Kasar, establish or transfer local security, then reposition to follow and support the 81st Brigade. **Purpose:** Secure the division support base and mass the division for North Pass.
- **Phase II — Task:** Follow and support the 81st Brigade; secure the North Pass approaches and division route. **Purpose:** Prevent isolation of the main effort and preserve crossing trafficability.
- **Phase III — Task:** Conduct movement to contact to PL Punch, gain and develop contact, and establish the northern guard. **Purpose:** Protect the corps northern flank and prevent Blue interference with the Veyra operation.
- **Phase IV — Task:** Guard along the northern portion of LOA Block, maintain contact, delay, and displace within division criteria. **Purpose:** Provide warning and time while protecting North Pass Bridge and the corps northern flank.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Main Effort

- Phase I: 82nd Brigade.
- Phase II: 81st Brigade.
- Phases III–IV: 82nd Brigade.

#### (2) Actions on Contact

Report, secure, and develop contact before committing the second brigade. Fix, attack, bypass, block, or displace according to the phase purpose. Do not bypass a force capable of interdicting North Pass Bridge, the Kasar route, division artillery support, or the boundary with 15th Division without containment or confirmed handover.

#### (3) Bridge Control

North Pass Bridge is not seized until both approaches are secured, trafficability is confirmed, and movement control is established. The 81st controls the objective until the division confirms transfer of approach security. Report bridge damage, obstruction, observation, or reduced capacity immediately.

#### (4) Guard and Disengagement

The 82nd Brigade establishes primary and alternate positions and displacement routes, maintains contact with the Blue main body, and reports delay achieved each turn. Disengage when directed, when the required delay is achieved, or when continued contact would prevent orderly withdrawal under preapproved division criteria. The 81st prepares a rearward handover and covering action.

#### (5) Reserve and Risk

The division has no dedicated reserve. In Phases III–IV, the 81st provides limited local response only while North Pass remains retained; it is not an uncommitted reserve. Request corps assistance before both brigades lose freedom of maneuver. The division cannot commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.

#### (6) Decision Points

- **DP 1 — Reposition 82nd:** Kasar secured or local security transferred; route to North Pass open.
- **DP 2 — Recommend Phase II:** North Pass defense developed; 81st and 82nd postured; fires and routes ready.
- **DP 3 — Complete bridge seizure:** Both approaches secured; trafficability and movement control confirmed; counterattack threat contained.
- **DP 4 — Recommend Phase III:** Bridge retained; 81st ready to assume the retain mission; 82nd, route, fires, and sustainment ready for westward movement.
- **DP 5 — Continue guard to LOA Block:** PL Punch security complete; 82nd guard routes and disengagement criteria coordinated; 81st able to retain the bridge and cover withdrawal.
- **DP 6 — Disengage guard:** Required delay achieved or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal.

#### (7) CCIR

**PIR:** North Pass defenses and obstacles; Blue artillery; forces in Saren Heights; reinforcement routes from Dalen/Veyra; location of Blue armored reserve; bridge condition.

**FFIR:** Either brigade at or below 70 strength; Kasar or North Pass route blocked; bridge unusable; brigades unable to maintain mutual support; artillery unavailable for bridge attack; guard unable to disengage; or corps reinforcement required.

#### (8) Reports and Constraints

Report contact, strength, objective, bridge, route, guard, supportability, sustainment, and phase readiness once per turn and on significant change. Do not destroy the bridge, cross LOA Block, or begin a new phase without authenticated authority. Protect civilians and critical infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Support flows through Kasar along the North Pass route. Before the bridge attack, distribute fuel, ammunition, recovery, and medical support so loss of one node does not halt both brigades. After seizure, establish controlled movement across the bridge without blocking the 82nd Brigade’s Phase III movement to contact or the 81st Brigade’s retain mission.

### b. Priorities of Support

- Phase I: 82nd Brigade at Kasar; 81st contact and fix; bridge-attack preparation.
- Phase II: 81st Brigade; 82nd approach security; recovery and traffic control.
- Phase III: 82nd forward movement and guard establishment; 81st bridge and route security.
- Phase IV: 82nd guard in contact or disengaging; 81st crossing defense; reconstitution.

### c. Supply, Maintenance, and Transportation

Fuel, direct-fire ammunition, and recovery capacity are pacing. Clear disabled equipment from the Kasar road and bridge approaches rapidly. Separate maneuver, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and any corps artillery traffic. Wet-weather movement begins early enough to preserve support for the next turn when practicable.

### d. Medical and Reconstitution

Casualty evacuation moves east through North Pass to Kasar; establish an alternate collection and transfer plan before Phase II. Reconstitution priority follows the current main effort, then the brigade essential to crossing retention. A brigade unable to perform its essential task reports for relief or mission adjustment.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The division command post initially operates east of Kasar and displaces toward North Pass after route security permits. The tactical command post follows the current main effort. Command-post movement does not compete with the 81st Brigade’s bridge assault, the 82nd Brigade’s Phase III movement, or either brigade’s sustainment route.

Succession of command is division commander, deputy division commander, 81st Brigade commander, then 82nd Brigade commander. Maintain liaison with 4th Corps, 15th Division, the 4th Corps Artillery Group, and units controlling adjacent guard sectors.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use division and corps command networks. Alternate means use brigade networks and liaison; contingency means use relay, schedules, and messenger. Bridge status, brigade separation, artillery windows, phase transition, guard disengagement, and FFIR have precedence.

Under degraded communications, continue the last confirmed task, retain North Pass Bridge, and preserve brigade mutual support. Do not begin a new phase or move beyond LOA Block without authenticated authority unless necessary to prevent destruction; report at the first opportunity.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from the Lestaran support routes west of Veyra east through Novar and Eren to Ruda and Selin. It includes the division boundary with the 3rd Compact Armored Division to the north, the 6th Compact Mechanized Division's eastern attack and Phase V guard sectors, and the Mora approaches to the south.

The division must monitor Red movement from Ruda toward Novar, from Selin toward Eren, and through the southern approaches toward Veyra. Actions against the 3rd Division at Dalen or Central Link Bridge may expose the division northern flank or congest the crossing network.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Veyra:** The national capital and seat of the elected government. Its loss or isolation would create operational and political effects greater than the loss of outlying terrain.
- **Novar:** The central crossing and principal road junction in the division sector. Control of Novar protects Veyra, connects the northern and southern sectors, and blocks the most direct Red route west.
- **Eren:** The southern principal crossing and gateway to the Mora approaches. Its loss would allow Red to turn the division and threaten Veyra from the south.
- **Arven River:** A major obstacle that canalizes heavy movement onto the crossings. The division must retain Novar and Eren while keeping their western approaches open for sustainment and reinforcement.
- **Western woods:** Wooded terrain between Veyra and the river conceals movement but restricts observation and can mask a Red force that crosses near Novar.
- **Southeastern marsh and Mora Hills:** Restrictive terrain channels Red movement toward Eren and limits rapid lateral movement between Novar and the southern edge.
- **Road network:** The Veyra-Novar and Veyra-Eren routes are the division's primary support and reserve corridors. They must remain clear enough for the 11th Brigade, artillery, recovery, and casualty evacuation.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for the early-autumn conditions described in B-00. Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce observation at the crossings and increase dependence on route-security reports. Wet conditions will slow movement through the wooded and marshy areas and may prevent the 11th Brigade from shifting rapidly between Novar and Eren. Weather effects not implemented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

The principal threat is the Karsovian 9th Maneuver Corps, consisting of the 12th Armored Division, 21st Mechanized Division, and 9th Corps Artillery Group. It is oriented generally on Ruda, Selin, Novar, and Eren. The 12th Armored Division can lead an attack along the Ruda-Novar road; the 21st Mechanized Division can attack toward Eren, secure Selin, or reinforce the central route.

The 5th Guards Armored Brigade remains the Eastern Army reserve and may reinforce a penetration toward Novar, counterattack the 6th Division, or exploit a crossing gained by the 9th Corps. Elements of the 4th Maneuver Corps may affect the division through the seam with the 3rd Division.

#### (2) Capabilities and Limitations

Red can mass armor against Novar, attack Eren simultaneously, use artillery to disrupt the crossings and Veyra routes, and exploit through either crossing before the division reserve can respond. Red may also bypass a defended city through restrictive terrain to isolate it.

Red is limited by the Arven obstacle, the canalizing road network, the need to protect Ruda and Selin, and the risk that forces attacking Novar and Eren become separated. A premature attack toward Veyra may expose Red flanks to the 6th Division or the corps reserve.

#### (3) Most Likely Course of Action

The 9th Maneuver Corps attacks toward Novar with armored and mechanized forces while a supporting force advances from Selin toward Eren. Artillery suppresses the crossing defenders and interdicts the routes toward Veyra. If either crossing is gained, Red commits mobile combat power to expand the bridgehead and isolate Veyra.

#### (4) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Red concentrates the 12th Armored Division and 5th Guards Armored Brigade against Novar while fixing Eren and the 3rd Division crossings. A rapid penetration at Novar divides the corps crossing defense, blocks the 6th Division's western support routes, and threatens Veyra before the 11th Brigade or corps reserve can counterattack.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

WCO JLC seizes and retains the designated Arven crossings, passes the 6th Division through PP South, destroys Red forces at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, and then establishes a defense in depth at the crossings while guarding along Guard Line Steel.

The corps purpose is to deny Karsovia the position required to partition Orda or replace the elected government. The 1st Division secures the political center, retains the southern crossing network, protects the corps flank, and receives the Phase V fight from the 6th Division.

#### (2) Adjacent and Supporting Units

- **3rd Compact Armored Division:** Corps main effort in Phase I. It seizes North Pass Bridge, Dalen, and Central Link Bridge, controls PP South, and retains the northern and central crossing set.
- **6th Compact Mechanized Division:** Follows and supports in Phase I and becomes the corps main effort in Phases II-V. It destroys Red forces at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, then guards along Guard Line Steel.
- **7th Coalition Armored Brigade:** Corps reserve. It may reinforce the division or counterattack a Red penetration on corps order.
- **Ordan civil and security authorities:** Protect government facilities, assist movement control, and manage displaced civilians where conditions permit.

### e. Civil Considerations

Veyra contains the national government and a dense civilian population. Novar and Eren contain critical bridges and transportation infrastructure. Civilian movement may block the same routes required for the division reserve, artillery, and casualty evacuation. Protect civilians and infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment and coordinate route control with Ordan authorities.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control unless a corps order directs otherwise.

### g. Assumptions

- Veyra remains the seat of the elected government.
- Novar and Eren begin trafficable and can be retained after seizure unless changed by scenario control.
- The division receives no additional playable maneuver counter unless directed by corps or introduced by scenario event.
- The 3rd Division retains the crossings north of the division boundary.
- The 6th Division will coordinate its Phase V disengagement before entering the division sector.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **1st Ordan Mechanized Division attacks from AA Hammer along Axis White to secure Veyra and seize Novar and Eren, then retains the capital and assigned Arven crossings and establishes the southern portion of the corps defense in order to preserve the elected government, deny Red penetration west of the Arven, and protect the corps southern flank.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander's Intent

#### Purpose

Preserve the elected government and deny Red the crossing network needed to isolate Veyra or divide the corps.

#### Key Tasks

- Secure Veyra and its western lines of communication.
- Seize and retain Novar and Eren and their associated crossings.
- Preserve lateral contact with the 3rd Division and keep the division seam secure.
- Establish the 11th Brigade as a mobile reserve after Veyra is secured.
- Maintain routes for reinforcement, artillery, casualty evacuation, and sustainment.
- Receive or support the 6th Division's Phase V guard disengagement.
- Preserve combat power and essential infrastructure.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Red cannot maintain an organized force west of Novar or Eren and cannot isolate Veyra through the division sector.
- **Friendly:** Veyra remains secure; the 12th and 13th Brigades retain their crossings; the 11th Brigade remains a mobile reserve or has restored the defense; and the division remains connected to the 3rd Division.
- **Terrain:** Veyra, Novar, Eren, and their western approaches remain under Blue control.
- **Civil and political:** The elected government continues to function and critical civilian infrastructure is preserved consistent with the mission.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division conducts a five-phase operation nested with B-00. In Phase I, the 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade is the division main effort and seizes Novar while the 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade seizes Eren and the 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade secures Veyra before transitioning to reserve. In Phases II-IV, the division retains Veyra, Novar, and Eren while the 6th Division attacks east of the Arven. In Phase V, the division establishes the southern portion of the corps defense and receives or supports withdrawing guard forces.

The division accepts risk in depth rather than at the crossings. The 12th and 13th Brigades defend the essential crossing sites; the 11th Brigade remains concentrated to counterattack a penetration or reinforce the threatened crossing. The division does not shift a crossing brigade in a manner that creates an unopposed Red route west.

#### (2) Phase I - Secure Veyra and Seize Novar and Eren

The 13th Brigade, division main effort, attacks along Axis White to seize Novar and its crossing. The 12th Brigade attacks to seize Eren and its crossing. The 11th Brigade secures Veyra, establishes contact with civil authorities, and transitions to division reserve. The 1st Ordan Artillery Group provides general support with priority to the 13th, then 12th, then 11th Brigade.

Phase I ends when Veyra is secure; Novar and Eren are under Blue control; the division seam and western routes are open; and the 11th Brigade is prepared to serve as reserve.

#### (3) Phase II - Retain the Crossing Defense

The 13th Brigade retains Novar and is the division main effort. The 12th Brigade retains Eren. The 11th Brigade remains reserve vicinity Veyra. Division artillery protects the crossings and shifts priority to the most dangerous Red attempt to penetrate west. The division coordinates with the 3rd Division during the 6th Division's passage at PP South.

Phase II ends on corps order after the Kasar force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective and the 6th Division is postured for Ruda. The division continues its terrain responsibilities without interruption.

#### (4) Phase III - Retain and Protect the Corps Flank

The division retains Veyra, Novar, and Eren while the 6th Division attacks Ruda along Axis Blue. The 13th Brigade remains the division main effort at Novar; the 12th Brigade denies Eren; and the 11th Brigade remains reserve. The division watches the Ruda-Novar and Selin-Eren routes for a Red counterattack or bypass.

Phase III ends on corps order after the Ruda force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective and the 6th Division is postured for Selin.

#### (5) Phase IV - Retain and Prepare to Receive the Guard

The division retains its crossing defense while the 6th Division attacks Selin along Axis Brown. It establishes liaison with the 6th Division, confirms routes and battle-handover procedures, and prepares positions and fires to receive forces withdrawing from the southern guard sector.

Phase IV ends when the Selin force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective, Guard Line Steel and PL Bronze are in effect, and the division is prepared to receive the Phase V fight.

#### (6) Phase V - Establish the Southern Corps Defense

The division establishes the southern portion of the corps defense centered on Veyra, Novar, and Eren. The 13th Brigade remains the main effort at Novar, the 12th Brigade retains Eren, and the 11th Brigade remains reserve. The division receives or supports the 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade and other designated 6th Division elements after their guard disengagement south of PL Bronze.

The phase continues through scenario end or until the corps directs a subsequent operation.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

The 13th Brigade seizes and retains Novar to deny Red the most direct route toward Veyra and to preserve the central road junction. It establishes depth west of the crossing and maintains contact with the 3rd Division along the northern seam. It does not pursue east in a manner that uncovers Novar.

The 12th Brigade seizes and retains Eren to block the southern crossing and Mora approach. It establishes observation and depth along the southern route and reports any Red force capable of bypassing toward Veyra. It may displace from nonessential forward terrain but does not abandon Eren without division approval or an immediate survival action under degraded communications.

The 11th Brigade secures Veyra and then remains the division reserve. It maintains one feasible route to Novar and one to Eren, with current travel-time and passage estimates. Preferred employment is a concentrated counterattack against a Red penetration or occupation of a blocking position that prevents a Red force from reaching Veyra. It is not committed solely to recover nonessential terrain.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 1st Ordan Artillery Group remains in general support. Initial priority of fires is 13th, 12th, then 11th Brigade. Priority shifts to:

1. the crossing threatened by a Red force capable of penetrating west;
2. the 11th Brigade when committed as the division decisive operation;
3. the sector receiving a Phase V guard-force battle handover; or
4. a corps-directed cross-boundary target when support to the division's essential crossing tasks remains adequate.

Essential fire-support tasks are to suppress crossing defenders in Phase I; disrupt and defeat Red crossing attempts in Phases II-IV; conduct counterfire; protect reserve movement; and support guard-force disengagement and battle handover in Phase V.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Formations

#### (1) 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade - Division Main Effort

- Attack to seize Novar and its crossing in Phase I. **Purpose:** Deny Red the central route to Veyra and secure the division's decisive crossing.
- Retain Novar in Phases II-V. **Purpose:** Block Red penetration west of the Arven and preserve the division's lateral road network.
- Secure the division seam with the 3rd Division. **Purpose:** Prevent a Red bypass between crossing sectors.
- Establish depth and prepare to receive Phase V withdrawing forces as directed. **Purpose:** Transfer the fight without exposing Novar.

#### (2) 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade - Supporting Effort

- Attack to seize Eren and its crossing in Phase I. **Purpose:** Deny Red the southern Arven route.
- Retain Eren in Phases II-V. **Purpose:** Block Red penetration through the southern crossing and Mora approaches.
- Maintain observation of the Selin-Eren route and report bypass indicators. **Purpose:** Provide time for fires or reserve commitment.
- Prepare to receive or support withdrawing 62nd Brigade elements in Phase V. **Purpose:** Preserve the guard force and transfer the fight to the crossing defense.

#### (3) 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade - Division Reserve

- Secure Veyra in Phase I, then remain concentrated as division reserve. **Purpose:** Preserve the elected government and division freedom of action.
- Maintain primary and alternate routes to Novar and Eren and report travel time each turn. **Purpose:** Enable timely counterattack or reinforcement.
- On authenticated division order, block or counterattack a Red penetration threatening Veyra, Novar, Eren, or the routes between them. **Purpose:** Restore the crossing defense and prevent operational breakthrough.
- Terminate under division criteria, consolidate on defensible terrain, and report ability to continue or reconstitute. **Purpose:** Restore a mobile response.

#### (4) 1st Ordan Artillery Group - General Support

- Provide general support to the division.
- Execute the priorities and essential fire-support tasks in paragraph 3.d and Annex C.
- Maintain coverage of Novar and Eren and displace before loss of route access.
- Support the 11th Brigade when it becomes the division decisive operation.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Effective Time and Phase Control

- This order is effective on receipt. Phase I begins 12 September 2031 at 0600 local.
- Corps controls phase transitions. Brigades continue their essential tasks during transition until handover is confirmed.
- The division commander may recommend transition and may adjust internal priorities without abandoning a corps task.

#### (2) Control Measures

- AA Hammer, Axis White, OBJ Veyra, OBJ Novar, OBJ Eren, division boundaries, and Phase V handover measures are as shown on the approved overlay.
- The 3rd Division boundary defines the northern limit of the division crossing defense.
- PL Bronze separates the 6th Division northern and southern Phase V guard sectors; it does not change the 1st Division boundary unless corps directs.
- No brigade crosses east of its assigned crossing for pursuit without division approval and a stated task, purpose, and limit.

#### (3) Commander's Critical Information Requirements

**PIR**

1. Is the 9th Maneuver Corps main effort oriented on Novar, Eren, or the 6th Division?
2. Where is the 5th Guards Armored Brigade, and can it reach Novar or Eren within two turns?
3. Is Red preparing a bypass through the Mora approaches or the division seam?
4. When will the Red counterattack from Ruda or Selin reach the Phase V handover area?

**FFIR**

1. Veyra, Novar, or Eren is lost, isolated, or cannot be retained.
2. The 12th or 13th Brigade falls below 70 percent nominal combat power or cannot perform its crossing task.
3. The 11th Brigade is not reserve-ready, is committed, or cannot reach a threatened crossing.
4. The division seam, a western route, or a guard-withdrawal route is blocked.
5. Division artillery cannot support the threatened crossing or a committed reserve.
6. Civilian congestion or infrastructure damage prevents maneuver or sustainment.

#### (4) Division Reserve Release Criteria

The division commander may release the 11th Brigade when a Red maneuver formation penetrates or is positioned to penetrate west of Novar or Eren; the defending brigade cannot restore the situation; Veyra or a western route is threatened; or a time-sensitive opportunity exists to defeat a significant Red force. Viable routes, positive identification, flank security, fires, and sustainment must support the mission.

The committing order specifies task, purpose, objective or blocking area, route, release time, supported unit, priority of fires, limit of advance, termination, and reconstitution requirements. Communications failure does not delegate planned reserve commitment authority.

#### (5) Phase V Battle Handover

- Establish liaison with the 6th Division before Phase V begins.
- Confirm the crossing, route, recognition, artillery, casualty evacuation, and traffic-control plan for withdrawing southern-sector guard forces.
- The 6th Division retains responsibility for the fight until its designated elements clear the battle-handover line and this division acknowledges responsibility.
- The receiving brigade does not move east in a manner that blocks the withdrawal route or becomes intermingled with the guard force.
- Shift fires to support the disengaging force, then to defeat the Red force entering the division engagement area.

#### (6) Risk Controls and Constraints

- Do not reinforce one crossing by uncovering the other without division approval and a handover or explicit acceptance of risk.
- Do not commit the 11th Brigade piecemeal or solely to recover nonessential terrain.
- Keep Veyra-Novar and Veyra-Eren routes clear for reserve, artillery, and evacuation movement.
- Preserve bridges and civilian infrastructure unless destruction is authorized to prevent a greater operational loss.
- Report before a crossing brigade becomes isolated or loses freedom to displace within its defense.

#### (7) Required Reports

- Contact and identified-enemy report after each new engagement.
- Strength and mission-capability report at the end of each turn and on crossing an FFIR threshold.
- Veyra, Novar, Eren, bridge, seam, and route status whenever control or access changes.
- 11th Brigade reserve readiness, routes, and travel times each turn.
- Artillery priority, ammunition, displacement, and supportability report each turn.
- Phase V guard-contact, handover readiness, passage, and transfer-of-responsibility reports.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Division support flows from Lestara and western Ordan through Veyra to Novar and Eren. The division preserves two support routes whenever possible and avoids concentrating all sustainment near one crossing. Support priorities preserve the crossing defenders and the mobility of the 11th Brigade.

### b. Priorities of Support

1. Brigade defending the crossing under the most immediate threat.
2. 11th Brigade upon reserve commitment.
3. 1st Ordan Artillery Group supporting the decisive operation.
4. Veyra government and route-security requirements represented within the division.
5. Recovery and reconstitution of a mobile reserve.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel priority supports the 11th Brigade and artillery displacement. Ammunition priority supports the threatened crossing and division artillery. Brigades maintain primary and alternate routes and report blockage immediately. During Phase V, withdrawing guard forces receive movement priority on the designated route until clear of the handover area.

### d. Maintenance and Recovery

Priority is the 11th Brigade, the brigade retaining the most threatened crossing, and artillery mobility. Recovery assets remain west of the crossing unless movement east is specifically coordinated and does not risk their loss.

### e. Medical and Casualty Evacuation

Casualty evacuation flows west through Veyra. Phase V routes are coordinated with the 6th Division to prevent conflict with withdrawing guard forces. Civilian casualties are coordinated with Ordan authorities where conditions permit.

### f. Personnel, Detainees, and Displaced Civilians

Ordan authorities retain primary responsibility for displaced civilians and civil administration. Brigades report population movement blocking a crossing or reserve route. Detainee handling follows Western Compact policy and Ordan law.

### g. Atlatl Abstraction

Detailed supply, medical, engineering, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, electronic warfare, and recovery functions are incorporated into parent counters or scenario adjudication.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

#### (1) Command Relationships

- The division has operational control of the 11th, 12th, and 13th Brigades.
- The 1st Ordan Artillery Group is in general support.
- The 11th Brigade becomes division reserve after Veyra is secure and remains so until released by the division commander.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control unless B-00 directs a temporary relationship after commitment.

#### (2) Command Posts and Commander Location

The division main command post remains in the Veyra support area with redundant communications to both crossings and corps. The tactical command element positions to control the threatened crossing or reserve commitment without blocking a route.

#### (3) Succession of Command

1. Commander, 1st Ordan Mechanized Division.
2. Division deputy commander.
3. Commander, 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade.
4. Commander, 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade.
5. Commander, 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade.

#### (4) Liaison

The division maintains liaison with corps, the 3rd Division, and the 6th Division. The 11th Brigade maintains coordination with both crossing brigades. If the corps reserve is oriented toward the division, liaison is exchanged before movement.

### b. Signal

- Primary communications are the division Ordan-coalition network.
- Alternate communications use national tactical networks through liaison teams.
- Contingency communications use relay, scheduled reports, and messenger procedures.
- Crossing, reserve, route, and Phase V handover reports have precedence.
- Brigades continue the last authenticated mission during degraded communications. The 11th Brigade may reposition to preserve itself or block an immediate threat to its own position but will not begin a planned counterattack without authenticated release.

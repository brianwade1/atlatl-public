## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The corps area of interest extends from the Lestaran approaches west of Veyra through the Ordan Basin to Karsovia's western military districts. It includes the Saren Heights in the north, Mora Hills in the south, the Arven River crossing network, and the eastern routes connecting Kasar, Ruda, and Selin with the Karsovian frontier.

Events outside the immediate area that may affect the operation include arrival of additional Karsovian forces, political action by the Ruda emergency council, disruption of Ordan civil administration, attacks on the western lines of communication, and changes in Western Compact access through Lestara. No additional playable Blue maneuver formation is expected during the scenario unless introduced by scenario control.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **Ordan Basin:** Open agricultural terrain favors rapid armored and mechanized movement, long-range observation, and artillery employment. The same openness exposes formations that advance without flank security or suppression.
- **Arven River:** The river is the principal operational obstacle. Heavy formations depend on the five represented crossing sites at North Pass Bridge, Dalen, Central Link Bridge, Novar, and Eren. Loss or blockage of a crossing can separate the corps, delay the 6th Division's passage, and restrict sustainment.
- **Saren Heights:** Rough northern terrain limits cross-country movement and channels forces toward North Pass Bridge and the Dalen route. It favors dismounted defense, concealment, and attacks against exposed flanks.
- **Mora Hills:** Rough terrain along the southern edge channels movement through Eren and the limited Mora approaches. A force using the southern route may threaten the corps flank but risks isolation from the basin road network.
- **Western woods and southeastern marsh:** Wooded terrain west of Dalen and Novar conceals movement and complicates observation. Marsh southeast of Ruda restricts heavy maneuver and channels movement onto the Ruda-Selin road network.
- **Cities and road junctions:** Veyra is the political center of the elected government. Novar is the most important central transportation junction. Dalen and Eren control the principal northern and southern crossings. Kasar, Ruda, and Selin are Red staging and logistics locations; destruction of Red forces there is more important than enduring occupation of the cities.
- **Road network:** The east-west roads through Dalen, Novar, and Eren carry maneuver, sustainment, casualty evacuation, and artillery displacement. The northern and southern pass routes provide alternatives but have reduced capacity. Route Blue is the corps' principal lateral and reserve movement route west of the Arven.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Scenario control has not imposed a separate weather advantage. Commanders plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and the possibility of morning fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn.

Operational effects are as follows:

- Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness may reduce observation and delay recognition of the enemy main effort.
- Wet conditions increase dependence on roads, bridges, and traffic control, particularly near the southeastern marsh and the river crossings.
- Artillery displacement and ammunition movement must remain synchronized with the 6th Division's passage and eastward attack.
- Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication and do not create an automatic advantage for either side.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

The Karsovian Eastern Army controls two maneuver corps and an army reserve:

- **4th Maneuver Corps:** 8th Guards Armored Division, 15th Mechanized Division, and 4th Corps Artillery Group. It is oriented generally on Kasar, Dalen, North Pass Bridge, and the northern-central basin.
- **9th Maneuver Corps:** 12th Armored Division, 21st Mechanized Division, and 9th Corps Artillery Group. It is oriented generally on Ruda, Selin, Novar, Eren, and the central-southern basin.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve, capable of exploiting a penetration, counterattacking a Blue advance, or reinforcing either corps.

Red has three armored brigades, six mechanized brigades, two infantry brigades, and two artillery groups. Its nominal combat power exceeds Blue by approximately 90 strength points, but Red must enter from the eastern edge, protect multiple routes, and decide whether to defend the eastern hubs or attack immediately toward the Arven.

#### (2) Capabilities and Limitations

The Eastern Army can:

- attack simultaneously on the Kasar-Dalen, Ruda-Novar, and Selin-Eren routes;
- mass armored and mechanized forces against one crossing or against the 6th Division during its passage;
- use the Saren and Mora routes to turn the corps flanks;
- employ artillery to disrupt crossings, passage lanes, reserve routes, and Blue artillery positions;
- commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade to counterattack an exposed Blue main effort; and
- defend Kasar, Ruda, and Selin as mutually supporting staging areas before transitioning to a coordinated counterattack.

The Eastern Army is limited by separated axes, extending lines of communication, restricted lateral movement near the map edges, and the risk that defending all three eastern hubs will disperse combat power. Red must also preserve a continuous corridor from the Karsovian frontier if it intends to establish the Ruda council as the effective government.

#### (3) Most Likely Course of Action

The Eastern Army secures Kasar, Ruda, and Selin while lead formations advance west to contest the Arven crossings. The 4th Maneuver Corps applies pressure toward Dalen and North Pass Bridge while the 9th Maneuver Corps advances toward Novar and Eren. Red artillery attacks Blue crossing sites and passage routes. If Blue penetrates toward an eastern hub, the threatened corps fixes the lead brigade while the 5th Guards Armored Brigade or an adjacent corps formation counterattacks its flank. Once Blue's eastern attack culminates, Red transitions to a coordinated westward counterattack designed to seize Novar, split the Blue divisions, and open a corridor toward Veyra.

#### (4) Most Dangerous Course of Action

The Eastern Army accepts risk at Kasar and Selin, concentrates both armored divisions and the army reserve on the Ruda-Novar axis, and attacks before the 6th Division completes its passage. Supporting attacks fix the 1st and 3rd Divisions at the northern and southern crossings while artillery disrupts PP South and the battle-handover line. A successful penetration at Novar could divide the corps, isolate the 6th Division east of the Arven, and threaten Veyra before the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade can restore the situation.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

Western Compact Joint Command supports the elected Ordan government in Veyra. It directs WCO JLC to secure the government and central transportation network, prevent Karsovia from creating a continuous occupied corridor to the Arven River, and preserve Ordan sovereignty while avoiding unnecessary damage to civilian infrastructure.

The higher commander's purpose is to deny Karsovia a rapid political and military fait accompli. The desired end state is an elected Ordan government able to function in Veyra, an Arven crossing network controlled by Blue, a Karsovian Eastern Army unable to sustain a westward offensive, and a joint land corps capable of continuing operations.

#### (2) Ordan Forces and Agencies

Ordan civil authorities, police, and non-playable security forces protect government facilities, assist movement control, manage displaced civilians, and maintain essential services where possible. Their activities do not relieve Blue formations of assigned crossing, route, or security responsibilities.

#### (3) Adjacent and Supporting Forces

Additional Western Compact formations remain outside the area and will not arrive during the immediate operation unless introduced by scenario control. Air, aviation, reconnaissance, engineers, air defense, electronic warfare, logistics, medical, and civil-affairs capabilities are incorporated into parent formations or represented through adjudication; they are not separate Atlatl counters.

### e. Civil Considerations

Veyra contains the elected government, national institutions, and a dense civilian population. Dalen, Novar, Eren, Kasar, Ruda, and Selin contain transportation infrastructure and civilians aligned with competing political authorities. Population movement may congest the same roads required for the corps reserve, artillery displacement, and casualty evacuation.

Commanders will preserve civilian life, bridges, rail facilities, utilities, hospitals, and government records consistent with mission accomplishment. The purpose of operations at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin is to destroy organized Red combat power, not to punish the population or establish a permanent occupation. Ordan authorities resume civil control as soon as tactical conditions permit.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None at the start of the operation. Cross-attachment of maneuver brigades requires corps approval. A division artillery group remains in general support of its parent division unless modified by authenticated corps order.

### g. Assumptions

- All five represented Arven crossing sites—Dalen, Novar, Eren, North Pass, and Central Link—begin trafficable. A change in crossing status requires scenario-control confirmation or a game effect.
- PP South is the designated forward-passage lane through Central Link Bridge. The 6th Division can use it after the 3rd Division secures the bridge and establishes passage control.
- No additional playable Blue formation arrives during the scenario.
- Blue retains legal authority to operate throughout Ordan territory but not to conduct a general attack into Karsovia.
- The elected government remains in Veyra unless a scenario event states otherwise.
- Kasar, Ruda, and Selin need not be retained after Red combat power at those locations is destroyed and the 6th Division transitions to the Phase V guard.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **Western Compact-Ordan Joint Land Corps attacks to seize and retain the designated Arven River crossings; passes the 6th Compact Mechanized Division through PP South; destroys Red forces sequentially at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin; and then establishes a defense in depth at the Arven crossings while guarding along Guard Line Steel in order to secure the elected Ordan government, prevent a Karsovian corridor to the Arven River, and preserve Ordan sovereignty.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander's Intent

Deny Karsovia the military position required to partition Orda or replace the elected government, while preserving a coherent Blue force capable of defeating the anticipated counterattack.

#### Key Tasks

- Secure Veyra and the western lines of communication.

- Open, control, and protect PP South for the 6th Division's forward passage.

- Maintain the 1st and 3rd Divisions as the corps crossing defense while the 6th Division operates east of the river.
- Detect, disrupt, and delay the Red counterattack along Guard Line Steel without allowing the 6th Division guard force to become decisively engaged.
- Preserve the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade for commitment against a decisive threat or opportunity.
- Protect civilians and essential infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

- **Friendly:** Veyra remains secure; the 1st and 3rd Divisions retain the crossing defense; the 6th Division completes or is executing an orderly guard disengagement; and the corps retains a mobile reserve or a force capable of reconstituting as one.
- **Terrain:** Blue controls the designated Arven crossings and the western road network. Kasar, Ruda, and Selin need not remain occupied after the required enemy effects are achieved.
- **Civil and political:** The elected Ordan government continues to function; essential infrastructure is not needlessly damaged; and Blue actions remain consistent with the stated defensive purpose of the intervention.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

WCO JLC conducts a five-phase operation. In Phase I, the 3rd Compact Armored Division is the corps main effort and seizes the northern, central, and southern crossings assigned to it while the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division secures Veyra, Novar, and Eren. The 6th Compact Mechanized Division follows and supports the 3rd Division and prepares to pass at PP South. In Phases II through IV, the 6th Division becomes the corps main effort and attacks east along Axes Black, Blue, and Brown to destroy Red forces at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin. The 1st and 3rd Divisions retain the Arven crossing network. In Phase V, the 6th Division guards along Guard Line Steel while the 1st and 3rd Divisions establish the corps defense in depth at the crossings. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains the corps reserve unless committed by authenticated order.

The operation relies on three forms of continuity. First, the 1st and 3rd Divisions retain the crossing network throughout the 6th Division's eastern attack. Second, the 6th Division shifts its decisive brigade sequentially while another brigade protects the division rear and routes. Third, artillery and sustainment displace by echelon so the corps can support the main effort without uncovering the crossings.

Phase transitions are conditions based. Estimated turn windows are planning aids only and do not authorize transition. Annex C synchronizes subordinate tasks, fires, sustainment, and decision points.

#### (2) Phase I - Seize the Arven Crossings

This phase begins at scenario start. The 3rd Compact Armored Division, the corps main effort, attacks from AA Axe along Axis Green. It seizes North Pass Bridge, Dalen, and Central Link Bridge. The 31st Coalition Armored Brigade is the division main effort because seizure of Central Link Bridge opens PP South for the 6th Division.

The 1st Ordan Mechanized Division attacks from AA Hammer along Axis White to secure Veyra and seize OBJ Novar and OBJ Eren. The 6th Division follows and supports the 3rd Division from AA Axe, preserves its combat power, and prepares for forward passage. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains concentrated as corps reserve with initial priority to the 3rd Division.

**Phase I ends** when Blue controls the designated crossing sites; Central Link Bridge and PP South are trafficable and secure; the 3rd Division has established passage control; and the 6th Division is postured to pass.

#### (3) Phase II - Destroy Red Forces at Kasar

The 6th Compact Mechanized Division becomes the corps main effort. It conducts a forward passage through the 3rd Division at PP South, accepts the battle handover east of the BHL, and attacks along Axis Black to destroy the Red force defending Kasar while remaining north of PL Gold. The 61st Coalition Armored Brigade is the 6th Division main effort. The 3rd Division controls PP South and retains its crossings; the 1st Division retains Veyra, Novar, and Eren. Corps reserve priority shifts to the 6th Division.

**Phase II ends** when the Red force defending Kasar is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; routes through or around Kasar are open; the 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade has assumed temporary local security; and the 61st and 62nd Brigades are postured for the Ruda attack.

#### (4) Phase III - Destroy Red Forces at Ruda

The 6th Division attacks along Axis Blue to destroy the Red force defending Ruda while remaining north of PL Silver. The 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort. The 61st Coalition Armored Brigade fixes the Ruda defense and protects the attack, while the 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade conducts temporary area defense near Kasar to protect the division rear and routes. The 1st and 3rd Divisions continue to retain the crossings, and the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains the corps reserve with priority to the 6th Division.

**Phase III ends** when the Red force defending Ruda is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; the division rear remains secure; the 61st Brigade is prepared to accept temporary security of the Kasar-Ruda corridor; and the 62nd and 63rd Brigades are postured for the Selin attack.

#### (5) Phase IV - Destroy Red Forces at Selin

The 6th Division attacks along Axis Brown to destroy the Red force defending Selin. The 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort. The 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade fixes and isolates the Selin defense. The 61st Coalition Armored Brigade accepts temporary responsibility for the Kasar-Ruda corridor to protect the division rear and lines of communication. The 1st and 3rd Divisions retain the crossing defense. The corps reserve remains prepared to reinforce the 6th Division or protect the transition to Phase V.

**Phase IV ends** when the Red force defending Selin is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; the 6th Division has recovered sufficient freedom of maneuver to establish the guard; Guard Line Steel and PL Bronze are in effect; and the 1st and 3rd Divisions are prepared to receive the fight.

#### (6) Phase V - Guard and Defend

The 6th Division establishes a guard along Guard Line Steel to detect, disrupt, and delay the Red counterattack without becoming decisively engaged. The 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade is the division main effort and guards north of PL Bronze. The 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade guards south of PL Bronze. The 61st Coalition Armored Brigade is the 6th Division reserve. The 1st and 3rd Divisions establish the corps defense in depth centered on the Arven crossings and prepare to receive withdrawing guard forces. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains the corps reserve, positioned for rapid access to the crossing network.

Phase V continues through scenario end or until the corps directs a subsequent operation. The desired result is timely warning and imposed delay, followed by an orderly guard disengagement and battle handover while Blue retains the Arven crossings and sufficient mobile combat power to defeat a penetration.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

#### (1) 1st Ordan Mechanized Division

The 1st Division is a supporting effort throughout the operation. In Phase I, attack from AA Hammer along Axis White to secure Veyra and seize Novar and Eren. Beginning in Phase II, retain Veyra, Novar, Eren, and their associated crossing approaches to secure the corps southern sector and western lines of communication. In Phase V, establish the southern portion of the corps defense and prepare to receive or support withdrawing 6th Division forces.

The division retains the 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade as its mobile reserve after Veyra is secured. It will not uncover Novar or Eren to reinforce the eastern attack without corps approval.

#### (2) 3rd Compact Armored Division

The 3rd Division is the corps main effort in Phase I. Attack from AA Axe along Axis Green to seize North Pass Bridge, Dalen, and Central Link Bridge. Establish and control PP South, support the 6th Division's forward passage, and transfer responsibility east of the BHL in Phase II. Retain the assigned crossings for the remainder of the operation. In Phase V, establish the northern portion of the corps defense and prepare to receive or support withdrawing 6th Division forces.

The 3rd Division retains responsibility for Central Link Bridge, the bridge, and the western approach after the 6th Division assumes responsibility east of the BHL. Its passage-support requirement ends only when the designated 6th Division rear element clears the passage and both division headquarters confirm completion.

#### (3) 6th Compact Mechanized Division

The 6th Division follows and supports in Phase I and is the corps main effort in Phases II through V. Conduct the forward passage at PP South and destroy Red forces sequentially at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin. Use Axis Black in Phase II, Axis Blue in Phase III, and Axis Brown in Phase IV. Remain within PL Gold and PL Silver restrictions as shown on the overlay.

Temporary security at Kasar and along the Kasar-Ruda corridor protects the division rear and freedom of maneuver; it is not an enduring terrain-retention requirement. In Phase V, guard along Guard Line Steel with the 63rd Brigade north of PL Bronze and the 62nd Brigade south of PL Bronze. Retain the 61st Brigade as division reserve. Preserve freedom to disengage and pass the fight to the corps defense.

#### (4) 7th Coalition Armored Brigade - Corps Reserve

Remain concentrated and uncommitted under corps control. Maintain current route, passage, liaison, fires, sustainment, and travel-time estimates to all three division areas. Initial priority is to the 3rd Division in Phase I and to the 6th Division in Phases II through IV. In Phase V, priority is to the threatened crossing sector or to preservation of the guard withdrawal.

Be prepared to:

- reinforce seizure or defense of Central Link Bridge and PP South;
- counterattack a Red penetration threatening an Arven crossing or Veyra;
- reinforce the 6th Division to complete destruction of the designated Red force;
- secure a route required for the 6th Division's passage or withdrawal;
- block the most dangerous Red avenue during Phase V; or
- cover the withdrawal of a guard brigade when the 6th Division reserve cannot do so.

The brigade will not cross a corps-designated release point or initiate a combat mission without authenticated corps direction. Reorientation within the reserve area does not constitute commitment.

### d. Scheme of Fires

Division artillery groups remain in general support of their parent divisions. Corps synchronizes cross-boundary fires and may shift priority or direct reinforcing fires by fragmentary order; no permanent change of command relationship is implied.

#### Phase I

- **3rd Division Artillery Group:** Priority among 3rd Division brigades is 31st, 33rd, then 32nd. Suppress crossing defenders, obscure exposed approaches, conduct counterfire, and protect seizure of Central Link Bridge and PP South.
- **1st Ordan Artillery Group:** Priority is 13th, 12th, then 11th Brigade. Suppress defenders and protect seizure of Novar and Eren.
- **6th Division Artillery Group:** Displace with the division and remain prepared to support the passage after coordination with 3rd Division Artillery.

#### Phase II

- **6th Division Artillery Group:** Priority is 61st, 62nd, then 63rd Brigade. Protect the passage, isolate Kasar, suppress defenders, and conduct counterfire.
- **3rd Division Artillery Group:** Protect PP South and the retained crossings. On corps order, provide reinforcing fires to the 6th Division's planned targets without abandoning immediate crossing-defense requirements.
- **1st Ordan Artillery Group:** Protect Novar, Eren, Veyra, and the southern crossing sector.

#### Phase III

- **6th Division Artillery Group:** Priority is 62nd, 61st, then 63rd Brigade. Isolate Ruda, interdict reinforcement from Selin, suppress defenders, and conduct counterfire.
- **3rd Division Artillery Group:** Protect the crossing network and PP South; provide cross-boundary fires on corps order.
- **1st Ordan Artillery Group:** Protect retained crossings and defeat Red attempts to turn the southern corps flank.

#### Phase IV

- **6th Division Artillery Group:** Priority is 63rd, 62nd, then 61st Brigade. Isolate Selin, suppress defenders, protect the attack's northern flank, and preserve ammunition for the Phase V guard.
- **3rd and 1st Artillery Groups:** Protect their assigned crossings and provide cross-boundary fires on corps order.

#### Phase V

- **6th Division Artillery Group:** Initial priority is 63rd, then 62nd, then 61st Brigade, shifting to the brigade in contact or disengaging. Disrupt Red forces east of Guard Line Steel, suppress forces threatening disengagement, obscure withdrawal routes, and displace by echelon before movement depends on one route.
- **3rd and 1st Artillery Groups:** Support the northern and southern portions of the corps crossing defense respectively and prepare fires to receive the Red counterattack after battle handover.

Across all phases, priority of corps-controlled collection, counterfire coordination, and enabling effects follows the corps main effort. Commanders will preserve bridges and critical civilian infrastructure unless destruction is required to prevent a greater operational loss and authorized by the corps commander.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Time and Phase Control

- This order is effective on receipt. Operations begin 12 September 2031 at 0600 local.
- The corps commander retains phase-transition authority.
- Conditions, not elapsed turns alone, govern phase transitions.
- A subordinate commander may recommend transition at any time but continues the current essential task until the receiving formation confirms handover or the corps directs otherwise.

#### (2) Passage of Lines and Battle Handover at PP South

- The 3rd Division is the stationary force and controls PP South, the passage lane, recognition procedures, movement timing, and local security until passage completion.
- The 6th Division is the passing force and provides movement tables, order of march, liaison, recovery, and traffic requirements.
- The 31st Coalition Armored Brigade secures the bridge and immediate passage area under the 3rd Division order.
- The 6th Division assumes responsibility east of the BHL when its lead brigade crosses the BHL, communications and fire-support coordination are established, and both division commanders confirm battle handover.
- The 3rd Division retains the bridge and western approach after handover.
- Passage is complete when the designated 6th Division rear element clears PP South and both division headquarters report completion.
- If PP South becomes unusable, the 6th Division halts short of the passage, preserves combat power, and prepares an alternate crossing proposal. Use of another division's crossing requires corps approval and a revised passage plan.

#### (3) Phase IV Security Handover

Before the 63rd Brigade departs the Kasar area for the Selin attack, the 61st Brigade accepts temporary responsibility for the Kasar-Ruda corridor. The handover requires liaison, transfer of enemy and route information, confirmation of fire-support coordination, and a report that responsibility has transferred. The purpose is to protect the division rear and does not establish an enduring terrain-retention mission.

#### (4) Phase V Guard Disengagement and Battle Handover

- The 63rd and 62nd Brigades delay from successive positions and avoid decisive engagement.
- A guard brigade requests disengagement when continued occupation would prevent orderly withdrawal, when the Red main body threatens bypass or envelopment, or when the corps-directed delay requirement is achieved.
- The 6th Division controls disengagement sequencing. Normally, the less heavily engaged brigade displaces first while the other maintains contact.
- The 61st Brigade remains uncommitted until required to restore a guard sector, block a penetration, extract a decisively engaged element, protect 6th Division Artillery, or secure a withdrawal route.
- The 6th Division transfers the fight only after its guard elements clear the designated battle-handover line and the receiving division acknowledges responsibility.
- The guard mission does not authorize sacrifice of the division east of the Arven. The 6th Division commander reports immediately when the reserve is committed or when an orderly disengagement is at risk.

#### (5) Corps Reserve Commitment

Only the corps commander may release the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade. A committing fragmentary order will specify:

1. the operational condition requiring commitment and the risk accepted elsewhere;
2. the supported formation and command relationship;
3. the brigade task, purpose, objective or blocking area;
4. primary and alternate routes, passage points, and movement priority;
5. fires and sustainment support;
6. limit of advance and terrain that may not be uncovered; and
7. termination, consolidation, reconstitution, and return-to-corps-control criteria.

Preferred employment is a concentrated counterattack against an exposed Red force, a block of the most dangerous operational avenue, or reinforcement necessary to complete the corps decisive operation. The brigade will not be committed piecemeal.

**Priority intelligence requirements**

- **PIR 1:** Where and when will the Eastern Army commit the 5th Guards Armored Brigade?
- **PIR 2:** Which Red corps or axis is the main effort, and is Red massing against PP South, Novar, or another crossing?
- **PIR 3:** What Red force defends each of Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, and when is it destroyed, displaced, or reinforced?
- **PIR 4:** When and where is the Eastern Army forming its Phase V counterattack force?
- **PIR 5:** Is Red attempting to bypass either guard sector through the Saren Heights or Mora Hills?

**Friendly-force information requirements**

- **FFIR 1:** Loss, blockage, or threatened loss of any designated Arven crossing, PP South, or Route Blue.
- **FFIR 2:** Any division main-effort brigade falls below 70 percent nominal combat power or cannot continue its assigned task.
- **FFIR 3:** The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade, 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade, 63rd Brigade in Phase II, or 61st Brigade in Phase V loses reserve readiness.
- **FFIR 4:** A division artillery group cannot support the current or next phase from available positions or ammunition stocks.
- **FFIR 5:** The Phase V guard risks decisive engagement, loses a withdrawal route, or commits the 61st Brigade.
- **FFIR 6:** Civilian congestion or infrastructure damage closes a principal route or passage point.

**Essential elements of friendly information**

- Timing, order of march, and route for the 6th Division's passage at PP South.
- Current and planned locations of corps and division reserves.
- Artillery displacement schedules, ammunition status, and coverage gaps.
- Guard disengagement triggers, withdrawal routes, and battle-handover timing.

#### (7) Decision Points

- **DP 1 - Begin Phase II:** Central Link Bridge and PP South secure; 6th Division postured to pass.
- **DP 2 - Commit Corps Reserve:** An essential crossing or Veyra is threatened, the 6th Division requires reinforcement to complete the decisive operation, or a Red force is fixed and vulnerable to concentrated counterattack.
- **DP 3 - Begin Phase III:** Kasar force destroyed or combat ineffective; routes open; attack force postured toward Ruda.
- **DP 4 - Begin Phase IV:** Ruda force destroyed or combat ineffective; rear security transferred; attack force postured toward Selin.
- **DP 5 - Begin Phase V:** Selin force destroyed or combat ineffective; guard line and crossing defense prepared.
- **DP 6 - Disengage Guard:** Delay requirement achieved or continued contact threatens orderly withdrawal.
- **DP 7 - Transfer the Fight:** Guard elements clear the battle-handover line and the receiving division confirms responsibility.

#### (8) Rules and Constraints

- Operations remain within internationally recognized Ordan territory.
- Protect civilians, bridges, rail facilities, government buildings, hospitals, utilities, and cultural sites consistent with mission accomplishment.
- Do not convert Kasar, Ruda, or Selin into enduring retain missions.
- Do not abandon an assigned Arven crossing without corps approval unless required to avoid destruction during communications failure; report at the first opportunity.
- Do not commit either corps or division reserve piecemeal.
- Do not pursue beyond the assigned axis, phase line, limit of advance, supporting fires, or sustainable route network.
- Unit aggregation, combat resolution, and removal follow the Atlatl scenario rules. Capabilities not represented by counters require scenario-control adjudication.

#### (9) Risk Controls

- Maintain primary and alternate routes for the corps reserve and 6th Division sustainment.
- Avoid simultaneous congestion of PP South by maneuver, artillery, and sustainment columns.
- Displace artillery by echelon and maintain coverage during every phase transition.
- Use liaison and positive identification during passage, boundary changes, and cross-boundary fires.
- Preserve at least one mobile reserve at corps or supported-division level whenever possible.
- Report before a defending or guarding force loses freedom to disengage.

#### (10) Required Reports

- Contact report after first engagement and upon identification of a new Red formation.
- Strength and mission-capability report at the end of each turn and immediately upon crossing an FFIR threshold.
- Objective, bridge, passage-point, route, and BHL status whenever control or usability changes.
- Phase-transition recommendation when a commander assesses conditions are met.
- Reserve readiness, route, travel time, liaison, and support status each turn.
- Guard contact, delay achieved, reserve commitment, withdrawal-route status, disengagement, and battle-handover reports during Phase V.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Sustainment flows from Lestara and the western Ordan support area through Veyra and the Arven crossing network. Support priorities shift with the corps main effort while maintaining minimum support to the crossing defense. The corps will not place all logistics, medical evacuation, or artillery ammunition movement on one crossing.

The 1st and 3rd Divisions sustain the crossing defense from western support areas. The 6th Division carries sufficient fuel, ammunition, recovery, and medical capacity through PP South to maintain the attacks on Kasar, Ruda, and Selin and to transition to the Phase V guard. The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade stages for rapid commitment without consuming routes required by the 6th Division.

### b. Priorities of Support

#### Phase I

1. 3rd Compact Armored Division.
2. 1st Ordan Mechanized Division at Novar and Eren.
3. 6th Compact Mechanized Division passage preparation.
4. 7th Coalition Armored Brigade readiness.

#### Phases II-IV

1. 6th Compact Mechanized Division and its designated main-effort brigade.
2. 7th Coalition Armored Brigade upon commitment.
3. 3rd Division forces controlling PP South and the crossing network.
4. 1st Division forces retaining Novar, Eren, and Veyra.
5. Artillery ammunition and recovery needed for the next phase.

#### Phase V

1. Guard brigade in contact or disengaging.
2. 61st Coalition Armored Brigade if committed as division reserve.
3. Division artillery groups supporting guard disengagement and crossing defense.
4. 7th Coalition Armored Brigade and the threatened crossing sector.
5. Reconstitution of a mobile reserve.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and ammunition are the principal pacing requirements. Divisions maintain primary and alternate supply routes and report loss or blockage immediately. Movement through PP South is governed by the corps movement priority and 3rd Division passage control until completion of the forward passage.

During Phase II, passage priority is lead maneuver forces, command and control, artillery required for immediate support, air defense and engineers represented within the formation, casualty evacuation, and follow-on sustainment. During Phase V, priority shifts to disengaging guard forces, supporting artillery, casualties and recovery, and sustainment required to reestablish the defense.

### d. Maintenance and Recovery

Priority is restoration of the current and next phase main-effort brigade, division and corps reserves, and artillery mobility. Recovery assets will not remain east of a disengagement line after their supported force withdraws. A formation approaching the game-removal threshold will not be expended to hold nonessential terrain when displacement preserves the higher mission.

### e. Medical and Casualty Evacuation

Evacuation flows west through the nearest secure crossing to Veyra and the western support area. Divisions coordinate routes to prevent conflict with reserve movement and artillery displacement. Ordan civil authorities manage civilian casualties where conditions permit.

### f. Personnel, Detainees, and Displaced Civilians

Ordan authorities retain primary responsibility for displaced civilians and civil administration. Blue formations report civilian movements that block routes or expose civilians to combat. Detainees are handled under Western Compact policy and Ordan law and will not divert maneuver counters from essential tasks.

### g. Reconstitution

Reconstitution priority is the force required for the next decisive operation. At the end of Phase IV, the 61st Brigade receives priority necessary to assume Phase V reserve duties if its Phase IV security mission reduced readiness. After battle handover, the corps restores the most capable armored formation to reserve status as soon as conditions permit.

### h. Atlatl Abstraction

Detailed supply, engineering, medical, air defense, airpower, reconnaissance, electronic warfare, and recovery functions are incorporated into parent counters or scenario adjudication. This paragraph establishes commander priorities but does not create additional game actions unless implemented by scenario rules.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

#### (1) Command Relationships

- WCO JLC exercises operational control over the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division, 3rd Compact Armored Division, 6th Compact Mechanized Division, and 7th Coalition Armored Brigade.
- Division artillery groups remain in general support of their parent divisions.
- Cross-boundary or reinforcing fires do not change command relationships.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control until an authenticated committing order establishes a temporary support or operational-control relationship.
- The receiving division may not divide or retask the corps reserve outside the committing order without corps approval.

#### (2) Command Posts and Commander Location

The corps main command post remains in the western Ordan support area with access to Veyra and Route Blue. The corps tactical command element positions to control PP South and then the 6th Division decisive operation without displacing onto a route required for maneuver or sustainment.

Division command posts remain positioned to control assigned sectors and preserve redundant communications across the Arven. During Phase V, the 6th Division command post displaces before loss of its final secure route and remains able to control both guard sectors and the 61st Brigade reserve.

#### (3) Succession of Command

1. Commander, Western Compact-Ordan Joint Land Corps.
2. Corps deputy commander.
3. Commander, 3rd Compact Armored Division.
4. Commander, 6th Compact Mechanized Division.
5. Commander, 1st Ordan Mechanized Division.

#### (4) Liaison

- Each division maintains liaison with corps and adjacent divisions.
- The 3rd and 6th Divisions exchange liaison before the Phase II passage and retain it through passage completion.
- The 6th Division establishes liaison with both receiving divisions before Phase V guard disengagement.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade maintains liaison with all three divisions and updates route and support estimates each turn.
- Interoperability or national-command restrictions affecting an assigned task are reported immediately.

### b. Signal

- Primary communications are the Western Compact joint tactical network.
- Alternate communications are national tactical networks routed through coalition liaison teams.
- Contingency communications are prearranged reports, retransmission, relay, and messenger procedures.
- Required reports are sent by the fastest available method and repeated when the primary network is restored.
- Friendly recognition and passage procedures follow the coalition standing operating procedure. Units confirm identity before cross-boundary fires or passage through another formation.
- Phase changes, corps-reserve release, changes to objectives or phase lines, and guard disengagement require authenticated orders.

#### Communications Failure

- Subordinate formations continue their last authenticated mission within the commander's intent.
- Communications failure does not delegate corps-reserve commitment or phase-transition authority.
- Before release, the 7th Coalition Armored Brigade may reposition locally to preserve itself or maintain route access but will not begin the planned corps combat mission.
- The 6th Division guard may displace to avoid destruction when communications are unavailable, provided it preserves the crossing defense and reports at the first opportunity.

### Task Organization

The group is represented as one aggregate artillery formation with an initial Atlatl strength of 80. Organic command, target acquisition, survey, communications, local security, air defense, sustainment, maintenance, recovery, and medical functions remain incorporated in the counter. No attachments or detachments are in effect at scenario start.

## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The area of interest includes Red assembly areas and artillery positions east of Novar and Eren, the Ruda-Novar and Selin-Eren approaches, the Mora Hills, the southern Arven crossing network, the seam with the 3rd Compact Armored Division, Veyra, and routes used by the 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade. During Phase V it includes the 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade's guard sector south of PL Bronze and the battle-handover area leading into the division defense.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

Novar is the principal central-southern road junction and the decisive crossing in the division sector. Eren controls the southern crossing and the firm route through the Mora approaches. The Arven River restricts lateral movement and makes bridge access, firing range, and route clearance decisive for artillery support. The Veyra-Novar and Veyra-Eren routes carry reserve, artillery, sustainment, and casualty traffic and must not be blocked by firing or displacement activity.

Open basin terrain permits observation and long-range engagement but exposes firing positions to Red counterfire. Woods west of Novar and rough ground near Eren provide concealment while limiting observation and cross-country displacement. The group must preserve coverage of both crossings while remaining able to shift to the threatened sector or the 11th Brigade; as one aggregate Atlatl formation, it cannot provide full simultaneous support to separated actions.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for early-autumn conditions with cool mornings, generally trafficable ground, variable cloud cover, and possible fog or short rain periods. Darkness occurs during each night turn. Fog, rain, smoke, and darkness may degrade target acquisition; wet conditions may increase route dependence and displacement time. Weather effects not represented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication.

### c. Enemy Forces

The Karsovian 9th Maneuver Corps can attack on the Ruda-Novar and Selin-Eren axes while its artillery interdicts crossings, reserve routes, and Blue firing positions. It may reinforce either axis with the 5th Guards Armored Brigade or use the Mora approaches to turn Eren. High-value threats are Red artillery supporting an attack on a crossing, armored or mechanized concentrations able to reach Novar or Eren, forces preparing a bypass toward Veyra, and units threatening the Phase V guard withdrawal.

Red's most likely course is to pressure Novar and Eren while fixing the division and counterattacking the 6th Division farther east. Its most dangerous course is a rapid concentration against Novar, supported by artillery and a southern demonstration, that severs the division seam and opens the route to Veyra before the 11th Brigade can intervene.

### d. Friendly Forces

The 1st Ordan Mechanized Division secures Veyra and seizes Novar and Eren in Phase I, then retains the southern crossing sector through Phase V. The 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade is the initial main effort at Novar. The 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade seizes and retains Eren. The 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade secures Veyra and then serves as division reserve. In Phase V, the division receives the 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade disengaging south of PL Bronze and assumes responsibility for the fight after battle handover.

The 3rd Compact Armored Division defends north of the division boundary. The 6th Compact Mechanized Division attacks east through Phases II-IV and guards along Guard Line Steel in Phase V. The 6th Division retains responsibility for the guard fight until designated elements clear the BHL and the receiving division acknowledges responsibility.

### e. Civil Considerations

Veyra, Novar, Eren, bridge approaches, and connecting roads contain civilians and critical government, medical, and transportation infrastructure. Fires require positive identification and proportional effects appropriate to the scenario. Preserve bridges and essential infrastructure unless destruction is authorized to prevent a greater operational loss.

### f. Assumptions

- No separate observer, radar, air, ammunition, or sustainment counters are created.
- Exact firing areas, range, target eligibility, ammunition effects, and combat results follow Atlatl and scenario-control adjudication.
- The group can retain practical coverage of both Novar and Eren from setup-designated positions but must displace or accept reduced responsiveness if the decisive fight shifts substantially.
- Cross-boundary fires do not change the group's general-support relationship.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2030 at 0600 local, the **1st Ordan Artillery Group provides general support to the 1st Ordan Mechanized Division, suppresses Red forces defending the southern Arven crossings, disrupts Red crossing and turning attempts, conducts counterfire, protects division reserve movement, and supports the Phase V guard disengagement and battle handover in order to enable the division to secure Veyra, retain Novar and Eren, and deny Red penetration into the western Ordan support area.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander's Intent

**Purpose:** Place responsive, survivable fires at the most dangerous point in the southern crossing defense while preserving the division's mobile response.

**Key tasks:** Support seizure of Novar and Eren; maintain coverage of both crossings; conduct counterfire; shift rapidly to the threatened crossing or committed reserve; support the 62nd Brigade's disengagement south of PL Bronze; transfer fires without a gap at battle handover; preserve ammunition, range, mobility, and survivability.

**End state:** Novar and Eren remain under Blue control; Red artillery and maneuver concentrations cannot mass without disruption; the 62nd Brigade disengages in order; the fight transfers to the 1st Division without a gap in fires; and the artillery group remains capable of supporting the crossing defense through scenario end.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Phase I - Secure Veyra and Seize Novar and Eren

Provide general support with priority to the 13th, then 12th, then 11th Brigade. Suppress crossing defenders at Novar and Eren, conduct counterfire, and protect the seizure forces and their approaches. Maintain enough range and route access to shift between the two objectives. This phase ends when Veyra is secure, Novar and Eren and their crossings are controlled, the seam and routes are open, and the 11th Brigade is reserve-ready.

#### (2) Phase II - Retain During the Kasar Attack

Protect Novar, Eren, Veyra, and the southern crossing sector while the 6th Division attacks Kasar. Initial priority remains 13th, 12th, then 11th Brigade. Shift to the crossing under the most immediate threat or to the 11th Brigade upon authenticated commitment. Conduct counterfire and disrupt Red formations preparing to force a crossing. This phase ends on corps transition to Phase III; crossing-defense tasks continue without interruption.

#### (3) Phase III - Retain and Protect the Southern Flank

Disrupt Red attempts to cross at Novar or Eren, turn the division through the Mora approaches, or exploit the division seam. Protect movement of the 11th Brigade and, when essential crossing tasks remain supported, execute corps-directed cross-boundary fires. This phase ends on corps transition to Phase IV.

#### (4) Phase IV - Retain and Prepare Guard Reception

Continue crossing defense and counterfire. Prepare targets, liaison, communications, recognition procedures, firing positions, and displacement routes for the Phase V reception of the 62nd Brigade south of PL Bronze. Preserve ammunition needed to support disengagement and defeat the following Red main body. This phase ends when Guard Line Steel and PL Bronze are effective, the 1st Division is prepared to receive the fight, and corps authorizes Phase V.

#### (5) Phase V - Support the Southern Corps Defense

Give priority first to the 62nd Brigade or other designated guard element in contact or disengaging into the southern sector. Suppress Red forces threatening withdrawal, obscure designated withdrawal routes when directed, and prevent Red from closing on the guard before it clears the BHL. After the 62nd Brigade clears the BHL, the 1st Division acknowledges responsibility, and fires coordination is transferred, shift priority to the Red force entering the division engagement area, the threatened crossing, or the committed 11th Brigade as directed. This phase continues through scenario end or subsequent order.

### c. Scheme of Fires

#### (1) Priority Hierarchy

1. **Phase I:** 13th, 12th, then 11th Brigade.
2. **Phases II-III:** The brigade defending the crossing threatened by a Red force capable of penetrating west; otherwise 13th, 12th, then 11th Brigade.
3. **Phase IV:** Threatened crossing while preserving Phase V reception fires.
4. **Phase V before battle handover:** The 62nd Brigade or designated southern guard element in contact or disengaging.
5. **Phase V after battle handover:** The crossing threatened by the Red main body, then the 11th Brigade when committed as the division decisive operation.
6. **Cross-boundary targets:** On corps or division order when support to the division's essential crossing tasks remains adequate.

The division commander directs priority shifts. The group recommends a shift whenever the enemy threat, supported brigade capability, range, route access, or survivability changes. A priority designation does not imply simultaneous full support to all listed formations.

#### (2) Essential Fire-Support Tasks

| Task | Phase | Action and target | Purpose | Effect / measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **B-1F-EFST 1** | I | Suppress Red forces defending Novar and Eren and conduct counterfire | Enable the 13th and 12th Brigades to seize their crossings | Assault brigades reach and secure the objectives without Red fires preventing the crossing seizure |
| **B-1F-EFST 2** | II-IV | Disrupt Red formations preparing to cross the Arven or turn the southern flank | Preserve Novar, Eren, Veyra, and division freedom of action | Red cannot mass an organized assault without being identified and engaged; reserve routes remain usable |
| **B-1F-EFST 3** | II-V | Suppress threats to the 11th Brigade's passage, block, or counterattack mission | Enable the division reserve to restore the crossing defense | Reserve reaches the assigned area and completes its task with protected flanks and routes |
| **B-1F-EFST 4** | V | Suppress Red forces threatening the 62nd Brigade and obscure its withdrawal as directed | Preserve the southern guard force and enable orderly disengagement | Guard clears the BHL without being fixed, enveloped, or cut off |
| **B-1F-EFST 5** | V | Transfer fires to the southern crossing defense after handover | Prevent a gap as responsibility passes to the 1st Division | Receiving brigade acknowledges responsibility and engages the following Red force while withdrawal routes remain open |

#### (3) Target Priorities

1. Red artillery directly supporting an attack on Novar, Eren, or the guard withdrawal.
2. Armored and mechanized concentrations able to reach a crossing or Veyra.
3. Forces threatening the 62nd Brigade's disengagement route or BHL.
4. Penetration shoulders, crossing approaches, and forces blocking the 11th Brigade.
5. Red command, control, and sustainment nodes whose disruption directly supports the current essential task.

### d. Scheme of Movement and Survivability

- Occupy setup-designated positions that cover Novar and Eren while retaining lateral and westward displacement routes.
- Do not block Veyra-Novar or Veyra-Eren reserve, evacuation, or sustainment traffic.
- Displace before enemy action, range loss, route closure, or battle-handover requirements make the current position untenable.
- During Phase V, keep the group west of the receiving defense unless the division commander explicitly accepts the risk and assigns a withdrawal plan.
- Use primary, alternate, and supplementary positions narratively within Atlatl; they do not create additional counters or split the group.

### e. Tasks to Organic Capabilities

- Maintain target acquisition on Red artillery, crossing approaches, Mora bypass indicators, and guard-withdrawal threats.
- Maintain continuous liaison with division headquarters and the 11th, 12th, and 13th Brigades.
- Establish liaison and fires coordination with the 62nd Brigade and 6th Division Artillery before Phase V.
- Prepare primary and alternate firing and displacement areas consistent with the approved overlay.
- Preserve ammunition for crossing defense, reserve commitment, and Phase V handover.
- Maintain local security, air-defense awareness, communications, maintenance, recovery, and casualty care internally.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Fire-Support Triggers

- Shift to Novar or Eren when a Red force can attack or isolate that crossing before the defending brigade can defeat it.
- Shift to the 11th Brigade on authenticated division commitment and confirmation of its route and task.
- Begin Phase V guard support when the 62nd Brigade enters the southern handover area or reports that disengagement fires are required.
- Transfer from guard support to crossing defense only after the 62nd Brigade clears the BHL, the receiving division acknowledges responsibility, and friendly locations are confirmed.
- Recommend immediate displacement when counterfire threat, route closure, or range loss could prevent the current or next essential task.

#### (2) Clearance and Constraints

- Confirm friendly locations before fires near the division seam, BHL, crossing approaches, populated areas, or intermingled guard and receiving forces.
- Do not uncover both Novar and Eren to support a nonessential cross-boundary target.
- Do not fire solely to restore nonessential terrain east of the BHL after the guard's delay purpose is achieved.
- Preserve bridges and essential civil infrastructure unless destruction is authorized by higher headquarters.
- Do not assume effects or ammunition expenditure beyond Atlatl mechanics and agreed adjudication.

#### (3) CCIR

**PIR:** Red main effort toward Novar or Eren; location and activity of Red artillery; armored concentration able to reach a crossing; Mora bypass indicators; Red main body's approach to the southern guard sector; threats to the 62nd Brigade's withdrawal route.

**FFIR:** Group below 70 percent nominal combat power; inability to support Novar, Eren, the committed reserve, or the guard handover; loss of communications with the priority formation; ammunition or mobility insufficient for the next essential task; firing position compromised; displacement or withdrawal route blocked; friendly positions uncertain near a requested target.

#### (4) Reports

Report each turn: current priority formation; supported essential task; range and coverage; readiness and strength; ammunition status; current and next firing position; displacement-route status; and ability to support the current and next phase. Report high-value target acquisition, recommended or directed priority shifts, inability to execute an essential task, guard-contact and handover status, and all FFIR immediately.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept

Sustainment preserves continuous coverage of Novar and Eren while retaining the mobility to support the division reserve and Phase V reception. Support moves on the Veyra road network under division movement control and remains clear of maneuver and evacuation routes.

### b. Priorities

1. Ammunition for crossing seizure and counterfire in Phase I.
2. Ammunition for the crossing under immediate threat.
3. Ammunition and fuel for a committed 11th Brigade mission.
4. Phase V guard disengagement, obscuration, and handover fires.
5. Fuel, maintenance, recovery, communications, and medical support needed to preserve mobility and aggregate fires capability.

### c. Transportation, Maintenance, and Recovery

Coordinate all artillery movement with division movement control. Do not displace on the same route and window required by the 11th Brigade or withdrawing guard forces unless specifically integrated. Repair priority goes to systems that preserve range, communications, mobility, and aggregate combat capability. Recover disabled systems when doing so does not risk loss of the group or block a decisive route.

### d. Medical and Abstraction

Casualties evacuate west through the Veyra support area or a division-approved alternate. Detailed ammunition handling, survey, target acquisition, maintenance, medical, air-defense, and recovery functions remain incorporated in the artillery counter and are adjudicated narratively.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

The group remains in general support under 1st Ordan Mechanized Division control. The artillery command post occupies a setup-designated protected position that enables centralized fires control and displacement toward either crossing. Succession is group commander, deputy commander, then senior organic fires commander. Liaison remains with division and all maneuver brigades; liaison with the 62nd Brigade and 6th Division Artillery is established before Phase V.

### b. Signal

Primary communications use division fires and command networks. Alternate communications use WCO fires networks through liaison. Contingency communications use relay, prearranged schedules, and messenger. Crossing threats, reserve commitment, guard disengagement, battle handover, priority shifts, and FFIR have precedence. Under degraded communications, support the last confirmed priority and do not fire near uncertain friendly positions.

## Acknowledgment

Organic leaders acknowledge and backbrief priorities, essential fire-support tasks, priority-shift triggers, displacement criteria, Phase V liaison and transfer conditions, protected infrastructure, and degraded-communications procedures.

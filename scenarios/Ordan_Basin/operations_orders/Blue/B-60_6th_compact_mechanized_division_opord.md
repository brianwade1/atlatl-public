## 1. Situation

### a. Area of Interest

The division area of interest extends from AA Axe and PP South east through Kasar, Ruda, and Selin to the Karsovian frontier. It includes the 3rd Division crossing defense and passage area, the 1st Division sector near Novar and Eren, the Saren and Mora flank approaches, Guard Line Steel, and Red routes capable of supporting a counterattack.

The division must monitor Red forces defending or reinforcing Kasar, Ruda, and Selin; the location of the 5th Guards Armored Brigade; attacks against PP South or the line of communication; and Red formations assembling east of Guard Line Steel for the anticipated counterattack.

### b. Area of Operations

#### (1) Terrain

- **PP South and the BHL:** The division depends on a controlled forward passage through the 3rd Division. Congestion, bridge damage, or loss of the passage lane can prevent the division from entering the fight as a coherent force.
- **Kasar:** A northeastern staging and logistics location connected to the Saren route and the northern basin road. Its defenders can threaten the division northern flank and the route toward Ruda.
- **Ruda:** The seat of the Red-recognized emergency council and the principal eastern hub. Roads from Kasar, Novar, and Selin meet near Ruda, allowing Red to reinforce or counterattack in several directions.
- **Selin:** A southeastern staging area connected to Ruda, Eren, and the Mora approaches. The marsh west and northwest of Selin restricts cross-country maneuver and channels the attack onto the road network.
- **Open basin:** Clear terrain favors the division's armored and mechanized mobility but exposes formations to observation, artillery, and flank attack.
- **Saren Heights and Mora Hills:** These features bound the division maneuver corridor and can conceal bypassing or reinforcing forces. The division cannot secure both flanks through continuous occupation and must rely on surveillance, tempo, and the corps crossing defense.
- **Guard Line Steel:** A forward security line, not the corps main defensive position. It provides depth and warning for the Arven crossing defense. PL Bronze divides the 63rd Brigade's northern guard sector from the 62nd Brigade's southern guard sector.
- **Road network:** The PP South-Kasar-Ruda-Selin routes are the division's primary maneuver and sustainment network. The division must prevent a single blockage from trapping artillery or the guard force east of the Arven.

#### (2) Weather and Light

Plan for the early-autumn conditions described in B-00. Fog, rain, smoke, or darkness can reduce observation in open terrain and delay recognition of a Red counterattack. Wet conditions will increase dependence on the Kasar-Ruda-Selin roads and may restrict movement near the southeastern marsh. The division will displace artillery and sustainment before routes become congested or threatened. Weather effects not implemented in Atlatl require consistent scenario-control adjudication.

### c. Enemy Forces

#### (1) Composition and Disposition

The division may engage elements of both Karsovian maneuver corps:

- **4th Maneuver Corps:** 8th Guards Armored Division, 15th Mechanized Division, and 4th Corps Artillery Group, oriented generally on Kasar and the northern basin.
- **9th Maneuver Corps:** 12th Armored Division, 21st Mechanized Division, and 9th Corps Artillery Group, oriented generally on Ruda, Selin, and the central-southern basin.
- **5th Guards Armored Brigade:** Eastern Army reserve, capable of reinforcing a city defense, attacking the division line of communication, or leading the Phase V counterattack.

Red may defend the eastern hubs with infantry and mechanized forces while holding armor for counterattack. Exact allocation of forces and the Eastern Army main effort remain uncertain.

#### (2) Capabilities and Limitations

Red can mass against the division lead brigade, reinforce between Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, attack the PP South line of communication, and commit armor against an exposed flank. Artillery can interdict the passage, isolate an objective, or delay the guard disengagement.

Red is limited by divided corps control, the need to protect three hubs and frontier routes, and the risk that forces fixed in one location cannot reinforce the next. Blue tempo and sequential main-effort shifts can defeat dispersed defenders before the army reserve restores coherence.

#### (3) Most Likely Course of Action

Red defends Kasar, Ruda, and Selin with mutually supporting forces while mobile formations delay between them. The threatened corps fixes the Blue main effort at the city while adjacent forces attack a flank or the line of communication. After Blue reaches Selin or culminates, the Eastern Army commits the 5th Guards Armored Brigade and surviving armored forces in a coordinated counterattack toward Ruda, Novar, and the Arven crossings.

#### (4) Most Dangerous Course of Action

Red allows Blue to enter through PP South, then masses the 5th Guards Armored Brigade and armored elements of both corps against the division rear near the BHL while artillery blocks the crossing. Simultaneous pressure from Kasar and Ruda isolates the division east of the Arven before the corps reserve can reopen the route.

### d. Friendly Forces

#### (1) Higher Headquarters Mission and Intent

WCO JLC seizes and retains the Arven crossings, passes this division through PP South, destroys Red forces at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, and then establishes a defense in depth at the crossings while this division guards along Guard Line Steel.

The corps purpose is to deny Karsovia the position required to partition Orda or replace the elected government. This division becomes the corps main effort in Phase II and remains so through establishment and conduct of the Phase V guard.

#### (2) Adjacent and Supporting Units

- **3rd Compact Armored Division:** Seizes and retains North Pass Bridge, Dalen, and Central Link Bridge. It controls PP South and supports this division's passage.
- **1st Ordan Mechanized Division:** Secures Veyra and retains Novar and Eren. It establishes the southern portion of the Phase V corps defense.
- **7th Coalition Armored Brigade:** Corps reserve. It may reinforce the division, secure PP South or a withdrawal route, or counterattack a Red penetration on corps order.
- **3rd Division Artillery Group:** Remains in general support of the 3rd Division and may provide reinforcing or cross-boundary fires on corps order.

### e. Civil Considerations

Kasar, Ruda, and Selin contain civilians, political institutions, and transportation infrastructure. The division destroys organized Red combat power rather than occupying the population. Avoid unnecessary damage to bridges, hospitals, utilities, and government records. Civilian movement may congest routes required for attack, sustainment, or withdrawal.

### f. Attachments and Detachments

None. Supporting fires from another division do not change command relationships unless an authenticated order states otherwise.

### g. Assumptions

- PP South is opened by the 3rd Division before Phase II.
- The 1st and 3rd Divisions retain the Arven crossings while this division operates east of the river.
- The division receives no additional playable maneuver formation unless committed by corps or introduced by scenario event.
- Destruction of the Red force at a city does not require enduring occupation of that city.
- The corps crossing defense is prepared to receive the Phase V fight before the division disengages from Guard Line Steel.

## 2. Mission

Beginning 12 September 2031 at 0600 local, the **6th Compact Mechanized Division follows and supports the 3rd Compact Armored Division, conducts a forward passage at PP South, attacks along Axes Black, Blue, and Brown to destroy Red forces sequentially at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, and then guards along Guard Line Steel in order to disrupt the Karsovian Eastern Army, provide warning and delay, and preserve the corps defense of the Arven crossings.**

## 3. Execution

### a. Commander's Intent

#### Purpose

Dislocate the Eastern Army by destroying its separated forces before they can combine, then impose delay without sacrificing the division east of the Arven.

#### Key Tasks

- Complete the forward passage at PP South as a coherent division.
- Maintain tempo and shift the main effort sequentially from the 61st to the 62nd to the 63rd Brigade.
- Destroy Red forces at Kasar, Ruda, and Selin; do not assume an enduring requirement to hold the cities.
- Protect the line of communication through PP South and the division artillery.
- Conduct the Kasar security handover between the 63rd and 61st Brigades before the Selin attack.
- Establish the Phase V guard with the 63rd Brigade north of PL Bronze and the 62nd Brigade south of it.
- Retain the 61st Brigade as the Phase V division reserve.
- Avoid decisive engagement during the guard and preserve an orderly route to the corps defense.

#### End State

- **Enemy:** Red forces defending Kasar, Ruda, and Selin are destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; the counterattack is detected, disrupted, and delayed before reaching the Arven crossing defense.
- **Friendly:** The division remains coherent; its guard forces retain freedom to disengage; the 61st Brigade is reserve-ready or has enabled disengagement; and artillery and sustainment remain westward mobile.
- **Terrain:** The line of communication to PP South remains usable until no longer required. Kasar, Ruda, and Selin need not remain occupied after the required enemy effects are achieved.
- **Civil:** Essential infrastructure is preserved consistent with the mission and civil control returns to Ordan authorities where possible.

### b. Concept of Operations

#### (1) Overall Concept

The division conducts five phases. In Phase I, it follows and supports the 3rd Division and prepares for passage. In Phase II, the 61st Brigade leads the attack along Axis Black to destroy the Kasar defenders while the 62nd Brigade fixes and isolates the objective and the 63rd Brigade serves as reserve. In Phase III, the 62nd Brigade leads along Axis Blue to destroy the Ruda defenders while the 61st Brigade fixes and protects the attack and the 63rd Brigade secures the rear near Kasar. In Phase IV, the 63rd Brigade leads along Axis Brown to destroy the Selin defenders while the 62nd Brigade fixes and isolates the objective and the 61st Brigade secures the Kasar-Ruda corridor. In Phase V, the 63rd Brigade guards north of PL Bronze, the 62nd Brigade guards south, and the 61st Brigade serves as reserve.

Tempo, isolation, and handover are central to the operation. The division avoids simultaneous decisive fights at more than one objective. A brigade that has completed the decisive operation transitions to a supporting or security task while the next brigade becomes main effort. The division artillery displaces by echelon and massed fires follow the main-effort shift.

#### (2) Phase I - Follow, Support, and Prepare Passage

The division moves from AA Axe behind the 3rd Division. The 61st Brigade prepares to lead the passage and Phase II attack. The 62nd Brigade follows prepared to support the Kasar attack. The 63rd Brigade remains positioned to become the Phase II reserve and secure the passage lane if required. The 6th Division Artillery Group displaces with the division and coordinates continuous coverage with 3rd Division Artillery.

Phase I ends when Central Link Bridge and PP South are secure and trafficable, passage control is established, and the division is postured in the approved order of march.

#### (3) Phase II - Destroy Red Forces at Kasar

The division passes through the 3rd Division at PP South, accepts responsibility east of the BHL, and attacks along Axis Black while remaining north of PL Gold. The 61st Brigade is main effort and destroys the Red force defending Kasar. The 62nd Brigade fixes the southern portion of the Kasar defense and isolates the objective from reinforcement or withdrawal. The 63rd Brigade remains division reserve. Division artillery protects the passage, isolates Kasar, suppresses defenders, and conducts counterfire with priority to the 61st Brigade.

Phase II ends when the Kasar force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; routes through or around Kasar are open; the 63rd Brigade has assumed temporary local security; and the 61st and 62nd Brigades are postured for Ruda.

#### (4) Phase III - Destroy Red Forces at Ruda

The division attacks along Axis Blue while remaining north of PL Silver. The 62nd Brigade is main effort and destroys the Red force defending Ruda. The 61st Brigade fixes the Ruda defenders and protects the 62nd Brigade's northern flank. The 63rd Brigade conducts temporary area defense near Kasar to protect the division rear and route network. Division artillery isolates Ruda, interdicts reinforcement from Selin, and shifts priority to the 62nd Brigade.

Phase III ends when the Ruda force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; the division rear remains secure; the 61st Brigade is prepared to accept the Kasar-Ruda security task; and the 62nd and 63rd Brigades are postured for Selin.

#### (5) Phase IV - Destroy Red Forces at Selin

The 63rd Brigade transfers Kasar security to the 61st Brigade, then becomes the division main effort and attacks along Axis Brown to destroy the Red force defending Selin. The 62nd Brigade fixes the Selin defenders and isolates the objective from reinforcement or withdrawal. The 61st Brigade conducts temporary area security of the Kasar-Ruda corridor to protect the division rear and lines of communication. Division artillery isolates Selin, protects the northern flank, and shifts priority to the 63rd Brigade.

Phase IV ends when the Selin force is destroyed or rendered combat ineffective; the division has sufficient combat power and route access to establish the guard; Guard Line Steel and PL Bronze are in effect; and the corps crossing defense is prepared to receive the fight.

#### (6) Phase V - Guard Along Guard Line Steel

The 63rd Brigade is the division main effort and guards north of PL Bronze. The 62nd Brigade guards south of PL Bronze. Both guard brigades detect, disrupt, and delay the Red counterattack from successive positions while avoiding decisive engagement. The 61st Brigade remains the division reserve near the central route network. Division artillery initially prioritizes the 63rd Brigade, then the 62nd, then the 61st, shifting to the brigade in contact or disengaging.

The division transfers the fight to the 1st and 3rd Divisions after guard forces clear the designated battle-handover line and the receiving formation acknowledges responsibility. The phase continues until battle handover is complete or corps directs a subsequent operation.

### c. Scheme of Maneuver

#### (1) 61st Coalition Armored Brigade

Lead the Phase II passage and attack to destroy the Kasar defenders. In Phase III, fix the Ruda defense and protect the 62nd Brigade's northern flank. In Phase IV, accept temporary security of the Kasar-Ruda corridor from the 63rd Brigade. Reconstitute sufficiently to become the Phase V division reserve.

In Phase V, remain concentrated and positioned to reach either guard sector, protect the artillery, or secure a withdrawal route. Preferred employment is a concentrated block or counterattack that enables guard disengagement. Do not become fixed in an attempt to retake nonessential terrain.

#### (2) 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade

Support the Phase II Kasar attack by fixing the southern defenders and isolating the objective. Become the Phase III main effort and destroy the Ruda defenders. Support the Phase IV Selin attack by fixing and isolating the objective. In Phase V, guard along Guard Line Steel south of PL Bronze and withdraw by successive positions under division control.

#### (3) 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade

Serve as the Phase II division reserve. After destruction of the Kasar force, assume temporary area defense near Kasar during Phase III. Transfer that security task to the 61st Brigade, then become the Phase IV main effort and destroy the Selin defenders. In Phase V, remain the division main effort and guard north of PL Bronze.

The brigade's Phase V task is to provide early warning and impose delay, not to hold Guard Line Steel at the cost of decisive engagement. It requests disengagement before loss of a viable route or freedom of maneuver.

### d. Scheme of Fires

The 6th Division Artillery Group remains in general support and displaces by echelon to maintain continuous coverage. Priorities are:

- **Phase I:** Support passage preparation and preserve coverage during movement.
- **Phase II:** 61st, 62nd, 63rd. Protect passage; isolate Kasar; suppress defenders; conduct counterfire.
- **Phase III:** 62nd, 61st, 63rd. Isolate Ruda; interdict reinforcement from Selin; protect the northern flank; conduct counterfire.
- **Phase IV:** 63rd, 62nd, 61st. Isolate Selin; suppress defenders; protect the northern flank; preserve Phase V ammunition.
- **Phase V:** Initially 63rd, 62nd, 61st; shift to the brigade in contact or disengaging. Disrupt Red east of Guard Line Steel, suppress forces threatening withdrawal, obscure routes, and protect battle handover.

The group coordinates with 3rd Division Artillery before the passage and with 1st and 3rd Division Artillery before the Phase V handover. Artillery will displace before a single route, bridge, or enemy action can trap the group east of the receiving defense.

### e. Tasks to Subordinate Formations

#### (1) 61st Coalition Armored Brigade

- Prepare as the lead Phase II force and pass at PP South. **Purpose:** Preserve division tempo and introduce the decisive force first.
- Attack to destroy the Red force defending Kasar. **Purpose:** Accomplish the Phase II decisive operation.
- Fix the Ruda defense and protect the 62nd Brigade's northern flank in Phase III. **Purpose:** Enable destruction of the Ruda force.
- Accept temporary security of the Kasar-Ruda corridor in Phase IV. **Purpose:** Protect the division rear during the Selin attack.
- Serve as division reserve in Phase V. **Purpose:** Reinforce either guard sector, block a penetration, protect artillery, or enable disengagement.

#### (2) 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade

- Fix and isolate the southern Kasar defense in Phase II. **Purpose:** Enable the 61st Brigade to destroy the Kasar force.
- Attack to destroy the Red force defending Ruda in Phase III. **Purpose:** Accomplish the division decisive operation.
- Fix and isolate the Selin defense in Phase IV. **Purpose:** Enable the 63rd Brigade to destroy the Selin force.
- Guard along Guard Line Steel south of PL Bronze in Phase V. **Purpose:** Detect, disrupt, and delay the southern portion of the counterattack while preserving freedom to disengage.

#### (3) 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade

- Remain division reserve in Phase II. **Purpose:** Reinforce success, defeat a counterattack, or protect the passage and flank.
- Conduct temporary area defense near Kasar in Phase III. **Purpose:** Protect the division rear and route network.
- Transfer Kasar security to the 61st Brigade and attack to destroy the Red force defending Selin in Phase IV. **Purpose:** Accomplish the division decisive operation.
- Guard north of PL Bronze in Phase V as division main effort. **Purpose:** Provide early warning and impose delay against the northern portion of the counterattack.

#### (4) 6th Division Artillery Group - General Support

- Provide general support to the division.
- Execute the priorities and essential fire-support tasks in paragraph 3.d and Annex C.
- Coordinate continuous fires during passage, objective transitions, and guard disengagement.
- Preserve ammunition and westward mobility for Phase V.

### f. Coordinating Instructions

#### (1) Effective Time and Phase Control

- This order is effective on receipt. Phase I begins 12 September 2031 at 0600 local.
- Corps controls phase transitions. The division commander recommends transition when the stated conditions are met.
- A brigade continues its current essential task until the receiving brigade confirms handover.

#### (2) Control Measures

- AA Axe, PP South, BHL, Axes Black, Blue, and Brown, PL Gold, PL Silver, Guard Line Steel, PL Bronze, objectives, and division boundaries are as shown on the approved overlay.
- Remain north of PL Gold during the Phase II Kasar attack and north of PL Silver during the Phase III Ruda attack.
- PL Bronze separates Phase V guard sectors and is not a movement corridor.
- Kasar, Ruda, and Selin are enemy-force objectives; destruction of the defending force does not create an enduring retain mission.

#### (3) Forward Passage at PP South

**Before passage**

- Exchange liaison with the 3rd Division and 31st Brigade.
- Provide order of march, movement tables, route density, recognition signals, recovery plan, artillery sequence, and support requirements.
- Confirm PP South trafficability, passage-lane security, BHL conditions, alternate-crossing procedures, and fire-support coordination.
- Order of march supports the Phase II tactical plan: 61st Brigade lead, 62nd Brigade follow, division command and support as directed, 6th Division Artillery by echelon, and 63rd Brigade positioned to retain reserve responsiveness.

**During passage**

- The 3rd Division controls PP South and the passage lane.
- This division maintains movement discipline, recovery, positive identification, and contact with the stationary force.
- Units do not halt in the passage lane except for immediate combat or safety requirements.
- The division reports lead element through PP South, lead element at BHL, artillery coverage established, rear element clear, and passage complete.

**Battle handover**

- This division assumes responsibility east of the BHL when the lead brigade crosses it, communications and fires coordination are established, and both division commanders confirm handover.
- The 3rd Division retains the bridge and western approach.
- If PP South becomes unusable, halt short of the passage, preserve combat power, and submit an alternate-crossing plan to corps. Do not redirect through another division sector without approval.

#### (4) Phase III-IV Security Handover

Before the 63rd Brigade departs Kasar for Selin, the 61st Brigade accepts responsibility for temporary security of the Kasar-Ruda corridor. Handover requires command-post liaison, enemy and route information, obstacle and traffic information, fire-support coordination, sustainment status, and an explicit report of responsibility transferred.

The 63rd Brigade remains responsible until the 61st Brigade acknowledges the task. The 61st Brigade establishes only the security necessary to protect the rear and does not become decisively engaged to retain either city.

#### (5) Phase II Division Reserve

The 63rd Brigade remains concentrated and uncommitted until released by the division commander. Commit it when the Kasar attack requires reinforcement; Red counterattacks from Ruda or the Saren route; the passage lane or southern flank is threatened; or a time-sensitive opportunity will complete destruction of the Kasar force.

The committing order specifies task, purpose, route, supported formation, priority of fires, limit of advance, termination, and follow-on security requirement.

#### (6) Phase V Division Reserve

The 61st Brigade remains concentrated and uncommitted. Commit it to:

- block a penetration of Guard Line Steel;
- counterattack a Red force threatening to divide the guard sectors;
- restore contact when loss of contact threatens warning or delay;
- extract a guard brigade at risk of decisive engagement;
- protect 6th Division Artillery; or
- secure a withdrawal route or battle-handover area.

The brigade does not counterattack solely to restore Guard Line Steel after the delay purpose has been achieved. It terminates when the guard regains freedom to disengage, the threatened route is secure, or further action risks loss of the reserve before battle handover.

#### (7) Guard Disengagement and Battle Handover

- Guard brigades delay from successive positions and avoid decisive engagement.
- Request disengagement when continued occupation would prevent orderly withdrawal, the Red main body threatens bypass or envelopment, or the corps-directed delay requirement is achieved.
- The division commander controls sequencing. Normally, the less heavily engaged brigade displaces first while the other maintains contact.
- Maintain contact with the Red main body without allowing a brigade to be fixed against a river crossing or blocked route.
- Artillery shifts priority to the brigade in contact or disengaging and displaces by echelon.
- The 1st and 3rd Divisions control their reception areas and accept the fight only after positive coordination.
- Battle handover is complete when designated guard elements clear the BHL, the receiving division confirms responsibility, fires are transferred, and routes are clear for continued withdrawal or reconstitution.

#### (8) Commander's Critical Information Requirements

**PIR**

1. What Red formation defends Kasar, Ruda, and Selin, and where are its flanks and withdrawal routes?
2. Where and when will the 5th Guards Armored Brigade commit?
3. Can Red reinforce the current objective from the next city before the main-effort brigade attacks?
4. Is Red attacking or interdicting PP South or the division line of communication?
5. Where is the Red Phase V main body, and which guard sector will it enter first?
6. Is Red attempting to bypass the guard through the Saren Heights or Mora Hills?

**FFIR**

1. The current main-effort brigade falls below 70 percent nominal combat power or cannot destroy the assigned Red force.
2. PP South, the line of communication, or a withdrawal route is lost, blocked, or threatened.
3. Division artillery cannot support the current objective, next phase, or guard disengagement.
4. The Phase II or Phase V division reserve loses readiness or is committed.
5. A brigade cannot complete a required handover or is at risk of decisive engagement.
6. Fuel, ammunition, recovery, or casualty evacuation cannot support the next phase.

#### (9) Risk Controls and Constraints

- Do not begin a new objective fight before the preceding enemy force is destroyed or contained and the rear-security handover is complete.
- Do not allow all three brigades to become decisively engaged simultaneously.
- Maintain at least one route to PP South or a corps-approved alternate until the Phase V handover is complete.
- Displace artillery, command posts, and sustainment before their withdrawal depends on one threatened route.
- Preserve the Phase V reserve; do not commit it to hold nonessential terrain.
- Protect civilians and infrastructure consistent with mission accomplishment.

#### (10) Required Reports

- Contact and identified-enemy report after each new engagement.
- Strength and mission-capability report each turn and on crossing an FFIR threshold.
- Passage, BHL, objective, route, artillery, and sustainment status whenever changed.
- Main-effort transition and rear-security handover reports.
- Reserve readiness, location, route, and commitment status each turn in Phases II and V.
- Guard contact, Red main-body estimate, delay achieved, disengagement readiness, withdrawal-route status, and battle-handover reports during Phase V.

## 4. Sustainment

### a. Concept of Support

Division support flows from the western Ordan support area through PP South and then along the Kasar-Ruda-Selin road network. The division carries sufficient fuel, ammunition, recovery, and medical capacity through the passage to maintain tempo while preserving the ability to disengage west. Support nodes displace by echelon and do not concentrate where loss of one route can isolate the division.

### b. Priorities of Support

- **Phase I:** Passage preparation, 61st Brigade readiness, artillery displacement, and route recovery.
- **Phase II:** 61st Brigade, 6th Division Artillery, 62nd Brigade, 63rd Brigade reserve readiness.
- **Phase III:** 62nd Brigade, 6th Division Artillery, 61st Brigade, 63rd Brigade rear security.
- **Phase IV:** 63rd Brigade, 6th Division Artillery, 62nd Brigade, restoration of 61st Brigade reserve readiness.
- **Phase V:** Guard brigade in contact or disengaging, 61st Brigade if committed, artillery displacement, withdrawal-route security, and reconstitution.

### c. Supply and Transportation

Fuel and ammunition are pacing requirements. Main-effort brigades receive first priority, but the division maintains minimum emergency stocks for the next phase and guard disengagement. The passage movement table separates maneuver, artillery, sustainment, recovery, and casualty-evacuation traffic.

During Phase V, disengaging guard forces and supporting artillery have movement priority. Sustainment elements displace before the guard routes become contested. No support node remains east of a line after its supported maneuver force withdraws unless ordered to enable recovery or casualty evacuation.

### d. Maintenance and Recovery

Recovery priority follows the current main effort, then the next phase main effort and reserve. Equipment that cannot be recovered without endangering the guard is handled under scenario-control procedures. Disabled vehicles will not block PP South, an objective route, or a withdrawal route.

### e. Medical and Casualty Evacuation

Casualty evacuation moves west through PP South or a corps-approved alternate. Phase V evacuation is synchronized with guard displacement and receiving-division traffic control. Civilian casualties are coordinated with Ordan authorities where possible.

### f. Personnel, Detainees, and Displaced Civilians

Ordan authorities resume civil control after tactical conditions permit. Brigades report civilian movement that blocks routes or exposes civilians to combat. Detainee handling follows Western Compact policy and Ordan law.

### g. Reconstitution

After each objective, the next main-effort brigade receives priority for fuel, ammunition, repair, and command-system restoration. During Phase IV, the 61st Brigade receives priority sufficient to assume Phase V reserve duties. After battle handover, the division reconstitutes the most capable armored or mechanized formation as directed by corps.

### h. Atlatl Abstraction

Detailed supply, engineering, medical, reconnaissance, air defense, aviation, electronic warfare, and recovery functions are incorporated into parent counters or scenario adjudication.

## 5. Command and Signal

### a. Command

#### (1) Command Relationships

- The division has operational control of the 61st, 62nd, and 63rd Brigades.
- The 6th Division Artillery Group is in general support.
- The 63rd Brigade is division reserve in Phase II; the 61st Brigade is division reserve in Phase V.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control unless B-00 directs a temporary relationship after commitment.
- Passage and battle handover do not change operational control of division formations.

#### (2) Command Posts and Commander Location

The division main command post displaces through PP South after the passage lane is secure and remains positioned to control the current and next objective without losing redundant communications to corps. Before Phase V, it displaces west of any route likely to be cut and remains able to control both guard sectors and the 61st Brigade.

The tactical command element positions with the current main effort or at the point of main-effort transition. It does not block the artillery or withdrawal route.

#### (3) Succession of Command

1. Commander, 6th Compact Mechanized Division.
2. Division deputy commander.
3. Commander, 61st Coalition Armored Brigade.
4. Commander, 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade.
5. Commander, 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade.

#### (4) Liaison

The division maintains liaison with corps and the 1st and 3rd Divisions. Passage liaison with the 3rd Division is established before Phase II. Guard handover liaison with both receiving divisions is established before Phase V. The division artillery group maintains direct coordination with adjacent artillery groups during passage and disengagement.

### b. Signal

- Primary communications are the division coalition tactical network.
- Alternate communications use national tactical networks through liaison teams.
- Contingency communications use relay, scheduled reports, prearranged triggers, and messenger procedures.
- Passage, objective, main-effort shift, reserve, guard, withdrawal-route, and battle-handover reports have precedence.
- Brigades continue the last authenticated mission during degraded communications. A guard brigade may displace to avoid destruction while preserving contact and the higher purpose, then reports at the first opportunity.
- Communications failure does not authorize the 61st or 63rd Brigade to begin a planned reserve mission beyond immediate self-protection.

## 5. Annex Reports

- Brigades report current-phase task, combat power, enemy, route, handover, reserve, and decision-point status each turn.
- The current main-effort brigade reports ability to destroy the assigned Red force and requirements for continued support after each engagement.
- The artillery group reports priority, range, ammunition, displacement, and ability to support the current and next phase.
- During Phase V, each guard brigade reports contact, Red main-body estimate, delay achieved, next position, route, disengagement readiness, and need for reserve support.
- Conflicts among this annex, the base order, B-00, or the overlay are reported immediately; the later authenticated order governs.

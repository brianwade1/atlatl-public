# The Race for the Ordan Basin

## Order of Battle

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All countries, military organizations, unit designations, and geographic locations in this document are invented for research and educational use.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document defines the brigade-level order of battle and command relationships for **The Race for the Ordan Basin**. Play begins on **12 September 2031 at 0600 local time** as Western Compact–Ordan and Karsovian forces enter the basin from opposite directions.

The playable force consists of:

- **Blue:** the Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps, with three divisions and one corps-reserve brigade.
- **Red:** the Karsovian Eastern Army, with two maneuver corps, four divisions, and one army-reserve brigade.

Each side has **13 playable counters**. Headquarters and enabling formations are represented in the command hierarchy but are not separate Atlatl counters.

## 2. Atlatl Representation

| Atlatl type | Scenario representation | Principal characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `armor` | Armored brigade | Mobile striking force for penetration, counterattack, and exploitation. |
| `mechinf` | Mechanized brigade | General-purpose formation for mobile combat, urban operations, and route security. |
| `infantry` | Infantry brigade | Formation suited to restrictive terrain, urban defense, and crossing security. |
| `artillery` | Division or corps artillery group | General-support fires with greater range than maneuver formations. |

The counters aggregate capabilities that would exist below the represented headquarters level:

- **Command and reconnaissance:** division and corps headquarters, staff functions, reconnaissance, liaison, and communications are reflected in player decisions and the written orders.
- **Mobility and protection:** engineers, bridging support, air defense, route clearance, and recovery are incorporated into parent formations or handled by controller adjudication.
- **Fires and information:** organic mortars, tactical fires, electronic warfare, cyber support, and intelligence are included in unit combat power; only the named artillery groups receive separate counters.
- **Sustainment:** logistics, maintenance, medical support, transportation, and replacement activity are abstracted into current strength and player-enforced route requirements.
- **Aviation:** rotary-wing and fixed-wing support is not represented by a playable counter in the base scenario.

These capabilities explain the formations' narrative tasks but do not create additional engine actions unless a controller explicitly adjudicates them.

| Side | `armor` | `mechinf` | `infantry` | `artillery` | Counters | Current strength |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps | 3 | 5 | 2 | 3 | 13 | 1,210 |
| Karsovian Eastern Army | 3 | 6 | 2 | 2 | 13 | 1,300 |
| **Scenario total** | **6** | **11** | **4** | **5** | **26** | **2,510** |

The sides are counter-balanced but not strength-balanced. Blue's four Ordan formations begin at 75–80 percent strength; all other units begin at full strength. Blue compensates with an additional artillery group and shorter access to Veyra from its entry area. Red has one more mechanized brigade and a 90-point aggregate strength advantage. All seven cities nevertheless begin neutral, so neither force receives engine control merely because a political partner is located there.

## 4. Blue Force: Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps

The Joint Land Corps supports the elected Ordan government in Veyra. Its mission is to secure that government and the basin transportation network in order to preserve Ordan sovereignty and deny Karsovia the ability to partition the country.

### 4.1 Command Relationship

```text
Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps
|-- 1st Ordan Mechanized Division
|   |-- 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade
|   |-- 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade
|   `-- 1st Ordan Artillery Group
|-- 3rd Compact Armored Division
|   |-- 31st Coalition Armored Brigade
|   |-- 32nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 33rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   `-- 3rd Division Artillery Group
|-- 6th Compact Mechanized Division
|   |-- 61st Coalition Armored Brigade
|   |-- 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade
|   `-- 6th Division Artillery Group
`-- 7th Coalition Armored Brigade — Corps Reserve
```

### 4.2 1st Ordan Mechanized Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 80 | Advance from the western setup zone to secure Veyra and the central routes. |
| 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | 75 | Move from the western setup zone toward Dalen and the northern crossing network. |
| 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | 75 | Move from the western setup zone toward Eren and the southern crossing network. |
| 1st Ordan Artillery Group | `artillery` | 80 | Support the advance to the capital and central crossings. |

The division is understrength because of defections, disrupted mobilization, and fragmented command. Its formations remain capable but cannot absorb losses as readily as the arriving Compact units. It is the Blue force best suited to early political-security and crossing tasks, but using all three maneuver brigades forward leaves no organic maneuver reserve.

### 4.3 3rd Compact Armored Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 31st Coalition Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Lead the division toward Novar and provide the northern striking force. |
| 32nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Advance toward Dalen or protect the northern flank. |
| 33rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Secure the Veyra–Novar route and reinforce the center. |
| 3rd Division Artillery Group | `artillery` | 100 | Provide general support to the division. |

The 3rd Division is Blue's principal crossing-seizure and passage force. The 31st Brigade supplies armored mass, while the 32nd and 33rd Brigades secure the northern and central shoulders. Its task is to create the conditions for the 6th Division's eastward counteroffensive rather than disperse permanently across every crossing.

### 4.4 6th Compact Mechanized Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 61st Coalition Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Serve as the division's mobile striking force. |
| 62nd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Advance toward Eren and the southern basin. |
| 63rd Coalition Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Secure the Mora Hills route and southern flank. |
| 6th Division Artillery Group | `artillery` | 100 | Support operations in the southern basin. |

The 6th Division is Blue's principal eastward exploitation force after passage through the Arven system. The 61st Brigade provides the initial armored striking power; the mechanized brigades successively support attacks, secure routes, and establish the later guard. Premature commitment before crossings and routes are ready risks fixing the division west of the river.

### 4.5 Corps Reserve

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 7th Coalition Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Remain uncommitted for counterattack, reinforcement, or exploitation. |

The 7th Brigade is the Joint Land Corps commander's only uncommitted maneuver reserve at scenario start. Its employment should answer a corps-level decision—defeat a penetration, reinforce a decisive crossing, or exploit an opening—not fill a routine terrain-holding requirement.

## 5. Red Force: Karsovian Eastern Army

The Eastern Army supports the emergency council in Ruda. Its mission is to seize the central and eastern Ordan Basin in order to establish the Ruda council as the effective national government and compel acceptance of a Karsovian-aligned Orda.

### 5.1 Command Relationship

```text
Karsovian Eastern Army
|-- 4th Maneuver Corps
|   |-- 8th Guards Armored Division
|   |   |-- 81st Guards Armored Brigade
|   |   `-- 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 15th Mechanized Division
|   |   |-- 151st Mechanized Brigade
|   |   |-- 152nd Mechanized Brigade
|   |   `-- 153rd Infantry Brigade
|   `-- 4th Corps Artillery Group
|-- 9th Maneuver Corps
|   |-- 12th Armored Division
|   |   |-- 121st Armored Brigade
|   |   `-- 122nd Mechanized Brigade
|   |-- 21st Mechanized Division
|   |   |-- 211th Mechanized Brigade
|   |   |-- 212th Mechanized Brigade
|   |   `-- 213th Infantry Brigade
|   `-- 9th Corps Artillery Group
`-- 5th Guards Armored Brigade — Army Reserve
```

### 5.2 4th Maneuver Corps

The 4th Maneuver Corps is oriented on Kasar, Dalen, North Pass, and Central Link. It is the Eastern Army's initial northern main effort and later provides northern security and reinforcing fires for the attack toward Veyra.

#### 8th Guards Armored Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 81st Guards Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Lead the advance through Kasar toward North Pass and retain the crossing. |
| 82nd Guards Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Secure the northern flank, protect the armored lead, and establish the later guard. |

The 8th Division is the corps' mobile northern formation. Its two-brigade structure gives it speed but little depth: if both brigades become fixed on crossing and guard tasks, the corps must rely on the 15th Division or Army reserve for a mobile response.

#### 15th Mechanized Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 151st Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Lead the attack toward Dalen and later establish the central guard. |
| 152nd Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Seize and retain Central Link and its approaches. |
| 153rd Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | 100 | Secure Kasar, Dalen, and the connecting northern route as directed. |

The 15th Division supplies the corps' staying power across the Dalen–Central Link sector. Its mechanized brigades generate the advance while the infantry brigade is best used to retain restrictive terrain and release mobile strength for subsequent phases.

#### 4th Corps Fires

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 4th Corps Artillery Group | `artillery` | 100 | Provide general support to 4th Corps and reinforce 9th Corps during the Veyra phase on Army order. |

The artillery group remains under 4th Corps control when it reinforces 9th Corps. The supporting relationship shifts fires priority, not command authority or ownership of the counter.

### 5.3 9th Maneuver Corps

The 9th Maneuver Corps is oriented on Ruda, Selin, Novar, Eren, and Veyra. It transitions from the initial supporting effort to the Eastern Army main effort for the central and western political objectives.

#### 12th Armored Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 121st Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Lead the attack through Selin toward Eren and establish the southern guard. |
| 122nd Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Secure Selin, retain Eren, and preserve the southern line of communication. |

The 12th Division is the corps' southern striking force. The armored brigade creates tempo, while the mechanized brigade converts movement into a sustainable crossing position. With only two brigades, it must avoid simultaneously accepting an unsupported attack, a wide guard frontage, and a fixed retain mission.

#### 21st Mechanized Division

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 211th Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Protect the corps seam, develop Blue forces, and establish the central guard. |
| 212th Mechanized Brigade | `mechinf` | 100 | Isolate and attack Veyra, then retain the political center. |
| 213th Infantry Brigade | `infantry` | 100 | Seize, restore, and retain Novar and its crossing. |

The 21st Division is the corps' central formation and becomes the decisive force for Veyra. Its plan deliberately moves the Novar retain force during the middle phase, creating a temporary crossing-security risk that corps must monitor and later correct.

#### 9th Corps Fires

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 9th Corps Artillery Group | `artillery` | 100 | Support Novar, Eren, and Veyra operations and integrate reinforcing fires during the decisive phase. |

The artillery group remains in general support to 9th Corps. During the attack toward Veyra it coordinates reinforcing effects from 4th Corps Artillery without assuming command of that formation.

### 5.4 Army Reserve

| Formation | Type | Strength | Operational role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 5th Guards Armored Brigade | `armor` | 100 | Exploit success or defeat a Blue penetration. |

The 5th Brigade is the Eastern Army commander's only mobile reserve and is not assigned routine city or crossing retention. Releasing it should correspond to an Army-level decision point, with route, replacement reserve, and follow-on command relationships made explicit.

## 6. Command and Support Relationships

### Blue

- The Joint Land Corps exercises operational control over all three divisions and the corps-reserve brigade.
- The 1st Ordan, 3rd Compact, and 6th Compact artillery groups remain in general support of their parent divisions.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control until committed.
- The 1st Ordan Division initially provides political-security and crossing forces; the 3rd Division establishes passage; the 6th Division conducts the principal eastward counteroffensive.
- Phase transitions, handoffs, supporting efforts, and reserve release are summarized in the [Blue master synchronization crosswalk](../operations_orders/Blue/blue_master_synchronization_crosswalk.md).
- Cross-attachment may occur through player orders, but the base OOB and scenario preserve the hierarchy above.

### Red

- The Eastern Army exercises operational control over the 4th and 9th Maneuver Corps and directly controls the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.
- Each corps controls two divisions and one artillery group.
- The 4th Corps is the initial northern main effort; the 9th Corps becomes the Army main effort for Eren, Novar, and Veyra.
- Corps artillery shifts priority between subordinate divisions as the fight develops. During the Veyra phase, 4th Corps Artillery reinforces 9th Corps fires while remaining under 4th Corps control.
- Phase windows, task handoffs, and reserve conditions are summarized in the [Red master synchronization crosswalk](../operations_orders/Red/red_master_synchronization_crosswalk.md).

## 7. Initial Strength and Readiness

The four Ordan counters begin below full strength:

| Formation | Current | Full |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade | 80 | 100 |
| 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade | 75 | 100 |
| 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade | 75 | 100 |
| 1st Ordan Artillery Group | 80 | 100 |

All other units begin at `currentStrength: 100` and `fullStrength: 100`. Because Atlatl removes a unit when it falls below 50 percent strength, the weakened Ordan formations require careful employment. This is a readiness distinction, not a deployment distinction: all 26 playable units begin in their side's edge setup zone rather than in a city.

## 8. Relationship to the Atlatl Files

The companion `oob.json` contains all 26 playable units and deliberately excludes headquarters and non-counter enabling formations.

- Each `longName` identifies the unit and its parent organization.
- `homeOrgId` and `taskOrgId` remain `null` because Atlatl does not enforce the command hierarchy.
- Unit locations in `oob.json` remain `null` so it can be used with the Atlatl unit-placement tool.
- The completed `race_for_the_ordan_basin.scn` assigns legal, unstacked default starting locations inside each side's edge setup zone; no playable unit starts in a city.
- Blue and Red may reposition during setup only within their respective 24-hex zones. All seven cities begin neutral.
- Unit information is open because fog of war is disabled.
- All counters use the native Atlatl types `armor`, `mechinf`, `infantry`, and `artillery`.

The OOB defines what formations exist. The `.scn` owns their default locations and runtime status, while the operations orders own phase-specific tasks and support relationships.

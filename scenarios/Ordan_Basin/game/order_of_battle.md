# The Race for the Ordan Basin

## Order of Battle

> **Scenario status:** Entirely fictional. All countries, military organizations, unit designations, and geographic locations in this document are invented for research and educational use.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document defines the brigade-level order of battle and command relationships for **The Race for the Ordan Basin**. Play begins on **12 September 2031 at 0600 local time** as Western Compact–Ordan and Karsovian forces enter the basin from opposite directions.

The playable force consists of:

- **Blue:** the Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps, with three divisions and one corps-reserve brigade.
- **Red:** the Karsovian Eastern Army, with two maneuver corps, four divisions, and one army-reserve brigade.

Each side has **13 playable counters**. Headquarters and enabling formations are represented in the command hierarchy but are not separate Atlatl counters.

## 2. Atlatl Representation

| Atlatl type | Scenario representation | Principal characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `armor` | Armored brigade | Mobile striking force for penetration, counterattack, and exploitation. |
| `mechinf` | Mechanized brigade | General-purpose formation for mobile combat, urban operations, and route security. |
| `infantry` | Infantry brigade | Formation suited to restrictive terrain, urban defense, and crossing security. |
| `artillery` | Division or corps artillery group | General-support fires with greater range than maneuver formations. |

Headquarters, reconnaissance, engineers, air defense, electronic warfare, logistics, aviation, and medical support are incorporated into parent formations or represented through scenario rules.

## 3. Force Summary

| Side | `armor` | `mechinf` | `infantry` | `artillery` | Counters |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Western Compact–Ordan Joint Land Corps | 3 | 5 | 2 | 3 | 13 |
| Karsovian Eastern Army | 3 | 6 | 2 | 2 | 13 | 1,300 |
| **Scenario total** | **6** | **11** | **4** | **5** | **26** |

The sides are counter-balanced but not strength-balanced. Blue's four Ordan formations begin at 75–80 percent strength; all other units begin at full strength. Blue compensates with an additional artillery group and the political advantage of Veyra lying nearer its entry area. Red has more mechanized combat power and a 90-point aggregate strength advantage.

## 6. Command and Support Relationships

### Blue

- The Joint Land Corps exercises operational control over all three divisions and the corps-reserve brigade.
- The 1st Ordan, 3rd Compact, and 6th Compact artillery groups remain in general support of their parent divisions.
- The 7th Coalition Armored Brigade remains under corps control until committed.
- Cross-attachment may occur through player orders, but the base OOB preserves the hierarchy above.

### Red

- The Eastern Army exercises operational control over the 4th and 9th Maneuver Corps and directly controls the 5th Guards Armored Brigade.
- Each corps controls two divisions and one artillery group.
- The 4th Corps is the northern effort; the 9th Corps is the central-southern effort.
- Corps artillery may shift priority between subordinate divisions as the fight develops.

## 7. Initial Strength and Readiness

The four Ordan counters begin below full strength:

| Formation | Current | Full |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 11th Ordan Mechanized Brigade | 80 | 100 |
| 12th Ordan Infantry Brigade | 75 | 100 |
| 13th Ordan Infantry Brigade | 75 | 100 |
| 1st Ordan Artillery Group | 80 | 100 |

All other units begin at `currentStrength: 100` and `fullStrength: 100`. Because Atlatl removes a unit when it falls below 50 percent strength, the weakened Ordan formations require careful employment.

## 8. Relationship to the Atlatl Files

The companion `oob.json` contains all 26 playable units and deliberately excludes headquarters and non-counter enabling formations.

- Each `longName` identifies the unit and its parent organization.
- `homeOrgId` and `taskOrgId` remain `null` because Atlatl does not enforce the command hierarchy.
- Unit locations in `oob.json` remain `null` so it can be used with the Atlatl unit-placement tool.
- The completed `race_for_the_ordan_basin.scn` assigns default starting locations inside each side's setup zone.
- All counters use the native Atlatl types `armor`, `mechinf`, `infantry`, and `artillery`.
